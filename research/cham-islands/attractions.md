# Attractions — Cham Islands

Slug: `cham-islands`
Vietnamese name: Cù Lao Chàm
Province: Đà Nẵng, xã Tân Hiệp
Kind: area
Corridor: south-central-highlands (South Central Coast & Central Highlands). The islands sit off Hội An. The board file still says `central-coast`. This dossier uses the corridor that holds Hội An and Đà Nẵng on the site.
Base a traveler sleeps in: a village house in Bãi Làng, the landing hamlet on Hòn Lao. Bãi Hương and Bãi Ông are the other named hamlets. No hotel brand.
Checked: 21 Oct 2026

## Why a night here

### The night on Hòn Lao

- The picture they expect: Hòn Lao from the sea, the forested ridge, the village shore, and the fishing boats in one frame. The water is the foreground. This is not a resort-tower beach.
- Where they sleep, as an area: a village house in Bãi Làng. Bãi Hương and Bãi Ông are the other hamlets on the same island (S5). The city secretary, at the 9 Jun 2026 meeting reported on 30 Jun 2026, asked Tân Hiệp to lengthen stays and to build the trip around community tourism, the sea culture, and the local trades, and said people already live legally in the core and take part in that tourism (S3). A hotel brand is not a pick. Most of the 30 Aug 2023 Border Guard report is a day crowd off the morning boats (S11). A night is the hamlet after that crowd has a boat back.
- Where the evening is: the shore road of Bãi Làng. The heritage centre’s 15 Sep 2025 food study says the evening meal now sits around 17:00–18:00, and that breakfast stalls line the Bãi Làng coast road and the area of Giếng Xóm Cấm (S12). No official page named a bar street.
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices):
  - Chùa Hải Tạng, Xóm Cấm, Bãi Làng. City page: https://danang.gov.vn/vi/w/chua-hai-tang-hai-tang-tu (S4). Heritage page: https://www.hoianworldheritage.org.vn/vi/news/Diem-di-tich/Chua-Hai-Tang-328.hwh (S6).
  - The landing at bến Bãi Làng, the island end of the Cửa Đại crossing (S11, S5). It is a pin, not a venue with a score.
- Hero must include: the island through the center, the ridge and the village shore, boats, and sea in the same frame.
- A crop that would fail: the 3.5 m Quan Âm in front of Hải Tạng, cropped tight, so the gate and the hill are gone (S4). Also a frame that is only open water, or only sky.

### Hòn Lao
- Vietnamese name: Hòn Lao
- Kind: zone
- Area: Tân Hiệp
- What the visitor does: Stays on the only inhabited island. The biodiversity agency says Hòn Lao is the largest island and the only one with residents (S1). The environment ministry’s 25 Feb 2025 notice says Hòn Lao is about 1,147 hectares and is the home of Tân Hiệp (S2). That hectare figure is one source.
- Time: A day from the morning boats, or a night in the hamlet
- Fee: The island visit fee is under the reserve entry below. It is not a separate Hòn Lao ticket on the pages read.
- From the base: This is the base
- When to go: The 2023 Border Guard report puts the pier crowd at 08:00–10:00 (S11). That is an arrival window from that date, not a gate hour.
- Mistake to avoid: Opening a second place page for Hòn Lao. The place is the islands.
- Local interest: The island itself
- Suggested slug: hon-lao
- Suggested dot: 5 — the island is the trip
- Photo must show: Hòn Lao from the water, ridge and shore together, boats in the frame
- Sources: S1, S2, S3

### Marine reserve and biosphere
- Vietnamese name: Khu bảo tồn thiên nhiên Cù Lao Chàm; Khu dự trữ sinh quyển thế giới Cù Lao Chàm – Hội An
- Kind: zone
- Area: The islands and the water around them, plus the Hội An shore in the biosphere’s transition zone
- What the visitor does: Crosses water that is zoned. The 17 Jul 2020 Quảng Nam decision says the marine protected area is seven islands — Hòn Lao, Hòn Cụ, Hòn Khô, Hòn Lá, Hòn Dài, Hòn Mồ, Hòn Tai — and 23,500 hectares, from 15°52'30" to 16°00'00" N and 108°24'00" to 108°33'30" E (S7). The environment ministry’s 25 Feb 2025 notice repeats those seven names, calls Hòn Lao the largest, and gives the newer nature reserve 23,530 hectares inside Tân Hiệp (S2). The city portal on 30 Jun 2026 says the cluster is eight islands and about 15 km² of land, in Tân Hiệp, and gives the biosphere 33,475 hectares: core 12,240, buffer 19,670, transition 1,565 across Tân Hiệp, Hội An Đông, Hội An, and Hội An Tây (S3). The biodiversity agency also says eight islands, about 15 km², 18 km from Hội An, and 947 aquatic species, and dates the UNESCO designation to 29 May 2009 at the Jeju session (S1). The visit-fee resolution uses 26 May as the UNESCO anniversary and does not narrate the session (S8). Do not collapse seven and eight, 23,500 and 23,530, or 26 May and 29 May. The UNESCO MAB page itself did not load on 21 Oct 2026, so it is not cited for species or for the date.
- Time: The zoning is the whole visit
- Fee: 70,000 VND per person per visit, Resolution 16/2026/NQ-HĐND of the Đà Nẵng council, signed 29 May 2026, in force 10 Jun 2026. The collector is Ban quản lý Khu bảo tồn Thiên nhiên Cù Lao Chàm. The same text is on the city Fatherland Front site and on the Hương Trà ward site (S8, S9). Free of that fee: Vietnamese citizens with a disability certificate under Decree 28/2012/NĐ-CP; Vietnamese citizens under the cultural-access policy in Article 2 of Decision 170/2003/QĐ-TTg; children under 16 and pupils under the Education Law; Vietnamese citizens 60 and over; poor and near-poor households under the government poverty line; diplomatic guests on a central or Đà Nẵng program; citizens of xã Tân Hiệp. Free days for everyone: four Tết days, from 29 or 30 tháng Chạp through the end of mùng 3 tháng Giêng; 29 March (Đà Nẵng liberation); 26 May (the UNESCO anniversary as printed in the resolution); 2 September. The boat fare is not inside the 70,000 VND. No boat fare was printed on these pages. Boat fare: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- From the base: The fee is for the visit, collected for the reserve. Where the desk sits: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: Free days above. A swim is not allowed in every zone. The 2020 regulation allows, in the strict zone, markers, approved research, and education — it does not list tourism there. It allows ecotourism in the restoration zone only when it does not harm the fishery or the marine ecosystem, and it forbids tourism boats from anchoring on coral or seagrass (S7). Named restoration waters include Rạn Mành, part of Hòn Tai, Bãi Nần on Hòn Lao, Rạn Mè – Bãi Ông on Hòn Lao, and Hòn Dài (S7). Strict waters include Hòn Tai, Bãi Tra on Hòn Lao, north Hòn Lao, Hòn Mồ, Hòn Lá, and Hòn Cụ with Hòn Khô (S7).
- Mistake to avoid: Paying 70,000 VND and thinking the boat is included. Swimming a strict zone because a day boat goes there.
- Local interest: The reserve
- Suggested slug: cu-lao-cham-reserve
- Suggested dot: 4 — the rules are why the water is not an open beach
- Photo must show: clear shallow water against the island shore, the island still in the frame. An underwater crop with no island does not say where it is.
- Sources: S1, S2, S3, S7, S8, S9

### Bãi Làng
- Vietnamese name: Thôn Bãi Làng
- Kind: area
- Area: Hòn Lao, Tân Hiệp
- What the visitor does: Lands, eats, and walks the hamlet. Hải Tạng, Đình Tiền Hiền, and Lăng Ông Ngư stand here (S5). The heritage food study says breakfast stalls line the coast road (S12). The 1998–1999 dig in the hamlet found Cham, Chinese, and West Asian ceramics, glass, stone, and carnelian, and traces of glass-working, and the centre reads the place as a Cham-period stop for foreign trading ships from the 8th to the 10th century. National relic, 2006 (S5). The page gives a coordinate string that does not sit inside the marine reserve’s latitude band in S7, so that string is not used as a pin.
- Time: The landing day, or the night
- Fee: The reserve visit fee (S8, S9). No separate hamlet ticket was printed.
- From the base: This is the base
- When to go: Morning, with the boats (S11)
- Mistake to avoid: Treating the archaeological trench as a museum with an hour and a door. The heritage page describes the dig, not an open gallery.
- Local interest: The fishing village
- Suggested slug: bai-lang
- Suggested dot: 5 — landing, food, and the night are here
- Photo must show: the landing, the boats, and the houses behind them in one frame
- Sources: S5, S11, S12

### Chùa Hải Tạng
- Vietnamese name: Chùa Hải Tạng (Hải Tạng Tự)
- Kind: sight
- Area: Xóm Cấm, thôn Bãi Làng, Hòn Lao
- What the visitor does: Walks to the island pagoda. It faces southwest onto the terraced fields called Đồng Chùa, with the hill behind it (S5). Built in Cảnh Hưng 19 (1758), about 200 m northeast of today’s site, and moved in Tự Đức 1 (1848) after storm damage (S4, S5, S6). The city page adds a 2002 repair of the main hall, a stone enclosure, a lotus pond, and a 3.5 m plaster Quan Âm in front of the three-door gate (S4). The heritage pages describe the yin-yang tile roof, bell and drum towers, wooden statues, and a bronze bell, and say the house serves both the islanders and trading ships that stopped to pray (S5, S6). National relic, 2006 (S5).
- Time: A short walk from the landing. Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Fee: Reserve visit fee if it is collected for the island (S8, S9). A separate pagoda ticket was not printed. Pagoda ticket: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- From the base: Walk through Bãi Làng
- When to go: After landing. Hours: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No clock was on the city page or the heritage pages.
- Mistake to avoid: A portrait of the Quan Âm that cuts off the gate and Đồng Chùa. Also reading the 1758 and 1848 lines as one construction date.
- Local interest: The island pagoda
- Suggested slug: hai-tang
- Suggested dot: 4 — the landmark inside a day on the island, not a day by itself
- Photo must show: the gate, the pond, and the hill behind, one frame. The statue stays whole, and so does the gate.
- Sources: S4, S5, S6

### Đình Tiền Hiền Tân Hiệp
- Vietnamese name: Đình Tiền Hiền Tân Hiệp

- Area: Thôn Bãi Làng

### Giếng Xóm Cấm

- Kind: sight
- Area: Thôn Bãi Ông

## Nightlife and evening

### Bãi Làng after the boats
- Vietnamese name: Đường ven biển Bãi Làng
- Kind: nightlife
- Area: Thôn Bãi Làng
- What the visitor does: Walks the shore road after the day boats have gone back to Cửa Đại. The heritage food study puts the evening meal around 17:00–18:00 (S12). It also says that, in the older fishing day, people ate between 16:00 and 17:00 and went out again until about 23:00 (S12). It does not say today’s visitor evening still runs to 23:00. No official page named a bar, a club, or a night market.
- Time: From the end of the afternoon into the early evening
- Fee: None recorded for the walk. The reserve visit fee is the daytime charge (S8).
- From the base: The shore road is the base
- When to go: After 17:00 if the meal hour in the 2025 study still holds. A later clock: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake to avoid: Importing an An Thuong bar street. The pages read do not have one.
- Scene landmarks, if any, with a primary URL: none that are bars. The landing and Hải Tạng orient the hamlet by day (S4, S11). During Cầu Ngư the evening exception is hát bả trạo at Lăng Ông Ngư; Báo Văn hóa, 20 May 2026, describes that program on the evening of 19 May 2026 (S15). That is the festival, not a weekly night.

## Lifestyle

### An ordinary day on Hòn Lao
- Vietnamese name: Một ngày ở Bãi Làng
- Kind: lifestyle
- What a day here actually is: The heritage centre’s 15 Sep 2025 study, after talking with islanders, says the old day had no breakfast: work from before dawn, first meal about 10:00, evening meal 16:00–17:00, then night fishing to about 23:00, mostly around the home reefs (S12). The same page says the day has shifted. People now eat lunch about 11:00–12:00 and the evening meal about 17:00–18:00, and breakfast stalls stand along the Bãi Làng coast road and by Giếng Xóm Cấm (S12). A visitor who landed on the morning boats walks to Hải Tạng across Đồng Chùa (S5), eats seafood, and is back on a boat unless they have a bed in the hamlet. The city wants that bed to be the point of the trip (S3). The hour the hamlet goes quiet was not given a clock beyond the meal times.
- When: Breakfast on the shore road; the walk in the middle of the day; the meal by 18:00 on the 2025 study
- Area: Bãi Làng, with the well as the other breakfast pin
- Suggested slug: bai-lang-day
- Photo must show: someone eating on the coast road with the sea behind the bowls, or the walk toward Hải Tạng with Đồng Chùa in the frame. Not a hotel lobby.
- Sources: S3, S5, S12
