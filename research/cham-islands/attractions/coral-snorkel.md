## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Snorkel with a permitted operator in the water the 23 Jan 2026 letter opens (S24). Service-zone shores: Bãi Xếp, Bãi Chồng, Bãi Bìm, Bãi Hương, and the named water at Hòn Dài. Restoration water, tight control: Rạn Mè – Bãi Ông, Hòn Tai (RT1–RT6), Hòn Dài, Rạn Mành, Bãi Nần. Activities named there include snorkel swimming, scuba, SUP, kayak, and glass-bottom boats. The strict zone is closed to swimming and snorkeling.
- How long on site: UNSOURCED.
- How long including the ride from the base: The boat to Bãi Làng first (S11). Stop minutes: UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Snorkeling the strict zone, or treating every named bay as the same rule (S24).
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The strict zone is closed. A business needs a permit from Sở Văn hóa, Thể thao và Du lịch (S24).
