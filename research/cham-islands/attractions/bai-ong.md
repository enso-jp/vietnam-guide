## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: The second hamlet on Hòn Lao. Giếng Xóm Cấm and Miếu Âm Linh stand here (S5). Offshore, Rạn Mè – Bãi Ông is restoration water where snorkeling is allowed under tight control (S24, and the 2020 names in S7).
- How long on site: Part of an island day. Ride minutes from Bãi Làng: UNSOURCED.
- How long including the ride from the base: UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating the 1999–2000 dig as an open trench. Anchoring was forbidden on coral in the 2020 regulation (S7). Decision 63/2026 replaced that decision (S22). The 2026 visitor notice allows snorkeling here only under tight control (S24).
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: This water is the controlled list, not the open service-zone beaches (S24).
