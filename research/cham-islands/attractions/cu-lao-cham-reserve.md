## What it is

- English name: Cu Lao Cham Reserve
- Vietnamese name: Khu bảo tồn thiên nhiên Cù Lao Chàm
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Zoned water around the islands off Hoi An
- Kind: zone

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Boat from Cửa Đại, the forested shore, then only the water the 2020 regulation allows a tourism boat to use.
- How long on site: UNSOURCED. The visit is the landing day. No minute count was printed.
- How long including the ride from the base: UNSOURCED. 18 km on the biodiversity agency page and about 20 km on the city temple page. Do not average them.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating the strict zone as a beach ticket, or assuming the 70,000 VND includes the boat.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The visit fee is not the boat fare.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: 70,000 VND per person per visit, from 10 June 2026. Resolution 16/2026/NQ-HĐND, signed 29 May 2026. Collector: Ban quản lý Khu bảo tồn Thiên nhiên Cù Lao Chàm.
- Child, height, or senior rule: Free of that fee: Vietnamese citizens with a disability certificate under Decree 28/2012/NĐ-CP; Vietnamese citizens under the cultural-access policy in Article 2 of Decision 170/2003/QĐ-TTg; children under 16 and pupils under the Education Law; Vietnamese citizens 60 and over; poor and near-poor households under the government poverty line; diplomatic guests on a central or Da Nang program; citizens of xã Tân Hiệp. Free days for everyone: four Tết days, from 29 or 30 tháng Chạp through the end of mùng 3 tháng Giêng; 29 March; 26 May; 2 September.
- What the basic ticket includes: The visit fee for the reserve. It does not include the boat.
- What costs extra: The boat fare. UNSOURCED. No snorkel price was printed.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S8 and S9 for the fee, the free cases, and the free days. S11 for the boat being a separate, unpriced crossing.

## Getting there

### From Hoi An

- Mode: Boat from bến thủy nội địa Cửa Đại – Cù Lao Chàm.
- Time: UNSOURCED
- Fare, if any: UNSOURCED
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S11 for the pier name and the morning crowd. S4 says about 20 km from the Hoi An shore to Hải Tạng. S1 says 18 km from Hoi An. Both stay.

### From Da Nang

- Mode: Road to the Cửa Đại pier, then the same boat. Da Nang International is VVDN, southwest of the city, 3.2 km from the city centre.
- Time: UNSOURCED for the road and the boat.
- Fare, if any: UNSOURCED
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S16 for the airport. S11 for the pier.

- Coordinates, only if a source prints them: S1 prints a box, 15°52'30"–16°00'00" N and 108°24'30"–108°34'30" E. The public place pin is the center of that box only, 15.938, 108.492.
