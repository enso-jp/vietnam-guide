## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: One of the islands in the cluster. The 2020 appendix splits it: a strict-zone polygon ST1–ST6 and a restoration polygon that includes RT1–RT4 with ST5 and ST6 (S7). The 5 Feb 2026 notice allows snorkeling under tight control inside RT1–RT6 and closes the strict zone to swimming and snorkeling (S24). Swiftlet caves on this islet are a cliff harvest, with Hòn Lao and Hòn Khô (S14).
- How long on site: UNSOURCED.
- How long including the ride from the base: UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Swimming the strict-zone side because a boat goes near the islet (S24).
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The strict zone is closed. The restoration points are the controlled snorkel water (S24).
