## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Land at Bãi Làng, continue to this hamlet, walk the bay and the sand, see Miếu Tổ nghề yến on the sand rise beside khe Cây Cừa. A permitted operator may run swimming and snorkeling in the service-zone water named for this shore (S24).
- How long on site: A short hamlet walk. On-site minutes: UNSOURCED.
- How long including the ride from the base: Ride from Bãi Làng: UNSOURCED. Crossing is the boat to Bãi Làng first (S4, S1, S11).
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Planning a walk into the nine swiftlet caves. S14 describes a cliff harvest, in heavy surf, on Hòn Lao, Hòn Tai, and Hòn Khô. The 10 Jun marker project in S17 is under discussion, not a monument the page says is built.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Swimming and snorkeling here need a permitted operator. The strict zone is closed (S24).

## Season

- Sight-specific months, or leave to the place: Leave the swim season to the place. No official month list was on S24. Cave harvests are mid-April to early May and mid-August to early September (S14). Those are harvests, not a visitor season for the beach.
- One concrete climate fact, if a source gives it: The caves are in heavy surf (S14).
