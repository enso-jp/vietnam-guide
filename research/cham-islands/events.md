### Lễ hội Cầu Ngư
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Cầu Ngư Tân Hiệp
- Kind: festival
- When: The heritage centre says the islanders hold Cầu Ngư at Lăng Ông Ngư on 4/4 of the lunar calendar (S5). Báo Văn hóa, 20 May 2026, says the festival is held on mồng 3 and mùng 4 tháng 4 âm lịch, and that in 2026 those days were 19 and 20 May (S15). Do not collapse 4/4 and 3–4/4 into one line. The next civil dates: Check on arrival. Báo Văn hóa’s 2026 order: 19 May, nghinh thần at the island’s relics in the morning, nghinh thần nhập điện at the shrine, cúng tế túc at 11:00–11:30, hát bả trạo in the day and again that evening; 20 May, cúng âm linh in the morning, the main Cầu Ngư rite, then a tea from 10:30 to 13:00 (S15). A second paper’s clock was not fetched in full. Treat the 2026 hours as that paper’s report of that year.
- Where: Lăng Ông Ngư, thôn Bãi Làng, xã Tân Hiệp (S5, S15)
- What happens: Thanks to Nam Hải and the sea spirits, and a prayer for a safe boat and a full catch. Bả trạo is the rowing-song with the oar gesture (S15).
- Fee: Not printed on the heritage page or the Văn hóa report. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The reserve’s free-entry day on 26 May is a different date (S8).
- Not the same as attraction slug: ong-ngu
- Sources: S5, S15

### Swiftlet harvest
- Vietnamese name: Khai thác yến sào
- Kind: activity
- When: The city portal, 28 May 2026, says two harvests: mid-April to early May, after the birds have laid both eggs, and mid-August to early September, when the young have left the nest. Each bout lasts about 4–5 days (S14).
- Where: Nine caves on the cliffs of Hòn Lao, Hòn Tai, and Hòn Khô. The page says the cliffs take heavy surf all year (S14).
- What happens: A licensed harvest of Aerodramus fuciphagus nests, not a visitor circuit. The geographic indication “Cù Lao Chàm – Hội An” was registered on 23 Nov 2020 (S14).
- Fee: Not a ticket. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED, and do not invent a cave fee.
- Not the same as attraction slug: mieu-to-nghe-yen
- Sources: S14

### Reef stop
- Vietnamese name: Du lịch sinh thái ở phân khu phục hồi
- Kind: activity
- When: Only when the boat is cleared. No clock was printed. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Where: Restoration water named in the 17 Jul 2020 regulation: Rạn Mành, part of Hòn Tai, Bãi Nần, Rạn Mè – Bãi Ông, Hòn Dài (S7). Not the strict zone.
- What happens: Ecotourism that does not harm the fishery or the ecosystem. Boats do not anchor on coral or seagrass (S7). No operator, no snorkel price, and no depth was on that regulation.
- Fee: The reserve visit fee is separate (S8). A snorkel price: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
