# Side trips — Cham Islands

Checked: 21 Oct 2026

The other islets in the cluster are part of this place, not a neighbor dossier. Hòn Dài, Hòn Tai, Hòn Mồ, Hòn Lá, Hòn Cụ, and Hòn Khô are named in the 2020 reserve regulation as water and cliffs (S7). They are not bases.

### Hội An
- Slug if it exists: `hoi-an`
- Travel time from this base: Boat from Bãi Làng to bến thủy nội địa Cửa Đại – Cù Lao Chàm, then the town. Crossing minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED (S11). The city temple page says about 20 km from the Hội An shore to Hải Tạng; the biodiversity agency says 18 km from Hội An city (S4, S1). Do not average them.
- Why go: The ancient town is the usual previous night, and the return the same day if there is no bed on the island. This pass does not rewrite the Hội An dossier.
- Overnight or day: Day, unless Hội An is the hotel and the island is the boat.
- Sources: S1, S4, S11

### Đà Nẵng
- Slug if it exists: `da-nang`
- Travel time from this base: Boat to Cửa Đại, then the road to the city. Da Nang International (VVDN) is southwest of the city centre, 3.2 km, on the AIP (S16). Minutes from the pier to the airport: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Why go: The airport and the city beach, a different base. Not an island afternoon.
- Overnight or day: Overnight if Đà Nẵng is the hotel. The island is still a boat day.
- Sources: S16, S11

### Mỹ Sơn
- Slug if it exists: `my-son`
- Travel time from this base: Boat to the mainland, then the road inland. Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No Mỹ Sơn timetable was opened. This pass does not rewrite that dossier.
- Why go: The Cham sanctuary is a different day from the island. Do not put it on the same morning as the 08:00 pier crowd.
- Overnight or day: Day from Hội An or Đà Nẵng, not from a morning on Hòn Lao.
- Sources: S11
