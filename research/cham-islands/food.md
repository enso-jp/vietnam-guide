## Comprehensive list of local food to try

Each line below is a public specialty unless the entry says it stays off the page.

### Canh cá
- Vietnamese name: Canh cá
- What it is: Fish soup in the daily rice. The 16 Sep 2025 study lists it with the other simple fish plates and says the kitchen uses little spice (S12). No fish, herb, or souring fruit is named.
- How to eat it: With rice. Lunch about 11:00–12:00, evening meal about 17:00–18:00 (S12).
- When to order it: Midday or evening.
- Area, not a shop: Bãi Làng and the other hamlets.
- Meal: main
- Sources: S12

### Chả cá Cù Lao Chàm
- Vietnamese name: Chả cá
- What it is: The island fish paste. Women sell the large fish and pound the small ones. Fried, in soup, or with tomato. Also the filling of bánh canh chả cá and bánh xèo chả cá. Some households sell the paste to visitors (S12). This is not Hanoi chả cá. Do not append it to that dish file.
- How to eat it: In the rice meal, or in the noodle soup and the crepe.
- When to order it: Lunch, evening, or the afternoon snack list (S12).
- Area, not a shop: Bãi Làng coast road and the stalls by Giếng Xóm Cấm.
- Meal: main
- Sources: S12

### Bánh bèo Cù Lao Chàm
- Vietnamese name: Bánh bèo
- What it is: Small steamed rice cakes. S12 names them in the afternoon list and prints no method. S21, Báo Quảng Nam carried on the heritage centre’s creative-city site, with no date printed: ordinary rice soaked and ground with Cham well water, steamed in cups over charcoal, a swirl in the middle when the fire is enough, soft topping of shrimp, pork, and wood ear, fish sauce from island fish, scallion. A brown sweet cake is eaten with the salty topping. Six to ten cups. A bamboo knife, not chopsticks or a spoon. Do not fold this into the Hue and Da Nang `banh-beo` file.
- How to eat it: Cut with the bamboo knife and spear a piece (S21).
- When to order it: Afternoon on S12. Hour Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Area, not a shop: Bãi Làng coast road and Giếng Xóm Cấm for the afternoon list. S21 names no shop. Do not equate giếng Chăm with Giếng Xóm Cấm. S21 does not.
- Meal: snack
- Sources: S12, S21

### Rau rừng
- Vietnamese name: Rau rừng, canh rau, rau luộc
- What it is: Greens in the daily rice, soup or boiled (S12). The 7 Jul 2014 note names forest greens (S13). The reserve board on 3 Mar 2026 names more than 16 leaves, including rau dớn, rau sân, rau lủi, and mã đề, boiled and dipped in mắm cái with chili and garlic (S23). S12 lists mắm cái among foods people now rarely use. Print both. Do not pick.
- How to eat it: With the midday or evening rice.
- When to order it: Midday or evening (S12).
- Area, not a shop: The hamlets.
- Meal: main
- Sources: S12, S13, S23

### Mắm
- Vietnamese name: Mắm
- What it is: A sauce islanders like. Many households have stopped keeping a jar through the storm season. A few still make it for visitors (S12). No method and no price. Household names stay in Notes. S23 still describes a mắm cái dip. S12 says mắm cái is now rare. Print both.
- How to eat it: On the side, if a household is still selling it.
- When to order it: With the rice (S12).
- Area, not a shop: The hamlets.
- Meal: main
- Sources: S12, S23

### Nước lá Lao
- Vietnamese name: Nước lá Lao
- What it is: A leaf drink. S13 says it is particular to Cù Lao Chàm and prints no recipe. S19, 25 Jun 2017, says the drink is also known elsewhere in Quảng, from 20 to 30 forest leaves, dried and mixed, astringent, fragrant, and a daily drink. Print both. Do not collapse “only here” and “also in Quảng.”
- How to eat it: Cooked and drunk. No cup hour.
- When to order it: Through the day. Hour Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Area, not a shop: The island. No stall named.
- Price: In 2017, about 20,000 VND for 300 g, wholesale about 50,000 VND a kilogram (S19). Not a current cup price. Current price Check on arrival.
- Meal: drink
- Sources: S13, S19

### Bánh ít Cù Lao Chàm
- Vietnamese name: Bánh ít, bánh ít lá gai; salty variant bánh ít mặn / bánh ít lạt; also bánh ít lạc (chay) on the 2015 note
- What it is: Ramie leaf, sticky rice, mung bean, banana leaf, steamed about one hour or one incense stick (S18, S20). Black outside, yellow inside, soft and chewy, lightly sweet leaf, nutty fragrant bean, keeps two or three days (S20). Salty cake adds mushroom, carrot, and sometimes pork (S18, S20). Own dish file. Do not edit `banh-it-la-gai.json`.
- How to eat it: As a sweet, or from the pier baskets (S20).
- When to order it: Summer is the busy making season on the 2015 note (S18). Hour Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Area, not a shop: Bãi Làng, Bãi Ông, and the pier baskets (S18, S20).
- Price: 15,000–20,000 VND for ten cakes on 28 Sep 2015 (S18). Current price Check on arrival. Household counts disagree: about four on S18; five in the commune on 25 Nov 2014, three of them for this cake (S20). Print both.
- Meal: sweet
- Sources: S18, S20, S13

### Bánh su sê Cù Lao Chàm
- Vietnamese name: Bánh su sê
- What it is: Tapioca starch cooked with water and white sugar about half an hour, shredded mature coconut, mung-bean filling, peanut oil on the hands, banana leaf tied with a banana-leaf string, steamed in an aluminum pot. The tie distinguishes it from bánh ít. No steam-minute count (S20). S13 names the cake and gives no method. The inventory says it had then been on the island about 20 years, learned from the mainland, and that one household made it. Do not edit `banh-su-se.json`. The Da Nang cake is coconut leaf and pomelo-flower water. This record does not use that method.
- How to eat it: From the pier baskets.
- When to order it: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED hour.
- Area, not a shop: The pier and Bãi Làng (S20). The maker’s name stays in the inventory and is not a pick.
- Price: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No sale price in S20.
- Meal: sweet
- Sources: S20, S13

### Cua đá
- Vietnamese name: Cua đá (Gecarcoidea lalandii)
- What it is: Off the daily table. A forest catch for visitors. Price described as high, no figure (S12). Decision 63/2026/QĐ-UBND, 28 May 2026, effective 10 Jun 2026, Article 13: carapace at least 7 cm, catch only 1 Mar–31 Jul, not in the strict zone or the restoration zone, only intertidal rock of the service-administration zone of the marine component (S22). The reserve board on 3 Mar 2026 says grill, or boil and eat with salt and pepper, and take only eco-labeled crab in season. Boiled meat: not fishy, sweet, fragrant, firm (S23). Article 13 as read does not say eco-label. Print the label only from S23.
- How to eat it: In season only. Out of season, do not order it.
- When to order it: 1 Mar–31 Jul (S22, S23).
- Area, not a shop: Tân Hiệp. No shop named.
- Price: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. S12 says high and prints no figure.
- Meal: main
- Sources: S12, S22, S23

### Mực một nắng
- Vietnamese name: Mực một nắng
- What it is: Unchanged method from S13. Tube squid, one sun, outside dry, inside raw. Vụ Nam June–September, vụ Bắc December–April. Confirm the seasons. S13 says the just-caught squid is what makes the plate sweet (ngọt). That taste is now on the dish. The 2014 headcount of dryers is not a current census.
- How to eat it: Grilled, or carried home (S13). S12 still lists dried squid.
- When to order it: The sunny months, if the 2014 seasons still hold.
- Area, not a shop: Bãi Làng. The dryer named in 2014 stays in Notes.
- Price: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Meal: main
- Sources: S13, S12

## Food streets and markets

### Bãi Làng coast road
- Vietnamese name: Đường ven biển Bãi Làng
- Kind: food-street
- What the visitor does: Eats at the breakfast stalls S12 places along this road, and at the stalls by Giếng Xóm Cấm. Afternoon choices named there: bánh xèo chả cá, bánh canh, bánh bèo, mì Quảng (S12). A market hall with the name Chợ Tân Hiệp appears on tour pages and was not on the heritage or city pages read. It is not a second entry.
- When to go: Morning for the breakfast stalls. The breakfast dish and the stall hour were not printed. Lunch 11:00–12:00 and the evening meal 17:00–18:00 (S12).
- Area: Thôn Bãi Làng, and the well in Bãi Ông as the other pin (S5, S12)
- Suggested slug: bai-lang-breakfast
- Photo must show: the coast road, the sea, and the bowls in one frame. Not a menu board cropped tight. No licensed frame of the bowls was filed. The public row uses the existing monogram.
- Sources: S12, S5
