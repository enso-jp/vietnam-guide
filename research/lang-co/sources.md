### S1
- URL: https://vietnamtourism.vn/index.php/tourism/items/477
- Publisher: Cục Du lịch Quốc gia Việt Nam (Vietnam National Authority of Tourism), destination page “Bãi biển Lăng Cô”
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: Beach about 10 km, beside National Highway 1A, near Hải Vân. Address as printed: thị trấn Lăng Cô, huyện Phú Lộc, tỉnh Thừa Thiên Huế. Club of the most beautiful bays, 30th member, June 2009; third Vietnamese bay after Hạ Long and Nha Trang. Gentle shore, white sand, clear water. Summer average about 25°C. Former Hành cung Tịnh Viêm under Khải Định, told as history, not as an open site. Small quiet town, beach games, seafood, Chân Mây, and a fishing village near the beach. About 60 km from Huế center, about 40 km from Phú Bài international airport, about 25 km from Đà Nẵng through the Hải Vân road tunnel. The pass road is longer and is the scenic choice. No second page was opened for the 40 km or 25 km figures.
