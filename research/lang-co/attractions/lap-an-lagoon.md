## What it is

- English name: Lap An Lagoon
- Vietnamese name: Đầm Lập An
- Kicker: Brackish lagoon between Phu Gia and the bay
- Kind: area

## What the visitor does

- The scene: Look from the shore, Phú Gia, or Nguyễn Văn. Oyster lines and round boats. Sand bridge (cây cầu cát) only at a strong low tide (S9).
- How long on site: About an hour from the bank if you only look. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED as a public duration. A boat outing of about two hours is one household feature (S18), not a public duration.
- Mistake: Walking the sand bridge as the tide turns, or folding An Cư into this lagoon.
- Notice: the sand strip is only there at a strong low tide.

## Fee

- Basic price: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED for lagoon access.
- The 300,000 VND figure is one raft in a feature (S18), not a gate. Do not publish the household as a pick.
- Source: S9, S18

## Hours

- Open: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Sand bridge: strong low tide only (S9)
- Best-looking months March–June: S9 only, not a harvest calendar
- Source: S9

## Getting there

- Nguyễn Văn, land side of the beach strip (S16, S17). About 450 m is the walking-segment design in S17, not a measured walk from the sand.
- Minutes from the beach: UNSOURCED
