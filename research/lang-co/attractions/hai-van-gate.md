Facts below are taken from the stored Lang Co dossier. No new web pass.

## What it is

- English name: Hai Van Gate
- Vietnamese name: Hải Vân Quan
- Kicker: 1826 gate on the pass above the bay
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene: Walk the restored gate and look back at the bay. Built 1826 under Minh Mạng. Open to visitors from 1 Aug 2024. Both cities manage it (S10).
- How long on site: About an hour on the summit — approximate, UNSOURCED. Public field is Check on arrival.
- Mistake: Using the tunnel and assuming you have seen the gate. Budgeting free entry from before 2 June 2026.
- Notice: the tunnel skips the gate.

## Fee

- Basic price: 70,000 VND per person per visit from 2 June 2026 through 31 Dec 2028, same for domestic and international visitors (S10, S11).
- Child and resident concessions: free under 1.2 m, half price for Hue and Da Nang residents and policy groups, in S11 only. Not in S10. Resolution PDF was not opened. Check the board.
- Source: S10, S11

## Hours

- Open: 07:00–17:30 daily, S11 only. S10 does not print a clock.
- Closed: no weekly close printed
- Source: S11 for the clock, S10 for the fee without a clock

## Getting there

- Up the pass road, not through the tunnel (S1, S10).
- From Da Nang the tunnel is about 25 km, S1 only, and it bypasses the gate.
- Drive minutes: UNSOURCED
- Coordinates: not recorded for this sight.
