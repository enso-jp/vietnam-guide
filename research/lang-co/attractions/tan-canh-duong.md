## What it is

- English name: Tan Canh Duong
- Vietnamese name: Bãi biển Tân Cảnh Dương
- Kicker (one line, what it is): The livelier beach north of the bay
- Kind: sight

It is a separate beach from Bãi biển Lăng Cô. Visit Hue gives it its own address, thôn Cảnh Dương, xã Lộc Vĩnh, huyện Phú Lộc, thành phố Huế, and its own paragraph (S2, re-read 23 Oct 2026). Decision 1774 calls the subzone Cù Dù - Cảnh Dương, inside the Lăng Cô - Cảnh Dương national tourism zone, not a new place (S5). VnExpress lists bãi Cảnh Dương, bãi Lăng Cô, and bãi Bình An as three beaches (S22). Dan Viet puts bãi tắm Cảnh Dương inside vịnh Chân Mây, with Bình An and the port, and says the beach is about 17 km from Đầm Lập An (S23). That 17 km is S23 only.

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Swim in clear water, then stay for bars, restaurants, and a beach bonfire with grilled seafood (S2). Dan Viet adds eating on a stilt house by the water and a rest (S23). VnExpress adds ordinary seafood restaurants and camping on the sand, and places the beach about 7 km off National Highway 1A (S22). The 7 km is S22 only. A meal price in S22, from 200,000 VND a person, is one article’s range, not a town fee. Do not publish it.
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The dossier’s “half a day” was approximate and stays UNSOURCED.
- How long including the ride from the base: Minutes from the Lang Co strip — UNSOURCED. Do not invent a drive time.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Expecting the quiet Lăng Cô curve under the pass. This is the louder beach (S2, S22).
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: This shore is not the Lang Co beach.
