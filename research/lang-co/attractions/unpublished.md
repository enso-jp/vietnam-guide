# Unpublished sights — Lang Co

Place: `lang-co`
Checked: 23 Oct 2026

Commons category “Lang Co” (120 files) and category “Phu Loc District” were listed on 23 Oct 2026. File search covered Cảnh Dương, Chân Mây, Phú Gia, Tịnh Viêm, Bãi Chuối, An Cư, Lộc Vĩnh, and Lộc Bình.

- `an-cu` — unpublished. VnExpress says Đầm Lập An’s other name is đầm An Cư, at the foot of đèo Phú Gia (S22, 23 Oct 2026). The zone office still describes Đầm An Cư at the foot of Hải Vân as its own “Venice of Huế” (S3, re-read 23 Oct 2026). The boat-race course runs from làng An Cư Đông across Đầm Lập An (S8). The village sits on that water. It stays off as a second lagoon. Both wordings are on the Lap An page.
- `bai-chuoi` — unpublished: no frame where the beach reads. `File:Bãi Chuối.jpg` (Christophe95, CC BY-SA 4.0, GPS 16.207956, 108.139766) shows a yellow strip far below a grassy slope. A card crop loses the beach. `File:Coast of Phú Lộc.jpg` is a nearer rocky cove, GPS 16.214562, 108.124249, and the caption says Lang Co is in the distance. It does not name Bãi Chuối. One unnamed cove is not a new sight.
- `binh-an` — unpublished: no frame tied to that name. VnExpress lists it as a third beach (S22) and does not locate it. No fee and no clock.
- `loc-binh-beach` — unpublished: no licensed photo of that beach. Visit Hue lists Bãi biển Lộc Bình at xã Lộc Bình, about 50 km south of Hue, on the same page as Tân Cảnh Dương (S2, 23 Oct 2026). It is north of the Lang Co strip. No second source for a visit length was opened, and no frame names the beach.
- `tinh-viem` — unpublished. The palace is history. The stele at làng An Cư Đông is not a gate with hours (S1). No Commons frame of the stele was found.
- `son-cha`, `bai-ca`, `hoi-mit`, `hoi-dua`, `cau-hai` — unpublished. Decision 1774 names them and the dossier left them without a visitor page (S5). Đầm Cầu Hai has frames, and the lagoon spans several districts. It is not a Lang Co sight. No visitor hour was opened on 23 Oct 2026.
- `lang-co-station`, `chan-may-port` — not sights. A station and a port stay off.
- `nguyen-van-evening`, `canh-duong-evening`, `bay-morning` — not their own pages. The evening at Tân Cảnh Dương is on that beach page. A morning on the bay is the Lang Co beach page.
- `phu-gia-pass` — unpublished: no frame of the pass road as its own sight. The lagoon page already uses the pass as the location of Đầm Lập An.
- `hai-van-pass` — not a second Lang Co page. The gate page stays. The Da Nang pass page is a different place and was not copied here.

Published after this pass: `lang-co-beach`, `lap-an-lagoon`, `hai-van-gate`, `tan-canh-duong`. The gap to five is one real sight that still has no licensed pair of frames.
