Province: Huế. Pages do not agree on the older names. The 2018 tourism-zone decision and the national tourism beach page still print thị trấn Lăng Cô, huyện Phú Lộc, tỉnh Thừa Thiên Huế (S1, S5). Visit Hue prints thị trấn Lăng Cô, huyện Phú Lộc, thành phố Huế (S2). The economic-zone office and the May 2026 market opening print xã Chân Mây - Lăng Cô, thành phố Huế (S3, S14, S15). This pass did not open the 2025 merger resolution. Use the name on the sign.
Kind: town
Corridor: North Central Coast, between Hue and Da Nang. The board file already says `central-coast` because that is the editorial beat. This dossier does not add a region and does not add a public place.

## Why a night here

- The picture they expect: A long curve of white sand and clear water under the Hải Vân ridge, with the lagoon on the land side of the same neck of land. Not the Huế citadel and not a Đà Nẵng city beach (S1, S2, S4).
- Where they sleep, as an area: Resort strip on the bay, which the national tourism page and Visit Hue both describe as a place of stays, without naming a brand as the reason to come (S1, S2). The 2018 plan also puts lower hotels on the south side of Đầm Lập An (S5). A hotel brand is not a pick.
- Where the evening is: Đường Nguyễn Văn, along the lagoon, if the walking nights are on. The 2024 trial was Friday, Saturday, and Sunday evenings and was explicitly subject to change (S16, S17). Tân Cảnh Dương is the louder beach in the same zone, with bars and a seafood bonfire in the Visit Hue note (S2). The town itself is described as small and quiet (S1).
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): The bay from the sand (S1). Đầm Lập An from Nguyễn Văn at sunset (S17). Đầm An Cư at the foot of the pass, which the economic-zone page separates from the bay (S3). No bar is a pin. Lăng Cô Beach Resort is named on the economic-zone page and stays in Notes, not pages.
- Hero must include: The curved sand, the water, and the Hải Vân ridge in one color frame, the shore through the center so a card crop still reads as a bay under a pass.
- A crop that would fail: Empty sand or empty sky. A resort facade with no water. A boat cut off so the bay is gone. A Đà Nẵng or Huế photograph reused for this town.

### Lap An Lagoon
- Vietnamese name: Đầm Lập An
- Kind: area
- Area: Brackish lagoon beside Highway 1A at đèo Phú Gia, Bach Ma on one side and the bay on the other (S9). The 2018 plan makes it the service and dining center of the national tourism zone, about 70 ha in that subzone (S5).
- What the visitor does: Looks from the shore, from Phú Gia, or from Nguyễn Văn. At a strong low tide a sand bridge (“cây cầu cát”) shows across the water. Oyster lines and round boats are the working landscape. Some visitors go out with fishers to pick oysters and eat on the water (S9, S17). One household’s reported price is not a town fee (S18).
- Time: About an hour from the bank if you only look. A boat outing was about two hours in one household feature (S18). Check on arrival — UNSOURCED as a public duration.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED for lagoon access. The 300,000 VND figure is one raft in a feature, not a gate (S18).
- From the base: The lagoon road is Nguyễn Văn, on the land side of the beach strip (S16, S17). Walk minutes Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: Dawn or dusk for the light on Bach Ma (S9). The sand bridge only at low tide. Báo Quốc Tế calls March–June the best-looking months. That line is one paper (S9).
- Mistake to avoid: Walking the sand bridge as the tide turns, or equating this lagoon with Đầm An Cư. No page opened here says they are the same water.
- Local interest: Oysters on Lap An
- Suggested slug: `lap-an-lagoon`
- Suggested dot: 5 — the other half of the picture, and the food
- Photo must show: Lagoon water, oyster stakes or a round boat, and the mountain behind, not a plate cropped tight
- Sources: S5, S9, S17, S18

### Hai Van Gate
- Vietnamese name: Hải Vân Quan
- Kind: sight
- Area: Summit of đèo Hải Vân, above 500 m, on the Huế–Đà Nẵng boundary (S10). The south edge of the 2018 tourism zone is the pass (S5).
- What the visitor does: Walks the restored Nguyễn gate and looks back at Vịnh Lăng Cô. Built in 1826 under Minh Mạng as the southern gate of the capital. Open to visitors from 1 Aug 2024. Both cities manage it (S10).
- Time: About an hour on the summit — approximate, UNSOURCED
- Fee: 70,000 VND per person per visit from 2 June 2026 through 31 Dec 2028, same for domestic and international visitors (S10, S11). VnExpress, citing the center, adds free entry under 1.2 m and half price for Huế and Đà Nẵng residents and policy groups (S11). Those concessions are not in the VTV piece (S10). The resolution PDF was not opened. Check the board.
- From the base: Up the pass road, not through the tunnel. The national page says the pass road is longer than the tunnel and is how you see the bay from above (S1). Drive minutes Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: VnExpress prints 07:00–17:30 daily (S11). VTV does not print a clock (S10). Morning if you want the bay without the gate in silhouette only.
- Mistake to avoid: Using the tunnel and assuming you have seen the gate. Budgeting the old free entry from before 2 June 2026.
- Local interest: Hai Van gate
- Suggested slug: `hai-van-gate`
- Suggested dot: 4 — the view that explains the bay, and a short outing, not the whole night
- Photo must show: The gate and the bay or the sea in one frame. A tight portrait of the brickwork loses the coast on a landscape card.
- Sources: S1, S5, S10, S11

### Tide on the lagoon, then a quiet town
- Vietnamese name: Nước ròng ở Lập An
- Kind: lifestyle
- What a day here actually is: Look at Đầm Lập An at dawn or dusk. If the tide is out, the sand bridge is the walk. Oysters are the meal. The national page calls the town small and peaceful (S1, S9). If Nguyễn Văn’s weekend nights are not running, the evening is the shore, not a downtown (S1, S16).
- When: Low tide for the sand bridge, which the paper says is short and easy to miss (S9). A clock for “the town goes quiet” was not printed. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Area: Đầm Lập An and the town streets
- Suggested slug: `lap-an-day`
- Photo must show: The sand bridge or the still lagoon with Bach Ma, people on the near shore so the frame is not empty water
