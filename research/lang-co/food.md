## Comprehensive list of local food to try

### Lap An oyster
- Vietnamese name: Hàu Lập An (hàu Lăng Cô)
- What it is: Oysters raised on the brackish lagoon since the late 1990s. Preparations named by Báo Quốc Tế: hàu nướng trui, cháo hàu, hàu xào hành tây, gỏi hàu (S9).
- How to eat it: On the lagoon, with the boats and the stakes in view, not as a ranked restaurant (S9). A household raft feature describes picking oysters then eating on the water (S18). That household is not a pick.
- When to order it: When the boats are working. The paper’s best-looking months for the lagoon are March–June, which is one source and not a harvest calendar (S9).
- Area, not a shop: Đầm Lập An and đường Nguyễn Văn (S9, S17)
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: `bun-bo-hue` if the night is actually in Huế
- Sources: S9, S17, S18

## Food streets and markets

### Lang Co market
- Vietnamese name: Chợ Lăng Cô
- Kind: food-street
- What the visitor does: Shops a new three-floor market that replaced an old one, with seafood, produce, and local specialties among 412 stalls. It sits beside Highway 1A, the lagoon, and the bay (S14, S15). The headline “ngôi chợ không ngủ” is not an opening hour. No clock was printed.
- When to go: Opened 19 May 2026 (S14, S15). Daily hours Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Area: Xã Chân Mây - Lăng Cô, thành phố Huế, on QL1A (S14, S15)
- Suggested slug: `cho-lang-co`
- Photo must show: The market front with the lagoon or the highway, stalls in the frame, not an empty facade
- Sources: S14, S15

### Nguyen Van lagoon street
- Vietnamese name: Đường Nguyễn Văn
- Kind: food-street
- What the visitor does: Eats along the lagoon. Tiền Phong says the street has long had specialty-food stalls used by visitors, and that it is where people watch sunset over Đầm Lập An (S17). The 2024 walking-street opening added a food stretch on the same road (S16).
- When to go: Daytime for the stalls. Weekend evenings only if the walking nights are still scheduled (S16). Confirm locally.
- Area: Lagoon bank, about 450 m from the new market toward Trường THCS Lăng Cô in the Tiền Phong description (S17)
- Suggested slug: `nguyen-van`
- Photo must show: Stalls or low tables, the lagoon, and the mountain, not a menu close-up
- Sources: S16, S17
