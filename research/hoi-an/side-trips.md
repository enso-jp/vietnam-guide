### Cu Lao Cham
- Or: Side trip placeholder: Cu Lao Cham
- Slug if it exists: none. Do not create `cham-islands`. The board still has it as a candidate.
- Travel time from this base: Boat from Cửa Đại. Minutes and the boat fare: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No operator timetable was opened this pass.
- Why go: The islands off the river mouth. Nhân Dân, 7 August 2026, reports the visit fee at Khu bảo tồn thiên nhiên Cù Lao Chàm as 70,000 VND per person per visit under Resolution 16/2026/NQ-HĐND, collected by the reserve’s management board (S22). The same article lists free cases, including children under 16 and pupils, Vietnamese citizens 60 and over, and residents of Tân Hiệp commune, plus free days: four Tet days, 29 March, 26 May, and 2 September (S22). The national tourism page says Tân Hiệp is about 15 km² and seven islands, Cù Lao Chàm the largest (S7). The Government portal left Tân Hiệp unmerged (S8). The boat is not inside the 70,000 VND.
- Overnight or day: Day if the boat is running. Confirm that morning.
- Sources: S7, S8, S22
