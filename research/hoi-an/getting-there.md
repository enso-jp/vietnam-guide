- Nearest airport: Da Nang International Airport, IATA DAD. The AIP names it VVDN — DA NANG/Da Nang International, administered by the Civil Aviation Authority of Viet Nam and Airports Corporation of Viet Nam, and places it southwest of Da Nang city, 3.2 km from the city centre. Aerodrome administration, customs, and air traffic services are H24 (S14). Vietnam Airlines uses the same IATA code, about 3 km southwest of the Da Nang city centre, about 10–15 minutes by car to that centre, with a domestic terminal and an international terminal, and gives the street as Nguyễn Văn Linh, Hai Châu District (S15). Those two lines agree on a short hop into Da Nang. They are not a Hoi An transfer time. Taxi and ride prices on the airline page are for central Da Nang hotels, and the page marks one fare caveat as “Source: Internet”. They are not copied here as a Hoi An fare.

- Getting around on site: Walk inside the ancient town during the pedestrian hours (S4, S5). The streets are narrow (S1). A ride or a bicycle is the way from An Bang. The 2024 coast article does not give those minutes (S6). Ride length: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Season limit on the journey: The Da Nang city portal, 4 May 2026, describes a tropical monsoon climate, a dry season from January to September, rain from October to December, rain concentrated in September–December, and floods usual in September–November with the northeast monsoon. Storms are mainly September–December (S12). The national tourism page gives Hoi An an average of 25ºC, 82 percent humidity, and 2,066 mm of rain a year, and does not split that into months (S7). Do not send someone over Hải Vân or along the Hoi An road in a flood week without checking that morning. The English city climate page was not re-checked on this pass.
