## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: The museum is the two-storey wooden house at 80 Trần Phú. The heritage overview says it was set up by Decision 139/QĐ-VH of 2 October 1995, with help from Japanese specialists. The collection is 368 objects, from the 9th and 10th centuries through the 19th, found at archaeological spots in Hoi An. They are the trade ceramics of the years when ships from East Asia and Europe used the port.
