## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: The yellow hall is Chợ Hội An. A heritage note of 7 June 2023 names the food hall inside it for cao lầu, and it does not give a street number. UNESCO’s list text includes an open market inside the inscribed property and does not name the street either.
