## What it is

- English name: Japanese Covered Bridge
- Vietnamese name: Chùa Cầu
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Covered wooden bridge at the west end of the old town
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: See the roof from the street, then the short wooden passage if the ticket in force includes the interior. Dogs at one end, monkeys at the other (S3).
- How long on site: About 20 minutes. The bridge is about 18 m (S3).
- How long including the ride from the base: A walk if you slept just off the old town. An Bang minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Budgeting an hour, or picking one build date.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The build date is not settled. Early 17th century, temple added 1653, name Lai Viễn Kiều in 1719 (S3). The French page says 1593 and also the early 17th century (S3b). UNESCO’s English text says the 18th century (S1). The ticket that includes the interior is also not settled (S19, S20).

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: No separate bridge price on the monument page. From 10 Aug 2026 the city fee is 20,000 VND per person per building (S19, S22). The centre’s Jan and Mar 2026 notices put Chùa Cầu or Miếu Quan Công on an 80,000 VND ticket and a 120,000 VND ticket (S20, S21).
- Child, height, or senior rule: January 2026 notice, quoting Resolution 02: children under 16 and pupils free, Vietnamese citizens 60 and over free, plus the other free cases on that notice (S20). Whether Resolution 98 kept those cases was not on the city portal summary (S19).
- What the basic ticket includes: On the combination notices, the streetscape plus one of the two landmarks plus other works (S20, S21). On the August resolution, one building (S19).
- What costs extra: Not printed for this bridge.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S19, S20, S21, S22. Which desk is in force: Check on arrival.

## Getting there

### From the old town

- Mode: Walk
- Time: Minutes depend on the lane. The bridge is at Nguyễn Thị Minh Khai and Trần Phú (S3).
- Fare, if any: None for the walk.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S3

- Coordinates, only if a source prints them: The town’s UNESCO point is N15 52 60 E108 19 60 (S1). That is the property, not a surveyed bridge pin. Do not add a map from it.

## Season

- Sight-specific months, or leave to the place: Leave to the place. Floods can put Bạch Đằng, Nguyễn Thái Học, and Lê Lợi under water (S13). The bridge page does not give its own flood line.

## Photos
