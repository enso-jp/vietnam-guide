## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: The monument page calls Quân Thắng one of the old houses that still shows how a merchant family lived, more than 150 years old, in the style of the Chinese coast. It says Kim Bồng carpenters made the woodwork. The plan is a one-storey house that runs through from Trần Phú to Nguyễn Thái Học, so you enter on one street and the back is the other.
