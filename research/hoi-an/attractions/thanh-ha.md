## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: The craft index places Thanh Hà in khối Nam Diêu, phường Thanh Hà, 3 kilometres west-southwest of the ancient town. A heritage article says the village is on the Thu Bồn bank, about 3 kilometres to the west, and that it was then in Cẩm Hà. Keep both bearings. The article gives the village more than 500 years and a busy stretch in the 16th and 17th centuries, when its pots and tiles were traded through the port. It also says Thanh Hà made the yin-yang roof tiles on the old houses.
