# Attraction research — Cam Thanh coconut water

Place: `hoi-an`
Slug: `cam-thanh`
Status: stored
Checked: 21 Oct 2026

## What it is

- English name: Cam Thanh Coconut Water
- Vietnamese name: Rừng dừa Bảy Mẫu
- Kicker: Basket boats in the nipa water
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- Ride a basket boat through the nipa water at Võng Nhi, Cẩm Thanh (S27, S40).
- The February 2025 notice approves 17 routes: 13 with state-managed landing points, 4 oriented to private ones, seven lively and six quiet in the centre, two for the biosphere buffer and the bamboo-and-coconut craft village, two quiet routes elsewhere (S40). The craft village is a route, not a separate sight.
- How long on site: A boat hour. The 2022 article prints 45 minutes for a wooden boat (S27). One source. Public duration stays a boat hour. Confirm the minutes.
- Mistake: Quoting the 2022 boat prices as this week’s tariff.

## Fee

- Entry, from 10 Aug 2026: 30,000 VND per person per visit. Resolution 94/2026/NQ-HĐND of 31 Jul 2026 (S38, S39). Same figure in both. Not a foreigner split.
- Free cases, named on the city portal only (S38): Vietnamese disabled people with the certificate, Vietnamese people covered by Decision 170/2003, children under 16 and pupils, Vietnamese citizens 60 and over, diplomatic guests. S39 says some groups are free and does not repeat the list.
- Boat: Check on arrival. S27 printed 150,000 VND for a basket boat for two adults and 450,000 VND for a wooden boat for up to six people for 45 minutes. No second source. Those figures stay off the public page.

## Hours

- February 2025 notice: basket boats 07:00–18:00. The commune can stop them in bad weather (S40).
- November 2022 article: summer 07:30–18:30, winter 08:00–17:00. Months not defined (S27).
- Public hours print both and say check on arrival. Do not collapse them.

## Getting there

### From the old town

- About 3 km and about 5 minutes by car on the 2022 article (S27). One source. Confirm the minutes. Cẩm Thanh is now inside Hoi An Đông (S8).
