## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Kim Bồng sits on the right bank of the lower Thu Bồn, opposite the ancient town, in what the 2011 heritage page calls xã Cẩm Kim. The page dates the village to about the 16th century. The work that is still practised is in four lines: wood for buildings, wood for the house, boatbuilding, and decorative carving. The old town’s carved houses, including Quân Thắng, are the work this village is credited with.
