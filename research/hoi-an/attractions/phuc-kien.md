## Fee

- No separate price in the monument file (S24). Assembly halls are optional works on the combination ticket (S20). August 2026 city fee is 20,000 VND per building (S19). Check on arrival.

## Getting there

### From the old town

- Walk to 46 Trần Phú. The file also describes the way from the Hoi An bus station (S24). Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.

## What the visitor does

- Three courts, about 120 m, main hall Thiên Hậu. Built 1697 as Kim Sơn Tự, rebuilt as the assembly hall from 1757 (S24).
