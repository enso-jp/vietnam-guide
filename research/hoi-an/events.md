### Hoi An lantern night
- Vietnamese name: Đêm phố cổ / Tái hiện Đêm phố cổ Hội An đầu thế kỷ XX
- Kind: festival
- When: Each 14th night of the lunar month (S33, heritage centre, 8 Jul 2025). The date moves. The Gregorian day is Check on arrival. The undated creative-city page prints 18:00–21:30 (S5). The dated July 2025 notice does not print that clock (S33). Use the lunar rule. Confirm the hour on arrival.
- Where: The ancient town. Electric light gives way to candles and lanterns (S33).
- What happens: Local groups keep the night: music, đường poetry, chess, traditional martial arts, tuồng, bài chòi, a folk-singing class, and traditional instruments. The July 2025 notice names the groups and does not sell a ticket for the night itself (S33).
- Fee: No night ticket on S33. The ancient-town sightseeing fee is a separate question (S19, S20).
- Not the same as attraction slug: japanese-bridge. The bridge is a sight. This is the monthly night.
- Sources: S5, S33

### Nguyen Tieu at Chua Ong
- Vietnamese name: Tết Nguyên tiêu
- Kind: festival
- When: The city page says people come to Chùa Ông on the 16th of the first lunar month (S25). For 2026 the heritage centre’s plan ran from 26 Feb 2026 to 4 Mar 2026, the 10th to the 16th of the first lunar month of Bính Ngọ, and the lantern night that cycle was the 14th, 2 Mar 2026 (S34). Next year’s civil dates: Check on arrival.
- Where: Chùa Ông at 24 Trần Phú, the assembly halls, and the ancient town (S25, S34).
- What happens: Worship, processions, and the monthly lantern night when it falls inside the festival. In the 2026 plan, Chùa Ông and Chùa Bà stayed open for incense across 3–4 Mar, and the centre said it would not sell sightseeing tickets for Chùa Ông, Chùa Bà, the Quảng Triệu and Triều Châu halls, Tụy Tiên đường Minh Hương, and đình Hội An during that festival (S34). That waiver is the 2026 plan, not a standing free year.
- Fee: Check on arrival outside that dated plan. The 2026 waiver is S34.
- Not the same as attraction slug: quan-cong. The temple is the sight. This is the lunar-new-year festival.
- Sources: S25, S34

### Hoi An summer beach festival
- Vietnamese name: Hội An - Cảm xúc mùa hè
- Kind: festival
- When: 2024 ran from 15 June to 21 July (S41). Opening days 14–16 June at Cửa Đại. The An Bàng program Mùa hè bất tận was at 16:00 on 12 July 2024 (S42). The next dates are Check on arrival. Do not treat 15 June as a standing annual date.
- Where: Cửa Đại, Tân Thành, An Bàng, and Cù Lao Chàm (S41). The July program is at An Bàng (S41, S42).
- What happens: At Cửa Đại, dance, music, food, a DJ, fire performance, beach games, kites, and OCOP stalls (S41). At An Bàng, sea-themed work in lanterns and recycled wood, coastal food, beach music, and sport (S41, S42).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Neither page prints a ticket.
- Photo: no licensed frame of the 2024 program. The card uses Cam An Beach 3, whose coordinates sit with the An Bang photographs. It is the sand, not the festival night.
- Not the same as attraction slug: an-bang
- Sources: S6, S41, S42
