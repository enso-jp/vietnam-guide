# Sources

Accessed 21 Oct 2026 unless a line says otherwise. Publishers are the pages themselves.

### S1
- URL: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/948/
- Publisher: UNESCO World Heritage Centre, Hoi An Ancient Town, inscribed 1999, criteria (ii) and (v)
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: North bank of the Thu Bon, 30 ha plus a 280 ha buffer, 1,107 timber-frame buildings, tiled houses in unbroken rows on narrow streets, open market, ferry quay, wooden Japanese bridge with a pagoda. English text says 18th century. Japanese text on the same page says the covered bridge dates from 1593. Coordinates N15 52 60 E108 19 60. The page still says Quang Nam Province. Entrance-ticket revenue is mentioned with no figure. Annual flooding is described as being controlled. The town is still lived in.

### S2
- URL: https://dsvh.gov.vn/khu-pho-co-hoi-an-478
- Publisher: Cục Di sản văn hóa (Department of Cultural Heritage), Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: Inscription on 1 December 1999. Ancient town on the lower Thu Bon, 30 km southeast of Da Nang. Japanese bridge (cầu Nhật Bản), assembly halls, market, narrow grid of streets, a living town. No drive time.

### S4
- URL: https://bvhttdl.gov.vn/hoi-an-mo-rong-khong-gian-pho-danh-cho-nguoi-di-bo-va-xe-khong-dong-co-20230510135809761.htm
- Publisher: Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, 10 May 2023
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: Pedestrian and non-motor scheme in the ancient town since 2004. Phan Chau Trinh extension from 15 May 2023, every day, 17:30–21:30 in summer and until 21:00 in winter, from the Nguyen Hue intersection to the Cao Hong Lanh junction. A later stage was expected from 1 January 2024, from 15:00, starting at the Hoang Dieu junction. Program stops named that month, including a music group at 49 Phan Chu Trinh. Not a 2026 schedule.

### S6
- URL: https://bvhttdl.gov.vn/quang-nam-hoi-an-khai-thac-khong-gian-du-lich-vung-ven-bien-20240619085052701.htm
- Publisher: Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism, 19 June 2024, citing Hoi An radio and television
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026

No hotel brand worth a pick.
