Accessed 21 Oct 2026 unless a line says otherwise. A direct fetch of the UNESCO list page returned 403. S1 is the brief synthesis that page returned in search the same day. Sentences not in that synthesis are not used.

### S1
- URL: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/948/
- Publisher: UNESCO World Heritage Centre, Hoi An Ancient Town, list page
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: North bank near the mouth of the Thu Bồn. Property 30 ha, buffer 280 ha. 1,107 timber-frame buildings in tight rows along narrow pedestrian streets, tiled, with an open market and a ferry quay. Street grid with one axis parallel to the river; building fronts on the street and backs on the river. Wooden Japanese bridge with a pagoda, dated on the English text to the 18th century. Coordinates N15 52 60 E108 19 60. The page still says Quang Nam Province. Inscribed 1999, criteria (ii) and (v).

### S2
- URL: https://dsvh.gov.vn/khu-pho-co-hoi-an-478
- Publisher: Department of Cultural Heritage, Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: Ancient town on the lower Thu Bồn, 30 km southeast of Da Nang. World Heritage recognition at the 23rd session in Marrakesh, 1 Dec 1999. Japanese bridge named among the surviving works. Moss-coloured streetscape and a chessboard of narrow streets. No clock time for the drive.

### S3
- URL: https://www.hoianworldheritage.org.vn/vi/news/Diem-di-tich/Chua-Cau-8.hwh
- Publisher: Hoi An World Heritage conservation centre, monument page dated 30 Dec 2011
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: Chùa Cầu built by Japanese merchants in the early 17th century. Temple added in 1653. Lai Viễn Kiều named by Lord Nguyễn Phúc Chu in 1719. Stands at the join of Nguyễn Thị Minh Khai and Trần Phú. Wooden bridge about 18 m, yin-yang tiles. Dogs at one end, monkeys at the other. National historic and cultural relic in 1990. The footer now gives the centre at 10B Trần Hưng Đạo, Phường Hội An, TP. Đà Nẵng.
