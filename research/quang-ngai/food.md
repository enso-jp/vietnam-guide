## Comprehensive list of local food to try

### Don
- Vietnamese name: Don
- What it is: A brackish bivalve that lives where the Trà Khúc meets the sea, the water locals call nước chè hai (S15). The culture office says it is caught on the river (S14). Yellow and black shells are both named (S15). It is not a priced menu item here.
- How to eat it: S15 scrubs the shells, soaks out the sand, boils them until they open, and puts the meat back with sliced onion and rice paper. Fish sauce and xiêm chili are enough, because mật don is the sweetness. Yellow and black shells are both named. S20 cooks don with hành tăm and serves grilled rice paper, herbs, fresh chili, pepper, and fried shallot, and calls it a small bivalve like a clam. S21 pours hot broth over chopped hành tăm, with herbs and rice paper. All three stay.
- When to order it: S15 peaks May and June. S21 says morning or evening. S15 prints no clock. A daily hour: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Price: S20 prints 75,000 VND for 250 g prepared, with nước cốt, and 15,000–30,000 VND for a cooked bowl. S15 and S21 print no price.
- Area, not a shop: The end of Trường Sa, where the river reaches the sea (S15), and the south side of cầu Cổ Lũy at thôn Phú Thọ when the festival is on (S14).
- Meal: breakfast
- Neighbor dish to link: none on this site yet
- Sources: S14, S15, S21
