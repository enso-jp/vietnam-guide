## Comprehensive list of local food to try

### Don
- Vietnamese name: Don
- What it is: A brackish bivalve that lives where the Trà Khúc meets the sea, the water locals call nước chè hai (S15). The culture office says it is caught on the river (S14). Yellow and black shells are both named (S15). It is not a priced menu item here.
- How to eat it: Shells are scrubbed, soaked to drop the sand, and boiled until they open so the broth stays sweet. The meat goes back with sliced onion and rice paper. The cook on the newspaper page says fish sauce and xiêm chili are enough, because a small part called mật don is the sweetness (S15).
- When to order it: The newspaper’s peak is May and June (S15). A commercial page says morning or evening (S21). Both stay.
- Area, not a shop: The end of Trường Sa, where the river reaches the sea (S15), and the south side of cầu Cổ Lũy at thôn Phú Thọ when the festival is on (S14).
- Meal: breakfast
- Neighbor dish to link: none on this site yet
- Sources: S14, S15, S21

### Banh xeo

- When to order it: No season was printed.
- Area, not a shop: Mộ Đức / Thi Phổ (S16). That town is not this city’s center.
