## Why a night here

### The river, then the local sand

- The picture they expect: Wake up on the Trà Khúc, the river the city is built on, with the south-bank street and cầu Thạch Bích in the same frame (S2, S11). The other picture is Mỹ Khê in Quảng Ngãi: 7 km of fine sand, casuarina behind it, and sông Kinh beside it, 12 km east of town (S3). That beach is not Mỹ Khê in Đà Nẵng. The airport is not the picture.
- Where they sleep, as an area: Rooms in the center, on the south bank around Nghĩa Lộ and Thạch Bích, or out at the Cổ Lũy / Mỹ Khê shore if the swim is the point (S3, S11). No hotel brand.
- Where the evening is: Phố đêm Sông Trà, Tôn Đức Thắng, from Công viên Thạch Bích to cầu Thạch Bích, on Friday, Saturday, Sunday, and holidays (S11). It is a walking street with food stalls, not a bar list.
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): The night street, https://quangngai.gov.vn/tin-tuc/chu-tich-uy-ban-nhan-dan-tinh-nguyen-hoang-giang-thi-sat-hoat-dong-cua-pho-dem-song-tra.html (S11). Mỹ Khê, https://vietnamtourism.vn/index.php/tourism/items/1326 (S3). Sơn Mỹ is a daytime memorial, not an evening pin, https://vietnamtourism.vn/index.php/tourism/items/1330 (S4). They are pins. No prices and no scores.
- Hero must include: The river, the south-bank street, and the bridge span in one frame, subject through the center. A beach frame, if that is the hero, needs the surf, the sand, and the casuarina line together, subject through the center. A standing figure is the wrong hero. The Sơn Mỹ statue loses the grounds if the crop takes only a torso (S4).
- A crop that would fail: Empty sky, empty sand, a hotel sign, fog with no shoreline, or a slice of the bridge railing.

The bureau dates the killings to 16 March 1968 and says the visitor stops at the statue, then the objects inside: a bronze tray with bullet holes, a child’s clothes and sandals, broken pots, a monk’s wooden bell, and a hair clip kept for eight years (S4). Outside the fence, stelae mark the hamlet sites.

### Thien An mountain
- Vietnamese name: Núi Thiên Ấn
- Kind: sight
- Area: Left bank of the Trà Khúc, xã Tịnh Ấn, huyện Sơn Tịnh (S6). About 2 km from cầu Trà Khúc 1 on the library page (S18).
- What the visitor does: Climbs the seal-shaped hill to the old pagoda and the tomb of Huỳnh Thúc Kháng (1876–1947). The bureau says Nguyễn Phúc Chu granted a plaque in 1716 and that the mountain, pagoda, and tomb were ranked a national relic in 1990 (S6). The library says the pagoda was built in Ất Hợi 1695 by thiền sư Pháp Hóa (S18). The plaque year and the building year are different facts. Both stay.
- Time: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Neither page prints a ticket.
- From the base: The library’s 2 km is from the bridge, not a timed drive (S18). Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: Morning, before the heat the bureau puts in April to August (S2). No gate hours were printed.
- Mistake to avoid: Quoting one height. The bureau prints 100 m (S6). The library prints 105 m (S18).
- Local interest: The pagoda hill
- Suggested slug: thien-an
- Suggested dot: 4 — the city’s own hill, a morning, not a whole day

### Chau Sa citadel
- Vietnamese name: Thành Châu Sa
- Kind: sight
- Area: Thôn Phú Bình, xã Tịnh Châu, huyện Sơn Tịnh (S5). The library puts it about 5 km east of Thiên Ấn, on the way toward Sơn Mỹ (S18).
- What the visitor does: Walks what remains of a Cham earth wall. The bureau says it is the only Cham earth citadel researchers had found, with about 3 km of wall still traceable, about 4 m wide and 6 m high, and a perimeter of about 4 km, ranked a national relic in 1994 (S5). The 3 km trace and the 4 km perimeter are both on that page. Both stay.
- Time: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. It is a wall in the open, not a museum hour the page describes.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- From the base: About 7 km northeast of the town center on the bureau page (S5). Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: Day, on the way to Sơn Mỹ (S4, S18).
- Mistake to avoid: Expecting a standing brick tower. The page describes earth ramparts and a moat (S5).
- Local interest: The earth wall
- Suggested slug: chau-sa
- Suggested dot: 3 — a short stop on the memorial road, not a day by itself
- Photo must show: The earth rampart and the moat, or the wall against the fields, subject through the center. A hole in the dirt with no wall fails.
- Unpublished: no licensed photo
- Sources: S4, S5, S18

## Lifestyle

### A day between the river and My Khe
- Vietnamese name: Một ngày sông Trà và Mỹ Khê
- Kind: lifestyle
- What a day here actually is: Morning is a bowl of don where the river meets the sea, at the end of Trường Sa, in the months the newspaper calls the peak (S15). The swim is Mỹ Khê, 12 km east, not a second city (S3). The walk back is the south bank. On Friday, Saturday, and Sunday the night street runs 17:00–22:00 (S11). A named cafe street was not on the provincial night page or the tourism transport page.
- When: Don’s peak on the newspaper page is May and June (S15). A commercial page says the bowl is a morning or an evening habit (S21). Both stay. The swim has no clock (S3). The street closes at 22:00 on the nights it runs (S11). A quiet hour for the whole city on other nights: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The old night market’s stop in 2021 is the only other night fact opened (S13).
- Area: Trường Sa at the river mouth for the bowl (S15). Cổ Lũy / Mỹ Khê for the swim (S3). Tôn Đức Thắng for the weekend evening (S11).
