## What it is

- English name: Tra Khuc river
- Vietnamese name: Sông Trà Khúc
- Kicker (one line, what it is): The river the city is built on
- Kind: area

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the bank. The evening, when it is on, is Phố đêm Sông Trà on Tôn Đức Thắng (S11, S12).
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED for a daytime walk. The street’s hours are 17:00–22:00 (S11, S12).
- How long including the ride from the base: This is the base if you sleep in the center (S2, S11). Minutes from the rail station: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating the whole merged province as this river. Kon Tum and Măng Đen are their own trips (S7). Naming the long trestle in the hero as cầu Thạch Bích. The dossier asked for that bridge in the frame. The licensed frame shows a long span and boats. It is not labeled Thạch Bích on the file. The second frame is cầu Cổ Lũy.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The street runs Friday, Saturday, Sunday, and holidays (S11, S12). Other nights, the older night market has been stopped since 2021 (S13).
