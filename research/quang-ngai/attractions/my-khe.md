# Attraction research — My Khe beach (Quang Ngai)

Place: `quang-ngai`
Slug: `my-khe`
Status: unpublished: no licensed photo
Checked: 23 Oct 2026

This is the beach at thôn Cổ Lũy, east of Quảng Ngãi city. It is not Mỹ Khê in Đà Nẵng. No public attraction file.

## What it is

- English name: My Khe beach
- Vietnamese name: Bãi biển Mỹ Khê
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Beach at Cổ Lũy, east of the city
- Kind: area
- Suggested dot, if a later pass publishes it: 4 (dossier). Do not invent a second dot.

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Swim and walk the sand. S3, reopened 23 Oct 2026, calls it fine, gently shelving sand, screened, with casuarina behind and sông Kinh beside it. The same page says visitors also go to the Sơn Mỹ memorial from here. S18, reread 23 Oct 2026 from the 14 Mar 2026 archive because the live library URL returned 503, calls the shore an arc between cửa Sa Kỳ and cửa Cổ Lũy, with a casuarina forest and fishing hamlets under the trees.
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No clock was printed. A beach day is the visit.
- How long including the ride from the base: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Kilometres are printed. Minutes are not.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating this as Mỹ Khê in Đà Nẵng, or treating the 342 ha plan as an open resort. S3 says the province has a master plan of 342 ha to build recreation areas, including khách sạn Mỹ Khê and a campsite. That sentence is a plan. It is not a hotel pick and not an open resort.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The sand length is 7 km on S3 and about 5 km on S18. Both stay. Do not average them.
