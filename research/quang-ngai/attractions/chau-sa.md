# Attraction research — Chau Sa citadel

Place: `quang-ngai`
Slug: `chau-sa`
Status: unpublished: no licensed photo
Checked: 23 Oct 2026

An earth wall on the way toward Sơn Mỹ. It is not a standing brick tower. No public attraction file.

## What it is

- English name: Chau Sa citadel
- Vietnamese name: Thành Châu Sa
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Cham earth wall on the memorial road
- Kind: sight
- Suggested dot, if a later pass publishes it: 3 (dossier). Do not invent a second dot.

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk what remains of the earth rampart and the moat. S5 says it is the only Cham earth citadel researchers had found, with about 3 km of wall still traceable, about 4 m wide and 6 m high, and a perimeter of about 4 km. The 3 km trace and the 4 km perimeter are both on that page. Both stay. S18 says it was built of earth and stone around the 9th–10th century and is more intact than other Cham earth walls in central Vietnam. Earth on S5, and earth and stone on S18, both stay.
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. It is a wall in the open. No visit clock was printed on S5, S18, or S30.
- How long including the ride from the base: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Expecting a standing brick tower. S5 describes earth ramparts and a moat.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The commune name is not one name. S5 says thôn Phú Bình, xã Tịnh Châu, huyện Sơn Tịnh. S30, dated 17 Sep 2026, says xã Tịnh Khê. S31, dated 20 Aug 2026, says xã Sơn Tịnh. All three stay. Do not pick one.

## Dates that both stay

- National relic in 1994 (S5, S30).
- S18: built around the 9th–10th century.
- S30: researchers had first put construction around the 8th–9th century. The same page says three tile kilns found in a 100 m² dig inside the walls date to the early centuries AD, earlier than that construction estimate. The kiln date is not a replacement for the wall date. The page calls the briefing result preliminary. The article is dated 17 Sep 2026 and reports the morning of 15 September. The body does not print the year beside 15/9.
