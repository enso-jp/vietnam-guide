## What it is

- English name: Thien An mountain
- Vietnamese name: Núi Thiên Ấn
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Pagoda hill on the left bank
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Climb the seal-shaped hill to the old pagoda and the tomb of Huỳnh Thúc Kháng (1876–1947) (S6, S18).
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. A morning, not a whole day.
- How long including the ride from the base: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Quoting one height. The bureau prints 100 m (S6). The library prints 105 m (S18).
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The plaque year and the building year are different facts. The bureau says Nguyễn Phúc Chu granted a plaque in 1716 (S6). The library says the pagoda was built in Ất Hợi 1695 by thiền sư Pháp Hóa (S18).
