## What it is

- English name: Son My memorial
- Vietnamese name: Khu chứng tích Sơn Mỹ
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Memorial grounds at Tịnh Khê
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the 2.4 ha memorial at xóm Khê Thuận. Stop at the statue, then the objects in the house of evidence. Outside the fence, stelae mark the hamlet sites (S4).
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Not a five-minute photo stop.
- How long including the ride from the base: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Adding the page’s counts together. The memorial commemorates 504 people (S4). The same page separately prints 47 at xóm Thuận Yên (thôn Tư Cung), 97 at xóm Mỹ Hội (thôn Cổ Lũy), 102 at the watchtower, and 15 at the cotton tree.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The management page prints no fee (S19). A taxi page prints about 20,000 VND and 7:00–17:00 (S23). A flower-shop page prints free entry and the same hours (S24). The fee is not chosen. Those hours are not the office’s.
