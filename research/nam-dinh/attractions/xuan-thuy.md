## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: A boat and a walk. Route 2, opened 23 Oct 2026, is one day for groups of 15 or more: visitor centre at 08:30, Cồn Ngạn ponds, the Cồn Ngạn hide, the Cồn Lu experimental forest by boat, cửa Ba Lạt, lunch on the boat, mangrove, and the clam beds (A2). Those clocks are the tour’s, not a gate hour. Route 3 is two days and one night, October to March, for groups of 5 to 15, with birding at Cồn Ngạn and Bãi Trong (A3).
