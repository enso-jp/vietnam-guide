## What it is

- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Enter by the five-door gate, cross the court and the lake, then walk Thiên Trường in the middle, Cố Trạch to the east, and Trùng Hoa to the west. The heritage page opened 23 Oct 2026 puts Trần Hưng Đạo in Cố Trạch and the 14 kings in Trùng Hoa (A1). The tourism rite page puts the 14 kings in Thiên Trường, Trần Hưng Đạo in Cố Trạch, and the 14 kings plus civil and military mandarins in Trùng Hoa (A2). Both assignments stay. The seal night is a different visit: the yard, not a daytime hall walk.
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No minute for a hall visit.
- How long including the ride from the base: About 3 km from the city center (A3). Minutes — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Coming on an ordinary evening for the seal, or treating this as Đền Trần Thương. Also picking one hall assignment.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The seal is one night, the 14th of lunar month 1. An ordinary day is the halls.
