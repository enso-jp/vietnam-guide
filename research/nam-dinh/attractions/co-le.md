## What it is

- English name: Co Le pagoda

- Kicker (one line, what it is): Lotus tower beside the hall
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: The tower in the pond, on a turtle, then the hall. A2, opened 23 Oct 2026, says the tower is 32 m, 9 storeys, built 1927, 64 steps, base 18 m by 10 m, on a turtle in the pond, with four mountains and four elephants. The tourism page stored 22 Oct 2026 says 12 storeys, built 1926–1927, 60 steps, and a bell of 9 tons and 3.2 m cast in 1936, and gives the road (A1). Vietnam.vn, stored the same day, repeats 32 m and 9 storeys, the bell at about 4.2 m with a mouth of 2.2 m, and the main hall at 29 m, and prints xã Cổ Lễ, tỉnh Ninh Bình (A3). All of those figures stay. Vietnamese Wikipedia, opened 23 Oct 2026, adds 98 steps and a second bell dated 2003 (A4). The 98 steps and the 2003 bell are one encyclopedia page. They stay in this file. They are not on the public page, because that page keeps the two figures already on A1, A2, and A3.
