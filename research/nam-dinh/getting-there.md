## What the maps print

Two geographies. Do not collapse them into one number.

The 28 Dec 2023 planning decision still describes tỉnh Nam Định: natural area 1,668.8 km², one city (thành phố Nam Định) and nine districts (Mỹ Lộc, Vụ Bản, Ý Yên, Nam Trực, Trực Ninh, Nghĩa Hưng, Xuân Trường, Giao Thủy, Hải Hậu). Its border sentence is north Thái Bình, south Ninh Bình, west and northwest Hà Nam, east vịnh Bắc Bộ. Coordinates on that page run from 19°54' to 20°40' N and 105°55' to 106°45' E (S2).

VietnamPlus on 24 Mar 2025 prints the area as 1,669 km² and the population on 1 Apr 2024 as 1,892,427. Its border sentence is northeast Thái Bình, southwest Ninh Bình, northwest Hà Nam, southeast vịnh Bắc Bộ (S3). Both the 1,668.8 and the 1,669 stay. The population figure is that date’s figure. A second population series was not opened.

Nghị quyết 202/2025/QH15, clause 8, passed 12 Jun 2025, puts the whole of Hà Nam, Nam Định, and Ninh Bình into a new province named Ninh Bình. After the arrangement that province is 3,942.62 km² and 4,412,264 people, bordering Hưng Yên, Phú Thọ, Thanh Hóa, Hà Nội, and Biển Đông (S1). That pair of figures is the new province. It is not a replacement for 1,668.8 or 1,669, and it is not the size of the city a traveler sleeps in. No street border is drawn here. The Government’s instruction to fix boundaries on the ground is the same resolution’s method, already used for other clauses. This dossier does not invent the line.

Pages opened in 2026 still use both vocabularies. The heritage page says phường Lộc Vượng, thành phố Nam Định, tỉnh Nam Định (S5). The 2026 ward plan and the police report say phường Nam Định, tỉnh Ninh Bình (S7, S27). The park’s own contact line says xã Giao Minh, tỉnh Ninh Bình (S15). The conservation page says huyện Giao Thủy, Nam Định (cũ) (S13). Where a page prints a name, that name is kept. A ward a page did not print is not invented.

The city portal footer prints the city office at số 10 Trần Đăng Ninh (S11). That is an office address, not a hotel street.
