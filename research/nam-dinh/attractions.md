## Why a night here

### The lake, the cathedral, the seal night

- The picture they expect: Wake up in a delta city, not in a karst bay and not in Hanoi’s old quarter. The city portal’s own list puts Hồ Vị Xuyên, the Trần Hưng Đạo statue on quảng trường 3-2, the cathedral, and the old Hàng streets at the center (S11). The other picture is the festival night at Đền Trần, when the yard is the point (S4, S6).
- Where they sleep, as an area: Rooms in the center, around the cathedral, Chợ Rồng, and the lake. Or rooms on the sand at Quất Lâm, Thịnh Long, or Rạng Đông if the night is the coast the planning decision names as the beach zones (S2, S11). No hotel brand. The city office at số 10 Trần Đăng Ninh is not a stay (S11).
- Where the evening is: The lake square, then the streets Dân Việt names for the afternoon bowl and the baked bun: Trần Hưng Đạo, Hàng Sắt, Lê Hồng Phong, Bắc Ninh, Lý Thường Kiệt (S18). On the 14th of lunar month 1 the evening moves to Đền Trần (S4, S6, S7). A bar street was not named on any page opened.
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): Đền Trần and chùa Phổ Minh, https://dsvh.gov.vn/di-tich-lich-su-va-kien-truc-nghe-thuat-den-tran-va-chua-pho-minh-2959 (S5). Nhà thờ Lớn, https://giothanhle.net/gio-le/nha-tho-nam-dinh-giao-phan-ha-noi/ (S20). Chợ Rồng, the 1922 market in the Dân trí piece, https://dantri.com.vn/mon-ngon-diem-dep/pho-thanh-nam-1418321945.htm (S17). They are pins, not a ranked list.
- Hero must include: The cathedral front and the square in front of it, subject through the center, with the tower kept whole. If the frame is the lake, the statue from the base of the pedestal to the top of the figure, and water in the same frame (S11, S20). A festival frame needs the hall gate and the yard, not a cropped seal.
- A crop that would fail: Empty sky, a single column, fog with no lake, a hotel sign, a Tam Coc karst, or the Phát Diệm stone church used as this city’s front.

Clause 8 of Nghị quyết 202/2025/QH15 put the old Nam Định province into the new Ninh Bình (S1). The 2023 plan’s 1,668.8 km² and VietnamPlus’s 1,669 km² stay beside 3,942.62 km². Those are different maps. See `getting-there.md`. No street border is invented here.

The yard has a five-door gate, a lake of about 1,000 m², and the giải vũ houses (S5). VietnamPlus calls this ground the second capital, Thiên Trường, with Phổ Minh (S3).
