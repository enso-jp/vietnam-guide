### Khai an at the Tran temple
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Khai ấn Đền Trần
- Kind: festival
- When: The tourism administration prints the 14th–15th of lunar month 1, and times the opening of the rite at 22:00 on the 14th (S6). The ministry prints the same night of the 14th, at phường Lộc Vượng (S4). For Xuân Bính Ngọ the ward plan prints a different clock on the solar days 27 Feb–4 Mar 2026 (lunar 11th–16th): guests leave the yard from 21:00 on 2 Mar, incense 22:15–22:40, the seal palanquin 22:40–23:10 (S7). The police report confirms that night happened (S27). Both the 22:00 sentence and the 2026 plan’s later clock stay. Do not pick one. The next solar date after 2026: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Where: Đền Trần. S5 and S6 say phường Lộc Vượng, thành phố Nam Định. S7 and S27 say phường Nam Định, tỉnh Ninh Bình. Đền Bảo Lộc is an incense stop at 21:30 on that plan, not a separate festival (S7).
- What happens: A seal rite for the Trần kings. S6 prints the seal text “Trần miếu tự điển tích phúc vô cương” and a procession of the seal chest from đền Cố Trạch to đền Thiên Trường, done by 14 elders of thôn Tức Mặc. The ministry adds the Ngọc Lộ palanquin, a water procession, and the fish rite (S4). S6 also names martial display, wrestling, bài bông, and the five-color flag with the character Trần made from Đông and A. S7 is the operating plan for one year, including an exhibition and a “chợ đồ xưa” on the Đông A square. It does not print a ticket.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No price on S4, S6, S7, or S27.
- Not the same as attraction slug: `tran-temple`
- Sources: S4, S6, S7, S27

### Phu Day festival
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Phủ Dầy
- Kind: festival
- When: The ministry prints the start of lunar month 3 (S4). VietnamPlus prints one solar window, 9–14 April, mapped on that article to the 3rd–8th of lunar month 3, and does not say those solar days repeat (S10). The next solar date: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Where: The Phủ Dầy complex, Vụ Bản on the district page (S9). Nearly 20 sites on the VietnamPlus article, centered on Phủ Tiên Hương, Phủ Vân Cát, and the mausoleum (S10).
- What happens: Processions, a torch procession at Phủ Tiên Hương, chầu văn, human chess, and the Mother Goddess practice UNESCO inscribed in 2016 (S4, S8, S10).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED

- Sources: S4, S8, S9, S10

- Kind: activity
- When: Route 3 on the park site prints October to March (S15). A daily gate hour was not printed. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
