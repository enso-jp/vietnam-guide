## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the shophouse streets. Hào Sỹ Phường, an alley on Trần Hưng Đạo, has kept its townhouses for the last 100 years. Mã Xa Hạng and Tuệ Huê Lý are lesser-known lanes. Bình Tây is the wholesale hall, more than 2,000 shops. An Đông is clothing. Đại Quang Minh is beads, ribbons, and fabric. Thiên Hậu is the pagoda. Nghĩa An communal house, a Guan Yu shrine in Teochew style, is a few steps from it. A late breakfast is Cantonese dim sum: prawn dumplings, rice rolls, radish cake.

- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating Bình Tây as Bến Thành, or photographing inside a pagoda without asking.
