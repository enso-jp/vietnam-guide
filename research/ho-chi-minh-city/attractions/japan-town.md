## What it is

- English name: Japan Town. VnExpress calls it Little Japan. Tuổi Trẻ calls it Little Japan Town and phố Nhật.
- Vietnamese name: Phố Nhật
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Japanese dining alleys off Lê Thánh Tôn
- Kind: area

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the alleys where Lê Thánh Tôn meets Thái Văn Lung, with Thi Sách and Ngô Văn Năm on the same walk. Eat sushi, sashimi, ramen, and tempura, and sit in the cafes. Alley 15B Lê Thánh Tôn is the older lane, with a graffiti wall. Alley 8A Thái Văn Lung is the lane of lights. By day the streets are quiet. From 17:00 the kitchens are open. The sound stays inside the doors.
