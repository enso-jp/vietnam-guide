## Why a night here

### The square, then Nguyen Hue at sundown

- The picture they expect: The cathedral front at the head of Đồng Khởi, both spires and the square, and the post office opposite (S1, S17). The evening picture is Nguyễn Huệ from City Hall down to Bạch Đằng, liveliest at sundown (S1). The airport is not the picture. The votive chapel now filed as the city hero is not the picture.
- Where they sleep, as an area: A room in the center, within a walk of Đồng Khởi and Nguyễn Huệ, or a room on Bùi Viện where it crosses Phạm Ngũ Lão, Đề Thám, and Đỗ Quang Đẩu, when the night is that street (S11, S16). S14 names historic hotels and coastal resorts. The brands are not picks. Ho Tram and Vung Tau are other nights. See `side-trips.md`.
