# Interests — Ho Chi Minh City

Slug: `ho-chi-minh-city`
Checked: 22 Oct 2026. A fact with a source id was opened and is stamped verified.

Titles are local to this city. They are not Da Nang’s beach groups. One card is the food guide. Resort Living is not a card for the walkable center. S14 names coastal resorts at Ho Tram and Vung Tau. Those are side trips, and the hotel brands are not picks. A beach card does not belong on the center.

The public food-guide slot is the first card. The local title below is the one to use.

### The southern table
- Why it is this place, not a generic label: S8, S9, S10, and S17 tie the day to cơm tấm, bánh mì, bánh xèo, hủ tiếu, and the night streets. S10 says Hanoi food stays lighter and that hủ tiếu, not phở, is the soup this city is known for. Both bowls stay on the food list because S14 and S11 also name phở here.

### Dong Khoi and the cathedral square
- Why it is this place, not a generic label: S1 and S17 put the cathedral at the head of Đồng Khởi, the post office opposite, and the opera further along the same street. S17 calls Đồng Khởi the walk that introduces those three. S1’s City Hall is the other end of the center, on Nguyễn Huệ, and it is not open.

The opened facade is Commons `File:20190923 Notre-Dame Cathedral Basilica of Saigon-8.jpg`, CC0, with scaffolding on the right and a gate in front. ### The night streets
- Why it is this place, not a generic label: S11 calls Bùi Viện a street that never sleeps and places it across Đề Thám, Phạm Ngũ Lão, and Đỗ Quang Đẩu. S1 says Nguyễn Huệ is liveliest at sundown, from City Hall to Bạch Đằng. S10 says Bùi Viện was made the second official walking street in 2017 and runs from dinner through the night.
- Fits interest key, if any: `nightlife`
- Icon: drink
- Links to attraction, dish, or place slug: `bui-vien`, `nguyen-hue`
- Food guide card: no
- Photo must show: Nguyễn Huệ after dark, the paving and the towers, people in the frame, not empty sky. The opened Commons frame is daylight and half sky, so it is a record of the street and a weak night card. Bùi Viện has no licensed street frame. Unpublished: no licensed photo of Bùi Viện.
- Sources: S1, S10, S11
