# Getting there — Ho Chi Minh City

Slug: `ho-chi-minh-city`
Checked: 22 Oct 2026. A fact with a source id was opened and is stamped verified. A line marked `UNSOURCED` is not.

The search slug stays `ho-chi-minh-city`. S17 calls it the former Saigon, the economic heart, and the main hub of the southern region. S15 says the city is still widely called Sài Gòn and that its history is a little more than 300 years. S18, clause 16 of Nghị quyết 202/2025/QH15, passed 12 June 2025, joins the whole of the former Ho Chi Minh City, Bà Rịa–Vũng Tàu, and Bình Dương into one city from 1 July 2025. The walkable center the visitor pages describe is still the old core. Vung Tau, Can Gio, and Cu Chi keep their own slugs.
