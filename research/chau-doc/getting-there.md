# Getting there — Chau Doc

Slug: `chau-doc`
Checked: 21 Oct 2026

## Nearest airport

- Can Tho Airport. Vietnam Tourism tells travelers flying from other Vietnamese cities to land there, then drive to Chau Doc (S1, S2). Chau Doc has no airport of its own on those pages.
- The provincial police page, writing in 2026, refers to An Giang before and after the merger with Kiên Giang (S19). This dossier does not use Phu Quoc or Rach Gia airports as the Chau Doc gateway. Vietnam Tourism names Can Tho.

## Nearest rail station

- None recorded on the pages checked. Vietnam Tourism’s Chau Doc transport section names the airport, the road, and river boats (S1, S2). The Chau Doc bus-station page names a road address and office hours, not a train (S22). A Vietnam Railways timetable was not opened in this pass. Do not invent a station.

## From the usual previous stop

The usual previous stop is Ho Chi Minh City, or Can Tho when the night before was already in the delta. Cambodia appears only as a river arrival Vietnam Tourism names. This dossier does not state a visa rule.

## Border gates, not a visa guide

- An Giang’s provincial geography page names two international gates, Tịnh Biên and Vĩnh Xương, plus Khánh Bình as a main gate being prepared for an upgrade (S24).
- The Ministry of Industry and Trade reported Government Resolution 146/NQ-CP opening an international road gate at Vĩnh Xương and joining it to the existing international river gate (S26). The economic-zone board’s 2016 plan puts Vĩnh Xương in then Tân Châu, Tịnh Biên in then Tịnh Biên, and Khánh Bình in then An Phú, with distances printed to Phnom Penh, not to the Chau Doc market (S27).
- Minutes from the river hotels to any gate: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Crossing rules are not in this dossier.

## Getting around on site

- Boats are ordinary transport. Vietnam Tourism describes ferries and sampans between the river, the islets, and the fish rafts (S3). A posted town-boat fare was not on an official tariff in this pass. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Sam Mountain is a short road from the old city. The national-tourism festival page prints both about 5 km and about 7 km in the same article (S23). Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The 2026 festival was held at Khu du lịch quốc gia Núi Sam, phường Vĩnh Tế (S7). The mountain is not inside the new phường Châu Đốc town ward (S21).
- Tra Su is 23 km outside Chau Doc on the Vietnam Tourism charms chapter (S3). Drive minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- The bus station’s own page prints office hours, morning 07:00–11:00 and afternoon 13:00–17:00, and a hotline used during office hours (S22). Those are office hours, not a departure board. Coach times: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Motorbike and taxi minutes between the market, the river, and Vĩnh Tế: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.

## Season limit on the journey

- Vietnam Tourism’s English card: rainy season May to November; best visit September to November, when floodwater fills Tra Su (S1). The Vietnamese place page: rain from May through October, high water August to November, dry and sunny November to April (S2). Record both.
- No page checked in this pass says the road from Ho Chi Minh City or Can Tho closes in flood season. Flood is the reason to be on the water, not a sourced road closure.
