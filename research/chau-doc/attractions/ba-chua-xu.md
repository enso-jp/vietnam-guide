## What it is

- English name: Ba Chua Xu Temple
- Vietnamese name: Miếu Bà Chúa Xứ Núi Sam

- Kind: sight
- District: Núi Sam (foot of the hill, phường Vĩnh Tế)

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Offering steps, then the statue inside. Pair with Tây An and the tomb.
- How long on site: Under an hour outside festival days. Quiet-day length UNSOURCED; the public line keeps “under an hour” from the dossier judgment, not a printed clock.
- How long including the ride from the base: Town road, about 5 km and about 7 km in one article (S23). Minutes UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Midday in the fourth lunar month.
- Notice: Heaviest days run from the first lunar month through the Via festival. UNESCO 22–27 of the fourth lunar month (S5). Rural tourism page 23–27 (S9).
