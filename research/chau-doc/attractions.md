# Research dossier — Chau Doc

Slug: `chau-doc`
Province: An Giang. In 2026 the provincial police page refers to the force before and after the merger of An Giang and Kiên Giang (S19). The provincial portal’s contact line is now in phường Rạch Giá (S14). This dossier is the Châu Đốc base only. It does not research Rạch Giá, Hà Tiên, or Phú Quốc.
Kind: city
Corridor: mekong-delta
Base a traveler sleeps in: River rooms in phường Châu Đốc, or a bungalow on Núi Sam in phường Vĩnh Tế. Never a hotel brand.
Checked: 21 Oct 2026

## Why a night here

- The picture they expect: The Hậu riverfront as Vietnam Tourism describes it for a puzzle: stilt houses, olive water, green islets, floating fish farms, barges and motorboats, under a bright southern sky (S3). Sam Mountain’s temple roofs sit just inland, not at the airport.
- Where they sleep, as an area: Small hotels along the Châu Đốc river, or a terracotta bungalow on Núi Sam so the morning is the plain and the sugar palms from above (S3). The mountain stay is in phường Vĩnh Tế, where the 2026 festival was opened (S7). The town ward after the 2025 rearrangement is phường Châu Đốc, formed from Vĩnh Nguơn, Châu Phú A, Châu Phú B, Vĩnh Mỹ, and part of Vĩnh Châu (S21). Ward office: 10 Lê Lợi (S20).
- Where the evening is: The Hậu riverfront after the temples (S3). At the colored fish-raft project, VnExpress describes colored lights on the water after dark (S15).
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): Miếu Bà Chúa Xứ at the foot of Núi Sam, the heart of the national tourism area (S7, S9). Rừng tràm Trà Sư, the flooded cajuput forest 23 km out (S3, S12). They orient the stay. They are not a ranked bar list.
- Hero must include: The river, a fish raft or a boat, and a green islet in one color frame, with enough of the far bank that a card crop still reads as a delta town. If the frame is taken from the mountain, it must include the plain and the sugar palms, not only sky.
- A crop that would fail: Empty sky and cloud. A tight portrait of the goddess statue that cuts off the offering steps and the mountain. A Can Tho market photo standing in for Châu Đốc. A resort pool with no river.

## Attractions (Sight, area of interests, zone, hub, etc.)

### Sam Mountain
- Vietnamese name: Núi Sam (Khu du lịch quốc gia Núi Sam)
- Kind: zone
- Area: Phường Vĩnh Tế, tỉnh An Giang. The 2026 opening ceremony was held here, not in the town ward (S7).
- What the visitor does: Walks a sacred hill covered with temples. Vietnam Tourism says about 200 large and small religious sites are scattered on the slopes (S1, S3). TITC says visitors can combine the Ba Chua Xu temple, the Thoại Ngọc Hầu tomb, Tây An pagoda, Phước Điền (Hang) pagoda, and a walk or cable car up the hill for a view over the delta (S9).
- Time: A morning for the cluster at the foot, longer if climbing. A fixed visit length: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Fee: Temple gates and the cable car: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No tariff was on the pages opened.
- From the base: Road from the river wards. The national-tourism festival page prints both about 5 km and about 7 km in one article (S23). Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: Outside the festival crush if the goal is the buildings. From the first lunar month through the Via festival, TITC describes crowds from early morning until late (S9).
- Mistake to avoid: Budgeting the whole mountain as one ticketed cave. The foot-of-the-hill temples and the climb are different efforts (S3, S9).
- Local interest: Sam Mountain pilgrimage
- Suggested slug: `sam-mountain`
- Suggested dot: 5 — the hill the town is known for
- Photo must show: The hill, a temple roof, and the flat delta in one color frame, subject through the center
- Sources: S1, S3, S7, S9, S23

### Ba Chua Xu Temple
- Vietnamese name: Miếu Bà Chúa Xứ Núi Sam
- Kind: sight
- Area: Foot of Núi Sam, phường Vĩnh Tế (S9)
- What the visitor does: Watches offerings on the steps — whole pigs, roast chickens, fruit, rice cakes, pink lotus — and incense in front of the statue inside. Vietnam Tourism says the statue is said to have been found on the mountain about 200 years ago (S3). TITC says the present temple sits at the foot, begun in the early 19th century as a bamboo-and-leaf shrine, later rebuilt with curved southern roofs (S9).
- Time: Under an hour outside festival days. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED for a quiet-day length.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- From the base: Same road as Sam Mountain (S23)
- When to go: Early, before the festival lines. TITC says the heaviest days run from the first lunar month to the Via festival (S9).
- Mistake to avoid: Arriving at midday in the fourth lunar month and expecting a quiet hall (S7, S9)
- Local interest: Sam Mountain pilgrimage
- Suggested slug: `ba-chua-xu`
- Suggested dot: 5 — the pilgrimage hall, not a side chapel
- Photo must show: The temple front and the offering steps in one frame, so a landscape crop does not cut the roof off the statue
- Sources: S3, S7, S9

### Thoai Ngoc Hau Tomb
- Vietnamese name: Lăng Thoại Ngọc Hầu
- Kind: sight
- Area: Foot of Núi Sam, visited with the temple (S9). The 2026 festival program includes bringing his spirit tablet from the tomb to the temple and returning it (S7).
- What the visitor does: Sees the tomb of the mandarin whose story is tied to the shrine. The culture department’s 2026 opening staged him and his wife Châu Thị Tế as the people who opened land and dug the Vĩnh Tế canal (S7). An architectural guide was not on the pages opened.
- Time: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- From the base: Same cluster as the temple (S9)
- When to go: With the temple, morning. During the festival the tablet procession uses this ground (S7).
- Mistake to avoid: Looking for the tomb in the town market. TITC places it at the mountain (S9).
- Local interest: Sam Mountain pilgrimage
- Suggested slug: `thoai-ngoc-hau-tomb`
- Suggested dot: 3 — part of the cluster, not the reason for the trip
- Photo must show: The tomb gate or mound with the mountain slope behind it
- Sources: S7, S9

### Tra Su Cajuput Forest
- Vietnamese name: Rừng tràm Trà Sư
- Kind: sight
- Area: Out of town, on the Vietnam Tourism charms chapter 23 km from Châu Đốc (S3). The rural-tourism page calls it a wetland conservation forest west of the Hậu, more than 845 ha, planted from the 1980s on acid lowland (S10). The charms chapter says 850 ha (S3). Record both areas.
- What the visitor does: Walks a bamboo bridge and takes a motor sampan (tắc ráng) or a rowed three-plank boat through duckweed, lotus, and water hyacinth, watching birds (S3, S11, S12). The operator price page describes the bamboo bridge as 2.2 km and calls it the longest in Vietnam (S12). The rural-tourism page says the bridge “Vạn bước xuyên rừng” is longer than 3,800 m (S10). Record both lengths. More than 70 bird types on the Chau Doc place page (S1).
- Time: Half a day from town. The on-site guide ticket is capped at 3 hours (S12). A full posted visit length: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Fee: Operator standing price page, applied from 1 Nov 2024, still the page online on 21 Oct 2026: gate 120,000 VND a guest; motorboat 60,000 VND a guest return; rowboat 60,000 VND a guest. Children under 1.3 m free; from 1.3 m, adult price (S12). The site homepage on this check showed a 30 June 2026 title about a new price notice. That notice was not opened, so the boat add-on should be confirmed at the gate. Check on arrival if the June notice changed the 2024 table. Observation-tower fee: not on the standing page. Spillway (đập tràn) trips are a separate product on the same page: 500,000 VND a guest for a group under 5, or 100,000 VND a guest return for a group of 5 or more (S12).
- Hours: An operator post for the April 2026 holiday says the forest is open 06:30–18:30 every day (S13). The price page’s bicycle loop is printed 07:00–16:00 (S12). The Vietnamese Tra Su story says bird decks are used 06:00–09:00 and 14:00–19:00 (S11). 19:00 sits after the 18:30 close on the operator post. Record both and confirm the last boat at the gate.
- From the base: Road, 23 km on the charms chapter (S3). Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: Flood season. English card: September to November (S1). Vietnamese place page: high water August to November, and that window is the one it names for Tra Su and the floating villages (S2). Dry-season green is also described (S11).
- Mistake to avoid: Paying only the gate and expecting the boat. The standing page lists the boat as a separate ticket (S12). Also do not confuse the forest with the spillway on the Vĩnh Tế canal (S12).
- Local interest: Tra Su by boat
- Suggested slug: `tra-su`
- Suggested dot: 5 — the wetland half-day
- Photo must show: Cajuput trunks, green duckweed or floodwater, and a small boat in one frame, subject through the center
- Sources: S1, S2, S3, S10, S11, S12, S13

### Fish-raft village
- Vietnamese name: Làng bè sông Châu Đốc (Làng bè Sắc Màu ngã ba sông Châu Đốc)
- Kind: area
- Area: The Hậu and the Châu Đốc river junction. The province places the colored project where visitors can combine it with Châu Phong and Đa Phước (S14). VnExpress places the rafts on the Châu Đốc river at then thị trấn Đa Phước, huyện An Phú, beside the old city, next to Cồn Tiên bridge (S15).
- What the visitor does: Goes by boat among raft houses that are also freshwater fish farms. Vietnam Tourism calls it a community about 50 years old, raising catfish and snapper for sale (S1). The province says the early-2024 “Làng bè Sắc Màu” painted 165 fish-farm rafts in six color blocks (S14). VnExpress says more than 160 cages, and also 161, over more than 1 km, painted red, yellow, orange, green, blue, and purple, and that lights show after dark (S15). Record both counts.
- Time: A boat hour. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED for a fixed length.
- Fee: A posted pier tariff was not opened. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Do not print a fare quoted only in a resident interview.
- From the base: Boat from the town bank, or the road to the Cồn Tiên bridge (S15). Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: High water, August to November, is the window the Vietnamese place page names for floating villages (S2). Lights after dark are described by VnExpress (S15).
- Mistake to avoid: Calling this Cái Răng. It is a fish-farm village, not Can Tho’s produce market (S1, S14).
- Local interest: Fish rafts on the Hậu
- Suggested slug: `fish-rafts`
- Suggested dot: 4 — the picture of the river stay
- Photo must show: Painted raft houses, the river, and a boat in one frame
- Sources: S1, S2, S14, S15

### Cham villages on the Hau
- Vietnamese name: Làng Chăm Châu Phong và Đa Phước
- Kind: area
- Area: Two banks of the Hậu. The province names Châu Phong in then thị xã Tân Châu, and Đa Phước in then huyện An Phú (S14). Vietnam Tourism’s English guide says Châu Giang and Châu Phong sit on two islets across the river and names the Mubarak mosque (S1, S3). The 2024 province article is the one that places Mubarak: it is in Châu Phong, listed as a national-level architectural monument, with Jamiul Azhar and Nia’mah also in Châu Phong, and Ihsan and Sunnah in Đa Phước (S14). This pass did not find a 2026 commune page that still uses Châu Giang as the official name. Do not merge the names into one building.
- What the visitor does: Visits a mosque, walks stilt houses and boardwalks, and watches women weave checked cloth. The province says the weaving in Châu Phong is a national intangible cultural heritage, recorded with lifecycle rituals at the end of 2023 (S14). Vietnam Tourism describes veils, sarongs, and prayers five times a day (S3).
- Time: Half a day if both banks are combined with the rafts. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Fee: Mosque and workshop gates: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- From the base: Boat or road. The province says the colored rafts make the link to both villages easy, without printing minutes (S14).
- When to go: Daylight, for weaving and the buildings. Prayer times are not a visitor timetable on these pages.
- Mistake to avoid: Publishing one mosque name for every village. Mubarak is Châu Phong on the province page (S14).
- Local interest: Cham weaving villages
- Suggested slug: `cham-villages`
- Suggested dot: 4 — a real half-day, across the river from the hotels
- Photo must show: A mosque front or a loom with a weaver, and the river or a stilt house in the same frame
- Sources: S1, S3, S14

## Lifestyle

### A river and mountain day
- Vietnamese name: Một ngày sông và núi ở Châu Đốc
- Kind: lifestyle
- What a day here actually is: Breakfast of bún cá at the market edge, then a boat to the rafts or the Cham bank, the Sam Mountain temples late morning, a palm-sugar sweet in the afternoon, and the riverfront when the heat drops (S1, S3). The town is a border river town of Kinh, Khmer, Cham, and Hoa observance, which is why the same day can hold a mosque and a goddess temple (S5, S14).
- When: Market first. Temples before the midday crush outside festival season (S9). Flood months push the boat earlier in the day (S1, S2). The hour the streets go quiet: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No page printed a curfew or a last-bus clock for the town.
- Area: Phường Châu Đốc for the market and the bank; phường Vĩnh Tế for the mountain (S7, S20)
- Suggested slug: `river-mountain-day`
- Photo must show: A morning bowl or the market produce, and a later frame of the river, not a single statue
- Sources: S1, S3, S5, S9, S14

Dawn boats selling fruit are described in the charms chapter as a floating market (S3). A second primary page naming a Châu Đốc produce market on the water, separate from the town market landing and from the fish rafts, was not opened. Treat that dawn scene as Check on arrival, not as a second Cái Răng.
