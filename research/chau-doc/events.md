## Events and to-do

### Festival of Ba Chua Xu at Sam Mountain
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Vía Bà Chúa Xứ Núi Sam
- Kind: festival
- When: UNESCO’s element page and the 2024 decision say the festival runs from the 22nd to the 27th day of the fourth lunar month (S5, S6). The An Giang culture department’s 2026 opening article says the same annual window, 22 to 27 of the fourth lunar month (S7). TITC’s temple page says 23 to 27 of the fourth lunar month (S9). An older national-tourism item says the night of 23/4 lunar through 27/4 lunar (S23). Record all three windows.
- 2026 civil dates, that year only: the culture department says the opening was on the evening of 6 June 2026 at the national tourism area (S7). The Vĩnh Tế ward notice says the ritual portion ran 6–12 June 2026, which it maps as 21–27 of the fourth lunar month, and that a wider program of performances ran 3 May–7 June 2026 (S8). Another year’s civil dates: Check on arrival.
- Where: Miếu Bà Chúa Xứ and Khu du lịch quốc gia Núi Sam, phường Vĩnh Tế, tỉnh An Giang (S7, S9). UNESCO still locates the observers in Châu Đốc city, naming Kinh, Khmer, Cham, and Hoa communities (S5).
- What happens: A palanquin procession, a bathing rite that changes the statue’s clothes and jewelry, and offerings (S5, S6). The 2026 culture-department list adds the street festival, the tablet procession for Thoại Ngọc Hầu, túc yết, xây chầu, chánh tế, and hồi sắc (S7). The ward notice adds art shows and says fireworks were part of the wider program that year (S8). Do not treat fireworks as an every-year fact unless a later notice repeats them.
- Recognition: Inscribed in 2024 on the Representative List, decision 19.COM 7.b.16, element 01999 (S5, S6). The An Giang English portal and the national tourism ceremony report date the session’s inscription to 4 December 2024 (S28, S29). The same portal says the Ministry recognized a national festival in 2001 and national intangible heritage in 2014 (S29).
- Fee: No ticket was printed on the UNESCO page or the culture-department article. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Not the same as attraction slug: `ba-chua-xu` and `sam-mountain` are the place. This row is the lunar festival.
- Sources: S5, S6, S7, S8, S9, S23, S28, S29

Other recurring town festivals were looked for on the culture-department festival article and the UNESCO record. Those pages describe this festival. A dated Chol Chnam Thmay or Ok Om Bok program for Châu Đốc was not opened. Do not add one.
