### Ba Chuc ossuary
- Or: Side trip placeholder: Ba Chuc
- Travel time from this base: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No kilometer figure was on the provincial pages opened.
- Why go: Khu di tích lịch sử Nhà mồ Ba Chúc commemorates 3,157 people killed in the Ba Chúc and Lương Phi area in the southwestern border war. The provincial portal dates that attack from the night of 18 April to 30 April 1978 in a literature-page retelling, and the 2026 leadership visit repeats the count of 3,157 (S17, S18). The same 2026 visit also went to the special national relic Óc Eo – Ba Thê, which is a different site (S17). A 2023 prosecutor outing paired the ossuary with Đồi Tức Dụp; that hill was not given a 2026 visitor page here, so it is not a separate sight (S30).
- Fee and hours: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Overnight or day: Day from Châu Đốc. The neighbor is not researched in this pass.
- Sources: S17, S18, S30
