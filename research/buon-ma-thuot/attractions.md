## Why a night here

- The picture they expect: Morning mist over coffee gardens, a phin cup on a wooden table, and red-bowl bun do in the afternoon — the highland city that trades in robusta, not beach sand (S18, S15).
- Where they sleep, as an area: Downtown Buon Ma Thuot, walking distance to the museums on Y Ngong and evening bun do streets. Not a resort strip. Lak Lake and Yok Don are day trips, not the usual hotel address (S3, S9).
- Where the evening is: Le Hong Phong and nearby streets when bun do vendors open; garden cafes in Ako Dhong for a quieter cup (S15, S17). No sourced nightclub strip; see Nightlife.
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): World Coffee Museum on Nguyen Dinh Chieu (S5). October 10 Square for festival nights (S12). Arul in Ako Dhong named on the provincial sample route (S17) — pin only, not a ranked bar list.
- Hero must include: Coffee plants or a phin cup in the foreground and enough city greenery or a longhouse roof to read as Buon Ma Thuot, not Da Lat pines alone.
- A crop that would fail: Military prison documents as the banner; an empty highway; a beach frame after the Phu Yen merger (coast is a long drive, S14).
