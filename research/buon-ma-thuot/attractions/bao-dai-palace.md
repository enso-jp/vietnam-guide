## What it is

- English name: Bao Dai Palace
- Vietnamese name: Biệt điện Bảo Đại
- Kicker: 1926 villa at 02 Y Ngong
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Stand in the court, look at the yellow villa under the trees, then walk the side of the long wing.
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The dossier’s 45–60 minutes has no page that prints a duration.
- How long including the ride from the base: Taxi, Grab, or a walk from the downtown hotels. Minutes Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating the villa as a hotel, or treating it as the same visit as Dak Lak Museum.
- Notice: The villa and the museum share 02 Y Ngong. The museum history describes a separate building, opened in 2011. The villa is the 1926 monument.

## Fee

Print both adult figures. Do not average.

- Provincial resolution 24/2024/NQ-HĐND, in force 16 Dec 2024 (A1): a public historic site, cultural work, or public museum is 30,000 VND an adult and 20,000 VND a child per visit point. A child under 6 is free. A Vietnamese citizen aged 60 or over pays half. The resolution does not name this villa.
- Visitor writeups (A2, A3): 10,000 VND a person, and 8:00–17:00. Both are secondary, and they may copy each other. The 10,000 VND figure stays beside 30,000 VND. It does not replace it.
- Public fee field: Check on arrival, with both adult figures in `admissionNote`.
- What the ticket includes: UNSOURCED.
- Source: A1 for the schedule. A2 and A3 for the other adult figure. A4 names the site and prints no price.
