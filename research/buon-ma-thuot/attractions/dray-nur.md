## What it is

- English name: Dray Nur Waterfall
- Vietnamese name: Thác Dray Nur
- Kicker: Wide waterfall on the Serepok
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- View the curtain. An optional walk behind the fall is only where local rules allow it (S3, S18).
- How long: Half day with the drive
- Mistake: Swimming where staff do not allow it. The rock is slippery.

## Fee

Print every figure. Do not average.

- Handbook, Dak Lak Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism (S3): 30,000 VND entrance and 50,000 VND combo with Gia Long. About 30 km from the city. About 250 m wide.
- News article, Dak Lak culture and tourism center (S19): about 25 km. Gate price cited at 50,000 VND for one reporting period.
- Department of Justice fee schedule (S20): scenic-site default 30,000 VND adult.
- Notice 19 Dec 2024, Dak Lak culture and tourism center (S11): new price announced. The amount is not printed. Current gate price is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED as a single number.
