## Fee

- Adult 150,000 VND. Child aged 7 to 12: 40,000 VND. Operator ticket page and FAQ agree (A1, A2).
- Under 7: UNSOURCED
- Guide, extra, groups of at most 30 (A2): Vietnamese indoor 150,000 VND a group, outdoor 175,000 VND, both 200,000 VND. Other languages (English, French, Chinese, Ede) indoor 200,000 VND, outdoor 250,000 VND. The “both” price for other languages was not in the text read.
- Group discount on A2: 7% over 50, 10% over 100, 15% over 200.
- Show tickets: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- A secondary itinerary printed about 100,000 VND and hours 7:00–17:45. It disagrees with the operator. Not used as the price. Recorded so it is not averaged in later.

## Hours

- Ticket page header and the gallery page: 7:00–18:00 (A1)
- FAQ: 8:00–17:00, Monday–Sunday, open in the evening on major holidays (A2)
- Both stay. Do not average. Public page not written, so the conflict is stored here.
