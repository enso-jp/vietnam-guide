# Attraction research — Dak Lak Museum

Place: `buon-ma-thuot`
Slug: `dak-lak-museum`
Status: unpublished — no licensed photo of the museum building or the outdoor longhouse
Checked: 23 Oct 2026

No public JSON. The current building opened in 2011 (A1, A2). Commons category “Dak Lak Ethnology Museum” holds interior cases and the yellow villa. The villa is Bao Dai Palace, filed on `bao-dai-palace`. A 2005 caption even calls that villa the museum. The museum’s own history describes a different building. Interior cases fail the photo brief, which asks for the building or the outdoor longhouse. No Commons file of the 2011 building or of the outdoor longhouse was found on 23 Oct 2026.

## What it is

- English name: Dak Lak Museum
- Vietnamese name: Bảo tàng Đắk Lắk
- Kind: sight
- Address: 02 Y Ngong, Buon Ma Thuot ward (A1, A3). The ward page still says Tan Tien ward (A2).

## What the visitor does

- Three display spaces: ethnic culture, history, and biodiversity (A1). The outdoor Ede longhouse is on the grounds (A4, dated 13 Aug 2026).
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED

## Fee

- Provincial resolution (A5): public museums 30,000 VND an adult and 20,000 VND a child. Under 6 free. A Vietnamese citizen aged 60 or over pays half. In force 16 Dec 2024.
- One visitor page (A6) agrees on 30,000 and 20,000 and instead frees a child under 1.2 m. That child rule is one secondary source. If a page is written later, print both child rules and do not average.
- The museum’s own “Vé Và Lệ Phí” page (16 Sep 2019) has the fee in images, not in the text. Not read as a price.

## Hours

- The museum’s “Thời gian mở cửa” page (9 May 2026) has the hours in images. The text has no hour. Check on arrival — the image was not transcribed.
- A6 prints Tuesday–Friday 7:30–16:30 and Saturday, Sunday, and holidays 8:00–16:00. One secondary source. Not adopted. It does not say Monday in words, so a Monday close is not inferred.

## Getting there

- Same ground as Bao Dai Palace, 02 Y Ngong. Walk, taxi, or Grab from the downtown hotels. Minutes Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- A6 names a second gate at 12 Le Duan. One secondary source. Not used until a second page confirms it.

## History used, with the disagreement

- A1: traditional house 2 Sep 1977 at 01 Le Duan; renamed 1990; groundbreaking 27 Feb 2008; inauguration 21 Nov 2011; more than 9,000 m2; 130 m by 65 m.
- A2: established 1976; new building invested in 2008; inaugurated 2011; 02 Y Ngong, Tan Tien ward.
- Founding year 1976 and 1977 both stay. Do not average. The 2011 opening is on both.
