## What it is

- English name: Ako Dhong
- Vietnamese name: Buôn Akô Dhông
- Kicker: Ede longhouses on the north edge
- Kind: sight. The dossier also marks it an area. It is one village walk, not a child place. The dossier says it stays an area card, not a child place. This pass publishes the village as one sight.

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the lane of Ede longhouses. Many are family homes, with a newer house behind or beside the old one. A pond with thatched shelters is part of the same village.
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The dossier’s 1–2 hours has no page that prints a duration.
- How long including the ride from the base: About 2 km north of the center (A1). Minutes Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. A blog’s 5–10 minutes is one secondary source and is not used.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating one cafe as the whole village. Stepping into a longhouse without asking. The 2019 report shows a homeowner living in hers (A2).
- Notice: none beyond the homes.
