# Attraction research — Trung Nguyen Coffee Village

Place: `buon-ma-thuot`
Slug: `coffee-village`
Status: stored
Checked: 23 Oct 2026

## What it is

- English name: Trung Nguyen Coffee Village
- Vietnamese name: Làng cà phê Trung Nguyên
- Kicker: Coffee gardens and a longhouse
- Kind: sight
- Separate from the World Coffee Museum on Nguyen Dinh Chieu. Different campus, different ticket.

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the old buildings, the coffee trees, and the Ede longhouse. The longhouse shows gongs, tools, farm tools, and weapons (A1). The photographed frames are the old-house court and the rock garden with a pond.
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The dossier’s 1–2 hours has no duration on A1. One visitor page says a full day. That single secondary line is not used.
- How long including the ride from the base: Taxi or motorbike from the center. A visitor page’s 5 km and 15–20 minutes by motorbike are one secondary source and are not used.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Going to the World Coffee Museum and thinking this village was included.
- Notice: The museum on Nguyen Dinh Chieu sells its own ticket. This village is the other campus.

## Fee

- Entry: No entry ticket. Two visitor pages agree (A2, A3). Drinks and food are separate.
- Drink and food prices: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED as a current band. A3 prints about 25,000–200,000 VND for drinks. That is one secondary source.
- The ward page (A1) names the village and does not print a price.
- Source: A2, A3 for free entry. A3 alone for the drink band, which stays Check on arrival.

## Hours

- Open: Check on arrival. A2 prints 7:00–22:00 every day, including holidays. That is one secondary source, so the hours field does not adopt it.
- A search snippet of another itinerary printed 6:30–22:00, and a Foody card printed 8:00–21:30. Those pages were not opened on this pass, so those figures are not printed.
- Closed: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Source: A2 only, and not adopted

## Getting there

### From the city center

- Mode: Taxi or motorbike
- Place: Ly Thai To at the Nguyen Huu Tho junction (A2, A3)
- Street number 163 and ward Tan An are on A2 only. Another snippet said Tan Loi. The number and the ward are not printed in public copy.
- Time: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Source: A1 names the village without a street. A2 and A3 place it on Ly Thai To.
