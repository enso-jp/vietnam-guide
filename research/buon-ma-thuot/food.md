# Food

Checked: 21 Oct 2026

## Comprehensive list of local food to try

### Bun do
- Vietnamese name: Bún đỏ
- What it is: Thick red-tinted noodles in a pork-and-crab broth with chives, blanched greens, fried shallots, and shrimp paste on the side.
- How to eat it: Stir shrimp paste and chili into the bowl; eat hot in the afternoon when stalls open.
- When to order it: From about 15:00 or 16:00 when the afternoon bun do counters appear (S15).
- Area, not a shop: Le Hong Phong has a row of bun do stalls (S15). Y Jut street named for a long-running vendor in the national tourism article — do not publish the vendor as a pick.
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: mi-quang (coastal), pho (nationwide)
- Sources: S15, S16

### Com lam with grilled chicken
- Vietnamese name: Cơm lam gà nướng
- What it is: Sticky rice cooked in bamboo tubes, often with forest chicken and sesame salt.
- How to eat it: Split the bamboo, mix rice with salt, and eat with grilled chicken pieces.
- When to order it: Lunch at tourist villages or Buon Don area restaurants.
- Area, not a shop: Buon Don tourism zone named in city media (S16, S17 sample route).
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none required
- Sources: S16, S17

### Highland coffee
- Vietnamese name: Cà phê
- What it is: Robusta and local pour-over or phin coffee; Buon Ma Thuot is a growing and trading center.
- How to eat it: Phin drip or iced milk coffee after breakfast; cup at a garden cafe counts as the scene.
- When to order it: Morning and late afternoon.
- Area, not a shop: Nguyen Chi Thanh and garden cafes in Ako Dhong (S17, S18).
- Meal: drink
- Neighbor dish to link: link existing `ca-phe` dish record
- Sources: S18, S5

### Wild vegetable and bitter eggplant salads
- Vietnamese name: Rau rừng; cà đắng
- What it is: Forest greens and bitter eggplant salad typical of central highlands meals.
- How to eat it: Shared plates with rice and grilled meat; bitter eggplant as a side or drinking snack.
- When to order it: Dinner with rice restaurants.
- Area, not a shop: City rice restaurants — no street named on sources checked.
- Meal: main
- Sources: S18 (editorial); detail thin — texture and taste left to copy pass if unsourced
