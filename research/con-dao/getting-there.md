# Getting there — Con Dao

Slug: `con-dao`
Checked: 21 Oct 2026

Côn Đảo is đặc khu Côn Đảo of Thành phố Hồ Chí Minh. The Government portal, citing Resolution 1685/NQ-UBTVQH15, says the special zone is the whole of the former huyện Côn Đảo (S6). The national park says that on 1 July 2025, under Decision 48/QĐ-UBND of the city, management of the park moved from the former Bà Rịa–Vũng Tàu provincial administration to the city (S1). That decision’s own PDF was not opened.

There is no railway on the island. No Vietnam Railways timetable was opened. A mainland station name for this trip was not on the pages read.

- Nearest airport: Cảng hàng không Côn Đảo, AIP indicator VVCS — BA RIA-VUNG TAU/CON DAO. The aerodrome reference point is the intersection of runway 11/29 and taxiway E2, printed as 084359N 1063747E. It is 10 km northeast of thị trấn Côn Sơn. Elevation 6 m. Reference temperature 27.5°C. Administration: the Civil Aviation Authority’s southern airport representative at Côn Đảo, and Airports Corporation of Vietnam, Cảng hàng không Côn Đảo. IFR/VFR. Getting around on site:
- Sleep and eat in the town. The night market sits on Võ Thị Sáu and Phạm Văn Đồng, in front of the daily market (S15).
- The airport is the Cỏ Ống side of the island, 10 km northeast of town on the AIP (S7). The park places Bãi Đầm Trầu northwest of Côn Sơn, near Cỏ Ống (S11, S12). Northeast and northwest are both printed. Do not average them. Minutes between the terminal, the beach, and the town: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- The park’s English tour page prints walk and boat legs: about 3 km from the town centre west to the Ông Đụng bay; about 25 minutes on foot from Ông Đụng back to town past Cầu Ma Thiên Lãnh; almost an hour on the Sở Rẫy trail to Ông Đụng; nearly 2 hours in the forest after a car or motorbike toward the Cỏ Ống airfield; about 45 minutes by boat to Hòn Bảy Cạnh; about 25 minutes by boat to Hòn Tài; almost an hour afloat before a stop at Hòn Tre Lớn (S12). The history museum’s “about 3 km” is a different leg: past the national park to the bridge (S4). Keep both.
- Bến Đầm is a named route end on the scenic-fee resolution (route 13: Ma Thiên Lãnh – mũi Cá Mập – Bến Đầm – Hòn Bà) (S13). Kilometres from the town to the port: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
