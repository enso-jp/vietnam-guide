### Lễ giỗ Võ Thị Sáu
- Vietnamese name: Lễ giỗ Anh hùng lực lượng vũ trang nhân dân Võ Thị Sáu
- Kind: festival
- When: The special-zone notice of 29 Dec 2025 sets the 74th memorial on 22 and 23 Jan 2026, and prints the span 23 Jan 1952 – 23 Jan 2026. The vigil at the grave was 09:00 on 23 Jan 2026 at Nghĩa trang Hàng Dương. The rite followed at Đền thờ Côn Đảo (S16). A provincial tourism page that discusses a lunar date returned 500 and is not used. Next year’s civil date, if the rite stays on 23 January: the day of the week moves. Confirm the next notice. Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Where: The grave in Hàng Dương, then Đền thờ Côn Đảo (S16). A street for the temple was not on that notice. The special-zone office on the same site is Nguyễn Văn Linh, khu 6 (S16). That is the office, not a temple door.
- What happens: A vigil, then incense, a dance to open the rite, offerings, a memorial reading, and the sharing of the offering. The day before includes a student quiz on the armed-forces heroes buried at Hàng Dương (S16).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: hang-duong
- Sources: S16

### Lễ giỗ bà Phi Yến
- Vietnamese name: Lễ giỗ bà Phi Yến
- Kind: festival
- When: 17 and 18 of the 10th lunar month, at An Sơn miếu (S17). The solar dates move. Check on arrival. The ministry article of 26 Aug 2023 says the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism has recognized this rite as national intangible cultural heritage, together with Nghinh Ông at Thắng Tam and Dinh Cô at Long Hải. Those two are not Côn Đảo rites. Do not import them.
- Where: An Sơn miếu, on the island (S17). A street number was not in the article. Check on arrival.
- What happens: An offering asking for calm weather and safety, then a fair: fruit trays, flower arranging, folk games, calligraphy, and đàn ca tài tử (S17).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: none. The temple is not given its own attraction entry because the article does not describe a building a visitor walks on an ordinary day.
- Sources: S17

### Xem rùa đẻ và thả rùa con
- Vietnamese name: Xem rùa biển đẻ trứng; thả rùa con về biển
- Kind: activity
- When: Night viewing from 18:00 until 06:00 the next morning. Hatchling release from 05:30 to 07:30 on mornings when there are hatchlings, in two short turns: 06:45–07:00 and 07:15–07:30 (S10). A calendar of nesting months was not in the notice. Check on arrival.
- Where: Bảy Cạnh, Bãi Dương, Hòn Cau, Hòn Tài, Hòn Tre Lớn, Bãi Đất Thắm (S10). These are park islets and beaches, not new places.
- What happens: A booked group watches a nesting turtle at night, or releases hatchlings in the early morning, after a briefing. Night cap: 50 people at one site, at most five groups of about 10. Morning cap: 100 people at one moment, in two groups of at most 50. Waste comes back to the big island. One-use plastic is forbidden. Outside guides do not give their own talk (S10).
- Fee: The scenic-route fee (S13) plus the 2017 guide fees: 350,000–600,000 VND per person for a night group of six down to one, half if no turtle is seen, and 50,000 VND per person for the release (S9). The 2017 table was still the page on the park site. A boat fare was not printed. Boat: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Not the same as attraction slug: hon-bay-canh
- Sources: S9, S10, S13
