## What it is

- English name: Con Dao National Park
- Vietnamese name: Vườn quốc gia Côn Đảo
- Kicker: Forest and water, not one gate
- Kind: zone

## What the visitor does

- Crosses a park of islands and water. Đầm Trầu and Ông Đụng are named in the prose and are not cards, because no licensed photo shows those beaches.
- Mistake: Paying 50,000 or 60,000 VND and thinking the boat and the prison ticket are included. Merging 355 coral species with 1,000 ha of reef.

## Fee

- 2022 resolution reprint, in force 1 Aug 2022, one publisher. Adult 60,000 VND per person per route on routes 1–3. Adult 50,000 on routes 4–17. Students 20,000. Children and pupils 10,000. Excludes ecotourism services. Council PDF not opened (S13).
- 2017 service table still on the park site is extra and excludes the scenic fee (S9).

## Hours

- Day walks in daylight. Turtle nights 18:00–06:00 (S10). A weekly closing day was not printed.

## Getting there

### From Côn Sơn town

- Park office: đường Ma Thiên Lãnh, khu 3 (S1). The fee desk was not given a street on the resolution.
