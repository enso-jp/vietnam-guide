## What it is

- English name: Van Son Pagoda
- Vietnamese name: Vân Sơn tự; also chùa Núi Một
- Kicker (one line, what it is): The island’s only pagoda, on Núi Một
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: slope of Núi Một, bell pavilion over the bay, wooden hall with a tiled roof. Newest special-zone page (S25) says it is the only pagoda on the island, grounds 19,434 m², founded 1964 under deputy province chief Trần Văn Khỏe for families of administrators and soldiers, worship of Thích Ca, Quan Âm, Quan Thánh, and Thiên Hậu. Operated until May 1975, then abandoned (rear roof, doors, and objects gone). Small repair agreed July 2000. Decision 4025/QĐ-UBND of 4 Dec 2009, provincial historic, cultural, and scenic relic. Restoration finished 3 Dec 2011. First and fifteenth of the lunar month, and Buddhist festivals, for incense.
- How long on site: UNSOURCED. Public timeNeeded is “A stop on the hill.”
- How long including the ride from the base: about 1.6 km from Côn Sơn town (S26). Minutes: UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: feeding or teasing monkeys, or burning votive paper (S25).
- Notice: leave the monkeys unfed, and leave votive paper unburned.

Conflicts, kept off the page:

- S27 (24 Feb 2025, same portal) says Hòa thượng Thích Tâm Giác of chùa Vĩnh Nghiêm founded a small hermitage in 1964 to pray for people who died in the prison, and that the rebuild finished in April 2011. It also puts a 2 m Quan Âm in the grounds and names the main hall as Thích Ca, Quan Âm, and Địa Tạng, plus a memorial space for Hồ Chí Minh, Võ Thị Sáu, and fallen soldiers. The public page uses S25 (18 Dec 2025) for the founder, the altar list, and the 3 Dec 2011 finish. The 2 m statue stays here.
- S26 (13 Oct 2020) agrees on 1964, families of administrators and soldiers, restoration dated 13 Aug 2010, inauguration 3 Dec 2011, and 1.6 km. It does not name Trần Văn Khỏe, the area, or the hours.
- S28 (store page, undated) says the 1964 build used prisoner labor from Phú Hải in April 1965, and that Mai Văn Xinh died in the stone cells on 1 May 1965, with the grave in zone C of Hàng Dương. It agrees on Decision 4025, completion 3 Dec 2011, and 19,434 m², and lists the gate, bell tower, Buddha statue, Địa Tạng shrine, Sơn Thần shrine, ancestral house, and guest house, with an investment of nearly 100 billion VND. That labor account stays here. The public page does not retell it.
