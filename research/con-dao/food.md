## Comprehensive list of local food to try

### Hạt bàng
- Vietnamese name: hạt bàng rang muối; mứt hạt bàng
- What it is: The kernel of trái bàng. The report says prisoners gathered leaves and fruit when they were kept without greens, and that the island now roasts the kernels with salt or makes candied kernels. It also says peak-season supply is bought in from the eastern and western provinces, so not every bag is fruit picked on the island (S14).
- How to eat it: As a gift pack from the market, not a sit-down dish. The report shows traders packing roasted kernels at chợ Côn Đảo (S14).
- When to order it: When leaving. It is the souvenir the report says visitors buy.
- Area, not a shop: Chợ Côn Đảo, in the town
- Meal: sweet
- Neighbor dish to link: none on this site
- Sources: S14

### Cua mặt trăng
- Vietnamese name: cua mặt trăng
- What it is: A crab the report says carries red spots on the shell like a moon. Locals told the reporter the meat is best in the week of the rising moon. Shells are also used as decoration (S14).
- How to eat it: As a seafood meal. The night market cooks crab and other seafood to order, steamed or grilled (S15). A recipe was not printed.
- When to order it: Evening, at the night market. The “rising moon” line is the newspaper’s local tip, not a tested calendar.
- Area, not a shop: Chợ đêm Côn Đảo
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none
- Sources: S14, S15

### Mực
- Vietnamese name: mực tươi; mực một nắng; mực khô
- What it is: Squid. The report says the fresh squid is hand-lined from small boats and still moving when it hits the tank, and that one-sun and dried squid are gift seafood (S14).
- How to eat it: Fresh, grilled or in a hotpot at the night market (S15 names squid among the seafood on the stalls). Dried, as a pack.
- When to order it: Evening for the fresh squid. Any day for the dried pack.
- Area, not a shop: Chợ đêm for the fresh squid. Chợ Côn Đảo for the dried pack.
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none. Do not link this to the Cham Islands one-sun squid page. That is a different coast.
- Sources: S14, S15

## Food streets and markets

### Chợ đêm Côn Đảo
- Vietnamese name: Chợ đêm Côn Đảo
- Kind: food-street
- What the visitor does: Eats seafood and street snacks in the open yard in front of the daily market. The gate is on Phạm Văn Đồng. The streets named are Võ Thị Sáu and Phạm Văn Đồng. Visitors pick seafood and the stall cooks it. Grilled snacks, corn, sweet potato, bánh tráng nướng, and cơm cháy are also there (S15).
- When to go: From 17:00. Busiest 19:00–22:00 every day in that report. Peak season and weekends can run until before 02:00. No roof, so rain moves the tables (S15).
- Area: Town centre
- Suggested slug: con-dao-night-market
- Photo must show: the open yard at night, stalls, and people at tables, the street still visible. A plate with no market fails.
