# Attractions — Con Dao

Slug: `con-dao`
Vietnamese name: Côn Đảo
Province: Thành phố Hồ Chí Minh, đặc khu Côn Đảo. Until 1 July 2025 the island district sat in Bà Rịa–Vũng Tàu (S1, S6).
Kind: area
Corridor: southeast
Base a traveler sleeps in: the town on Côn Sơn, or the Cỏ Ống side near Bãi Đầm Trầu. No hotel brand.
Checked: 21 Oct 2026

This is one place. Hòn Bảy Cạnh, Hòn Cau, and the other islets are park stops, not new slugs. Vũng Tàu already has `vung-tau`. Do not make this a second Vũng Tàu page.

## Why a night here

### The bay in the morning

- The picture they expect: Côn Sơn from the town shore. Water in the foreground, Cầu tàu 914 out into the bay, the low town, and the forested ridge behind. The prison is the other picture, and it is inland of that shore, not the thing in the bedroom window.
- Where they sleep, as an area: the town (the old thị trấn Côn Sơn, now inside đặc khu Côn Đảo), or a stay near Cỏ Ống if the morning swim is Bãi Đầm Trầu (S7, S11). The park office is on đường Ma Thiên Lãnh, khu 3 (S1). No hotel brand.
- Where the evening is: the night market on Võ Thị Sáu and Phạm Văn Đồng, in front of the daily market, then the short walk to the pier (S15, S4). The newspaper puts the market from 17:00, busiest 19:00–22:00, and on peak weekends ending before 02:00 (S15). No bar street was named on the official pages read.
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices):
  - Chợ đêm Côn Đảo, on Võ Thị Sáu and Phạm Văn Đồng. Report: https://www.vietnam.vn/lac-loi-thien-duong-hai-san-o-cho-dem-con-dao (S15). It is a pin for the evening, not a ranked stall list.
  - Cầu tàu 914, on the town’s main beach, started 1873 (S4). The history-museum chapter is the pin: https://baotanglichsu.vn/vi/Articles/3096/62104/djoi-net-ve-he-thong-nha-tu-con-djao.html
- Hero must include: the bay, the pier, the town front, and the ridge in one frame. Subject through the center. Water is the foreground.
- A crop that would fail: a cell door with no yard, a portrait of a statue, or a frame that is only sand or only sky. Bãi Đầm Trầu, if it is the photo, needs the yellow sand, the water, and the casuarina together, with the hill still in the frame (S11).

- Vietnamese name: thị trấn Côn Sơn, now inside đặc khu Côn Đảo
- Kind: area
- Area: Côn Sơn, the largest island
- What the visitor does: Sleeps, eats, and walks the shore. The AIP still measures the airport from thị trấn Côn Sơn (S7). The prison, the pier, Hàng Dương, and the night market are in this town (S4, S15, S16). The park calls Côn Sơn the largest of 16 islands (S1). - When to go: Morning for the prison slots if the indexed extract holds (S19). Evening for the market (S15).

- Local interest: The island town
- Suggested slug: con-son
- Suggested dot: 5 — the night, the market, and the prison walk start here
- Photo must show: the shore road, the pier, and the ridge behind the roofs, one frame

### Nhà tù Côn Đảo
- Vietnamese name: Nhà tù Côn Đảo; Khu di tích lịch sử Nhà tù Côn Đảo
- Kind: sight
- Area: The town
- What the visitor does: Walks the camps, not one building. The history museum says Governor Bonard signed the founding decision on 1 Feb 1862. It counts 127 cells, 42 isolation cells, and 504 solitary rooms called chuồng cọp. After 1975 the prison function ended. In 1979 the Ministry of Culture and Information ranked the site a national relic with 17 component monuments. Decision 548/QĐ-TTg of 10 May 2012 ranked the prison a special national relic (S4). The ministry’s 2019 ceremony article repeats 29 Apr 1979, Decision 54/QĐ-VHTT, and 10 May 2012, Decision 548/QĐ-TTg, and says the protected area is more than 110 ha (S5). A ticket desk’s 110.69 ha was not opened as a full page and is not used.
- Camps the museum chapter names, so a later page does not split them into new places: Trại 1 / Phú Thọ, built 1928; Trại 2 / Phú Hải, built 1862 and rebuilt in solid form in 1896, the oldest large camp; Trại 3 / Phú Sơn, 1916; Trại 4 / Phú Tường, 1941; Chuồng Cọp Pháp, 1940, 120 solitary rooms and 60 open “sunbath” rooms; Trại 5 / Phú Phong, 1962; Trại 6 / Phú An, 1968; Trại 7 / Phú Bình, the American-period tiger cages, 1971, 384 solitary rooms; Trại 8 / Phú Hưng, 1971; Trại 9 left unfinished at the Paris Agreement; Chuồng Bò, begun 1876 as a cowshed and later used as cells; the lord’s house, 18,600 m² of grounds, workplace of 53 governors across 113 years (1862–1975), now the display house; Nhà Công quán in the same grounds (S4). The indexed registration extract of 27 Dec 2024 lists a route through the museum, Phú Tường and the French tiger cages, the lord’s house, Phú Hải, Phú Sơn, Phú Bình, and Phú An (S19). That page timed out on a direct fetch.
- Counts that disagree: the museum chapter’s opening line says nearly 2,000 revolutionary fighters were held (S4). The ministry says tens of thousands were held and about 20,000 fell (S5). Do not pick one.
- Time: A morning or an afternoon on the indexed route (S19). Minutes inside the walls: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Fee: Check on arrival. A ticket desk cites Resolution 03/2020/NQ-HĐND of 4 Aug 2020 at 50,000 VND, and 25,000 VND for the half-price group (S20). The resolution PDF was not opened. No second publisher printed those figures on a page that loaded. This is not the park’s scenic-route fee (S13).
- From the base: Walk in town. The indexed extract gives số 02 Nguyễn Huệ, khu 9 (S19). Confirm the door on arrival.
- When to go: The indexed extract says morning 07:30–11:30 (slots 07:30, 08:00, 08:30, 09:00) and afternoon 14:00–17:00 (slots 14:00, 14:30, 15:00) (S19). The page did not load. Hours: Check on arrival.
- Mistake to avoid: Budgeting the tiger cages as a separate island, or paying the park boat fee and thinking the prison is included.
- Local interest: The prison
- Suggested slug: con-dao-prison
- Suggested dot: 5 — the camp system is a day by itself, inside a longer stay
- Photo must show: a camp yard with the cell block and the gate in one frame. A tight crop of a barred door loses the yard.
- unpublished: no licensed photo
- Sources: S4, S5, S19, S20

- Area: The town

Zone B: 695 graves (17 mass), 276 named, including Võ Thị Sáu and Cao Văn Ngọc, mostly 1945–1960. Zone C: 373 graves (1 mass), 332 named, including Lê Văn Việt, mostly 1960–1975. Zone D: 157 graves, 14 named, gathered from Hòn Cau and Hàng Keo (S4). The 2026 memorial notice puts Võ Thị Sáu’s grave here (S16).

### Cầu Ma Thiên Lãnh
- Vietnamese name: Cầu Ma Thiên Lãnh
- Kind: sight
- Area: The ridge west of town
- What the visitor does: Walks up to two unfinished bridge abutments. Built 1930. The museum chapter says prisoners were building a road from the Núi Chúa junction toward Bãi Ông Đụng, and that 356 people died before the work stopped in August 1945 with two abutments, each about 8 m. The approach it describes is about 3 km past the national park, up a small road (S4). The park’s English tour page puts Ông Đụng about 3 km west of the town centre, and the walk from Ông Đụng back past the bridge at about 25 minutes (S12). Those are different legs. Do not add them into one distance.
- Time: The walk in S12, about 25 minutes on the return leg, or the museum’s approach. A single official minute count for the climb was not printed. Check on arrival for anything beyond S12.
- Fee: Route 8, 9, 10, and 12 of the scenic fee pass the bridge (S13). Adult fee on those routes is 50,000 VND per person per route on the 2022 resolution reprint. See the park entry. A bridge-only ticket was not printed.
- From the base: Walk from town toward Ông Đụng, or the park trail (S12)
- When to go: Daylight. The forest path is the point. Hours: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake to avoid: A photo of one concrete pier with no forest and no second abutment.
- Local interest: The forest road
- Suggested slug: ma-thien-lanh
- Suggested dot: 4 — a walk paired with Ông Đụng, not the whole day
- Photo must show: both abutments, the gap, and the forest slope in one frame
- unpublished: no licensed photo

### Vườn quốc gia Côn Đảo
- Vietnamese name: Vườn quốc gia Côn Đảo
- Kind: zone
- Area: The islands and the water around them
- What the visitor does: Crosses a park, not a fence with one gate. Founded by Decision 135/QĐ-TTg of 31 Mar 1993, on the forbidden forest of Decision 85/CT of 1 Mar 1984 (S1, S2). From 1 Jul 2025 the park says the city runs it (S1). Area on the park page: 19,883.15 ha, of which forest 5,883.15 ha and sea 14,000 ha, plus a marine buffer of 20,500 ha (S1). The biodiversity agency says nearly 20,000 ha, forest nearly 6,000, sea nearly 14,000, buffer 20,500 (S2). Ramsar site 2203 is 19,991 ha, designated 18 Jun 2013 (S3). Island count: 16 on the park page, 14 on the Ramsar sheet (S1, S3). Highest points on the park page: Núi Thánh Giá 577 m and Núi Chúa 515 m (S1). Species lines from the biodiversity agency, attributed to the park: more than 1,000 vascular plants, 29 mammals, 85 birds, 46 reptiles and amphibians, sóc đen Côn Sơn, about 31 ha of mangrove, coral reefs about 1,000 ha, dugong and sea turtles (S2). Ramsar’s sheet says 355 coral species, 56 of them vulnerable on the IUCN list, and names leatherback and hawksbill (S3). Do not merge 355 species with 1,000 ha.
- Fee: Resolution 07/2022/NQ-HĐND, as reprinted, in force 1 Aug 2022. Adult 60,000 VND per person per route on routes 1–3 (the small-island circuit, Hòn Tài–Hòn Bảy Cạnh, Hòn Bảy Cạnh–Hòn Cau). Adult 50,000 VND per person per route on routes 4–17, including Đầm Trầu, Ông Đụng, Bến Đầm, Cỏ Ống, and the Ma Thiên Lãnh walks. Students and trainees 20,000. Children and general-school pupils 10,000. The resolution says this fee does not include ecotourism services (S13). The council’s own PDF was not opened. No second publisher printed these đồng figures on a page that loaded. The park’s 2017 service table is extra: night turtle guide from 350,000 to 600,000 VND per person by group size, hatchling release 50,000, Vietnamese guide 300,000 for half a day and 600,000 for a day, snorkel set 70,000 a turn (S9). That table says it excludes the scenic fee and dates from 1 Mar 2017. A later replacement table was not on the page.
- From the base: The fee desk was not given a street on the resolution. The English tour page starts some walks at the park headquarters (S12). The park office address is đường Ma Thiên Lãnh, khu 3 (S1).
- When to go: Day walks in daylight. Turtle nights are 18:00 to 06:00 (S10).
- Mistake to avoid: Paying 50,000 or 60,000 and thinking the boat, the guide, and the prison ticket are inside it.
- Local interest: The park
- Suggested slug: con-dao-national-park
- Suggested dot: 5 — the water and the forest are why the night is on the island
- Photo must show: forested island, reef water, and a shore in one frame. An underwater crop with no island does not say where it is.
- unpublished: no licensed photo
- Sources: S1, S2, S3, S9, S10, S13

### Bãi Đầm Trầu
- Vietnamese name: Bãi Đầm Trầu

The Vietnamese page calls it the most beautiful beach on the island, with yellow, fine, flat sand and quiet clear water, and names sóc mun and sóc đen Côn Đảo in the trees (S11). ## Nightlife and evening

### The town market after dark
- Vietnamese name: Chợ đêm Côn Đảo, on đường Võ Thị Sáu and đường Phạm Văn Đồng

- Area: The town centre, in front of the daily market
- What the visitor does: Eats at the night market and walks to the pier. The newspaper says the market has run since 2023, the gate faces Phạm Văn Đồng, and people also come in from the side. - From the base: Walk in town
- When to go: After the prison day. In rain the report says people move seats because there is no roof (S15).
- Mistake to avoid: A ranked bar list. The evening is this market and the shore.
