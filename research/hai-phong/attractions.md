## Why a night here

### The square, then the river

- The picture they expect: Wake up in the port center, not at the airport and not in a karst bay. The city portal’s center piece is the opera house, the Lê Chân statue, and the same strip when the flamboyant trees are out (S8). The other picture is Tam Bạc at night: the old street on the right bank, the lights, and the water (S14).
- Where they sleep, as an area: Rooms in the center wards around the opera and Tam Bạc. Or rooms on the Đồ Sơn peninsula if the night is the city’s own sand (S8, S21). The administrative campus in phường Thủy Nguyên is where the portal’s contact address sits (S3, portal footers). It is not the hotel street. No hotel brand.
- Where the evening is: Tam Bạc, the pedestrian street along the river, from the end of December 2018 on the ward page (S14). The 2020 center article adds the night food after the two Cấm bridges (S8). The 2021 friendship article adds pavement bánh đa cua on Trần Phú opposite the opera from about 18:00 (S12).
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): The opera, named Nhà hát thành phố on the center article, https://haiphong.gov.vn/Du-lich/Nhung-diem-du-lich-hap-dan-tai-khu-vuc-noi-do-Hai-Phong-52536 (S8). Tam Bạc, https://hongbang.haiphong.gov.vn/di-san-van-hoa-78115/pho-co-tam-bac-767535 (S14). Đền Nghè of Lê Chân, https://www.haiphong.gov.vn/di-tich-danh-thang/den-nghe-622037 (S7). They are pins, not a bar list.
- Hero must include: The opera facade, the square in front of it, and the trees, in one frame, subject through the center. If the frame is the river, both banks and the water, not a railing. A standing statue is the wrong hero: Lê Chân’s figure loses its plinth if the crop takes the face (S8, S7).
- A crop that would fail: Empty sky, a single column, fog with no river, a hotel sign, or a Cát Bà karst used as this city’s front.

The 2025 resolution did not move the name. Clause 7 keeps thành phố Hải Phòng and names the neighbors as Bắc Ninh, Hưng Yên, Quảng Ninh, and Biển Đông (S1). The Government is told to fix the boundary on the map and on the ground (S1). No street border is invented here. Older area figures are in `getting-there.md`. Both sets stay.

### Nghe temple of Le Chan
- Vietnamese name: Đền Nghè (miếu An Biên)
- Kind: sight

The first shrine was thatch. The page says the solid building is 1919 (S7). Ranked in 1975 (S7).

- Mistake to avoid: Going to the Đồ Sơn Nghè. That temple is a different building, for a sea god, more than 20 km away (S20).

### Hang Kenh communal house
- Vietnamese name: Đình Hàng Kênh (Nhân Thọ đình)
- Kind: sight
- Area: Nguyễn Công Trứ, phường Hàng Kênh, quận Lê Chân (S5).
- What the visitor does: Walks a 7-bay ironwood hall. The page says the stele dates the present building to 1719, under Lê Dụ Tông, rebuilt 1841–1850. The grounds are about 6,000 m². Forty-two columns, the main ones over 5 m with a girth near 2 m, stand on lotus stones. More than 100 carved panels hold nearly 400 dragons. The floor keeps a rare boat-shaped plank floor. The rear hall has Ngô Quyền on a dragon throne, a small boat, and a piece of wood standing for the Bạch Đằng stakes (S5). Ranked in 1962 (S5).

- Mistake to avoid: Calling this the stake field. The wood in the rear hall is a symbol. The excavated stakes are at Cao Quỳ (S5, S18).
- Local interest: The carved halls
- Suggested slug: hang-kenh
- Suggested dot: 4 — the carved hall in the city

### Pier K15
- Vietnamese name: Bến tàu không số K15
- Kind: sight
- Area: Vụng Sét, under đồi Vạn Hoa, on the Đồ Sơn peninsula. The 16 March 2025 article still says quận Đồ Sơn (S13).
- What the visitor does: Sees the first pier of the sea Hồ Chí Minh trail. The page says the old trace is rows of piles above the water near Vụng Sét. The name is the military code, from the 15th Party resolution on the southern revolution. The first wooden boat, 30 tons of weapons, left on the night of 11 October 1962 and reached Vàm Lũng in Cà Mau after six days. The page counts 88 military sailings, nearly 5,000 tons of arms, and thousands of tons of other cargo. National ranking in 2008. On 16 March 2025 the city said Decision 1473 had ranked the sea-trail cluster, including this pier, a special national relic in the 16th batch of 2024. A restoration broke ground on 8 January 2025 on 2.28 ha, investment printed as nearly 235 billion đồng (S13). What is open to a visitor during the works was not printed.
- Time: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- From the base: On the Đồ Sơn peninsula (S13). Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The works started 8 January 2025. An opening after the works was not printed.
- Mistake to avoid: Confusing these piles with the Bạch Đằng stakes at Cao Quỳ (S13, S18).
- Local interest: Stakes and the unnumbered pier
- Suggested slug: pier-k15
- Suggested dot: 4 — the sea-trail pier on the city’s peninsula

### Cao Quy stakes
- Vietnamese name: Bãi cọc Cao Quỳ
- Kind: sight
- Area: The ward page says more than 30,600 m² in phường Lưu Kiếm, formerly xã Liên Khê, huyện Thủy Nguyên (S18).
- What the visitor does: Looks at a covered stake pit of more than 1,000 m² and a display house of 360 m². Excavation with the Institute of Archaeology and the museum was at the end of 2019. The page’s find list is 37 wooden stakes, 2 wood clusters, 22 stake holes, and 4 dark soil holes. Diameters on the excavation sentence: small 10–18 cm, large 28–32 cm, and some 37–40 cm, mostly sến nhựa and lim. The site-management sentence on the same page says the longest stake is nearly 5 m, the shortest more than 2 m, the greatest diameter 0.5 m, and the smallest 0.2 m (S18). Both size sets stay. The page reads the field as a late-13th-century position tied to the 1288 battle, blocking the Giá river. It is not a house site: no ceramics and no food remains (S18).
- Time: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- From the base: Thủy Nguyên, north of the center. The Bạch Đằng stretch through Thủy Nguyên is printed as a bit more than 20 km of river, from the Đá Bạc to the Nam Triệu mouth (S18). Minutes from the opera: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: A gate hour was not printed. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake to avoid: Quoting one diameter. The excavation sentence and the management sentence do not match (S18). Also do not send this visit to a Hạ Long boat.
- Local interest: Stakes and the unnumbered pier
- Suggested slug: cao-quy
- Suggested dot: 4 — the stake field in this city
