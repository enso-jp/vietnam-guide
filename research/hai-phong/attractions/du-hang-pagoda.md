## Visit

- Vietnamese name: Chùa Dư Hàng (Phúc Lâm tự)
- Area: xã Dư Hàng Kênh, now phường Hồ Nam, quận Lê Chân (S6)
- What you see: a 7-bay Buddha hall and a 3-storey bell tower. The bell is inscribed Phúc Lâm tự chung (S6). The stele is tied to the early Lê (980–1009) and the Trúc Lâm line to Trần Nhân Tông. Memorial days: the 2nd of lunar month 11 for Trần Nhân Tông, the 3rd for Huyền Quang (S6). Ranked a historic relic in 1986 (S6). The 1926 memorial for Phan Chu Trinh happened here. That is history, not a current festival.
