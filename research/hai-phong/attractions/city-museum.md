## What it is

- English name: Hai Phong Museum
- Vietnamese name: Bảo tàng Hải Phòng
- Kicker (one line, what it is): City museum on Điện Biên Phủ
- Kind: sight

This is the museum at 66 Điện Biên Phủ. It is not Bảo tàng Hải Phòng cơ sở 2 in Hà Bắc, the former Hải Dương museum.

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the rooms. A1 says about 19,000 objects, including Cái Bèo tools from Cát Bà dated 6,000–7,000 years. A2, the director’s account on 17 August 2025, names 12 permanent rooms and one thematic room: prehistory, the northern-occupation period, the old port city, Hồ Chí Minh, and maritime heritage.
