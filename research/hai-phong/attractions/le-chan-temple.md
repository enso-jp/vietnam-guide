## What it is

- English name: Le Chan temple
- Vietnamese name: Đền Nghè (miếu An Biên)
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Lê Chân’s temple where the Tam Bạc meets the Cấm
- Kind: sight

A separate visit from Đồ Sơn’s sea-god temple, and a different subject from the Lê Chân statue on the opera strip.

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Enter the Nguyễn-style compound. Tam quan, a 5-bay worship hall on 16 ironwood columns, a 3-bay rear hall, stone elephants and horses, statue in a red-and-gold shrine (A1). The solid building is 1919. Ranked 1975.
