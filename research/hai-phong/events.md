### Do Son buffalo festival
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội chọi trâu truyền thống Đồ Sơn
- Kind: festival
- When: The Đồ Sơn temple page says the weight of the festival falls in lunar months 6 and 8, and it prints the couplet that calls people home on the 9th of lunar month 8 (S20). The city portal’s 2026 plan sets the final at 07:30 on 19 September 2026, which that page equates with the 9th of lunar month 8, at the central stadium of phường Đồ Sơn (S9). The same plan prints eight rites: incense and flag at the Nghè, Nam Hải Thần Vương, đình Nam, đình Ngọc Hải, and đình Ngọc Xuyên on the morning of 11 September; water procession at the Nghè on the morning of 17 September; offerings on the morning of 18 September; a night rite on 18 September; the procession of the tutelary gods to the stadium early on 19 September; the champion buffalo rite early on 20 September; thanks on the morning of 20 September; sending off the gods on the morning of 26 September (S9). Fifteen buffalo reach the final, paired by direct elimination (S9). Next year’s solar dates move with the lunar calendar. Check on arrival. A ticket price is not printed. Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Where: phường Đồ Sơn. The rites name the Nghè at Đồ Sơn, not the Lê Chân temple in the center (S9, S20).
- What happens: Rituals at the temples and communal houses, then the buffalo matches at the ward stadium, plus a culture-and-trade week, a three-region food gathering, a wrestling meet, and a street procession (S9).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: do-son-temple, do-son
- Sources: S9, S20

### Le Chan temple festival
- Vietnamese name: Hội đền Nghè (nữ tướng Lê Chân)
- Kind: festival
- When: The temple page says the hội runs from the 8th to the 10th of lunar month 2 (S7). The 2020 center article adds three offering days: birthday the 8th of lunar month 2, death anniversary the 25th of lunar month 12, and khánh hạ the 15th of lunar month 8 (S8). Both lists stay. Solar dates: Check on arrival.
- Where: The city temple, phường Mê Linh, which that page also calls phường An Biên, quận Lê Chân (S7). This is not the Đồ Sơn Nghè.
- What happens: Offerings for Lê Chân. The page does not print a program of performances.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: le-chan-temple
- Sources: S7, S8

### Hon Dau island festival
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội đảo Hòn Dấu
- Kind: festival
- When: The 8th, 9th, and 10th of lunar month 2. The main rite starts at 23:00 on the 9th (S21). Those calendar days overlap the Lê Chân hội and are a different place. Solar dates: Check on arrival.
- Where: Hòn Dấu, offshore from Đồ Sơn (S21)
- What happens: Elders make the offering. Visitors take a boat, walk about 100 m, and light incense at the Nam Hải Thần Vương temple (S21).
- Fee: The boat fare is not printed. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: hon-dau
- Sources: S21
