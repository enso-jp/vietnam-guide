# Side trips

Checked: 21 Oct 2026

Times are the figures the cited page prints. Two Vietnam Tourism pages do not agree on the Ha Long road. The return to the quarter is the same ride in the other direction unless a page says otherwise. None of these pages does.

### Ninh Binh
- Slug if it exists: ninh-binh
- Travel time from this base: Rail, southbound thống nhất board, about 2 hours 16 minutes on SE7, SE5, and SE3, 2 hours 17 on SE9, 2 hours 18 on SE1. Ninh Bình is km 115 (S11). A Vietnam Tourism north page says the province is less than 100 km southeast of Hanoi and calls it a weekend (S31). The kilometres and the rail minutes are different measures. Do not turn 100 km into a drive time. The return rail board was not copied.
- Why go: Karst and river country the same page calls “Ha Long Bay on land,” with boat trips. Tràng An and Tam Cốc are the two areas it names (S31).
- Overnight or day: The page says weekend. A day return is not ruled out. The rail times allow a morning out and an evening back on some trains, and that is a reading of the clock, not a promise the sights stay open.
- Sources: S11, S31

- Sources: S31, S11

### Ha Long
- Slug if it exists: ha-long
- Travel time from this base: The Ha Long place page says about two and a half hours by road from Hanoi, then a transfer from Tuần Châu or Bãi Cháy (S30). The north page’s heading says “(3 hours ride)” (S31). Keep both. Do not pick one.
- Why go: The bay. The place page’s picture is a cruise among the karsts, including waking on the water (S30). The north page calls it a UNESCO site (S31). The place page’s fetched text did not repeat the UNESCO line.
- Overnight or day: The cruise copy includes a morning on the bay, so an overnight is the trip that page describes (S30). A day trip is not given its own clock.
- Sources: S30, S31

### Mai Chau
- Slug if it exists: mai-chau
- Travel time from this base: The north page’s heading says “(2.5 hours)” (S31). Road, not rail, is the implication of a heading with no station. Treat the mode as road and the number as that page’s figure only.
- Why go: A weekend in the countryside, rice, stilt houses, and White and Black Thái communities among seven groups the page names. It suggests a homestay and does not name a hotel brand (S31).
- Overnight or day: Weekend. The page says fall asleep there.
- Sources: S31
