### Bach Ma festival
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội đền Bạch Mã
- Kind: festival
- When: The 12th and 13th of the second lunar month. Check on arrival for the solar dates. The management board says a larger festival every five years from 2010 (so 2015, 2020, and the same step), run by the district, and a smaller one in the other years, run by the ward (S21). Whether 2025 or 2026 was a large year was not rechecked against a new notice.
- Where: 76 Hàng Buồm, nearby streets, and the lake area at the Lý Thái Tổ monument (S21)
- What happens: The page ties the festival to the tiến Xuân Ngưu rite, a spring-buffalo procession, and says an old procession of more than 500 people left the temple, went through Mã Mây and đền Bà Kiệu, around the lake, to Hàng Đào, and back (S21). That route is the elders’ account on the page, not a 2026 traffic plan.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. S21 does not print a ticket.
- Not the same as attraction slug: bach-ma. The temple is the sight. This is the festival.
- Sources: S21

### Dong Xuan Saturday folk show
- Vietnamese name: Hà thành 36 phố phường
- Kind: activity
- When: Saturday evenings, as the management-board market page states it. No clock and no 2026 calendar were on that page. Check on arrival (S18).
- Where: Chợ Đồng Xuân, on the night-market line from the lake (S18)
- What happens: The page says Đồng Xuân Joint Stock Company runs a folk performance under that name on Saturday evenings, linked to the night market (S18).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: dong-xuan. The hall is the sight.
- Sources: S18
