# Sources — Old Quarter

Accessed 22 Oct 2026.

### S1
- URL: https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/11-must-see-attractions-ha-noi
- Publisher: Vietnam Tourism (vietnam.travel)
- Accessed: 22 Oct 2026
- Supports: Old Quarter is one square kilometre of 36 streets. Since the 15th century, streets housed artisans named for their goods. Admission free. Hours 7am to midnight. Address: north of Hoan Kiem Lake. Night tip: bia hoi corner, Ta Hien and Luong Ngoc Quyen. No drink price.

### S2
- URL: https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/explore-old-quarter-your-way
- Publisher: Vietnam Tourism (vietnam.travel)
- Accessed: 22 Oct 2026
- Supports: 36 guild streets north of the lake. Tube houses: narrow facades, deep and high, courtyards for light. Hang Bac silver, Hang Ma festival goods, Hang Gai silk, Lan Ong herbs. Cyclo, then streets on foot. Pho and bun cha named on the streets. Egg coffee named at Cafe Giang. Cha ca La Vong named as a quarter dish. Do not publish Cafe Giang or La Vong as a pick on this page. The Hanoi food guide holds the menu. Weekend night food on Hang Dao is named. Ta Hien is the beer corner. No prices.

### S4
