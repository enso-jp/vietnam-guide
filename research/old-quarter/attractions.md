## Why a night here

### The lanes
- The picture they expect: tube houses and the street, or the market front. Narrow facades, shop goods at street level, the lane running back. Not a metal-roof house in another city, not a magazine cover, not a cropped sign.
- Where they sleep, as an area: near Hoan Kiem when the lake and the market are one walk (S9). Hang Buom, Ma May, and Hang Be when the walk to Dong Xuan is the point (S9). No hotel brand.
- Where the evening is: Ta Hien and Luong Ngoc Quyen, the bia hoi corner Vietnam Tourism names (S1, S2). No drink price.
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): Bach Ma at 76 Hang Buom (S5). Dong Xuan on Dong Xuan street (S8, S9). Ta Hien corner (S1). Cafe Giang is named for egg coffee on S2 and stays off this page as a pick. The menu is the Hanoi food guide.
- Hero must include: the tube-house front and the street in one frame, subject through the center, color, daylight. A 16:9 crop still shows the shutters and the shop.
- A crop that would fail: a sign filling the card, a portrait that is not the temple, a house front from Nam Dinh, a shrimp close-up that does not show the market.

- Vietnamese name: Đền Bạch Mã
- Kind: sight
- Area: Hang Buom
- What the visitor does: Walk in from the lanes and see the yellow hall, tiled roof, and dragons. The altar holds a white horse. Address 76 Hang Buom (S5).
- Time: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No duration on S5 or S6.
- Fee: 20,000 VND a visit from 8 Dec 2025 (S5, S6). Under 16 free, or under 1.3 m with no papers (S5). 50 percent for the groups S5 names. VnExpress adds free days on 23 Nov and the last lunar day plus the first two days of Tet (S6). S5 does not restate those days.
- From the base: Walk north from Hoan Kiem Lake. Minutes Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: Day hours 08:00–12:00 and 13:30–17:30 (S5, S6). Evening Friday–Sunday 19:00–21:30 (S5) or 19:00–21:00 (S6). 2018 door board: 08:00–11:00 and 14:00–17:00, Monday closed, and 08:00–21:00 on the 1st and 15th lunar (S7). Print every figure.
- Mistake to avoid: Trusting one clock. Read the board at the gate.
- Local interest: old towns
