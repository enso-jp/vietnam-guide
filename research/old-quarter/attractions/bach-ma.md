## Fee
- 20,000 VND a visit from 8 Dec 2025. S5 and S6.
- Under 16 free, or under 1.3 m with no papers. S5.
- 50 percent for the groups S5 names. A double qualification is still half. S5.
- Free on 23 Nov and the last lunar day plus the first two Tet days. S6 only. S5 does not restate it.

## Hours
- S5: 08:00–12:00 and 13:30–17:30 daily. Friday–Sunday 19:00–21:30.
- S6: same day hours. Evening 19:00–21:00. Do not average.
- S7, 2018 door board: 08:00–11:00 and 14:00–17:00. Monday closed instead of Sunday. 08:00–21:00 on the 1st and 15th lunar. Do not drop this figure.
