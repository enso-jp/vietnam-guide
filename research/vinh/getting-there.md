# Getting there — Vinh

Slug: `vinh`
Checked: 22 Oct 2026. A fact with a source id was opened and is stamped verified. A line marked UNSOURCED is not.

The city stays the search slug `vinh`. It is the Nghệ An city. It is not filed as a second `dong-hoi`, `hue`, or `bach-ma`. Cửa Lò is the beach of this place. After 1 December 2024 the whole former thị xã Cửa Lò sits inside thành phố Vinh (S4). From 1 July 2025 the beach wards are phường Cửa Lò (S5). Do not open a `cua-lo` place file.

Province on the pages: the national tourism page and the city portal still print tỉnh Nghệ An (S1, S2). Nghị quyết 1678/NQ-UBTVQH15, passed 16 June 2025, still arranges communes of tỉnh Nghệ An and does not rename the province in the text that loaded (S5). A merger of Nghệ An into another province was not in that resolution. Do not invent one.

Area and population, all kept:

- The national tourism page prints 105 km² and 438,796 people in 2008, and lists the pre-2010 wards (S1).
- The city portal prints 104.96 km², and a planning line that talked about growing toward 250 km² (S2). That planning line is not the 2024 resolution.
- A 12 April 2022 news page prints more than 104 km² and more than 545,000 people (S7).
- Nghị quyết 1243/NQ-UBTVQH15, in force 1 December 2024, prints the city after the Cửa Lò merger as 166.22 km² and 580,669 people (S4).
- Nghị quyết 1678 does not reprint a city-wide area. It forms six wards — Trường Vinh, Thành Vinh, Vinh Hưng, Vinh Phú, Vinh Lộc, and Cửa Lò — and sets commune governments to operate from 1 July 2025 (S5). An April 2025 council draft, reported by VOV, had called five of them Vinh I through Vinh V and kept Cửa Lò (S22). The resolution that passed uses the six names above, not the numbers (S5). Keep both lists. The passed names are the ones on the resolution.

The Jetstar Pacific name is what that portal page prints. The Airports Corporation page for this airport returned 404 on 22 Oct 2026, so a terminal address, a phone, an IATA code, and a block time are not taken from the operator.
