## What it is

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Climb to the Quang Trung temple and look over the city, sông Lam, and Hồng Lĩnh. The temple is on the second summit, 92 m above the sea, where the 1 October 1788 edict sited Phượng Hoàng Trung Đô (S2). The city page says that capital was started and not finished (S2).
- How long on site: No visit field was printed. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- How long including the ride from the base: Southwest of the center, by the river (S2). Minutes and kilometres from the square were not printed. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Sending the visit to Kim Liên. Kim Liên is another commune, 15 km on the city portal (S2) and 13 km by Quốc lộ 46 on the 2025 news page (S12).
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The two large days are lunar. The solar date for a given year was not opened. Check on arrival.
