## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the gate, then the moat, then the yard. Earth in 1804 under Gia Long, stone in Minh Mạng’s 12th year, 1831 (S2, S6). The city introduction prints a six-sided wall, perimeter 603 trượng (2,412 m), height 4.42 m, area 420,000 m², and a moat 28 m wide and 3.20 m deep (S2). The roundup says the moat has been rebuilt as a lit lake with a grass bank (S6). It is a gate and a yard, not a reconstructed palace (S6).
- How long on site: A walk around the moat. A ticketed duration was not printed. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- How long including the ride from the base: The 2025 news page puts the museum, and the citadel yard with it, nearly 2 km from the square (S12). Minutes were not printed. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Budgeting a full fort or a palace.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: This is a gate, a moat, and the yard.
