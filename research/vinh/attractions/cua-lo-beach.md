## What it is

- English name: Cua Lo beach
- Vietnamese name: Bãi biển Cửa Lò
- Kicker (one line, what it is): The long sand along Bình Minh
- Kind: area

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Swim and walk the sand. Casuarina lines đường Bình Minh. The national beach page calls the shore long, the water clear enough to see the sand, and the salinity moderate rather than harsh (S3). Length is longer than 10 km on that page (S3) and longer than 12 km on the 2012 news page (S18). Both stay.
- How long on site: A beach day. A clock for the swim was not printed. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- How long including the ride from the base: 18 km and about 30 minutes by car on the Núi Cấm road (S3, primary). Also 17 km (S2), 16 km east (S18), and about 20 km (S12). The other three distances have no minutes. Do not invent them.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating Cửa Lò as a second place, or treating Quảng trường Bình Minh on this sand as the statue square in town (S2, S13).
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Quảng trường Bình Minh is on this sand. Quảng trường Hồ Chí Minh is at Hồ Tùng Mậu and Trường Thi.
