These stay off the public attraction list. A line here is the reason.

## Hong Son temple — `hong-son`

Đền Hồng Sơn is a separate visit in the dossier: the võ miếu east of the old citadel, with festivals on the 3rd of the 3rd lunar month and the 20th of the 8th (S1, S2, S6). Visit Nghe An lists it at Hồng Sơn, phường Thành Vinh. Commons and Vietnamese Wikipedia were searched on 23 Oct 2026. Files named Hồng Sơn are a bishop, not this temple. Unpublished: no licensed photo of the temple.

## Nghe An Museum — `nghe-an-museum`

Not the same building as the Soviet museum. Before the 2025 merger the provincial seat was số 39 Đào Tấn and the Soviet seat was số 10 (see `xo-viet-museum.md`, A3). They are now one institution. No Commons file shows a facade that is the provincial museum rather than the yellow Soviet building. Unpublished: no licensed photo of that building. The Soviet page carries the merger and both address pairs.

## Military Region 4 Museum — `military-region-4-museum`

File:4th army division museum Vinh.jpg, Phó Nháy, public domain, opened 23 Oct 2026. The sign reads BẢO TÀNG QUÂN KHU 4 and 189 Lê Duẩn, Thành phố Vinh. That confirms a door the city introduction did not. It is one frame. The courtyard statue is a soldier, and no second frame of the building was found. Hours and a fee were not on a page that loaded. A one-photo stub would still leave the place sentence “Its door was not confirmed,” which this pass cannot rewrite. Unpublished: one frame, no hours, no fee.
