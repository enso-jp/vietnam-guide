## What it is

- English name: Nghe Tinh Soviet Museum
- Vietnamese name: Bảo tàng Xô viết Nghệ Tĩnh
- Kicker (one line, what it is): The 1930–1931 rooms in the citadel yard
- Kind: sight

The yellow building in the photograph is this museum. On 23 May 2025 the province merged it with Bảo tàng Nghệ An into one institution, Bảo tàng Nghệ An – Xô Viết Nghệ Tĩnh (A1). Before the merger the Soviet museum’s seat was số 10 Đào Tấn and the provincial museum’s seat was số 39 Đào Tấn (A3). After the merger, A1 and the museum’s own site (A2) give the seat as số 7 and số 10 Đào Tấn. A3 gives the merged seat as số 10 and số 39. Both address pairs stay. They are not one building. No licensed photo of số 39 was found, so the provincial rooms do not get a second page.

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the rooms of the 1930–1931 movement. Visit Nghe An says 10 display rooms, founded in Vinh on 15 January 1960, a branch of the revolution museum in 1987, and a Hồ Chí Minh inscription on 3 February 1964 (A4). A 3 May 2025 news page says more than 5,000 objects and photographs, outdoor space next to the citadel walls, nearly 2 km from the square (S12).
