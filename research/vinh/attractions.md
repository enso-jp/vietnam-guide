## Why a night here

### The sand at Cửa Lò, then the square

- The picture they expect: Wake on the beach. A long sand, water the national page calls clear enough to see the sand, and the casuarina along đường Bình Minh, because the sand is the center and the trees are the front (S3). The city picture, the one a beach crop never holds, is Quảng trường Hồ Chí Minh and the statue at Hồ Tùng Mậu and Trường Thi (S2, S12).
- Where they sleep, as an area: Beachfront rooms along Bình Minh when the day is the swim. A city room within a walk of the square when the night is the walking street. No hotel brand.
- Where the evening is: Friday and Saturday on Hồ Tùng Mậu, Nguyễn Văn Cừ, Nguyễn Trung Ngạn, and Nguyễn Tài, the walking loop by the square (S7, S8). An English report of the same opening adds Sunday and names Nguyễn Trãi instead of the last two streets (S9). Both lists stay. In summer the other evening is the sand at Cửa Lò (S3, S13, S21).
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): The beach, https://vietnamtourism.vn/index.php/tourism/items/1244 (S3). The square, described on the city introduction at the corner of Hồ Tùng Mậu and Trường Thi, https://vinh.nghean.gov.vn/dieu-kien-tu-nhien/-/asset_publisher/ynVJdsHuw4tR/content/gioi-thieu-thanh-pho-vinh-1 (S2). The walking street as the April 2022 city project, reported at https://vnexpress.net/tp-vinh-lan-dau-co-pho-di-bo-4450356.html (S7). The Quang Trung temple on núi Quyết, on the city roundup https://vinh.nghean.gov.vn/pho-di-bo/-/asset_publisher/ynVJdsHuw4tR/content/nhung-iem-en-hap-dan-o-thanh-vinh (S6). They are pins, not a bar list.
- Hero must include: The surf, the wet sand, and the casuarina or the buildings along Bình Minh in one frame, subject through the center. If the frame is the square, the full statue and the open paving, not a face. A standing crop of the 18 m figure loses the feet (S12). The mountain frame needs the temple and the river, not a railing.
- A crop that would fail: Empty sky, empty sand, a hotel sign, fog with no shoreline, or a slice of the statue’s torso.
