# Food — Kon Tum

## Comprehensive list of local food to try

### Dry pho (two bowls)
- Vietnamese name: Phở khô (phở hai tô)
- What it is: Rice noodles tossed with meat and fermented black soybean sauce, with hot broth in a second bowl (S10).
- How to eat it: Alternate bites of dry noodles and sips of broth (S10).
- When to order it: Breakfast, roughly morning shop hours on Go Vietnam (S10).
- Area, not a shop: Central Kon Tum and streets near the market (S10).
- Meal: breakfast
- Neighbor dish to link: None required
- Sources: S10

### Leaf salad
- Vietnamese name: Gỏi lá
- What it is: Dozens of forest and garden leaves with boiled pork, shrimp, pork skin, and a fermented rice–dried shrimp dipping sauce; Ba Na tradition (S14).
- How to eat it: Stack leaves, add meat, sauce, pepper, and eat in one bite (S14).
- When to order it: Lunch or dinner
- Area, not a shop: Tran Cao Van area named in press for restaurants serving the dish (S14). Do not publish shop names as picks.
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: None
- Sources: S14

### Highland coffee
- Vietnamese name: Cà phê
- What it is: Arabica country; cafés as the social hub (S11).
- How to eat it: Slow drip with condensed milk or black, at a café table (S11).
- When to order it: Morning or after the river walk
- Area, not a shop: City cafés and Dak Ha coffee belt north of town (S11).
- Meal: drink
- Neighbor dish to link: Da Lat coffee culture when copy links highlands
- Sources: S11

### Bamboo-tube rice
- Vietnamese name: Cơm lam
- What it is: Glutinous rice grilled in bamboo; Ba Na specialty named on Go Vietnam cluster page (S4).
- How to eat it: Split the tube; eat with salt or chicken (S4).
- When to order it: Lunch in village visits
- Area, not a shop: Kon K’Tu and Ba Na villages (S4).
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: None
- Sources: S4

- Kind: food-street

- When to go: Lunch or dinner
- Area: Tran Cao Van, Kon Tum city
