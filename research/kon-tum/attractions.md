## Why a night here

### The wooden church and the river bridge

- The picture they expect: Cà chít timber and stained glass at the wooden cathedral, then the Kon Klor cable bridge over the wide Đăk Bla River with a Ba Na communal house roofline beyond (S4, S6).
- Where they sleep, as an area: Small hotels and guesthouses around central Kon Tum, walking distance of Nguyen Hue and the river. Not a resort strip (S4).
- Where the evening is: Tran Phu and the riverside cafés facing the Đăk Bla, with coffee rather than a named club quarter (S11). No provincial page names a bar street.
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): Kon Tum Wooden Church on Nguyen Hue (S6). Kon Klor suspension bridge (S4, S5). No prices on those primary rows.
- Hero must include: The church façade in colour, roof and steeple complete in frame, with sky enough to read the timber. A portrait crop must not cut the spire.
- A crop that would fail: Only the prison gate, only coffee cups, or a military memorial used as the city banner.

## Attractions (Sight, area of interests, zone, hub, etc.)

### Kon Tum Wooden Church
- Vietnamese name: Nhà thờ Gỗ Kon Tum (Nhà thờ chính tòa Giáo phận Kon Tum)
- Kind: sight
- Area: Nguyen Hue, Thong Nhat ward
- What the visitor does: Walk the Romanesque–Ba Na timber cathedral built 1913–1918 in cà chít wood; stained glass and carved interior (S4, S6).
- Time: About one hour (S4)
- Fee: Free entry on Go Vietnam cluster page (S4). No fee on kontum.gov.vn photo page — UNSOURCED for 2026 gate rules.
- From the base: Walk from central hotels on Nguyen Hue
- When to go: Dry season November–April on cluster climate note (S4). Sunday Mass at 6:00, 8:00, and 17:00 per Go Vietnam (S4); confirm locally.
- Mistake to avoid: Treating it as a quick photo stop when you wanted Kon K’Tu across the river
- Local interest: Culture, photography
- Suggested slug: wooden-church
- Suggested dot: 5 — the signature sight and worth the city stop
- Photo must show: Full timber façade, colour, one frame
- Sources: S4, S6

### Kon Klor suspension bridge
- Vietnamese name: Cầu treo Kon Klor
- Kind: sight
- Area: Over the Đăk Bla River toward Kon K’Tu
- What the visitor does: Walk or ride the 292 m cable-stayed bridge opened 1 May 1994; river views (S4, S5).
- Time: About 30 minutes (S4)
- Fee: Free on foot on Go Vietnam cluster page (S4). One page prints 5,000 VND for motorbike — not on kontum.gov.vn; Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- From the base: About 3 km from central Kon Tum to the communal house side; bridge links to Kon K’Tu (S5)
- When to go: Early morning or late afternoon light on Go Vietnam (S4)
- Mistake to avoid: Crossing in high wind or heavy rain when the deck moves
- Local interest: Photography, mountains
- Suggested slug: kon-klor-bridge
- Suggested dot: 4 — the river picture paired with the church
- Photo must show: Bridge deck, river, and banks in one frame
- Sources: S4, S5

### Kon K’Tu village
- Vietnamese name: Làng Kon K’Tu
- Kind: area
- Area: Dak Ro Wa commune, across Kon Klor bridge
- What the visitor does: Ba Na stilt houses and communal house; gong culture (S4, S12).
- Time: 2–3 hours (S4)
- Fee: Free entry on Go Vietnam cluster page (S4). Workshop or performance fees: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- From the base: Cross Kon Klor bridge, about 5 km from centre (S4)
- When to go: Dry season; Pơ Thi atmosphere in 12th lunar month — Check on arrival for exact dates (S4)
- Mistake to avoid: Entering homes without a host invitation
- Local interest: Culture, photography, family
- Suggested slug: kon-ktu-village
- Suggested dot: 4 — the village paired with the bridge day
- Photo must show: Stilt houses or communal house, not a posed shop interior
- Sources: S4, S12

- Vietnamese name: Phở khô hai tô
- Kind: lifestyle
- What a day here actually is: Wake to two-bowl dry phở near the market, walk the church and museum, cross Kon Klor in the afternoon, coffee on the river at dusk (S10, S4).
- When: Shops mainly morning per Go Vietnam phở page (S10)
- Area: Central streets and market
- Suggested slug: morning-pho
- Photo must show: Two bowls — dry noodles and broth separate
- Sources: S10
