# Getting there — Kon Tum

## Nearest airport

- Pleiku Airport (PXU), Gia Lai. Vietnam Tourism’s domestic-air summary lists Pleiku among highland gateways; FlightConnections (checked 21 Oct 2026) shows nonstop domestic flights from Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City on Vietnam Airlines and VietJet Air. Kon Tum has no passenger airport of its own (S1, S2).

## Getting around on site

- Kon Tum city sights in the dossier cluster sit within about 10 km (S4). Kon Klor communal house is about 3 km from central Kon Tum (S5). Kon K’Tu village is about 5 km across the Kon Klor bridge (S4). Motorbike or taxi is the usual way between the church, prison, museum, bridge, and village. ## Season limit on the journey

- Govietnam’s cluster page advises avoiding June–September for heavy rain and slippery forest roads on highland side trips (S4). That is guidance, not a road-closure notice.
