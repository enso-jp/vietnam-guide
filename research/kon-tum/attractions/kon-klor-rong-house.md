## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the yard of the Ba Na nhà rông, look up the thatched gable, and step inside only when the hall is open to visitors. Gongs and festival objects are inside; the yard is for ceremonies (A1).

## Size and rebuild

- 17 m long, 6 m wide, roof about 22 m. Timber, bamboo, reeds, thatch (A1). A2 gives the same 17 m, 6 m, and 22 m to the roof. Both stay.
- Burned in 2010 and reopened in June 2011 at nearly the same site and size (A1 only). A date with one source. Left out of public copy.
- “Largest nhà rông in the Central Highlands” is on A1 and A2. Skip the superlative.
