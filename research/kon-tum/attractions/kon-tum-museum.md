## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: From 18 Aug 2026, adult 20,000 VND a visit and child 10,000 VND a visit (A1, A2), under Resolution 52/2026/NQ-HĐND of 30 May 2026.
- Child, height, or senior rule: The same notices list children and school students as free, and also list a child at 10,000 VND. Both stay. Seniors, students in training programmes, recognised artisans, residents of specially difficult hamlets, people on a culture-benefit policy, and visitors on Vietnam Culture Day (24 Nov) pay half. People with recognised service to the revolution, people with a disability, and people in poor or near-poor households are free, as are visitors to a special display for a political event. If more than one reduction applies, only the largest one is used. Proof is required (A1, A2).
- What the basic ticket includes: A visit. Gallery inclusions are not itemised.
- What costs extra: UNSOURCED — Check on arrival.
- A second adult figure: 10,000 VND, and a child 5,000 VND, on A3 only. Both stay beside 20,000 and 10,000. Do not average.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: A1 and A2 for the 18 Aug 2026 tariff. A3 for 10,000 and 5,000.
