## What it is

- English name: Kon Tum Prison
- Vietnamese name: Nhà tù Kon Tum (Nhà ngục Kon Tum)
- Kicker (one line, what it is): French-era prison museum in the centre
- Kind: sight
- Address: Đường Trương Quang Trọng, phường Quyết Thắng (A3, A4). North bank of the Đăk Bla (A3, A4).

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Memorial yard, traditional house, displays, and the “Bất khuất” statue group (A3, A4). The hero photograph is that yard and the statues. A second frame of the cells was not found, so the public page has no body.
- How long on site: About 1.5 hours on A5 only. One publisher. Public duration: Check on arrival.
- How long including the ride from the base: Walk or ride from the centre. Minutes UNSOURCED — Check on arrival.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Expecting a light scenic walk. The subject is colonial prison history.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The adult fee is 20,000 VND or 30,000 VND. See Fee.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: From 18 Aug 2026, adult 20,000 VND and child 10,000 VND (A1, A2), Resolution 52/2026/NQ-HĐND of 30 May 2026. A5 still prints 30,000 VND for an adult. Both adult figures stay. Do not average.
- Child, height, or senior rule: On the 20,000 VND tariff, a child is 10,000 VND, and children and school students are also free (A1, A2). Both stay. On the 30,000 VND line, no child price was printed. Public child price for that line: Check on arrival.
- Free on the 20,000 VND tariff: people with recognised service to the revolution; relatives of fighters who died at the site; people with a disability; children and school students; poor and near-poor households; people at a periodic memorial ceremony (A1, A2).
- Half price: seniors; people on a culture-benefit policy; residents of specially difficult hamlets; students in training; People’s Artisans and Elite Artisans; visitors on 24 November (A1, A2). One reduction only, the largest, with proof.
- What the basic ticket includes: A visit. A5’s list of cells, a film, and object counts is one publisher. Left out of the public card because there is no second photo and no body.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: A1 and A2 for 20,000 / 10,000. A5 for 30,000.
