## What it is

- English name: Kon Klor Suspension Bridge
- Vietnamese name: Cầu treo Kon Klor
- Kind: crossing, not a sight page
- The bridge links the Kon Tum side of the Đăk Bla with Đăk Rơ Wa and Kon K’Tu (A1, A2). The separate stop on the near bank is the Kon Klor communal house. The village page carries the crossing. This file does not get public JSON.

## Figures kept for the other pages

- Length 292 m, width 4.5 m. Begun 3 Feb 1993, completed 1 May 1994 (A1). A2 gives 292 m, opened 1994, and places the hall beside the bridge.
- Walk free, or 5,000 VND for a motorbike or light car, on A3 only. Not copied onto a bridge page. The motorbike toll stays Check on arrival and off a page that is not published.
- A licensed deck photo exists (File:Konklor Bridge, Kon Tum City, Kon Tum, Viet Nam.JPG, public domain). It is not used, because the bridge is not a page.
