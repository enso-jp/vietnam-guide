### Xên Mường
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Xên Mường
- Kind: festival
- When: Báo Phú Thọ prints it as annual in the first lunar month, on the 9th and 10th, with the 2026 opening on 26 February, the 10th, in xã Mai Hạ (S13). The ministry records a reception on 19 February 2024, also mùng 10 tháng Giêng, then in the district (S8). The next civil date moves. Check on arrival for the next year’s day.
- Where: Mai Hạ for the 2026 opening (S13). The 2024 rite the ministry describes was at the Tướng Sứ shrine, xóm Chiềng Châu, then xã Chiềng Châu (S8). People from Mai Châu, Mai Hạ, Bao La, Tân Mai, and Pà Cò are named at the 9th and 10th (S13).
- What happens: Ceremony and games. A mo leads the rite. Offerings named in 2026: buffalo, pigs, chickens, sticky rice, wine, and two decorated buffaloes (S13). Procession toward the Tướng Sứ shrine. Keng Loóng is performed (S8, S13). Games named across the two pages: kéo co, ném còn, bắn nỏ, đẩy gậy, đi cà kheo, múa xòe, hát đối, chọi gà, and a snail-cake wrapping contest and a Thai cooking contest (S8, S13). Food stalls, no dish list on the ministry page (S8).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Neither page prints a ticket.
- Not the same as attraction slug: lac
- Sources: S8, S13

### Keng Loóng at Tết
- Vietnamese name: Keng Loóng
- Kind: activity
- When: Tết Nguyên đán is named by the ministry as one occasion. The civil dates of Tết move. Check on arrival for the next Tết (S8).
- Where: Thai communities in Mai Châu (S8)
- What happens: Pestle dance on a wooden trough. National intangible heritage for the social custom and belief, listed in a November 2023 ministry decision. The article does not print the decision number (S8). The February 2024 reception covered Keng Loóng and Xên Mường (S8).
