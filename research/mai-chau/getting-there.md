# getting there — Mai Chau

Checked: 23 Oct 2026

No town pin is stored. The published place file has a pin. This pass did not find a listed source that prints that point for the town, so the pin is not adopted and no replacement is stored. A village house, a pass, a reserve center, and a bounding-box midpoint are not the town. S16 prints a center for the Hang Kia – Pà Cò reserve. That point is not stored.

The usual previous stop is Hà Nội. The night is the valley, not Hòa Bình city and not the pass.

- From the usual previous stop:
  - Hà Nội by road | S2 and S5 both print about 140 km. S1 and S3 print about three hours by car or bus, S1 via Hòa Bình. S4 prints 2.5 hours in parentheses. The 140 km figures agree. The hours do not. Keep about three hours (S1, S3) and 2.5 hours (S4). Do not pick one.
  - Other kilometres, not chosen | S18 prints about 150 km in the intro, 130 km from central Hà Nội, and 157 km from Nội Bài. Those three disagree with each other and with 140 km. They stay named. They are not the figure to print alone.
  - The pass | S3 says leave in the morning for the view at Thung Khe and the cliff-side view of the valley. S5 names đèo Thung Khe. Neither prints a pass altitude or a pass fee. Pass fee — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
  - Bus | S3 names a bus from My Dinh, and also a shuttle, a private car, or a motorbike. S2 says local buses run to Mai Châu town, a short walk from Bản Lác and Pom Coọng. S2 says a traveler from Sơn La or Điện Biên should ask the driver to stop at the Mai Châu turn-off. No station timetable and no fare were on those pages. Operator clocks and the fare — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Commercial coach tables were not adopted.
  - From Nội Bài | S18 names the airport and then the road through Hòa Bình and QL6. Its kilometres are the disagreeing set above. Minutes — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Getting around on site: S2 says bicycles are the way across the valley floor, and that electric carts can be hired between villages. S5 prints bicycle hire at 20.000–50.000 đồng a trip. No second source printed that hire price. Electric-cart hire — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. S5’s valley page prints “Mở cửa 24h”. That reads as a directory flag for the valley, not a ticket gate. No walking ticket is printed. Walking fee — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Season limit on the journey: S2 says June–August monsoon can flood local roads. S1 says heavier rain from May to September. Both stay. S3 says the morning is the better time to drive, for the pass view. A closure calendar — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
