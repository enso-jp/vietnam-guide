# Sources — Mai Chau

Checked: 23 Oct 2026

### S1
- URL: https://vietnam.travel/places-to-go/northern-midlands-mountains/mai-chau
- Publisher: Vietnam Tourism (vietnam.travel)
- Accessed: 23 Oct 2026
- Supports: About three hours by car or bus from Hà Nội via Hòa Bình. Best Sep–Nov, with March–May also pleasant. Heavier rain May–September. Lac and Pom Coong stilt-house villages. Cycling the valley paths. Sunday morning market. Homestay dinner. Day trips named as Hòa Bình Lake kayaking and Pù Luông.

### S2
- URL: https://www.vietnam.travel/vi/places-to-go/northern-vietnam/mai-chau
- Publisher: Vietnam Tourism (vietnamese page)
- Accessed: 23 Oct 2026
- Supports: About 140 km from Hà Nội. Buses from the capital to Mai Châu town, a short walk from Bản Lác and Pom Coọng. From Sơn La or Điện Biên, ask the driver to stop at the Mai Châu turn-off. Motorbike or private car from Hà Nội. Bicycles on the valley floor. Electric carts between villages. Best March–May and September–November. October called the most ideal harvest month, rice golden. June–August: monsoon can flood local roads.

### S3
- URL: https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/7-essential-things-do-mai-chau
- Publisher: Vietnam Tourism
- Accessed: 23 Oct 2026
- Supports: Morning walk and cà phê phin. White Thai weaving. Hoa Ban+ named as a social enterprise in Lac. Cycling, and electric carts if not cycling. Cơm lam, gà đồi, thịt lợn xiên nướng, stir-fried greens, ve sầu chiên. After-dinner White Thai music and dance. Stilt houses in Lac and Poom Coọng. Homestay price bands. About three hours from Hà Nội. My Dinh bus station, shuttle, private car, motorbike. Thung Khe pass and the valley view on the way in. Best March–May or harvest September–November. Morning kayak on the Hòa Bình reservoir. Full-day trek in neighbouring Pù Luông. Mai Châu Lodge named only as a desk for guides.

### S4
- URL: https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/explore-relax-play-northern-vietnam
- Publisher: Vietnam Tourism
- Accessed: 23 Oct 2026
- Supports: A parenthetical 2.5 hours, which disagrees with the “about three hours” on S1 and S3. Seven ethnic groups. White Thai and Black Thai called the largest share. Rice, footpaths, stilt houses. Homestay meals. Early night under countryside dark. Pù Luông as a trek just outside town. Hòa Bình Lake as a half day of kayaking.

### S5
- URL: https://dulichphutho.gov.vn/diemden/thung-lung-mai-chau
- Publisher: Trang thông tin chính thức du lịch Phú Thọ
- Accessed: 23 Oct 2026
- Supports: 140 km northwest of Hà Nội. Address line prints both “tỉnh Hòa Bình” and “Thành phố Việt Trì, Phú Thọ”. Bản Lác, Pom Coọng, đèo Thung Khe, hang Mỏ Luông, Bản Văn, thác Gò Lào. Núi Đầu Rồng as the valley viewpoint. Walking the lanes. Bicycle hire 20.000–50.000 đồng a trip. Homestay at Bản Lác. Listing status “Mở cửa 24h”. Food names: vịt cỏ, lợn cắp nách, lợn mán, rượu cần, gà nướng, dế nướng, xôi nếp, rượu Mai Hạ, nhộng ong rừng, măng chua, cơm lam. Quang Dũng, “Tây Tiến”. Contact maichau@myhoabinh.vn.

### S7
- URL: https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/toan-van-nghi-quyet-so-1676-nq-ubtvqh15-sap-xep-cac-dvhc-cap-xa-cua-tinh-phu-tho-nam-2025-119250616204317326.htm
- Publisher: Cổng thông tin điện tử Chính phủ. Number and header also read from the Nhân Dân CDN copy of 1676/NQ-UBTVQH15
- Accessed: 23 Oct 2026
- Supports: Resolution 1676/NQ-UBTVQH15. Clause 119 forms xã Mai Châu from thị trấn Mai Châu, xã Nà Phòn, Thành Sơn, Tòng Đậu, and part of xã Đồng Tân. Clause 120 forms xã Bao La from Mai Hịch, Xăm Khòe, and Bao La. Clause 121 forms xã Mai Hạ from Chiềng Châu, Vạn Mai, and Mai Hạ. Clause 122 forms xã Pà Cò from Cun Pheo, Hang Kia, Pà Cò, and the rest of Đồng Tân. Clause 123 forms xã Tân Mai from Sơn Thủy (old Mai Châu district) and Tân Thành. The resolution does not print an area or a population for xã Mai Châu. It takes effect on the day it is passed, and the new commune governments operate from 1 July 2025. Công báo lists the issue date as 16 June 2025. The catalog field “ngày hiệu lực 01/01/1900” on one Công báo card is a blank and is not used.

### S8
- URL: https://bvhttdl.gov.vn/keng-loong-va-le-hoi-xen-muongdon-nhan-di-san-van-hoa-phi-vat-the-quoc-gia-20240219170343645.htm
- Publisher: Bộ Văn hóa, Thể thao và Du lịch
- Accessed: 23 Oct 2026
- Supports: 19 February 2024, mùng 10 tháng Giêng, reception in Mai Châu district, then Hòa Bình, for national intangible heritage Keng Loóng and the Xên Mường festival. Games: kéo co, ném còn, hội trống chiêng. Food stalls at the festival, no dish names. Ethnic groups named: Thái, Mường, Kinh, Dao, H’Mông, Tày, Hoa.

### S9
- URL: https://nongthon.vietnamtourism.gov.vn/nhung-diem-den-du-lich-cong-dong-hap-dan-o-huyen-mai-chau-hoa-binh/
- Publisher: Chuyên trang du lịch nông thôn, Cục Du lịch Quốc gia Việt Nam. Byline Báo Hòa Bình
- Accessed: 23 Oct 2026
- Supports: Bản Lác, then xã Chiềng Châu, White Thai, more than 700 years, receiving foreign guests from the 1960s. Pom Coọng in the old township. Pom = hill, Coọng = drum. Bản Văn quieter than Lác. Bản Hịch, xã Mai Hịch, about 12 km from the district center. Weaving and basketry. Dishes at Hịch: vịt đầu xanh, rau sắng, cá dầm xanh. Suối Xia, raft or boat. Bản Bước, xã Xăm Khòe, palm hills and terraces. Nearly 30 km from the township on QL6 toward Sơn La to Hmong villages in Hang Kia and Pà Cò. Bản Chà Đáy, xã Pà Cò, 1.140 m. Saturday night market and Pà Cò market on Sunday morning. About 6 km on to Hang Kia village. Spring plum and peach, cloud sea, Hmong weaving and dyeing. Hang Kia dishes: xôi nếp nương, lợn bản, gà nướng, bánh dày. Restored rites: Xên bản, Xên Mường, Gầu Tào. Highland market and Pà Cò night market. Old district lodging: more than 200 businesses, 30 resorts and hotels, more than 170 community houses, 15 hamlets. 2022: more than half a million visits. That is the old district, not the 2025 commune figure.

### S11
