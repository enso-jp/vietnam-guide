# Food — Mai Chau

Checked: 23 Oct 2026

Sourced specialties stored below: 25 named dishes, drinks, and one sauce. The bar for this pass is 20. The list is not padded to 40. A specialty is a dish. Streets and restaurants are not specialties.

Names that may be the same plate are kept apart, because the sources use different words. Gà đồi and gà nướng both stay. Vịt cỏ and vịt đầu xanh both stay. Xôi nếp and xôi nếp nương both stay. Lợn cắp nách, lợn mán, and lợn bản all stay. Cá dầm xanh, cá suối, and cá suối chiên all stay. Do not collapse them into one line.

No stall price is stored. Commercial pages printed prices and restaurant doors. Those were not adopted.

- How to eat it: S3 says with minced peanuts. No second method was adopted.
- When to order it: With the homestay meal (S1, S3, S11). A clock — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED

### Smoked buffalo

- What it is: S19 says Thai villages in Mai Châu smoke buffalo. The cut it names is loin and shoulder, sliced with the grain, seasoned with lemongrass, ginger, garlic, dried chili, and mắc khén, then hung in the smoke for months. It says a good piece stays somewhat red.
- How to eat it: With rượu Mai Hạ (S19)

### Pa Co market
- Vietnamese name: Chợ phiên Pà Cò
- Kind: food-street
- What the visitor does: S9 says the Pà Cò market meets on Sunday morning, and that Bản Chà Đáy has a night market on Saturday evening. The rural desk also says the district holds a Mai Châu highland market and a Pà Cò night market, without hours (S9).
- When to go: Sunday morning for the Pà Cò market. Saturday evening for the Chà Đáy night market (S9). Hours — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Area: Pà Cò and Bản Chà Đáy, about 30 km from the old township on QL6 toward Sơn La (S9)
- Suggested slug: pa-co-market
- Photo must show: the highland market, not the Bản Lác rice floor
- unpublished: no licensed photo in this pass
- Sources: S9
