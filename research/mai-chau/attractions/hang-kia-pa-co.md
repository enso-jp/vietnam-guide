## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Ride out of the rice valley. Bản Chà Đáy, then about 6 km on to Hang Kia village. Saturday night market at Chà Đáy. Sunday morning market at Pà Cò. Spring plum and peach, a cloud sea, Hmong weaving and dyeing (S9).
