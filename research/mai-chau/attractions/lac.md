## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the flat lanes between stilt houses and paddies. Weaving is in the lanes. Sleep in a house if the night is the point. The morning is the rice (S1, S3, S5, S9).

- Notice: Stay if the morning rice is why you came. A midday walk misses the floor after dinner.

## Season

- Sight-specific months: September–November, October the golden month, and March–May also pleasant (S1, re-read 23 Oct 2026). S11, re-read 23 Oct 2026, says the rice starts to turn gold in late May and early June, and is gold again in September and October, with cool weather then. Both stay. `bestMonths` is left unset so the place months still show. The body states both windows.
- One concrete climate fact: Heavier rain May–September (S1). June–August monsoon can flood local roads (S2, stored). A closure calendar is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
