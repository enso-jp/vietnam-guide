# Research dossier — Mai Chau

Slug: `mai-chau`
Province: Phú Thọ. Resolution 202/2025/QH15 of 12 June 2025 merges Vĩnh Phúc, Hòa Bình, and Phú Thọ into Phú Thọ (S6). Resolution 1676/NQ-UBTVQH15 forms xã Mai Châu from the old township plus Nà Phòn, Thành Sơn, Tòng Đậu, and part of Đồng Tân, and the new commune governments operate from 1 July 2025 (S7). The resolution does not print an area or a population. The Phú Thọ foreign-affairs page prints 147,741 km² and more than 19.000 people for the commune (S14). That pair is one source. It is not checked against a second census table. The same page prints 2025 visits as “trên 29 nghìn” and, in that sentence, domestic 21.370 and international 27.831, with revenue over 76 tỷ đồng (S14). Those visit counts disagree. Both stay. Do not pick one. S9’s “more than half a million” is the old district in 2022, not this commune figure.
Kind: town
Corridor: `northern-midlands` on the published place file. The board row says `northern-vietnam`. This dossier follows the published corridor and does not edit either file.

## Why a night here

- The picture they expect: a wide rice valley, the crop in the frame, and Thai stilt houses at the edge of the fields, with the limestone ridge behind. S1, S2, S5, and S11 all describe that morning. The published hero is this picture. It matches.
- Where they sleep, as an area: stilt-house homestays in Bản Lác and Pom Coọng (S1, S3). S3 also says four-star hotels and lodges exist, and prints bands of about 200.000 đồng for a rustic homestay, about 600.000 for a boutique farm stay, and about 1.500.000 for a four-star hotel. Those are the tourism office’s bands, one source, not a brand. No hotel brand is a pick. S14’s commune count is 45 community homestays, 15 guesthouses, and 3 hotels. S9’s old-district count is more than 200 lodging businesses, 30 resorts and hotels, and more than 170 community houses. Different geography and a different year. Both stay.
- Where the evening is: the stilt-house floor. After dinner, White Thai music and dance (S3). In Pom Coọng, xòe, sạp, and instruments (S11). Khắp Thái in the evening exchange (S14). A bar street was not found.
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): Bản Lác and Pom Coọng as the two homestay villages (S1). Núi Đầu Rồng for the whole valley (S5). Đèo Thung Khe on the way in (S3, S5). The Sunday morning market (S1, S2). These are pins, not a ranked list.
- Hero must include: the valley floor, the rice, and the stilt houses in one frame, subject through the center, in color. The ridge can sit at the back. A portrait crop fails.
- A crop that would fail: one house front with no field; a loom with no valley; the pass with no valley floor; empty sky or fog with no roofs; a plate of food; a statue or a dancer cropped at the torso.

### Hang Kia and Pa Co
- Vietnamese name: Hang Kia – Pà Cò

- Mistake to avoid: Using a Mai Châu valley photo for this highland, or folding it into the Mộc Châu page
