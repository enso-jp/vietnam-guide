## What it is

- English name: Thung Nham Bird Park
- Vietnamese name: Vườn chim Thung Nham (Khu du lịch sinh thái Thung Nham)
- Kicker: Bird valley and caves in a karst basin
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- Basin more than 40 ha inside the Hoa Lư special-use forest; bird garden more than 300 ha, about 40,000 birds, about 5,000 nests, 46 species; egrets, night herons and others; the boatman stops at a distance from the bird island; bicycle or boat to reach the garden (S37).
- Động Vái Giời: largest cave, 439 stone steps, about 5,000 m², three levels (S7, S37).
- Động Tiên Cá: water cave of more than 1,500 m, walked on a bamboo raft walkway (S37).
- Location: about 5 km west of chùa Bích Động (S7). Thôn Hải Nham / Đam Khê (S7); "thung đồi Hải Nham, phường Nam Hoa Lư" (S37).
- Hang Bụt is listed by S37 as a Thung Nham cave, but S3 sells it on a separate Linh Cốc–Hải Nham row and S7 gives two locations. Left off this page.
