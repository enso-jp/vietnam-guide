- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- About 20,000 m², 22 old houses collected mainly in Ninh Bình and the northern delta, mostly from the Nguyễn period onward; added to the Tam Cốc–Bích Động site by the provincial culture and tourism sector in 2008 (S7, S40).
- Bounds: east the road to Đền Thái Vi, west sông Ngô Đồng, south the Tam Cốc pier, north the cửa Quen hills (S40).
- Houses on the page (S40): Lưu Phương house from Kim Sơn, 90 m², five bays, forest xoan wood from Thanh Hóa, fish-scale tiles, handmade red brick, now the reception. Ý Yên house, built 1883, from Ý Yên (Nam Định), Lý ceramics of the 12th–13th centuries. Thọ Xuân house, more than 100 Gia Long-era dragon jars, tallest 65 cm, smallest 25 cm.
- Skipped from S40: the "largest collection in Vietnam" claim and the shaman story.
