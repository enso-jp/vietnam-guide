# Attraction research — The three caves

Place: `tam-coc`
Slug: `three-caves`
Status: stored
Checked: 23 Oct 2026

## What it is

- English name: The three caves
- Vietnamese name: Tam Cốc — hang Cả, hang Hai, hang Ba
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Sampan through three water caves
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Board at the pier at Đình Các in thôn Văn Lâm, row the Ngô Đồng through hang Cả, hang Hai, and hang Ba, return to the same pier (S5, S7).
- Cave sizes on the page (heritage bureau, official, S5, re-opened 23 Oct 2026): hang Cả khoảng 180 m, width ước chừng 30 m, ceiling higher than 5 m, most stalactites because floods hardly reach the roof; hang Hai khoảng 90 m, ceiling chừng 3.5 m; hang Ba hơn 80 m, ceiling under 3 m, often flooded, stalactites worn smooth. Past hang Ba: river, streams, forest, mountains (S5).
- Conflict kept here, off the page: Vietnamese Wikipedia prints 127 m, 60 m, and 50 m (S7). The page uses the heritage bureau.
- End of the route, conflict kept here, off the page: heritage bureau says đền Nội Lâm (Cao Sơn đại vương) (S5); Vietnamese Wikipedia says đền Thần Nông (S7). The page does not name an end temple.
- How long on site: khoảng 2 giờ out and back (S7 only). The ticket table prints no duration (S3).
- Mistake that wastes the visit: treating this as the Tràng An boat (different river, sông Sào Khê, different ticket row, S3, S8).
- Notice: the ticket covers the boat, Đền Thái Vi, Động Tiên, chùa Bích Động. Thung Nham, Thạch Bích–Thung Nắng, and Hang Chùa–Hang Ghé–Hang Bụt are separate rows (S3).

## Fee

- Basic price: adult 250,000 VND without the shuttle; with the electric shuttle adult 350,000 VND (S3, updated 27 Feb 2026, re-opened 23 Oct 2026).
- Child: 1–1.3 m 120,000 VND, or 190,000 VND with the shuttle. Under 1 m: no line. Left off the page.
- Includes: Tam Cốc, Đền Thái Vi, Động Tiên, chùa Bích Động.
- Older lines kept here only: 23 May 2023 tourism-center page 120,000 đồng a person plus boat 150,000 đồng a trip (S24); DulichToday 2024 adult 120,000, child 60,000, boat 150,000 one way (S17). The page prints the newest official table only.

## Getting there

### From Hà Nội

- Mode: train SE7, 06:00 from Hà Nội; arrives ga Ninh Bình 08:16 on the railway table, 08:13 on Asia Train Time, Vexere, and Baolau (see `getting-there.md`). Page prints "a little after 08:10".
- Station to site: about 7 km (S4: 7 km from Ninh Bình city). Minute UNSOURCED.
- Road: chừng 100 km from Hà Nội (S8).

### From thôn Văn Lâm

- Mode: on foot to the pier; bicycles common, hire at most tourist points (S7). Fare UNSOURCED, not printed.

## Season
