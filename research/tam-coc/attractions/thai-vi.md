- Kicker: Stone temple to the Trần kings among the karst
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- History on the page: inside the old Vũ Lâm base (S6, S39). Trần Thái Tông (r. 1225–1258) founded the base near hang Cả; on 7 May 1285 Trần Thánh Tông and Trần Nhân Tông beat part of the Mongol-Yuan army there (S39). The heritage bureau says the temple honours the Trần kings (S5).
- Building on the page: Nguyễn style, one hall of three bays, 28 stone columns 3.2 m high, 22 square; Duy Tân bell tower, two storeys, eight roofs, 32 lim columns; national historic relic 1994 (S6).
- Deity lists, conflict kept here, off the page: S6 (2016): Trần Thái Tông, Trần Thánh Tông, Hiển từ Hoàng thái hậu, vọng Trần Nhân Tông and Trần Anh Tông. S7: Trần Thái Tông, Trần Thánh Tông, Trần Hưng Đạo, Trần Quang Khải, Trần Thị Dung. The page uses the official general line "the Trần kings" (S5).
- Động Thiên Hương on the path from the river, about 15 m above the ground (S7).
