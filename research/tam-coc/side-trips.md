### Mua Cave viewpoint
- Slug if it exists: place `ninh-binh`, attraction `mua-cave`
- Travel time from this base: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No page opened here prints the minutes from the Tam Coc wharf.
- Why go: The culture department’s page of 3 Jul 2026 puts Hang Múa in thôn Khê Đầu, phường Hoa Lư, and says 486 stone steps give the view over Tam Coc and the rice at harvest (S25). It is not in Nam Hoa Lư, and the 14 Aug 2025 Tam Coc page does not name it (S2). The same culture page calls the best lotus weeks June and July, and says the lotus pond is best early in the morning (S25). That is not the May–June rice sentence used for the boat (S5, S6). The 27 Feb 2026 price list: 150,000 VND for a visitor over 1 m, free under 1 m, phone 0987.111.312 (S3). The 1 Aug 2025 support list prints a different phone, 02293.619.679 (S18). Hours: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Overnight or day: day
- Sources: S2, S3, S5, S6, S18, S25

### Trang An boat
