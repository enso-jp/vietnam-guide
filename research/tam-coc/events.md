# Events — Tam Coc

Festivals only. The boat, the pagoda, and the caves stay in `attractions.md`.

### Lễ hội Sắc vàng Tam Cốc
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Sắc vàng Tam Cốc – Tràng An
- Kind: festival
- When: 2026 only, as printed. The culture office, 18 Apr 2026, says Tuần Du lịch Ninh Bình runs about 10 days at the end of May 2026, and places Lễ hội Sắc vàng Tam Cốc in cuối tháng 5/2026 (S14). The tourism department, dated 24 May 2026, says the festival opened on the morning of 24 May 2026 at Khu du lịch Tam Cốc – Bích Động (S13). An end date was not on those two pages. Next year’s solar dates move with the rice. Check on arrival.
- Where: Khu du lịch Tam Cốc – Bích Động. The procession left bến thuyền Tam Cốc and entered Đền Thần Nông. Stops named for the folk shows on the river route: bến Thánh, hang Cả, hang Hai, hang Ba (S13, S14).
- What happens: A rite to Thần Nông, a procession of produce and medicinal plants from 34 provinces on sông Ngô Đồng, an art field titled “Song mã chầu báo công”, rice cutting, and a photo exhibition (S13). The culture office also names a photo tour “Mùa vàng Tam Cốc”, an exhibition at the pier, and water puppetry, chèo, xẩm, and folk song four times a day at tourist sites during the week (S14). About 2,000 performers are on the tourism department’s opening report (S13).
- Fee: No festival ticket was printed apart from the site tickets. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Not the same as attraction slug: than-nong, three-caves
- Sources: S13, S14

### Giỗ Tổ nghề thêu Văn Lâm
- Vietnamese name: Lễ giỗ Tổ nghề thêu
- Kind: festival
- When: The 20th of the first lunar month, every year, on the 23 Apr 2026 tourism page (S15). The solar date moves. Check on arrival.
- Where: Đền thờ Tổ nghề thêu in thôn Văn Lâm (S15).
- What happens: The anniversary of the person who brought embroidery to the hamlet. The page does not print a programme beyond the anniversary. Who that person was is disputed. See the embroidery entry in `attractions.md`.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Not the same as attraction slug: van-lam-embroidery
- Sources: S15

### The scenic-complex festival on sông Sào Khê
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội đền Trần, ngày 18 tháng Ba âm lịch
- Kind: festival
- When: The 18th of the third lunar month, every year (S5). The solar date moves. Check on arrival. The same page puts the Hoa Lư capital festival on the 6th to the 8th of the third lunar month. That capital rite is `ninh-binh`, not this boat.
- Where: Đền Trần, which the heritage bureau places in xã Ninh Hải, huyện Hoa Lư, on sông Sào Khê, tied to the Tràng An caves (S5). Sông Sào Khê is not sông Ngô Đồng (S8).
- What happens: A rite for Quý Minh đại vương, ending with boats on the Tràng An line (S5). It is recorded so it is not copied onto the Tam Coc boat.
- Fee: Not a Tam Coc ticket. The Tràng An row is a different price on the 2026 table and belongs to `ninh-binh` (S3).
- Not the same as attraction slug: three-caves
- Sources: S3, S5, S8
