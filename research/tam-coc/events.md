# Events and to-do — Tam Coc

Checked: 21 Oct 2026

These are the recurring dates the pages actually print. A date that is only “expected” stays expected. Do not copy a sight into this file.

### Ninh Binh Tourism Week
- Vietnamese name: Tuần Du lịch Ninh Bình. The 2025 theme was “Sắc vàng Tam Cốc – Tràng An”. The 2026 theme on the 27 Mar 2026 page is “Ninh Bình – Hành trình di sản qua các thời đại”.
- Kind: festival
- When: The department page dated 21 May 2025 sets the 2025 week at 23–31 May 2025, opening at 13:30 on Friday 23 May at a stage in the rice near Hang Hai (S22). The page dated 27 Mar 2026 says the 2026 week is expected in the last 10 days of May, and that the opening is expected at Quảng trường Đinh Tiên Hoàng Đế, not at the wharf (S24). Those are not the same calendar. The solar dates for 2026 are Check on arrival. The walking street, when the week is on, is 07:00–22:00 in the 2025 plan (S23).
- Where: Tam Cốc – Bích Động is one of the centers. In 2025 the opening was in the rice by Hang Hai, and the countryside market was at the boat landing (S22). In the 2026 preview the opening square is not given a Tam Coc address, and the page also names Tràng An, Tam Chúc, and Thịnh Long (S24). Lễ hội sắc vàng Tam Cốc and the photo tour “Mùa vàng Tam Cốc” are the Tam Coc pieces. Forestival is named at Tràng An, not at this wharf (S24).
- What happens: Rice is the picture. The 2025 page says the field that year was planted as “Cá chép vượt vũ môn” on nearly 10,000 m² (S22). A 2026 field design was not on the 27 Mar 2026 page. Folk singing, water puppetry, and an evening exchange at the boat yard belong to the week (S22, S24). The road from Đồng Gừng is the food-street entry, not a second festival.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No ticket for the week was printed on these pages.
- Not the same as attraction slug: `three-caves`. The boat still runs as its own ticket. The week is the rice and the yard.
- Sources: S22, S23, S24

### Thai Vi festival
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội đền Thái Vi
- Kind: festival
- When: from the 14th to the 17th of the third lunar month (S16). The solar date moves. Check on arrival.
- Where: Đền Thái Vi, thôn Văn Lâm (S16)
- What happens: The page says people remember the Trần kings (S16). It does not print a program, a procession, or a ticket.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The temple visit on an ordinary day is the route ticket (S3). The festival page does not say the ticket changes.
- Not the same as attraction slug: `thai-vi`. This is the lunar festival, not the stone courtyard on a normal morning.
- Sources: S16, S3

### Hoa Lu festival, the neighbor
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Cố đô Hoa Lư
- Kind: festival
- When: from the 6th to the 8th of the third lunar month, in the heritage essay (S8). The same essay puts a separate rite for Quý Minh đại vương on the 18th of the third lunar month at đền Trần, on the Sào Khê, which is the Tràng An water, not the Ngô Đồng (S8). Both dates move. Check on arrival.
- Where: the ancient capital, and the Sào Khê for the 18th. The essay says Tam Cốc – Bích Động sits in that cultural space because it is not far from Hoa Lư (S8). It does not move the festival to the Tam Coc wharf.
- What happens: not described beyond the dates and, for the 18th, a rite on the water ending at đền Trần (S8).
- Fee: the Hoa Lư gate on the 27 Feb 2026 list is 20,000 VND for an adult and 10,000 VND for a child 6–15, a person 60 or over, or a disabled visitor (S3). That is the ordinary gate, not a festival surcharge. A festival ticket was not printed.
- Not the same as attraction slug: `hoa-lu`, which is published under `ninh-binh`, not under Tam Coc.
- Sources: S3, S8
