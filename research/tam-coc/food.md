## Comprehensive list of local food to try

### Crispy rice
- Vietnamese name: Cơm cháy. The department page also titles it Nhất hưởng Thiên Kim.
- What it is: Cooked rice spread thin in a round, cooled and dried, then fried until yellow and crisp, broken into pieces. The 20 Dec 2016 page pours a hot stir-fry over it: sliced beef tenderloin, pork heart and kidney, tomato, carrot, onion, and dried mushroom. The rice crackles (S20). The 23 May 2023 Tam Coc page names cơm cháy as one of the dishes to try on this visit and does not describe a goat sauce (S6). Do not swap the 2016 topping for a goat gravy.
- How to eat it: Break the crisp rice into a bowl and pour the hot stir-fry over it, then eat it while it still crackles (S20).
- When to order it: With the Tam Coc day, at lunch, which is when the 23 May 2023 page puts the meal (S6). A breakfast hour was not printed.
- Area, not a shop: restaurants in the Tam Coc tourist area or nearby, unnamed (S6). Not a stall row.
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none on the dish page. Goat is the other Tam Coc name, not an ingredient in S20.
- Sources: S6, S20

### Mountain goat
- Vietnamese name: Thịt dê núi Ninh Bình. Dê núi on the Tam Coc page (S6).
- What it is: Goat that the 23 Aug 2021 page puts on the limestone, eating forest leaves. The dishes it names: tiết canh dê, tái dê, dê nướng, dê xào lăn, lẩu dê. Tiết canh uses ear, tongue, tenderloin, and rib cartilage, with roasted peanut, roasted sesame, onion, and herbs. Tái comes as tái nhúng (sliced into boiling water, still pink), tái lăn (through a hot pan), and tái vừng (the same, then crushed roasted sesame) (S19).
- How to eat it: The rare goat is eaten with fig fruit, fig leaves, a few slices of green banana, lá ngổ, lá mơ, lemongrass, and a bowl of tương bần from Hưng Yên with crushed old ginger. The page says that sauce is what “matches” this goat. A sip of rượu Đam Khê goes with the tiết canh (S19).
- When to order it: At the lunch the Tam Coc page describes, with cơm cháy (S6). No hour.
- Area, not a shop: the karst, and the unnamed restaurants of the Tam Coc tourist area (S6, S19). Đam Khê, the hamlet on the Thung Nắng page, is where that rice-wine name points (S17, S19). Not a brand.
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: cơm cháy, on the same Tam Coc sentence (S6).
- Sources: S6, S17, S19

### Yen Mac fermented pork
- Vietnamese name: Nem chua Yên Mạc. The department page also says nem tiến vua.
- What it is: A fermented pork of xã Yên Mạc, not of the Tam Coc wharf. The 23 May 2023 Tam Coc page still names nem Yên Mạc among the dishes to try on this visit (S6). The 24 Aug 2021 page says it is eaten as soon as it has fermented, and that after 5 to 7 days it is still pink (S21).
- How to eat it: Pull it apart, take a small pinch, roll it in lá ổi tầu, lá sung, and herbs, and dip it in fish sauce with lime, crushed garlic, and chili or pepper (S21).
- When to order it: As a plate with the Tam Coc lunch, or as something carried away. The page does not say which (S6, S21).
- Area, not a shop: Yên Mạc. The 2021 page sends a visitor out of Ninh Bình city on QL1A toward Thanh Hóa, to km 9, then left on the main road of the old huyện Yên Mô (S21). That is not a walk from the wharf. Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: this is the neighbor. Do not describe it as a Tam Coc village recipe.
- Sources: S6, S21

### Dam Khe rice wine
- Vietnamese name: Rượu Đam Khê
- What it is: The drink the goat page names with tiết canh dê. It says the wine is clear (S19). No recipe.
- How to eat it: A sip beside the goat blood pudding (S19). No jar ritual was printed.
- When to order it: With that dish. Not a breakfast.
- Area, not a shop: the name points at Đam Khê, the hamlet of the Thung Nắng route (S17, S19). A shop was not named.
- Meal: drink
- Neighbor dish to link: dê núi
- Sources: S17, S19

## Food streets and markets

### Dong Gung to the boat landing
- Vietnamese name: Đoạn đường từ bến xe Đồng Gừng đến nhà hàng Nam Hoa; chợ quê ẩm thực at bến thuyền Tam Cốc
- Kind: food-street
- What the visitor does: During Ninh Bình Tourism Week, walks that road. The 2025 provincial plan sets the walking street from 07:00 to 22:00 on the days of the week, from bến xe Đồng Gừng to nhà hàng Nam Hoa, in thôn Văn Lâm, then xã Ninh Hải, thành phố Hoa Lư. The stalls sell souvenirs and food, and the plan also names embroidery from Ninh Hải (S23). The department’s 21 May 2025 page puts the “phố đi bộ và chợ quê ẩm thực” at the Tam Coc boat landing, and the OCOP stalls at bến xe Đồng Gừng, for the week of 23–31 May 2025 (S22). The same plan tells the old xã Ninh Hải to tidy the road from Đồng Gừng to chợ xã Ninh Hải (S23). That names a commune market. It does not say what the market sells, or that it is open the rest of the year. A year-round night market was not on these pages. A tour blog’s stall list was not used.
- When to go: the days of Tourism Week, 07:00–22:00 on the walking-street line (S23). The 2025 week was 23–31 May 2025 (S22). The 2026 dates are not in that page. Check on arrival for the next week.
- Area: thôn Văn Lâm, between Đồng Gừng and the wharf
- Suggested slug: `dong-gung`
- Photo must show: the road of stalls with the karst behind, in daylight or early evening, subject through the center. Not a single dish filling the frame, and not a restaurant sign as the subject.
- Sources: S22, S23
