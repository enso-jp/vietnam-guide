# Getting there — Tam Coc

Checked: 21 Oct 2026

Tam Coc is not a station and not an airport. The night is in Nam Hoa Lư. The usual previous stop is Hanoi. The train stops at Ga Ninh Bình. The wharf is a further road the department does not time.

### Getting around on site
- The 14 Jul 2023 page, inside a notice that also paused the boat, says points inside Tam Coc – Bích Động are 5–10 minutes apart, and points outside the area are about 15–20 minutes by motorbike or car (S7). Keep the date on that sentence.
- The 27 Feb 2026 ticket is the route Tam Cốc – Thái Vi – Động Tiên – Bích Động. The electric car is the transfer between those points, priced inside the higher ticket, not a city bus (S3).
- Bích Động is about 2 km from the Tam Coc wharf (S5). Thái Vi is about 2 km along the country roads from the wharf, on the 23 May 2023 page (S6).
- Cycling the rice is named as community tourism on 7 Jul 2025 (S5). A rental price was not printed. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.

### Season limit on the journey
- Rice is the reason to time the trip, not a road closure. Months 5–6 on the 7 Jul 2025 page (S5). Mid-May to early June on the 23 May 2023 page, green and gold together (S6). Both stay.
- Rain: the department’s useful-information page, dated 4 Dec 2016, says rainfall is concentrated from May to November, average annual temperature 23.1°C, and “average total rainfall reached 151.9 mm per year”, with 125–157 rain days (S12). The 2021 climate assessment of the provincial environment department says a humid subtropical climate, a hot humid season with a lot of rain from month V to month IX, a dry cold season from month XI to month III, annual rain 1,700–1,800 mm, and mean temperature 23.5°C. The next paragraph of the same assessment calls summer month V to month X and winter month XI to month IV (S13). The 151.9 mm figure and the 1,700–1,800 mm figure are not the same number. Do not average them, and do not “correct” the 2016 page. This pass is 21 Oct 2026, inside the 2016 May–November window, and on the edge of the 2021 summer if summer runs through October.
- The 17 Jul 2024 department notice says the last six months of 2024 were forecast to be wet, and it orders site managers to stop a cave, river, or lake route when the weather is unsafe and to tell visitors, companies, and the press (S14). That is the rule for a flood week. It is not a statement that the Tam Coc boat is closed on 21 Oct 2026. Check on arrival.
- The 14 Jul 2023 page calls months 6, 7, and 8 the low season and says the sites stay open. The same page paused the Đình Các – Tam Cốc boat from 9 Jul 2023 until further notice (S7). The 2025 department page and the 2026 price list still present the boat as the visit (S2, S3). Do not print the 2023 pause as the current gate, and do not print June–August as a 2026 low season unless a later page repeats it.
