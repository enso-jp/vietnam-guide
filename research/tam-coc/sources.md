# Sources — Tam Coc

Checked: 23 Oct 2026

### S1
- URL: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1438/
- Publisher: UNESCO World Heritage Centre
- Accessed: 23 Oct 2026
- Supports: Inscribed 2014. Minor boundary modification 2016. Current property 6,226 ha, buffer 6,026 ha. Trang An–Tam Coc–Bich Dong Scenic Landscape is one of three protected areas. Towers up to 200 m. Sampans rowed by local guides. Green and yellow rice. About 14,000 residents. No Tam Coc place coordinate.

### S2
- URL: https://whc.unesco.org/en/decisions/6099/
- Publisher: UNESCO World Heritage Centre, decision 38 COM 8B.14
- Accessed: 23 Oct 2026
- Supports: 2014 inscription. Property 6,172 ha, buffer 6,079 ha. “200m-high” towers. Sampans. 14,000 residents. The name on this text is Trang An–Tam Coc–Bich Dong Scenic Area. The hectare pair disagrees with S1. Both stay. The decision says a tourism plan sets visitor limits and does not print the number.
