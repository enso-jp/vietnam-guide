## Why a night here

### The city sand, with the tower upriver

- The picture they expect: Wake up on the city beach, east of town along Độc Lập: white sand, a beach tens of metres wide and longer than 10 km, casuarina behind it, and the beachfront buildings on that road (S12, S13). The handbook’s night line is the same sand at about 10 km of frontage, sea on one side and the town lights on the other (S13). That is the picture. The airport is not. The other famous frames, the stone plates and the cape light, are day trips from this bed (S4, S7).
- Where they sleep, as an area: Rooms on the beachfront along Độc Lập, or in the center by the river and Quảng trường 1/4 (S12, S13). The handbook says the city still has few high-end resorts and names brands (S14). A brand is not a pick. The sand is the reason to sleep here.
- Where the evening is: The beach walk on Độc Lập after dark, and Quảng trường 1/4 in the center (S13). Nghinh Phong, at the Nguyễn Hữu Thọ and Độc Lập corner, is the square the handbook puts a light show on at night (S14). Saturday at 19:30 the tourism page puts a show in the tower yard (S20).
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): The city beach, https://phuyentourism.gov.vn/diem-du-lich/bai-bien/bai-bien-tuy-hoa.html (S12). The tower hill, https://dsvh.gov.vn/thap-nhan-3238 (S3). The stone plates, https://vietnamtourism.vn/index.php/tourism/items/2930/ (S4). The cape, https://vietnamtourism.vn/index.php/tourism/items/2719 (S7). They are pins. No prices and no scores.
- Hero must include: The surf, the sand, the casuarina line, and the buildings along Độc Lập in one frame, subject through the center. A stone-plate frame, if that is a second photo, needs the black columns and the sea together. A tower frame needs the full tower and the river, not a crop of brick.
- A crop that would fail: Empty sand, empty sky, a hotel sign, fog with no shoreline, or a slice of the tower that loses the river.

### Tuy Hoa beach
- Vietnamese name: Bãi biển Tuy Hòa
- Kind: area
- Area: East of the city, along Độc Lập (S12). The night page calls it the beach in the center of town (S13).
- What the visitor does: Swims and walks a long white-sand beach. The beach page says the sand is tens of metres wide, longer than 10 km, with casuarina, and that people swim and eat seafood here every day (S12). The night page says the morning is for exercise and the evening is the walk, sea one side and town lights the other, and prints about 10 km (S13). The “longer than 10 km” and the “about 10 km” stay.

### Nhan Tower
- Vietnamese name: Tháp Nhạn
- Kind: sight
- Area: North bank of the Đà Rằng, near QL1A, in the city (S2). The heritage page puts it on Núi Nhạn, phường I, south side on Bạch Đằng and sông Chùa, about 1,5 km from cửa Đà Diễn (S3). The English tourism page says ward 1 (S20).
- What the visitor does: Climbs the hill to the Cham tower. The bureau says it was built in the late 11th or early 12th century, brick, four storeys, nearly 23,5 m high, each side of the foot 10 m, door to the east, a lotus-bud stone at the top, and a small shrine to bà Thượng Đỉnh Chúa Thiết A Na Diễn Ngọc Phi (S2). The heritage page describes three parts, a base of 12,2 m by 12,2 m, a body of about 9 m by 9 m, base and body together 12,4 m, a yard of 38 m by 38 m, and worship of Poh Nagar (S3). The nearly 23,5 m and the 12,4 m are different measurements. Both stay. Ranked a national architectural relic on 16/11/1988 (S2). Ranked a special national relic by Quyết định 1820/QĐ-TTg on 24/12/2018 (S3, S20).
- Time: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No office clock was printed. It is a hill and a tower, not a museum hour the pages describe.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The bureau, the heritage page, and the English tourism page print no ticket.
- From the base: In town, on the river (S2, S3). Minutes from Độc Lập: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: Morning for the brick. Saturday 19:30 for the show the English page names “Phu Yen – the attractive and friendly destination” (S20). The hill height is about 60 m on the heritage page and the handbook (S3, S14) and 64 m on the English page (S20). Both stay.
- Mistake to avoid: Quoting one height, or expecting the interior the bureau describes (no statue, a small shrine) to match a full museum. The heritage page says the east door had collapsed and the present door is 2,4 m high (S3).
- Local interest: The Cham hill
- Suggested slug: thap-nhan
- Suggested dot: 4 — the city’s own tower, a morning or a Saturday evening, not a day by itself
- Photo must show: The full tower, the hill, and the river in one frame, subject through the center. A brick close-up loses the river.
- Unpublished: no licensed photo
- Sources: S2, S3, S14, S20

Another news page prints a national ranking in 2005 and starts a December 2020 sentence that was cut off in the extract, so that sentence is not finished here (S5).

All three routes stay. Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The road was paved with signs in 2010 on the bureau page (S4).
