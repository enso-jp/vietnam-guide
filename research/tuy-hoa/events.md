### Fish wishing festival
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội cầu ngư
- Kind: festival
- When: From the first lunar month through the sixth lunar month, every year (S21). The solar window moves. Check on arrival.
- Where: Coastal and lagoon villages, at the tomb where the whale is worshipped (S21). A single city address was not printed.
- What happens: A rite, then games. The page names greeting the god, welcoming a royal honor, a funeral oration, hò bá trạo, boat racing, and swinging coracles, and says bộ singing is part of it. It says the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism recognized the province’s fish-wishing festival as national intangible heritage (S21). The handbook’s line on hát bài chòi is a separate national intangible mention, not this festival’s date (S14).

### O Loan boat race
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội đua thuyền đầm Ô Loan
- Kind: festival
- When: The rite starts on the 6th of the first lunar month at the village đình and Lăng Ông. The races are on the morning of the 7th (S16). Solar date: Check on arrival.
- Where: On the lagoon, 25 km north of the city, beside QL1 and the railway (S16).
- What happens: Dragon boats, sõng chài, sõng lưới, sõng chống sào, lắc thúng chai, plus múa siêu, múa lân, and hò bá trạo (S16).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Not the same as attraction slug: o-loan
- Sources: S16
