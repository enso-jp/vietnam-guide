## Sources

### A1
- URL: https://vietnamtourism.vn/index.php/news/items/26618
- Publisher: Cục Du lịch Quốc gia Việt Nam
- Accessed: 23 Oct 2026 (search extract; direct fetch timed out)
- Supports: Xã An Thạch, huyện Tuy An, Phú Yên. One of the oldest churches in the country. Gothic, nineteenth-century lines. Built in 1892 by Father Joseph de la Cassagne (Cố Xuân) for Tu hội Thừa sai Paris. First parish and the largest church in the province at that time. Keeps Phép giảng tám ngày by Alexandre de Rhodes, made in Rome in 1651. In 1641 he baptized more than 90 people here, including Anrê Phú Yên, and a memorial stands in the grounds. No fee and no clock in the extract.
