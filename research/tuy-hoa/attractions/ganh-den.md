# Attraction research — Ganh Den

Place: `tuy-hoa`
Slug: `ganh-den`
Status: unpublished
Checked: 23 Oct 2026

Unpublished: no licensed photo of the red-and-white light.

The light is a separate stop about 1 km north of Gành Đá Đĩa, xã An Ninh Đông, huyện Tuy An, not the Đại Lãnh light (handbook S14, 22 Oct 2026; Du lịch Phú Yên “Cảnh sắc Phú Yên”, search extract 23 Oct 2026). Both of those pages say the tower is red and white and 22 m above the sea, over the fishing boats and Xuân Đài bay. The heritage page for Gành Đá Đĩa names Gành Đèn as the north end of that shore, at the mouth of Xuân Đài, and does not print the 1 km or the 22 m (https://dsvh.gov.vn/ganh-da-dia-tinh-phu-yen-3307, 23 Oct 2026).

Fee, hours, and minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The 22 m and the 1 km sit on the handbook and a tourism feature that repeats it. No second independent measurement was opened.
