## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the basalt columns where they meet the sea. The north point tilts and bends. The south point stands mostly upright in three steps. A tide pool holds small sea life. Dawn and sunset change the color on the black rock (A1, A2).

## Sizes, age, and rank (do not average)

- More than 50 m wide and about 200 m long (A1).
- 50 m wide and more than 2,000 m long (A4).
- Main column field about 2,700 m² (A2, A3).
- North point: more than 40 m out from the cliff, widest about 30 m. South point: three steps, about 9 m out at the low step (A2).
- Protected zone 200 ha: zone I 11.3 ha, zone II 188.7 ha (A2). Decision 1570/QĐ-TTg of 2023 repeats 200.0 ha, with 94.0 ha of land and 106.0 ha of sea, and the same 11.3 / 188.7 split (A5). That decision is a planning task, not a ticket.
- Columns: tens of thousands (A1). About 11,500, mostly five-sided and six-sided, average face about 43 cm, disc thickness about 25–30 cm (A2). A1 also describes square and round faces.
- Age: nearly 200 million years, formed at the Vân Hòa plateau, about 30 km as the crow flies (A1). A2 dates the reasonable age of this basalt from 5 million years to 0.7 million years, and puts strong volcanism at Vân Hòa at about 10 million years. Both ages stay.
- Place: thôn Phú Hạnh, xã An Ninh Đông, huyện Tuy An, tỉnh Phú Yên (A2). A3 places the same rocks at xã Tuy An Đông, tỉnh Đắk Lắk, old name An Ninh Đông, Tuy An, Phú Yên.
- Rank: national landscape in 1998 (A1). National ranking in 1997 (A3). National ranking in 2005 (A4). Special national relic by Quyết định 2280/QĐ-TTg on 31/12/2020 (A2, A3).
