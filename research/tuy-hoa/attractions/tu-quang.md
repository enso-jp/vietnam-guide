A separate visit, about 35 km north, on Núi Xuân Đài. Not the night in town. Left off until two frames show the roof and the white rock.

## What it is

- Vietnamese name: Chùa Từ Quang. Also chùa Đá Trắng and chùa Bạch Thạch.
- Kind: sight

## Facts checked, not published

- A1, https://vietnamtourism.vn/index.php/tourism/items/2231/ , Cục Du lịch Quốc gia Việt Nam, opened 23 Oct 2026. Xã An Dân, huyện Tuy An. About 35 km north. About 500 m from QL1A. Founded 1797 by thiền sư Pháp Chuyên. A thatch hut in 1793. Fire in 1929, then rebuilt on the old plan. About 100 m above the sea. About 5,000 m². Faces south toward sông Cái (sông Ngân Sơn) and sông Nhân Mỹ. Eight tombs. Bell of 330 cân, cast in Huế in 1915. Xoài Đá Trắng. National historic-cultural relic in 1997. No fee and no clock.
- A2, https://phuyentourism.gov.vn/diem-du-lich/van-hoa-tam-linh/chua-tu-quang.html , Du lịch Phú Yên, search extract 23 Oct 2026 (direct fetch failed). Thôn Cần Lương, xã An Dân. About 35 km north. Nearly 100 m. Built 1797 in bamboo and leaf. Royal charter 1889. Main hall rebuilt 1988. Bell 330 kg, cast at Phú Xuân. National historic-art relic in 1997. Mango trees named a heritage tree in 2013. Festival on the 10th of the first lunar month. No fee and no clock in the extract.

Where they disagree: about 100 m and nearly 100 m; 330 cân and 330 kg. Both stay for a later page. Do not convert cân to kilograms. The 1929 fire and the 1988 rebuild are different sentences. The festival date is one extract. Hours and a fee stay Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
