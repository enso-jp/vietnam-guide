# Attraction research — Nghinh Phong

Place: `tuy-hoa`
Slug: `nghinh-phong`
Status: unpublished
Checked: 23 Oct 2026

Unpublished: no licensed photo of the twin towers.

The square is a separate visit of this place, at Nguyễn Hữu Thọ and Độc Lập (A1, A2). It is not Tháp Nhạn. It stays off the public list until two frames show the twin towers.

## What was opened

- A1, https://phuyentourism.gov.vn/diem-du-lich/thap-nghinh-phong-phu-yen.html , Du lịch Phú Yên, search extract 23 Oct 2026 (direct fetch 504). Plaza of granite, 7,190 m². Two clusters of 50 stone columns. Tall columns 34 m and 28 m. Wind gap wide enough for two people. Work from April 2019, opened 30/4/2022. Night 3D mapping and laser. No fee and no show clock in the extract. World-award lines are skipped.
- A2, https://phuyentourism.gov.vn/tin-tuc/bieu-tuong-moi-cua-du-lich-phu-yen.html , Du lịch Phú Yên, search extract 23 Oct 2026 (direct fetch 504). Columns 35 m and 30 m. Wind gap 2 m wide and 15 m long. 50 blocks under the columns. Granite facing. Heights disagree with A1. Both pairs stay for a later page. No fee and no clock.
- Handbook S14 (22 Oct 2026): 50 blocks on each side, granite, a wind gap, the idea taken from Gành Đá Đĩa, a night light show, early morning and early afternoon for photos. No show clock.
