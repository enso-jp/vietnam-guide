- Publisher: Vietnam National Authority of Tourism (vietnam.travel). The southern-vietnam URL redirects here.
- Accessed: 22 Oct 2026
- Supports: Pearl Island nickname in the truncated intro. Dry season November–April. Rainy season May–October, rougher seas and short heavy showers. Phu Quoc International Airport, direct flights from Hà Nội, Hồ Chí Minh City, and abroad, ferries from Rạch Giá and Hà Tiên. Bãi Sao as powder-white sand and calm turquoise water, early on weekdays. Cable car to Hòn Thơm, called one of the world’s longest over-sea. Night market near Dương Đông: grilled seafood, fruit, snacks, cook the catch you pick. Fish sauce and black pepper, a barrel house or a pepper garden. National park, half-day trek or motorbike ride in the north. ### S5
- URL: https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/phu-quoc-signature-noodle-bun-quay
- Publisher: Vietnam National Authority of Tourism (vietnam.travel)
- Accessed: 22 Oct 2026
- Supports: Bún quây, also called stirring vermicelli. Brought from central Vietnam in 1955 as ground shrimp, called bún tôm until 1996. Kiến Xây family’s change in 1996. The sauce of chili, sugar, fish sauce, and citrus. Rice-flour noodles, fish, shrimp, squid, herbs, onion. Traditionally morning, now all day and evening. Over ten rooms. Kiến Xây at 28 Bach Dang Road. Thanh Hùng at 77 30/4 Road. The street across from the harbour. Both rooms are not picks.
