## Province and the special zone

Nghị quyết 1654/NQ-UBTVQH15, clause 95, joins the whole area and population of phường Dương Đông, phường An Thới, and the communes Dương Tơ, Hàm Ninh, Cửa Dương, Bãi Thơm, Gành Dầu, and Cửa Cạn into one unit named đặc khu Phú Quốc. The resolution was passed on 16 June 2025. It takes effect on the day it was passed. The new commune-level governments operate from 1 July 2025. Clause 97 counts An Giang, after the rearrangement, as 102 commune-level units: 85 communes, 14 wards, and 3 special zones. The resolution is made under Nghị quyết 202/2025/QH15 (S26).

Older pages still print the old layer, and that layer stays:

- The airport contact page prints Đặc khu Phú Quốc, Tỉnh An Giang, and “Phu Quoc SEZ” (S24).

The island is not the province. The province-area and province-population figures in Nghị quyết 202 were not opened, so they are not copied here.

## Nearest airport

Phu Quoc International Airport. Vietnam Tourism names it and says there are direct flights from Hà Nội, Hồ Chí Minh City, and abroad (S1).

The operator page says Sun Group has managed and operated it since 1 January 2026. The same page’s milestones, all kept: construction of the new airport in Duong To Commune began 23 November 2008, on about 905 hectares; the airport commenced operations on 2 December 2012, replacing the former airport; Resolution 01/2025/NQ-CP on 1 June 2025 approved the expansion to serve APEC 2027; the expansion was approved on 19 June 2025 with Sun Group as principal investor; ACV transferred the infrastructure assets on 18 November 2025 (S23).

Address on the contact page: Duong To quarter, Phu Quoc SEZ, An Giang province. Phone (+84) 98 498 4341. Email pqc.airports@sungroup.com.vn (S24). The mailbox uses the letters pqc. ## Season limit on the journey

Vietnam Tourism: dry season November–April, calm seas and clear skies; rainy season May–October, rougher seas and short heavy showers (S1).

The district page uses the lunar calendar: dry season from lunar month 11 to lunar month 4 of the next year, rainy season from lunar month 5 to lunar month 10. It calls the climate tropical monsoon, set by a low latitude and the Gulf of Thailand (S9). The lunar window and the solar window both stay. They were not converted into each other.

Superdong says departure clocks can change with tide and weather (S25). A cancellation table for the southern islets was not opened.

Island size, one page: 36 islands, the main island 573 km², 50 km long, widest in the north at 25 km (S9). The whisper page calls it the country’s largest island (S6). The district page calls huyện đảo Phú Quốc the largest island district in Vietnam (S9). Both “largest” sentences stay.
