# Research dossier — Da Nang

## Why a night here

- The picture they expect: My Khe in the morning, surf in front of the resort towers, Son Tra on the horizon.
- Where they sleep, as an area: a beachfront resort on My Khe, or a river hotel if the museum and Dragon Bridge matter more than the night.
- Where the evening is: An Thuong, the grid a few streets inland (west) of the sand, bars and kitchens close enough to walk. The beach night itself is on the sand.
- Scene landmarks: Kalà Kalà Beach Club, 51 Vo Nguyen Giap, on My Khe. Entry free. The same official page lists both 08:00–midnight and 08:00–02:00, so the closing hour is confirm-that-night (S23). East West Brewing, beachfront taproom on My Khe, 08:00–01:00 every day (S24). No street on that page, so the address is Check on arrival. These two orient the evening. They are not a ranked list and they have no published drink price in this dossier.
- Hero must include: sand, surf, and the resort towers in one frame, subject through the center. Not the river, not a military frame, not a field of empty sand.
- A crop that would fail: a panorama whose center is only water, a statue cropped through the chest, a foggy road with no pass, the same bridge reused for the fireworks.

### Museum of Cham Sculpture
- Vietnamese name: Bảo tàng Điêu khắc Chăm
- Kind: sight
- Area: Han River, Hai Chau
- What the visitor does: Walk galleries of sandstone and terracotta Cham sculpture, more than 400 works dated from the 5th to the 15th century, arranged by the places they were found, including My Son, Tra Kieu, Dong Duong, and Thap Mam. The building opened in 1919.
- Time: About 1 hour
- Fee: 60,000 VND for a general visitor. 20,000 VND for a Da Nang permanent resident and for university, college, and vocational students. Free for children under 16, school pupils, and Vietnamese citizens 60 and over, plus other cases listed in the resolution. Free on the first day of Tet, 29 March, Hung Kings’ day, 2 September, and 24 November. — S5, and the same 60,000 / 20,000 split on S6
- From the base: On the Han River. Visit Da Nang gives No. 2, 2 Thang 9 Street, Hai Chau (S4). Resolution 47 gives 01 Trung Nu Vuong, Hai Chau ward (S5). Treat those as the published addresses of the same museum, and confirm the door on arrival.
- When to go: Morning, before the beach. Hours disagree: Visit Da Nang says 07:00–17:00 (S4). The city tourism price list says 07:30–17:00 (S6).
- Mistake to avoid: Treating the galleries as a substitute for the ruins at My Son. The museum holds sculpture from My Son. It is not the sanctuary.
- Local interest: Cham Museum
- Suggested slug: cham-museum
- Suggested dot: already published — do not change
- Photo must show: the museum building or a sandstone figure inside, one frame, in color. Not a document and not a military scene.
- Sources: S4, S5, S6

### Dragon Bridge
- Vietnamese name: Cầu Rồng
- Kind: sight
- Area: Han River
- What the visitor does: Walk the river path and look at the yellow dragon bridge. On show nights the dragon breathes fire and then water.
- Time: An evening look. The show itself is short.
- Fee: Free to watch from the bank. — S9 does not list a ticket
- From the base: Across the Han, between the center and the beach side. The city portal gives the length as 666 m and the opening as 29 Mar 2013 (S9).
- When to go: Dusk for the picture. The show is 21:00 on Friday, Saturday, Sunday, and major holidays (S9, S10). During the fireworks festival the night can move (S10).
- Mistake to avoid: Waiting on a weeknight. Also, the two official write-ups do not agree on length: the tourism portal says about 2 minutes of fire and 3 minutes of water (S9); Visit Da Nang describes about 10–15 minutes all together (S10). Check that week.
- Local interest: Dragon Bridge, evening walk
- Suggested slug: dragon-bridge
- Suggested dot: already published — do not change
- Photo must show: the dragon bridge over the river, in color, one frame. The banner for the city can be this bridge or the beach, never a military photograph.
- Sources: S9, S10

### My Khe Beach
- Vietnamese name: Biển Mỹ Khê
- Kind: area
- Area: East of the Han, the city beach
- What the visitor does: Swim when the sea is open, and walk the sand in front of the beachfront resorts. The night is not on the towel. It is a few streets inland in An Thuong, plus Kalà Kalà and East West on the sand (see Why a night here).
- Time: Half a day
- Fee: The beach itself is not on the city’s paid-ticket list (S6). Chair or umbrella hire — Check on arrival, UNSOURCED
- From the base: A ride from the river or the airport. The Dragon Bridge page describes the bridge as a direct road toward My Khe and Non Nuoc (S9).
- When to go: Morning in the drier months. The English city page calls January–July the dry season (S3). The Vietnamese city page calls January–September dry and puts floods in September–November (S2). Do not promise a swim in October.
- Mistake to avoid: Judging the city’s food by a beach grill. Noodles are a market lunch. Seafood is the My Khe dinner.
- Local interest: Beach
- Suggested slug: my-khe
- Suggested dot: already published — do not change
- Photo must show: surf, sand, and the resort towers in one frame, with that meeting line through the center so a 3:2 card and a tall interest card both keep the beach. Not empty foreground sand. Not a logo.
- Sources: S2, S3, S6, S9, S23, S24, S25

### Marble Mountains
- Vietnamese name: Ngũ Hành Sơn
- Kind: sight
- Area: Ngu Hanh Son ward, on the road toward Hoi An
- What the visitor does: Climb Thuy Son, the peak visitors actually use. Caves, Tam Thai pagoda, Linh Ung pagoda on the hill, and lookouts over the coast. The range has six peaks. Thuy Son is the one to plan.
- Time: The tourism portal suggests about 3–4 hours for a fuller morning, and about 15 minutes by road from the center (S7). A visit that only climbs the one hill is shorter. Am Phu is extra time.
- Fee: Thuy Son, adults 40,000 VND. Students 10,000 VND. Children under 6 free (S6, S7). Elevator 15,000 VND one way, 30,000 VND return (S7). Am Phu cave is a second ticket: adults 20,000 VND, students 7,000 VND, under 6 free (S6, S7). Tickets at Gate 01 or Gate 02.
- From the base: 81 Huyen Tran Cong Chua, about 8 km southeast of the center (S7). On the way to Hoi An. About 30 minutes from the hills to Hoi An by the coastal road (S7).
- When to go: Gates 07:00–17:00 (S6). The portal says leave the center around 07:30–08:00, and avoid the middle of a summer day (S7).
- Mistake to avoid: Trying to walk all of the peaks on a transfer day. Climb Thuy Son. Add Am Phu only if the second ticket and the darker cave are the point.
- Local interest: Hills and caves on the Hoi An road
- Suggested slug: marble-mountains
- Suggested dot: already published — do not change
- Photo must show: the marble hill, a cave mouth, or the stair, in color. Not a statue catalog and not a map.
- Sources: S6, S7

### Linh Ung Pagoda, Son Tra
- Vietnamese name: Chùa Linh Ứng Sơn Trà
- Kind: sight
- Area: Son Tra peninsula, Bai But
- What the visitor does: Ride the peninsula road, then walk the pagoda terrace. The Quan The Am stands 67 m, on a lotus pedestal 35 m across, facing the sea (S11). The pagoda says it is about 10 km northeast of the center and that it opened on 30 Jul 2010 (S22).
- Time: Half a day with the ride
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The city decision that recognized the site on 6 Jun 2017 states no fee (S12). The city portal states no fee (S11).
- From the base: Northeast of the river, by car or Grab. Not a walk from My Khe unless the hotel is already on that shore.
- When to go: Morning, when the peninsula road is clear. There is no published gate hour.
- Mistake to avoid: Budgeting it as a quick stop between the beach and dinner. The ride is the visit.
- Local interest: View over the bay
- Suggested slug: son-tra
- Suggested dot: already published — do not change
- Photo must show: the face and the hands of the Quan The Am, because a 3:2 card cannot hold the full 67 m figure without cutting the head. Do not pad the sides with invented sky. Not a military post on the peninsula.
- Sources: S11, S12, S22

### Ba Na Hills and the Golden Bridge
- Vietnamese name: Bà Nà / Cầu Vàng
- Kind: hub
- Area: Hills west of the city, Ba Na commune
- What the visitor does: Ride the cable car and walk the Golden Bridge, the bridge held by the stone hands. The basic ticket also covers the French Village and Fantasy Park. This is a built hill resort, not a wilderness hike.
- Time: A full day
- Fee: 2026 basic ticket, non-resident adult 1,000,000 VND. Child from 1 m to under 1.4 m, 800,000 VND. Under 1 m free. Da Nang resident adult 650,000 VND (S8). The city tourism list matches those figures and gives hours 08:00–22:00 (S6). The ticket includes the round-trip cable car and the Golden Bridge. Sun World says a 2026 ticket can be upgraded at the office to three consecutive days. Some attractions, including the wine cellar, are extra (S8).
- From the base: By car from Da Nang. Drive time — approximate, UNSOURCED
- When to go: When a whole day is free. Not on the morning you fly, and not on the day you also want Hoi An.
- Mistake to avoid: Going for a quick photo of the bridge. The cable car and the hill take the day. Skip it if the beach and Hoi An already fill the nights.
- Local interest: Golden Bridge
- Suggested slug: ba-na
- Suggested dot: already published — do not change
- Photo must show: the golden bridge in the stone hands, in color, one frame. Not a park logo.
- Sources: S6, S8

- Time: Half a day as a detour. Road duration Da Nang to Hue over the pass — approximate, UNSOURCED
- Fee: No ticket found. Treat the road as free. Tunnel and hire fees — Check on arrival, UNSOURCED

- When to go: A clear day only. September–December is the storm and heavy-rain window (S2, S3).
- Mistake to avoid: Riding it in cloud, or treating it as a required stop on the way to the airport
- Local interest: Coast road
- Suggested slug: hai-van
- Suggested dot: already published — do not change

### Ngu Hanh Son
- Vietnamese name: Ngũ Hành Sơn
- Kind: zone
- Area: South of Mỹ Khê, on the road toward Hội An

Mỹ Khê and An Thượng are the beach and the night, north of the hills along the same shore. The tourism portal puts the hills at 81 Huyền Trân Công Chúa, about 8 km and about 15 minutes from the center, and about 30 minutes on to Hội An by the coastal road (S7).

- Local interest: The south shore
- Suggested slug: no district page in this pass. The hill, the village note, the beach, and An Thượng already split the ward.

- Photo must show: the marble hills with the coast, or the village workshops with a finished stone in the frame. A resort pool is the wrong subject.

### Da Nang Cathedral
- Vietnamese name: Nhà thờ Chính tòa Đà Nẵng (Nhà thờ Con Gà)

- Area: Center, near the river
- What the visitor does: Look at the pink Gothic front and the rooster on the roof. Built 1923, nearly 70 m, under Father Louis Vallet. The parish’s Vietnamese page says the bird is Peter, not a French emblem. The official parish name dates from 1963.
- Time: A short look
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The parish notice prints no fee.
- From the base: The parish’s own about page, 8 Mar 2014, prints 156 Trần Phú (S42). The 15 Sep 2015 tourism parish page prints no street number. Use the parish address and confirm the door.
- When to go: The parish notice of 28 Dec 2022, in force from 1 Jan 2023, sets visitor hours Monday to Saturday, 08:00–11:30 and 13:30–16:30, and asks for modest dress (S41). Sunday is not in that visitor window. The 2015 tourism page prints no timetable. Do not copy a tour company’s window.
- Mistake to avoid: Treating the 2015 tourism page’s silence as “no hours exist.” The parish later printed a window. A fee is still unpublished.
- Local interest: Culture
- Suggested slug: cathedral
- Suggested dot: already published — do not change
- Photo must show: the pink facade, rose window, and rooster, whole in the frame
- Sources: S41, S42, and the 15 Sep 2015 tourism parish page already on the attraction file

### Han Swing Bridge
- Vietnamese name: Cầu quay Sông Hàn
- Kind: sight
- Area: Han River, Le Duan–Tran Phu to Pham Van Dong
- What the visitor does: Stand on the bank and watch the middle span turn for ships. Opened 29 Mar 2000. Length 487.7 m, width 12.9 m, 11 spans, plus two cable-stayed spans totaling 122.7 m (S40).
- Time: A late look. A timed visit length is UNSOURCED.
- Fee: No ticket from the bank on the tourism bridge page.
- From the base: Walk from a river hotel to the Le Duan–Tran Phu junction. Minutes from My Khe are Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: The tourism bridge page prints Saturday and Sunday 23:00–midnight for visitors, and also says the span turns about 23:00 every night for ships. A later key-times page names only the weekend. The city portal says about 01:00, back about 04:00, and that large boats rarely pass now so turns are mostly maintenance (S40). Print all three. Do not replace 23:00 with 01:00.
- Mistake to avoid: Promising a weeknight turn, or copying the portal’s slogan about having to see the turn.
- Local interest: River walk
- Suggested slug: han-swing-bridge
- Suggested dot: already published — do not change
- Photo must show: the lit bridge from the bank. The frame does not show the span mid-turn.
- Sources: S40, and the tourism bridge page already on the attraction file

### My Khe swim
- Vietnamese name: Tắm biển Mỹ Khê
- Kind: lifestyle
- What a day here actually is: The swim, in front of the resort towers, only while a rescue team is on duty and only inside the buoyed water. The city tourism beaches page, dated 15 Jan 2016, says local beaches are good for swimming all year and especially from May to August, when the waves are gentle. The same page says lifeguard teams on some beaches work from 5am to 8pm, and its travel tip says swim only from 05:00 to 19:00, when rescue teams are available. Both clocks are on that page. A 12 Apr 2023 city-portal report of beach-board document 217/BQL-QLB, 29 Mar, says rescue teams are posted on Hoàng Sa – Võ Nguyên Giáp – Trường Sa and on Nguyễn Tất Thành. It says swim where the safety buoys are set and a rescuer is on duty, follow the warning boards, do not swim in bad weather or where a danger board forbids it, and stop when the end-of-duty signal is given. That report does not print the duty clock. It points at a link. The hour on the sand wins. Chair and umbrella hire stay Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When: Morning in the drier months (S2, S3). Go in only inside the buoy line, and only during the duty the board is posting that day.
