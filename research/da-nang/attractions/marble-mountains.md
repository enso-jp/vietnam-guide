## What it is

- English name: Marble Mountains
- Vietnamese name: Ngũ Hành Sơn
- Kicker: The marble hill on the Hoi An road
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Climb Thủy Sơn. Caves, Tam Thai pagoda, Linh Ung on the hill, and lookouts. Six peaks. Visitors are pointed at Thủy Sơn (S7).
- How long on site: The portal suggests 3–4 hours for a fuller morning (S7). A single-hill climb is shorter. Am Phu is extra time.
- How long including the ride: About 15 minutes from the center, about 8 km, address 81 Huyền Trân Công Chúa (S7). About 30 minutes on to Hội An by the coastal road (S7).
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Adding Am Phu, the elevator, and the other peaks, then trying to do Hội An the same morning.
- Notice: Am Phu is a second ticket.

## Fee

- Thủy Sơn adults 40,000 VND. Students 10,000 VND. Children under 6 free (S6, S7).
- Elevator 15,000 VND one way, 30,000 VND return (S7).
- Am Phu adults 20,000 VND, students 7,000 VND, under 6 free (S6, S7).
- Tickets at Gate 01 or Gate 02 (S7).
- Source: S6, S7

## Hours

- Open: 07:00–17:00 (S6)
- Closed: no weekly closing day is published
- Source: S6

## Getting there

- From the center: about 15 minutes, about 8 km, 81 Huyền Trân Công Chúa, Ngũ Hành Sơn (S7).
- Toward Hội An: the hill is on that road. About 30 minutes from the hills to Hội An by the coastal road (S7).
- Non Nước village is at the foot. No village ticket on S6 or S7. It is a side note, not this page’s second sight.
