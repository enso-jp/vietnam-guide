# Attraction research — Dragon Bridge

Place: `da-nang`
Slug: `dragon-bridge`
Status: stored
Checked: 21 Oct 2026

Sources: dossier S9 and S10. S33 for the east-bank walking stretch. S37 for the Bạch Đằng weekend crowd.

## What it is

- English name: Dragon Bridge
- Vietnamese name: Cầu Rồng
- Kicker: The dragon bridge over the Han
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the river path. Look at the yellow dragon. On show nights it breathes fire, then water.
- How long on site: An evening look. The show itself is short, and the two pages do not agree how short.
- How long including the ride: A walk from a river hotel. From My Khe the minutes are Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Waiting on a weeknight.
- Notice: Show length disagrees. The tourism portal says about 2 minutes of fire and 3 minutes of water (S9). Visit Da Nang says about 10–15 minutes all together (S10).

## Fee

- Basic price: Free to watch from the bank. S9 does not list a ticket.
- Source: S9

## Hours

- Open: The bridge is a road. The show is 21:00 on Friday, Saturday, Sunday, and major holidays (S9, S10).
- Closed: No show on an ordinary weeknight. During the fireworks festival the night can move (S10). Check that week.
- Bridge length 666 m, opened 29 Mar 2013 (S9).
- Source: S9, S10

## Getting there

- From Bạch Đằng: Walk the promenade. The 2016 tourism page says the sidewalk fills on show weekends (S37).
- East bank: VietnamPlus, 22 Aug 2025, says the road in front of the moved night market is closed to vehicles after 17:30 (S33). That is the market approach, not a bridge ticket.
- From My Khe: The bridge is the crossing toward the beach. Minutes Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
