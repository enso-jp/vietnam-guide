## What it is

- English name: Hai Van Pass
- Vietnamese name: Đèo Hải Vân
- Kicker: The coast pass between Da Nang and Hue
- Kind: area

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Cross the pass by car or with a licensed rider when the sky is clear. Sea on one side, the ridge on the other.
- How long on site: Half a day as a detour.
- How long including the ride: Road time Da Nang to Hue over the pass is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The rail alternative does not use the pass. On the Vietnam Railways board, SE3 is Hue 07:55 and Da Nang 10:43, about three hours (S16). Other trains differ.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Riding it in cloud, or treating it as a required stop on the way to the airport.
- Notice: Go only when the sky is clear. September–December is the storm and heavy-rain window (S2, S3).

## Fee

- No ticket found. Treat the road as free.
- Tunnel and hire fees: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Source: none for a toll. S16 for the train alternative.

## Hours

- Open: Daylight, clear sky. No gate hour is published.
- Closed: Do not treat cloud as open. The wet-season window is S2 and S3.
- Source: S2, S3

## Getting there

- From Da Nang: north, by car or a licensed rider.
- If you skip the pass: the train, about three hours on the published SE3 times (S16).
