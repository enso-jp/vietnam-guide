## What it is

- English name: Museum of Cham Sculpture
- Vietnamese name: Bảo tàng Điêu khắc Chăm
- Kicker: Cham sandstone on the Han River
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the galleries. More than 400 works, 5th to 15th century, grouped by find-site, including My Son, Tra Kieu, Dong Duong, and Thap Mam. The building opened in 1919 (S4).
- How long on site: About 1 hour
- How long including the ride from the base: A morning if you slept on the river. From My Khe the ride minutes are Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating the galleries as a substitute for My Son.
- Notice: The door time is published twice. Visit Da Nang says 07:00–17:00 (S4). The city tourism price list says 07:30–17:00 (S6). Both close at 17:00.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: General visitor 60,000 VND (S5, S6).
- Child, height, or senior rule: Free for children under 16, school pupils, and Vietnamese citizens 60 and over, plus other cases in Resolution 47/2026. Da Nang permanent residents, and university, college, and vocational students, 20,000 VND (S5, S6).
- What the basic ticket includes: The galleries. No inclusion list beyond entry was copied.
- What costs extra: Not stated.
- Free days: first day of Tet, 29 March, Hung Kings’ day, 2 September, and 24 November (S5).
- Source: S5, S6

## Hours

- Open: 07:00–17:00 (S4) and 07:30–17:00 (S6). Do not collapse them.
- Last entry: not stated
- Closed: no weekly closing day is published
- Source: S4, S6

## Getting there

- From the Han River: Walk. Visit Da Nang gives No. 2, 2 Thang 9 Street, Hai Chau (S4). Resolution 47 gives 01 Trung Nu Vuong, Hai Chau ward (S5). Both are printed. Confirm the door.
- From My Khe: A ride. Minutes Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
