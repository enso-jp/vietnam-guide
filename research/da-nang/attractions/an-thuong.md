## What it is

- English name: An Thuong
- Vietnamese name: An Thượng (Khu phố du lịch An Thượng)
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Bar and kitchen lanes behind My Khe
- Kind: area
- Tourist quarter under Decision 489/QĐ-UBND of 31 Jan 2018. The name stone was set on 23 Apr 2018 at the corner of Võ Nguyên Giáp and Hoàng Kế Viêm. Bounded by Võ Nguyên Giáp, Hoàng Kế Viêm, Châu Thị Vĩnh Tế, and Ngô Thì Sĩ, about 0.15 km² including the beach, with 141 businesses serving visitors at the time of the report (A1). The scheme proposes walking streets on the cross streets and activity after midnight. Those are plans in the report, not a published closing hour (A1).

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: After the swim, walk in from the beach road. The lanes of bars and kitchens are the night (A1, S25 in the city dossier). On the sand, Kalà Kalà at 51 Võ Nguyên Giáp is the club (A2). East West Brewing’s taproom faces My Khe (A3). The night market at No. 4 An Thượng 4 is a separate stop (A4).
- How long on site: An evening. City dossier time line, no official duration. UNSOURCED as a number.
- How long including the ride from the base: Walk from a My Khe hotel. From the river, minutes UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Sleeping on the Han River and expecting to walk here after dinner. Treating the An Thượng 4 night market as the lanes.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: From a river hotel this is a ride, not a walk.
