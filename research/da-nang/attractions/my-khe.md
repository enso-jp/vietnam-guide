## What it is

- English name: My Khe Beach
- Vietnamese name: Biển Mỹ Khê
- Kicker: The city beach in front of the resorts
- Kind: area

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Stand on the sand in front of the resort towers. Swim only inside the buoy line, and only while a rescuer is on duty.
- How long on site: Half a day
- How long including the ride: From the river, a ride. Minutes Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. A 2016 page’s “about 10 minutes” is for the way to Phạm Văn Đồng beach, not a My Khe door time, so it is not used here (S35).
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Swimming outside the buoys, or after the end-of-duty signal.
- Notice: Duty clocks disagree. The 15 Jan 2016 beaches page prints both 5am–8pm and 05:00–19:00 (S35). The 12 Apr 2023 city report of document 217/BQL-QLB does not print a clock (S36). Read the board on the sand.

## Fee

- The beach is not on the city’s paid-ticket list (S6).
- Chair or umbrella hire: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Source: S6, S35

## Hours

- Swim only during rescue duty, inside the buoys, and not where a danger board forbids it (S35, S36).
- The two clocks on S35 are both kept. S36 tells you to stop when the end-of-duty signal is given.
- The same 2016 page says swimming is possible all year and especially May to August, when the waves are gentle (S35). The city’s own dry-season wording stays on the place. This page does not replace it.
- Source: S35, S36

## Getting there

- From a river hotel: by car or Grab. Minutes Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- The night is not on the towel. It is An Thượng, a few streets inland.
