## What it is

- English name: Linh Ung Pagoda, Son Tra
- Vietnamese name: Chùa Linh Ứng Sơn Trà
- Kicker: The sea-facing Quan The Am on Son Tra
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Ride the peninsula, then walk the terrace. The Quan The Am is 67 m, on a lotus pedestal 35 m across, facing the sea (S11). The pagoda places itself at Bai But, about 10 km northeast of the center, and says it opened 30 Jul 2010 (S22).
- How long on site: Half a day with the ride
- How long including the ride: The ride is the visit. A minute count beyond the 10 km is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating it as a quick stop between the beach and dinner, or riding the loop in rain.
- Notice: No entry fee is published (S11, S12). If a gate asks, check on arrival.

## Getting there

- From Da Nang: by car or Grab to Bai But. About 10 km northeast of the center, on the pagoda’s own page (S22).
- Not a walk from My Khe unless the hotel is already on that shore.
- Official page: https://danang.gov.vn/vi/w/chua-linh-ung-son-tra-i and https://chualinhung.vn/gioi-thieu-tieng-anh.html
