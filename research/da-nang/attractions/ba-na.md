## What it is

- English name: Ba Na Hills and the Golden Bridge
- Vietnamese name: Bà Nà / Cầu Vàng
- Kicker: Hill resort built around the Golden Bridge
- Kind: hub

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Cable car from the foot of the hill, then the Golden Bridge held by the stone hands. The basic ticket also covers the French Village and Fantasy Park.
- How long on site: A full day
- How long including the ride from the base: A full day. Drive minutes are not published.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Coming for a quick photo of the bridge, or expecting every ride on the basic ticket.
- Notice: The basic ticket is not every ride. Wine cellar, light area, toboggan, climbing, and the cinema are extra (A1).

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: Non-resident adult 1,000,000 VND. Da Nang resident adult 650,000 VND.
- Child, height, or senior rule: Under 1 m free. Child from 1 m to under 1.4 m, and a visitor over 70, 800,000 VND non-resident and 550,000 VND for a Da Nang resident. Over 1.4 m pays the adult price (A1). The city list states the same 1,000,000 / 800,000 and 650,000 / 550,000 split without the height sentence (A2).
- What the basic ticket includes: Round-trip cable car, Golden Bridge, French Village, Fantasy Park (A1, and the city dossier S8).
- What costs extra: Debay wine cellar, light interaction area, toboggan, climbing, lucky games, 10D cinema (A1). Combo and night-ticket prices stay off the page.
- 2026 upgrade: a one-day ticket can be upgraded at the ticket office to three consecutive days if the visitor registers before the first entry (A1).
- Source: A1, A2
