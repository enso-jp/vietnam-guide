# Side trips

Checked: 20 Oct 2026

### Hoi An
- Slug if it exists: `hoi-an`
- Travel time from this base: About 30 minutes by car from the Marble Mountains along the coastal road (S7). From the river hotels the same direction is longer by the crossing. A single door-to-door figure for every hotel — approximate, UNSOURCED beyond that 30 minutes.
- Why go: The old town at night, and the dishes Da Nang does not cook as its own (cao lau, white rose).
- Overnight or day: Day from Da Nang, or sleep there if the lanterns are the reason for the trip.
- Sources: S7, S1

### Hue
- Slug if it exists: `hue`
- Travel time from this base: About three hours by train. On the Vietnam Railways board, SE3 is published as Hue 07:55 and Da Nang 10:43 (S16). Other trains differ. Road time over Hai Van — approximate, UNSOURCED.
- Why go: The citadel and a tomb, and bun bo Hue if the bowl is the reason.
- Overnight or day: Overnight. A same-day return plus a Da Nang beach is how both are rushed.
- Sources: S16

### My Son
- Slug if it exists: `my-son`
- Travel time from this base: Approximate, UNSOURCED. It is inland, past Hoi An, not a lunch stop.
- Why go: The Cham sanctuary. The museum in the city holds sculpture from the site. It does not replace the ruins.
- Overnight or day: Day. The sanctuary hours on the city list are 06:00–18:00. Tickets on that list: Vietnamese 100,000 VND, international 150,000 VND (S6).
- Sources: S6
