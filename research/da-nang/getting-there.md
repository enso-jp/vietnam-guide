# getting there

Checked: 20 Oct 2026

- Nearest airport: Da Nang International Airport, IATA DAD, ICAO VVDN, operated as a branch of Airports Corporation of Vietnam (S13, S14). The aviation AIP places it 3.2 km southwest of the city (S13). The city portal’s aviation page says 1 km east of the center (S14). Those two official lines disagree. Use the AIP for the distance, and say the ride is short. Vietnam Airlines says about 10–15 minutes by car and about 3 km (S15).
- Nearest rail station: Ga Đà Nẵng, on the north–south line. Vietnam Railways publishes SE trains that stop there (S16). The station address used by ticket sellers is 791 Hai Phong, Thanh Khe — that street number is widely repeated and was not on the railways timetable page itself, so treat the street address as Check on arrival if a page needs a door.

- Getting around on site: The beach and the river are a ride apart. Walk only inside the base you slept in. A car or a ride-hailing car is the ordinary way across town and out to Son Tra, the Marble Mountains, and Ba Na. The Marble Mountains page says about 15 minutes from the center to the hills (S7). Ba Na drive time is approximate, UNSOURCED.
- Season limit on the journey: Two official wordings. The English city page: dry January–July, wet August–December, heaviest rain in October and November (S3). The Vietnamese city page: dry January–September, rain October–December, with rain already concentrated from September and floods usual in September–November (S2). Do not send someone over Hai Van or along the Hoi An road in a flood week without checking that morning. Ba Na is cooler than the city (S3).
