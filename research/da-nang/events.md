### Da Nang International Fireworks Festival
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội pháo hoa quốc tế Đà Nẵng (DIFF)
- Kind: festival
- When: In 2026 the nights were 30 May, 6 June, 13 June, 20 June, 27 June, and 11 July, on the Han River (S17, S18). The calendar is set for the year. The next year’s dates are Check on arrival.
- Where: Han River
- What happens: Teams compete over six nights. The 2026 theme on the festival site is “United Horizons.”
- Fee: Paid grandstands are sold for the year. The price is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED in this dossier. Do not copy a ticket number from a secondary page.
- Not the same as attraction slug: dragon-bridge. The weekend fire-and-water show is a different event.
- Sources: S17, S18

### Dragon Bridge fire-and-water show
- Vietnamese name: Cầu Rồng phun lửa, phun nước
- Kind: activity
- When: 21:00 on Friday, Saturday, Sunday, and major holidays (S9, S10). Extra or moved nights during DIFF and big holidays. Check that week.
- Where: Dragon Bridge, from the river path
- What happens: The dragon breathes fire, then water. S9 says about 2 minutes of fire and 3 minutes of water. S10 says the program runs about 10–15 minutes. Both are published. Do not pick one and hide the other.
- Fee: Free from the bank (S9)
- Not the same as attraction slug: the show is the activity. The bridge page is `dragon-bridge`.
- Sources: S9, S10

### Avalokitesvara festival, Marble Mountains
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Quan Thế Âm — Non Nước — Ngũ Hành Sơn
- Kind: festival
- When: 17th to 19th day of the second lunar month. The date moves. Check on arrival for the civil date.
- Where: Quan The Am pagoda and the Marble Mountains
- What happens: A Buddhist festival the city tourism portal describes as ceremonial rites plus three days of festive activity. It dates the modern annual form to 1991 and a national-festival listing to the end of 1999.
- Fee: The hill’s usual tickets still apply unless a notice says otherwise — Check on arrival, UNSOURCED for a festival-week exception
- Not the same as attraction slug: marble-mountains. Do not create a festival record in the copy pass. There is no separate public festival file yet, and this pass does not add one.
- Sources: S7
