### Saturday market
- Vietnamese name: Chợ Sa Pa. The church page also calls the Saturday gathering in front of the church a phiên chợ tình (S10, S13).
- Kind: festival
- When: Saturday evening. No clock on either page. Check on arrival for the hour.
- Where: The town market, and the square in front of the stone church. The two pages may be one gathering. They were not merged into a map pin.
- What happens: VNAT says Hmong and Dao people trade local goods, and that young people sing and talk into the night (S13). The church page says the square in front is where that Saturday gathering happens (S10). This pass did not open a second description that disputes the rite. Print the pages. Do not add a history they do not print.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No ticket on these pages.
- Not the same as attraction slug: the church and the square are the place. This is the Saturday gathering.
- Sources: S10, S13

### Gầu Tào
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Gầu Tào. Bao Phap Luat also spells Grâu Taox (S35).
- Kind: festival
- When: 28 Feb 2026, the 12th of the first lunar month, at Cát Cát (S34, S35). The next date moves with the lunar calendar. Check on arrival.
- Where: Bản Cát Cát, xã Tả Van (S34, S35).
- What happens: A pole, a blessing rite, then games and dance. Lao Dong glosses the name as “chơi ngoài trời” or “hội chơi đồi” (S34). Bao Phap Luat glosses “hội chơi ngoài trời” and says the core is a rite for children, health, and luck (S35). Both glosses stay. Stalls at the 2026 gathering included thắng cố and rượu ngô (S34).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: `cat-cat` is the village walk. This is one festival day there.
- Sources: S34, S35

### Vietnam Mountain Marathon
- Vietnamese name: Giải Marathon Vượt núi Việt Nam
- Kind: activity
- When: 17–20 Sep 2026 (S36, S37, S38). The 2027 dates: Check on arrival.
- Where: Sa Pa, with 18–20 Sep on xã Tả Van. Named checkpoints: Cầu Lao Chải, Cát Cát, Hầu Chư Ngài, Sử Pán (S36).
- What happens: A trail race. Counts disagree. Bao Lao Cai, from a 9 Sep 2026 meeting: about 5,400 runners from 45 countries; distances 10, 21, 50, 70, 100, and 160 km; the 13th edition (S36). Vietnam News: more than 5,300 from 54 countries; 10, 21, 70, 100, and 160 km, plus the 50 km as a World Trail Majors distance (S37). Nhan Dan: more than 5,300 from 54 countries; 10, 21, 70, 100 km, and 100 miles (about 161 km). The same Nhan Dan article says the first race was 2013 with 200 runners, “after 13 seasons,” and also “mùa giải thứ 3” (S38). Vietnam Tourism’s undated page says about 4,000 competitors and 10 to 100 km (S1). Record every figure. Do not pick a headcount or a distance list.
- Fee: Entry fee Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Not on these reports.
- Not the same as attraction slug: the valley walks and Fansipan. The race uses those landscapes. It is not a sight card.
- Sources: S1, S36, S37, S38
