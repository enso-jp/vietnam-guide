# Exploration by interest area — Sapa

Local titles. Not another city’s list. Dining is the food guide. Shopping is the market, because a primary page names goods there. No resort brand.

### The valley terraces
- Why it is this place, not a generic label: The town sits at the head of a valley that is still farmed (S1). Muong Hoa is the named valley, 8 km southeast in one feature (S28).
- Fits interest key, if any: `rice`
- Icon: view
- Links to attraction, dish, or place slug: `muong-hoa`, `ta-van`, `lao-chai`, `cat-cat`
- Food guide card: no
- Photo must show: Stepped fields through the center, a roof or a path, and the ridge. The opened CC0 frame does this and also shows a town on the far ridge (S53). It does not name the village. The pig fails (S56).
- Sources: S1, S3, S28, S53

### Fansipan by cable car
- Why it is this place, not a generic label: The summit at 3,143 m is the peak above this town, reached by the Sun World cable car from Nguyễn Chí Thanh (S1, S5, S22).

### The stone church square
- Why it is this place, not a generic label: The 1895 stone church is the town center, with Ham Rong behind it (S10). It is a hill-station church, not an old-town grid.
- Fits interest key, if any: none of the old-town keys fit cleanly. Do not file it under `oldTowns` as if it were Hội An.
- Icon: view
- Links to attraction, dish, or place slug: `stone-church`
- Food guide card: no
- Photo must show: The stone front, the whole tower and cross, and the square (S51). A crop of the children fails.
- Sources: S10, S51

- Why it is this place, not a generic label: Thắng cố is the northwest Mong, Tay, and Thai stew, served at Gầu Tào in Cát Cát (S32, S34). The town’s own named eating street is the grill row at the lake and the road down to the market (S33).
- Fits interest key, if any: `food`
- Icon: food
- Links to attraction, dish, or place slug: thắng cố, the lake grills, `com-lam`
- Food guide card: yes
- Photo must show: A bowl of thắng cố with the herbs visible, or the lake stall row at dusk. A generic hotpot from another city fails.

- Sources: S32, S33, S34

- Why it is this place, not a generic label: The national park holds the summit paths, Love Waterfall, and the forest around the town (S15, S16). Silver Waterfall is the headwater of the Muong Hoa stream (S12).
- Fits interest key, if any: `mountains`
- Icon: view
- Links to attraction, dish, or place slug: `hoang-lien`, `love-waterfall`, `silver-waterfall`
- Food guide card: no
- Photo must show: For the falls, the white water and the rock in one frame. For the park, a trail and the ridge. A cable-car cabin is the Fansipan card, not this one.

- Fits interest key, if any: `markets`

- Links to attraction, dish, or place slug: `sapa-saturday-market`, `silver-waterfall`
