# Comprehensive list of local food to try — Sapa

No stall prices. A restaurant name is not a page. Taste is recorded only when the source says it.

### Thắng cố
- Vietnamese name: Thắng cố
- What it is: The national ethnic-tourism page is about the northwest, not a Sa Pa stall. Its lede says Mong thắng cố is made from horse meat. Its body says thắng cố of the Mong, Tay, and Thai is usually horse meat and horse offal, or buffalo meat and buffalo offal, stewed with at least eight mountain herbs, including thảo quả, quế, and hồi (S32). Both meat lines stay. Do not pick one. The page says a first taste can be hard because of a ngai ngái smell. That is the page’s note.
- How to eat it: The same page says it is eaten with rượu ngô (S32).
- When to order it: The page ties northwest feast food to guests, festivals, and visitors in a village (S32). At Cát Cát, thắng cố was among the foods at Gầu Tào on 28 Feb 2026 (S34). A weekday stall street in town was not named.
- Area, not a shop: The festival ground at Cát Cát when the rite is on. Otherwise Check on arrival.
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none on this site. Do not point it at a southern dish.
- Sources: S32, S34
