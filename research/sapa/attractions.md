# Research dossier — Sapa

Slug: `sapa`
Province: Lào Cai. Resolution 1673/NQ-UBTVQH15, 16 Jun 2025, forms phường Sa Pa from six former wards and puts the valley communes into xã Tả Van, xã Tả Phìn, and xã Bản Hồ. Xã Lao Chải was not merged. The new communes start 1 Jul 2025 (S19). Older pages still say thị xã Sa Pa, thị trấn Sa Pa, and xã Hoàng Liên (S4, S15, S18). Both wordings stay. A province-merger decision that would explain former Yên Bái names inside this province was not opened. Chế Tạo, Nậm Có, and Tà Xi Láng do appear on the same Lao Cai commune list (S19).
Kind: town
Corridor: northern highlands. The public record’s corridor field was not used as a source.
Base a traveler sleeps in: The town around the stone church, or a homestay in the valley outside that hub (S1). Not a hotel brand. Not Lao Cai city, which is the railhead.
Checked: 22 Oct 2026

## Why a night here

- The picture they expect: Terraces still farmed in the valley under the town, and the Fansipan ridge above (S1). Mist is in the weather description (S1). The public hero is a tethered black pig and textile stalls (S56). That is not this picture.
- Where they sleep, as an area: Hotels in the town hub, or lodges and homestays set in the terraces outside that hub (S1). No brand.
- Where the evening is: Saturday evening in the market and the church square (S10, S13). Other evenings, the grill stalls at hồ Sa Pa and the road down to the market (S33). A night-time economy was still a workshop topic on 13 Oct 2026, with no opening hour (S39).
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): The stone church, Vietnam National Authority of Tourism, https://vietnamtourism.vn/index.php/tourism/items/2956/ (S10). Ham Rong, https://vietnamtourism.vn/index.php/tourism/items/2953/ (S11). They are pins, not a ranked bar list.
- Hero must include: Stepped terraces through the center, a roof or a path, and the ridge or the town, in color. The opened CC0 frame does that (S53). A church alternative is the stone front, the whole tower and cross, and the square (S51).
- A crop that would fail: The pig, a textile filling the frame, a 1924 museum print, empty fog, a cabin window with no valley, a costume portrait with no hill, or a tower with the cross cut off.

Town elevation, all of them, do not pick one: 1,600 m (S22); the Commons ward category prints both 1,500 m and 1,718 m (S60). Muong Hoa’s valley floor is printed at 950–1,000 m in a search extract whose page then errored (S9).

### Muong Hoa valley
- Vietnamese name: Thung lũng Mường Hoa
- Kind: zone
- Area: The feature places it at Hầu Thào, 8 km southeast of town (S28). The 2025 xã Tả Van absorbed xã Mường Hoa (S19). The valley floor is printed at 950–1,000 m in the errored Fansipan extract (S9).
- What the visitor does: Looks at terraces from a mid-slope, or walks. The feature says Giay wet-rice in the narrow valley and Mong and Dao fields higher up (S28). A stream about 15 km passes Lao Chai, Ta Van, Hau Thao, and Ban Ho, fed by 22 tributaries, and is called the lower section of Silver Waterfall (S28).
- Time: A half day is the planning shape of the 8–12 km Ta Van walk (S29). A fixed hour for the viewpoint was not sourced.

- When to go: September in the valley feature (S28). Mid-August to early September on the rice page (S3). About mid-August to October in the Ta Van feature (S30). September–October, and also Sep–Nov, on the Sa Pa page (S1). All stay.
- Mistake to avoid: Picking one harvest month, or labeling the CC0 terrace frame as one village when the file does not
- Local interest: The valley terraces
