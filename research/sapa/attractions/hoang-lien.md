## What it is

- English name: Hoang Lien National Park
- Vietnamese name: Vườn quốc gia Hoàng Liên
- Kicker: Park of forest and summit trails
- Kind: zone

## What the visitor does

- Tram Tôn about 10.5 km, one day or two days and one night, overnight 2,800 m, the route most people use (S15)
- Sín Chải about 18 km, three days, overnights 2,200 m and 2,800 m
- Cát Cát more than 12 km, three days, overnights 2,600 m and 2,800 m
- Love Waterfall 34 m, about 14 km, 3 km from the crest (S15). Tour price 70,000 adult / 30,000 child on the center page, re-read 23 Oct 2026
- Trek price: the park fee answer does not print one (S15)
- Under 16 barred from the climb and old-forest route in the 2012 article (S14). Later FAQ does not repeat it.

- Trek: Check on arrival

## Getting there

- Office 89 Nguyễn Chí Thanh (S15)
- Center: tổ dân phố Cầu Mây 1, phường Sa Pa (re-read 23 Oct 2026)
- Center page also says the night train leaves you in Sa Pa. That conflicts with the Lao Cai railhead. Do not use it.
- Communes on that page: San Sả Hồ, Lao Cải, Tả Van, Bản Hồ, plus Lai Chau communes. Older wording.
- Minutes to Tram Tôn: UNSOURCED

## Season

- Center page: any month; December–April drier for trekking; snow possible in winter. bestMonths set to December–April from that page.

## Area figures

- Core 29,845 ha, buffer 38,724 ha, decision 90/2002/QĐ-TTg of 12 Jul 2002, ASEAN heritage park 2006, earlier reserve 1996 on the center page
- Decision 194/CT of 9 Aug 1986 at 5,000 ha on S17. Both stay.
- Elevation 1,000–3,000 m. Summit 3,143 m.
