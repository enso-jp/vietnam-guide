## What it is

- English name: Muong Hoa funicular
- Vietnamese name: Tàu hỏa leo núi Mường Hoa
- Kicker: Five-minute train to the cable station
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- Rides from Sun Plaza to Hoang Lien station (S6)
- About five minutes, two tunnels, four viaducts, longest of its kind (Vietnam Tourism page, re-read 23 Oct 2026)
- About 2 km, launched 31 Mar 2018 (S28)
- This is not a second cable-car card. The cable car is the Fansipan page.
- Hotel named beside the station on the Vietnam Tourism page is a location clue. It is left off the public page.
