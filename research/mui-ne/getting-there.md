# Getting there — Mui Ne

Checked: 23 Oct 2026

No town pin is stored. The published pair 10.957, 108.289 was not printed by any page opened here. GeoNames prints 10.93314, 108.28327 for the populated place (S21). English Wikipedia prints 10°56′N 108°17′E (S19). Vietnamese Wikipedia prints 10°58′55″N 108°15′05″E for the merged ward of 118.59 km² (S20). A ward centroid, a gazetteer point, a dune crest, and a resort gate are not adopted. Do not add a pin from this dossier.

Phan Thiet is the neighbor. Vietnam Tourism calls Mui Ne a coastal town in Phan Thiet and puts the resort center 25 km from that city (S4, S6). English Wikipedia says the developed sand foreigners call Mui Ne Beach is Rang Beach in Ham Tien, west of the fishing ward (S19). The 2018 national tourism zone runs from Hoa Phu in Tuy Phong to the Phu Hai boundary and includes both the fishing ward and Ham Tien (S9). The slug stays `mui-ne`. The city center is not this dossier.

- Nearest airport: No civil passenger airport is open for this coast. The Phu Quy page says there is no airport in Phan Thiet, so the flight is to Ho Chi Minh City, Nha Trang, or Da Lat, then a bus or a train (S8). The Civil Aviation Authority records the civil-terminal groundbreaking at Phan Thiet on 27 Apr 2026. The military side was already operating. The page prints no passenger opening date (S13). Decision 1772, written in 2018, names Tan Son Nhat and Cam Ranh as the air links, and a Phan Thiet airport only after 2025 (S9). Decision 1772 names Vo Nguyen Giap and provincial roads 715, 716, and 716B as the north edge of the tourism zone (S9). Taxi and bicycle fares, and any bicycle hire: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. - Season limit on the journey: Vietnam Tourism’s coast page says November to April is dry, sunny, and windy, the time for kites and dune slides (S5). The adventure page says kite winds run October to April, and the kite window on the beach is 11:00–16:00 (S4). The dunes-and-sports page says steady breeze and warm water year-round (S2). English Wikipedia says the tourist season is December to April and prints an average of 27°C (S19). The Binh Thuan feature prints warm sun all year and daily temperatures around 27°C (S6).
