### Kate
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Katê
- Kind: festival
- When: The morning of 2 Oct 2024 was the 1st of the 7th Cham month, at Po Sah Inu and at Po Klong Garai (S12). The next civil date is not printed. Check on arrival. A lunar-calendar sentence was not on the page opened here.

- What happens: Robe procession of the goddess Po Sah Inu up the main tower, fan dance, pottery demonstration, then the festival moves to Cham villages and to families (S12). The ministry’s 2021 note also names the festival as one to put on a Binh Thuan tour and does not give a date (S11).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No ticket on S12.

- Sources: S11, S12

- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
