## What it is

- English name: Ham Tien beach
- Vietnamese name: Hàm Tiến. English Wikipedia calls the built sand Rang Beach, in Ham Tien, and says foreigners call it Mui Ne Beach (S19).
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Kite sand on the resort strip
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: A first kite lesson in the gentler morning, or the stronger thermal wind from 11:00 to 16:00 (S1, S4). IKO instructors on the main beach strip (S4). The sports page says the breeze is year-round (S2). Day passes at seaside properties are a separate beach day (S1).
- How long on site: A lesson, or the 11:00–16:00 window (S4). A minute count of a lesson: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- How long including the ride from the base: This is the base if the night is on the strip (S9).
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Calling the whole strip the fishing ward. Rang is Ham Tien. Ganh and Suoi Nuoc are the ward’s beaches (S19).
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Morning for a first lesson. The stronger wind is 11:00–16:00 (S1, S4).
