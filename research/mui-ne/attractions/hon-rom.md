# Attraction research — Hon Rom

Place: `mui-ne`

Unpublished. The 2018 plan names a Suoi Nuoc service center and an entertainment park east of the old ward (S9). English Wikipedia lists Hon Rom Beach as secluded and does not describe the swim (S19). Vietnamese Wikipedia puts Hòn Rơm on the road east of the dunes (S20). No fee, hour, or visit text was opened.

A licensed Commons frame exists (File:Hòn Rơm.jpg, Liftold, CC BY-SA 3.0). It is not used. A public card waits on a sourced visit.
