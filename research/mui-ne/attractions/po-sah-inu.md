## What it is

- English name: Po Sah Inu towers
- Vietnamese name: Tháp Pô Sah Inư. Also Poshanu and Pho Hai Cham towers (S16).
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Three Cham towers on Ba Nai Hill
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the three brick towers. The main tower holds a Linga-Yoni altar. A stone bull once in the middle tower is described as gone (S16). Vietnam Tourism calls them 1,200-year-old Hoa Lai national monuments (S5).
- How long on site: The English catalogue says no limit at the point. The older catalogue says 120 minutes. Both stay (S16, S18).
- How long including the ride from the base: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Filing the towers under the fishing ward. The pages place them in Phu Hai / Phu Thuy (S16, S17, S18).
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Close is 17:00 on S16 and S18, and 18:00 on S17.
