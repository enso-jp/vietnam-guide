## What it is

- English name: Red dunes
- Vietnamese name: Đồi cát bay. The 2019 workshop report uses that name for the pink flying dunes (S10). Vietnam Tourism says red dunes (S1, S2).
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Orange slope at sunset
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the orange slope and sandboard at sunset (S1, S2).
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- How long including the ride from the base: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The 22 km figure is from the city center, not a resort gate (S10).
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating this as the white field. The white dunes are Bàu Trắng, in Hoa Thang (S4, S9, S1).
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Go at sunset, not at the white-dune dawn hour.
