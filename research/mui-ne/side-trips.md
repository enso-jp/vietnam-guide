# Side trips — Mui Ne

Slug: `mui-ne`
Checked: 23 Oct 2026

Phan Thiet is the neighbor. The sources treat the resort strip as that city’s beach and still print Mui Ne as the fishing place and the tourism-zone name (S4, S6, S9, S19). This file does not research Phan Thiet’s own dossier. The slug stays `mui-ne`.

## Side trips

### Ho Chi Minh City
- Slug if it exists: `ho-chi-minh-city`

### Phan Thiet
- Or: Side trip placeholder: Phan Thiet
- Travel time from this base: 25 km on the Vietnam Tourism feature (S6). About 22 km on Vietnamese Wikipedia and on the 2019 dune report, which measures to the city center and to the dunes (S10, S20). Both stay. Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Why go: The city center has the harbor, outdoor markets, temples, and the Fish Sauce Museum, 14 spaces, on the tourism feature (S6). The rail station is here (S5). Those are the neighbor’s sights. They are not given entries in `attractions.md`.
- Overnight or day: Day, if the night is on the strip.
- Sources: S5, S6, S10, S20

### Ta Cu
- Or: Side trip placeholder: Tà Cú
- Travel time from this base: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No kilometre from Mui Ne.
- Why go: Two pagodas on the coast page, Linh Son Truong Tho and Long Doan, called the biggest reclining Buddha in Vietnam (S5). The feature places the mountain in Thuan Nam town, Ham Thuan Nam, names a third pagoda Chua Nui, 1,000 stone steps or a tram, and a reclining Buddha 49 m long called the largest in Southeast Asia (S6). “Biggest in Vietnam” and “49 m, largest in Southeast Asia” both stay. Do not pick one.
- Overnight or day: Day if the tram or the steps are the plan. The ride length was not printed.
- Sources: S5, S6
