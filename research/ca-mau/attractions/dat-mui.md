## What it is

- English name: Dat Mui

- Kicker (one line, what it is): Southern tip where the two seas meet
- Kind: zone

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: The sail marked Mũi Cà Mau, GPS0001, the end of the Hồ Chí Minh road, the sea wall, Cột cờ Hà Nội, then the view of the two seas (S6, reopened 23 Oct 2026).
- How long on site: The park listing, which shares this address, says time at one point is not limited (S5, reopened 23 Oct 2026).
- How long including the ride from the base: The 58.7 km section starts at cầu Ông Tình, Năm Căn (S21). A city-to-gate minute is UNSOURCED. Check on arrival.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: A start from the city after lunch can miss the gate.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Gate hours on the culture-office listing are 07:00–18:00 (S5). A tour page dated 30 September 2026 prints 06:30–17:30 (S31). Public page uses the official close. The conflict stays here.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: 15,000–30,000 VND on the office listing (S5, reopened 23 Oct 2026). Adult 30,000 VND on the tour page (S31).
- Child, height, or senior rule: The tour page prints 15,000 VND from 1 m to 1.3 m and for students, and no ticket under 1 m or for a severe disability. The office page does not split adult and child.
- What the basic ticket includes: Not printed as a split.
- What costs extra: Cart 10,000–15,000 VND and canoe fares are on the tour page only. Check on arrival.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S5 and S31. Public page prints the official band only.
