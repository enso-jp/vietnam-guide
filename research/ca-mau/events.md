### Lễ hội Nghinh Ông Sông Đốc
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Nghinh Ông Sông Đốc
- Kind: festival
- When: the ministry, writing on 14 March 2025, says the peak that year was 11–17 March, which it equates to 12–18 of the second lunar month, with the opening on the evening of 13 March at thị trấn Sông Đốc (S26). Báo Cà Mau, before that opening, printed 14–16 of the second lunar month, 13–15 March 2025 (S46). Keep both 2025 windows. Báo Cà Mau on 2 April 2026 says the festival runs on the 14th and 15th of the second lunar month, and that the 2026 day was 2 April, the 15th of the second lunar month in the year Bính Ngọ, the 101st time, counting from a temple built in 1925 (S39). The provincial police page also dates that 2026 rite to 15 February lunar, 2 April 2026 (S47). The next solar dates move. Check on arrival.
- Where: xã Sông Đốc in the 2026 paper. Thị trấn Sông Đốc, huyện Trần Văn Thời, in the 2025 ministry piece (S26, S39). The procession goes from Lăng Ông Nam Hải to the fish port and then to sea (S39).
- What happens: boats escort Ông, the whale the fishers venerate. The ministry says more than 90 fishing boats registered in 2025, plus a market of seafood (S26). The 2026 paper names the long palanquin, flags, drum, and lion, and a whale skeleton in the main hall (S39). Dân trí, 7 January 2026, summarizing the provincial year plan, adds net-mending, đờn ca tài tử, a flower-boat parade, a short fishing trip for visitors, and a shore run (S48). Those extras are the plan, not a line in the 2026 rite report.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No ticket was printed.
- Not the same as attraction slug: song-doc
- Sources: S26, S39, S46, S47, S48
