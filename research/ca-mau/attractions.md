# Research dossier — Ca Mau

Slug: `ca-mau`
Province: Cà Mau. Since 1 July 2025 the province name also covers the former Bạc Liêu province. This dossier is the cape and the city a traveler uses to reach it. See `STATUS.md`.
Kind: city
Corridor: `mekong-delta`
Base a traveler sleeps in: the city center, along the river wards. A second night at ấp Mũi only if they want the dawn at the gate.
Checked: 22 Oct 2026

## Why a night here

- The picture they expect: the last land, mangrove and mudflat, and the water on both sides of the tip. The object in the frame is the ship-shaped panel or the GPS0001 marker, with forest and sea still in the picture. It is not a Cần Thơ boat market at dawn, not a Bến Tre coconut canal, and not a Phú Quốc resort beach.
- Where they sleep, as an area: city rooms in the river wards the city tourism list now prints as phường An Xuyên and phường Tân Thành, beside the older phường numbers (S12). A night near ấp Mũi, xã Đất Mũi, is the other area, for a dawn inside the gate (S8). No hotel brand.
- Where the evening is: the city center and the Gành Hào boats, not a bar street. The cape gate is a daytime gate on every hour printed below. A night walk at the marker was not on the management-board pages.
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices):
  - Ban Quản lý Khu du lịch Mũi Cà Mau, ấp Mũi, xã Đất Mũi. Director Nguyễn Văn Sự. Email bqlkdlqgmcm@gmail.com. https://muicamau.camau.gov.vn/ (S8)
  - The marker cluster as the Ngọc Hiển culture page describes it. https://dulichdatmui.camau.gov.vn/vi/detailnews/?id=news_11695&t=cac-dia-diem-du-lich-noi-bat-dat-mui-ca-mau (S6)
  - Chùa Monivongsa Bopharam, đường Đinh Tiên Hoàng, the Khmer pagoda the city list puts in the center. https://dulichtpcamau.camau.gov.vn/vi/places (S12)
- Hero must include: the ship panel or GPS0001, mangrove or mudflat, and sea, in one frame, the marker through the center.
- A crop that would fail: the column alone against sky, a slice of the sail with no water, or an empty mudflat with no marker and no tree line.

### Đất Mũi marker cluster
- Vietnamese name: Khu du lịch Mũi Cà Mau / Đất Mũi
- Kind: zone
- Area: ấp Mũi, xã Đất Mũi. Older pages say huyện Ngọc Hiển.
- What the visitor does: Stands at the southern tip. The Ngọc Hiển page, 23 August 2024, names the ship-shaped panel (in place from the end of 2020), the line “Tổ quốc tôi như một con tàu / Mũi thuyền ta đó Mũi Cà Mau”, the sail text “Mũi Cà Mau”, and the coordinates printed on it: 8°37'30" north, 104°43' east. The same page names mốc GPS0001, the end-of-road marker for đường Hồ Chí Minh (work started 15 February 2017, investment about 70 billion đồng; the area figure on that page is not clear enough to record), the sea wall, and Cột cờ Hà Nội. It says the visitor looks at the place where the East Sea and the West Sea meet (S6). UNESCO describes the same tip as the place two tidal regimes meet and new mudflats form (S1, S2).
- Time: the culture-office listing says time at one point is not limited (S5). It does not print a recommended stay. A same-day return depends on the road, which is a separate entry in getting there.
- Fee: the Ngọc Hiển listing for the national park, which shares this address, prints 15,000–30,000 VND and hours 07:00–18:00 (S5). A tour page dated 30 September 2026 prints the gate at 06:30–17:30 every day including Tết, adult 30,000 VND, child from 1 m to 1.3 m and students 15,000 VND, and no ticket under 1 m or for a severe disability (S31). Hours disagree. The price band matches; the office page does not split adult and child. Keep both. Cart and canoe fares are only on the tour page. Check on arrival for those.
- From the base: the Hồ Chí Minh road south through Năm Căn. Not a walk. See getting there. 58.7 km is the Năm Căn-to-gate project, not the city-to-gate total (S21).
- When to go: inside the hours above. The tour page prefers 08:00–10:00 and after 15:00, and says a departure from the city after 13:00 misses the gate (S31). One page. The office listing does not print that advice.
- Mistake to avoid: treating the marker as a second Cần Thơ morning, or arriving on a motorbike via the expressway. The expressway ends at xã Hồ Thị Kỷ and does not take motorbikes (S18).
- Local interest: The cape and the two seas
- Suggested slug: dat-mui
- Suggested dot: 5 — the night exists for this tip
- Photo must show: the ship panel or GPS0001 through the center, with mangrove or mudflat and the sea in the same frame. A head-on crop of the column against sky fails.
- Published 23 Oct 2026. Hero File:Muicamau.jpg. Second frame File:Tuongdaimuicamau.jpg. See attractions/dat-mui.md.

### Mũi Cà Mau National Park
- Vietnamese name: Vườn quốc gia Mũi Cà Mau
- Kind: sight
- Area: xã Đất Mũi. The national conservation page still says Dat Mui commune, Ngoc Hien district (S4).
- What the visitor does: Boat or walk in the mangrove that sits behind the tip. The park was upgraded from Đất Mũi nature reserve by Decision 142/2003/QĐ-TTg on 14 July 2003. The reserve dated from Decision 194/CT on 9 August 1986. The conservation page prints an area over 41,800 hectares (S4). The Ramsar sheet prints 41,862 hectares, about 13,400 hectares of mangrove and about 26,000 hectares of mudflat (S2, S34). The opened RIS PDF prints the designation date 13/12/12 and site number 2088 (S34). The Ramsar news page is dated 27 November 2012 (S2). The conservation page says the park was recognized as a Ramsar site in 2013 (S4). The RSIS page returned 404 on 22 October 2026; the search record of that URL also showed 12 December 2013 (S3). Keep the dates. Do not pick one. UNESCO names it, with U Minh Hạ and the west-coast protection belt, as a core of the 2009 biosphere reserve (S1, S4). The visitor is here for đước, the mud, and the birds, not for a museum hall.
- Time: the office listing says time at one point is not limited (S5). A boat duration was not on that listing. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED for a boat hour.
- Fee: 15,000–30,000 VND on the office listing, hours 07:00–18:00, phone 0290.3870.545, email vuonqgmcm@gmail.com (S5). The tour page’s 06:30–17:30 and the adult/child split are the other figures (S31). Keep both.
- From the base: the same cape road as the marker. The gate and the park are one visit on the office address line (S5).
- When to go: inside the hours above. A dry-month recommendation was not taken from travel pages that disagreed with each other.
- Mistake to avoid: budgeting the park as a separate day from the marker when the listing puts them at one Đất Mũi address, or promising a Ramsar “ticket” beyond the gate fee.
- Local interest: Rừng đước
- Suggested slug: mui-ca-mau-national-park
- Suggested dot: 5 — the forest is the cape, not a side exhibit
- Photo must show: a boat or boardwalk under mangrove, water in the lower half, canopy meeting over the center. Empty sky or a single trunk fails.
- Published 23 Oct 2026. Hero File:Rhizophora apiculata in Dat Mui, Vietnam.jpg. Second frame the broken-stump mangrove file. See attractions/mui-ca-mau-national-park.md.
- Sources: S1, S2, S3, S4, S5, S31, S34

### Hòn Đá Bạc
- Vietnamese name: Hòn Đá Bạc
- Kind: sight
- Area: the west coast. The national tourism extract says xóm Kinh Hòn, xã Khánh Bình Tây, huyện Trần Văn Thời. The relic page says ấp Kênh Hòn, xã Đá Bạc (S23, S24). Keep both names. The park homepage for U Minh Hạ is also in xã Đá Bạc now (S25). Do not merge the rock and the melaleuca into one gate.
- What the visitor does: Walks a granite cluster on the Gulf side. The national tourism extract says about 6.43 hectares and three rocks, Hòn Ông Ngộ, Hòn Đá Lẻ, and Hòn Đá Bạc, the highest about 50 m, and shapes the page calls sân tiên, giếng tiên, bàn chân tiên, and bàn tay tiên. It also names Lăng Ông Nam Hải and a whale skeleton, and the 7 December 1971 fight over a 105 mm gun position. The relic page dates the national listing to Decision 2245/QĐ-BVHTTDL on 22 June 2009, under the name Hòn Đá Bạc – Trung tâm chỉ huy kế hoạch phản gián CM12 (9 September 1981–9 September 1984), and names a victory monument, a tradition house, and a Bác Hồ temple on the site (S23, S24). UNESCO includes the rock in the biosphere’s offshore groups (S1).
- Time: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED on the official extracts. One tour page prints 07:00–19:00 (S35). One source for the hour.
- Fee: the national tourism extract and the relic page do not print a fee. Two tour pages print 20,000 VND for an adult and 10,000 VND for a child (S35, S36). Keep the silence and the tour figures. Do not choose one for a page. Confirm on arrival.
- From the base: the national tourism extract says about 50 km by water and 42 km by road (S23). The airline guide says about 50 km (S17). Keep both road figures. The road it describes goes toward U Minh Hạ, then Nông trường Minh Hà, Cơi Năm, and ấp Đá Bạc B, then one bridge onto the rock (S23).
- When to go: the tour pages say the drier months, December to April (S35). One cluster of commercial pages. The relic page does not print a season.
- Mistake to avoid: a half-day promise that ignores the 42 km or 50 km, or calling the whale temple a separate beach club.
- Local interest: Biển Tây and the rock
- Suggested slug: hon-da-bac
- Suggested dot: 4 — the west-coast day, after the cape or instead of a second cape day
- Photo must show: the granite stack, the sea, and the bridge or the tree line, the rock through the center. A portrait crop that cuts the top of the stack fails.

### U Minh Hạ National Park
- Vietnamese name: Vườn quốc gia U Minh Hạ
- Kind: sight
- Area: ấp Vồ Dơi, xã Đá Bạc. The park homepage prints that address, director Trần Công Hoằng, phones 0290 3 910 020 and 0290 3 910 070, email vqguminhha@gmail.com (S25).
- What the visitor does: Takes a boat under melaleuca on peat, the other forest from the mangrove cape. UNESCO and the conservation page name it as a core of the 2009 biosphere, with the cape park and the west-coast belt (S1, S4). The city handbook describes flooded tram forest, birds, and a boat (S13). Commercial pages add a watchtower they call 24 m and a honey season. The park homepage opened on 22 October 2026 does not print the tower height, a fee, or an hour, so the tower height is not settled.
- Time: commercial pages say half a day to two days (S32, S33). The park homepage does not print a stay. Do not pick a duration.
- Fee: the park homepage does not print one. One commercial cluster prints 10,000 VND and hours 07:30–17:00, and a boat at 150,000 VND a person an hour or 80,000 VND for 30 minutes (S32). Another commercial page prints the same boat prices and an entrance of about 30,000–50,000 VND (S33). A third prints hours 07:20–17:00 (S37). Keep both entrance figures and both hours. Distance on those pages is about 40 km and about 1 hour, or 35 km and about 50 minutes to 1 hour (S32, S33). Keep both.
- From the base: north, not south. Do not ride the cape road and expect this gate.
- When to go: a season was not taken from commercial pages that reverse each other’s wet and dry months. The park homepage does not print a best month.
- Mistake to avoid: confusing this forest with U Minh Thượng, which is not this park and is not researched here.
- Local interest: Rừng tràm U Minh Hạ
- Suggested slug: u-minh-ha
- Suggested dot: 4 — the second landscape, a day of its own
- Photo must show: a narrow canal, melaleuca trunks on both sides, brown water, the boat’s bow in the lower center. A tower against empty sky fails.
- Unpublished: no licensed photo. The only Commons file titled as this park is a banana grove. See attractions/u-minh-ha.md. Checked again 23 Oct 2026.
- Sources: S1, S4, S13, S25, S32, S33, S37
