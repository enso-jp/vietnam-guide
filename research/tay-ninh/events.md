### Lễ vía Bà Linh Sơn Thánh Mẫu
- Vietnamese name: Lễ vía Bà Linh Sơn Thánh Mẫu – Núi Bà Đen
- Kind: festival
- When: Every year on lunar days 4, 5, and 6 of the fifth month (S8, S22). In 2026 the management program ran 18 Jun to 20 Jun, year Bính Ngọ, at Linh Sơn Tiên Thạch Tự (S22). The operator’s side-event page runs 18 Jun to 22 Jun, lunar 4 to 8 (S24). A Sun World article prints 18–21 Jun (S25). Print all three windows. The next solar date: Check on arrival.
- Where: Chùa Bà and Điện Bà on Núi Bà Đen, phường Bình Minh (S22, S23)
- What happens: The heritage page says the bath of the statue starts at 0:00 on the 4th at Điện Bà, doors shut, and that the 5th is the main day (S23). The 2026 program puts the bath at 18:00 on 18 Jun, with a noon rite each day and a farewell of the monks at 12:30 on 20 Jun (S22). Print 0:00 and 18:00. Recognition: 4 Sep 2018 on the mountain introduction (S8). The festival page says the decision was received on 14 Aug 2019 (S23). Both dates stay.
- Fee: The June 2026 festival cable (125,000 for Chùa Bà, combo 450,000 / 350,000 on 18–26 Jun) is a closed window (S25). The tariff in force on 22 Oct 2026 is the Sep–Dec table on the mountain entry. A 2027 festival fare: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Not the same as attraction slug: ba-den
- Sources: S8, S22, S23, S24, S25
