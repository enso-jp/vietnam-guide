# Getting there — Tay Ninh

Slug: `tay-ninh`
Checked: 22 Oct 2026

The bed is the old city, now phường Tân Ninh, and the temple quarter in phường Long Hoa. The usual previous stop is Ho Chi Minh City (`ho-chi-minh-city`). Cu Chi (`cu-chi`) is a district you can pass on Quốc lộ 22. It is not this page.

## getting there

- Nearest airport: Tân Sơn Nhất, in Ho Chi Minh City. The cable operator’s transfer page starts the road there (S28). The provincial tourism homepage does not name an airport inside Tây Ninh (S3). A passenger terminal in the province was not on the pages opened. Minutes from the kerb to a gate: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Nearest rail station: None recorded on the provincial tourism homepage or on the operator’s road page (S3, S28). Do not print a train. A proposed line is not a way to arrive.
- From the usual previous stop: Ho Chi Minh City by road. The operator’s page leaves Tân Sơn Nhất toward Cộng Hòa, then Quốc lộ 22, through Trảng Bàng and Gò Dầu, into the city, and on to the mountain if that is the first stop (S28). A 2018 national-tourism article uses Quốc lộ 22A to the Trảng Bàng junction, then tỉnh lộ 782 (S31). Print both road numbers. Do not collapse them into one route.

Lunar days 4, 5, and 6 of the fifth month are the Bà pilgrimage (S8). Those days fill the cable. The operator says the cable clock can change in bad weather (S10).

## What the new province is, and what this night is not

Resolution 202/2025/QH15, passed 12 Jun 2025, joined all of old Long An and all of old Tây Ninh into a new province still named Tây Ninh: 8,536.44 km² and 3,254,170 people, bordering Đồng Nai, Đồng Tháp, Thành phố Hồ Chí Minh, and Cambodia. New provincial governments started work on 1 Jul 2025 (S1). The tourism homepage repeats those two figures and the 96 communes of Resolution 1682: 82 xã and 14 phường (S2, S3).

Travelers do not sleep in Tân Ninh in order to wake up in Tân An. The homepage also promotes Láng Sen, the hundred-pillar house, Tân Lập, and Chợ Đào. Those are side-trip placeholders in `side-trips.md`. They are not cards on this page. Cu Chi already has `cu-chi`. Ho Chi Minh City already has `ho-chi-minh-city`.
