## What it is

- English name: Ba Den Mountain
- Vietnamese name: Núi Bà Đen
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Peak and cable lines over the plain
- Kind: sight

One visit. The Sun World lines (Vân Sơn to the summit, Chùa Hang to the Black Lady temple cluster, Tâm An) and the gate are one park. Ba Den Station is the change from the road to the cable, not a separate sight. Linh Sơn Tiên Thạch (Chùa Bà), Chùa Hang, and Chùa Trung are one temple walk on this mountain. The summit Phật Bà and Di Lặc are on that same visit. The history museum and the slide sit inside the park. A second cable company ran a pilgrimage window on 24–27 Sep 2026 (S32). Its everyday fare is UNSOURCED, so it is not a second page.

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Gate at khu phố Ninh Phú, then a cable, then the summit or the temple cluster.
- How long on site: Most of a day when the summit line and the temple line are both ridden. One line is the shorter visit. No printed minute count.
- How long including the ride from the base: UNSOURCED minutes from Tân Ninh.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Paying only the gate, or using the closed 18–26 Jun 2026 festival fares.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The gate ticket leaves the cable unpaid.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: Through 31 Dec 2026 (S9, re-opened 23 Oct 2026). Gate 10,000 adult / 5,000 child. Vân Sơn 450,000 / 350,000. Chùa Hang round trip 250,000 / 150,000. Chùa Hang one way 150,000 / 100,000. Tâm An 450,000 / 350,000. Under 1 m free. From 1 m to under 1.4 m child. From 1.4 m adult.
- After 17:00 on that same window: Vân Sơn 300,000 / 200,000. Chùa Hang round trip 150,000 / 100,000. Tâm An 300,000 / 200,000.
- Buffet 300,000 / 200,000. Combo tickets cost more. Students, Tây Ninh residents, monks with church papers, and visitors 80 and older have their own fares. Monks and visitors 80 and older ride free (S9).
- Closed window, not the October tariff: 18–26 Jun 2026, Chùa Bà cable 125,000, summit combo 450,000 / 350,000 (S25).
- S11 (16 Feb–3 May 2026) printed the same gate and the same three round-trip cable prices. S9 does not label Vân Sơn or Tâm An as round trip. The page prints the prices without adding “round trip” to those two lines.
- Source: S9, S11, S25

## Getting there

### From Tân Ninh

- Mode: Road to the gate
- Time: UNSOURCED minutes
- Distance: a little more than 10 km from the city centre (S3, S33, S33 re-opened 23 Oct 2026). 11 km northeast of the old town (S13).
- Address: Núi Bà Đen, khu phố Ninh Phú, phường Bình Minh (S9). Old line xã Thạnh Tân also printed (S33).

### From Ho Chi Minh City

- Mode: Car
- Time: about 2.5 to 3 hours from Tân Sơn Nhất when the mountain is the first stop (S28)
- Distance: about 110 km northwest (S12). A first stop at the mountain can exceed 110 km (S28).
- Coordinates: none printed on the tourism pages opened. Map stays off.
