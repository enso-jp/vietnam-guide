## What you see

The long pale front, the bell tower, the drum tower, and the courtyard. The Divine Eye is a detail on that front (dossier hero line). The hall plan is a dragon-horse. The mountain board prints the hall at 97.5 m by 22 m (S4). The ward page prints twelve carved gates (S6).

## Hours and fee

- Services: 0:00, 6:00, 12:00, and 18:00, called cúng tứ thời (S4). Noon is the service the provincial card tells visitors to see (S5).
- Shoes off. Men take the right door. Women take the left door. Dress is modest (S5).
- Visit length: about 60 minutes on the Bình Dương page only (S7). No second figure.
- Fee: UNSOURCED. Check on arrival.
- Grounds closing hour: UNSOURCED. Check on arrival.
- From Tân Ninh: about 4 km southeast (S4). Minutes: UNSOURCED. Check on arrival.

## Disagreements, both kept

- Started 1931 (S4). Started 1933 (S5, S6, S7).
- Completed 24 Jan 1947 and inaugurated 1 Feb 1955 (S4). Ward page: completed 1947, inaugurated 1955 (S6). Provincial card: finished in 1955 (S3, S5).
- Grounds: more than 1 km² and nearly 100 ha (S6). About 1 km² (S7).
- Chí Tôn: 9th of lunar January (S4). 8th of lunar January, more than 30,000 people (S6).
- Canopy: 3,027 stars and 3,072 globes (S6).
