# Attractions — Tay Ninh

Slug: `tay-ninh`
Vietnamese name: Tây Ninh
Province: Tây Ninh. Resolution 202/2025/QH15 joined old Long An and old Tây Ninh and kept the name Tây Ninh. 8,536.44 km², population 3,254,170. Borders Đồng Nai, Đồng Tháp, Thành phố Hồ Chí Minh, and Cambodia. Passed 12 Jun 2025. The new provincial government started 1 Jul 2025 (S1).
Kind: city
Corridor: southeast
Base a traveler sleeps in: phường Tân Ninh, the old city centre, or a room near the mountain gate in phường Bình Minh. No hotel brand.
Checked: 22 Oct 2026

This is the Holy See and Ba Den. Ho Chi Minh City already has `ho-chi-minh-city`. Cu Chi already has `cu-chi`. The 2025 province also contains old Long An. Tân An, Láng Sen, the hundred-pillar house, and Chợ Đào are side-trip placeholders. They are not a second night here.

From 1 Jul 2025 the old city wards are phường Tân Ninh, the Holy See’s old phường Long Hoa is still inside a phường Long Hoa, and the mountain’s old xã Thạnh Tân and phường Ninh Sơn are inside phường Bình Minh (S2).

## Why a night here

### The Holy See at noon, the mountain the next morning

- The picture they expect: the Holy See’s long pale front, the bell tower and the drum tower, and the courtyard under them. The Divine Eye is a detail on that front, not the whole frame. The second picture is Ba Den standing out of the plain, cable cabins in the middle distance, the summit in the frame.
- Where they sleep, as an area: phường Tân Ninh, which is the old city centre (S2). People who want the first cable sleep nearer the gate in phường Bình Minh. No hotel brand. A day trip from Ho Chi Minh City is what the homepage allows. A night is how you keep the noon service and the next morning’s cable without driving the highway twice (S3, S10).
- Where the evening is: the Holy See forecourt at the 18:00 service (S4). On 25 Sep 2026 the flower procession was at 18:30 and the great rite at 22:00 (S26). The other evening is the mountain after 17:00, when the operator posts a lower cable fare and the cabins are still running (S9, S10). A bar street was not named on the pages opened.
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices):
  - Tòa Thánh Cao Đài. Provincial card: https://dulich.tayninh.gov.vn/tin-tuc/toa-thanh-cao-dai-tay-ninh-1293 (S5). Church notice: https://www.caodai.com.vn/vn/news-detail/chuong-trinh-dai-le-hoi-yen-dieu-tri-cung-nam-binh-ngo-2026.html (S26).
  - Sun World Ba Den Mountain, the gate at khu phố Ninh Phú, phường Bình Minh. Tariff: https://sunworld.vn/vi/ba-den/tin-tuc-sunworld/cap-nhat-gia-ve-nui-ba-den-2026-va-cac-combo-uu-dai-30294 (S9).
- Hero must include: the Holy See facade, both towers, and the courtyard, in one frame. Subject through the center. People on the steps can be small. The mountain hero, if it is used instead, must include the peak, the plain, and a cabin or the pilgrim stairs. Subject through the center.
- A crop that would fail: the Divine Eye alone, a portrait of a statue that cuts the headdress, a cable cabin against empty sky, empty courtyard, or the Ho Chi Minh City skyline. A plate of rice paper is a food picture, not this hero.

## Attractions (Sight, area of interests, zone, hub, etc.)

### Tòa Thánh Cao Đài
- Vietnamese name: Tòa Thánh Cao Đài Tây Ninh
- Kind: sight
- Area: phường Long Hoa. The mountain board still writes the old line, phường Long Hoa, thị xã Hòa Thành, about 4 km southeast of the city (S4). Clause 90 kept old Long Hoa inside the new phường Long Hoa (S2). The footer on that same page, khu phố Ninh Phú, phường Bình Minh, is the mountain office. Do not move the temple there.
- What the visitor does: Walks the courtyard and looks into the prayer hall at a service. The plan is a dragon-horse. The hall is 97.5 m by 22 m on the mountain board (S4). Twelve carved gates on the ward page (S6). Noon is the service the provincial card tells visitors to see. Shoes come off. Men take the right door. Women take the left door (S5). Daily services are also 0:00, 6:00, and 18:00. The page calls the four together cúng tứ thời (S4).
- Time: about 60 minutes on the Bình Dương tourism page only (S7). Tây Ninh pages do not print a visit length. Do not invent a second figure.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. None of the temple pages opened print a ticket.
- From the base: about 4 km southeast of the old city if you slept in Tân Ninh (S4). Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: 12:00 for the service visitors are pointed to (S5). 18:00 if the stay includes the evening rite (S4). The 2026 Mother Goddess procession was 18:30 and the rite 22:00 (S26).
- Mistake to avoid: treating this as a Buddhist pagoda with incense you buy at the door, or as a wing of the Cu Chi tunnels. It is the Cao Dai central temple. Also avoid pasting the mountain office address onto the temple.
- Local interest: The Holy See
- Suggested slug: cao-dai-holy-see
- Suggested dot: 5 — the noon service is the reason to sleep here
- Photo must show: the facade, both towers, and the courtyard in one frame, in color. The Eye can be sharp and must not be the only subject.
- unpublished: no licensed photo
- Dates, all kept: started 1931 on the mountain board (S4). Started 1933 on the provincial card, the ward page, and the Bình Dương page (S5, S6, S7). Completed 24 Jan 1947 on the mountain board, with inauguration on 1 Feb 1955 (S4). The ward page says completed 1947 and inaugurated 1955 (S6). The provincial card says the work was finished in 1955 (S3, S5). Print 1947 and 1955. Do not pick one year as “finished.”
- Grounds, all kept: more than 1 km² and nearly 100 ha on the ward page (S6). About 1 km² on the Bình Dương page (S7). 1 km² is 100 ha, and the ward page still uses both phrases. Print both.
- Chí Tôn day, both kept: the 9th of lunar January on the mountain board (S4). The 8th of lunar January on the ward page, which also says more than 30,000 people (S6). Print both days.
- Stars, both kept, same ward page: 3,027 stars on the canopy, and 3,072 globes (S6).

### Núi Bà Đen
- Vietnamese name: Núi Bà Đen, Khu du lịch quốc gia Núi Bà Đen
- Kind: sight
- Area: phường Bình Minh. The tourism card prints both “Phường Bình Minh” and the old line “Xã Thạnh Tân, Thành phố Tây Ninh” (S33). The operator prints khu phố Ninh Phú, phường Bình Minh (S9). Clause 88 put old Thạnh Tân and old Ninh Sơn into phường Bình Minh (S2).
- What the visitor does: Pays the gate, rides a cable, and walks the summit and the temple cluster. The mountain board calls it nóc nhà Nam Bộ at 986 m, with núi Heo at 335 m and núi Phụng at 660 m (S8). The national card also says 986 m and about 24 km² for the whole complex, and names walking, a 1,200 m cable, and a slide of 1,190 m up and 1,700 m down (S12). Sun World’s 2026 pages name three lines, Vân Sơn to the summit, Chùa Hang toward the Bà temples, and Tâm An, and do not reprint those metre lengths (S9, S10). On the summit the mountain board describes a 72 m copper Phật Bà of 170 tons, and a sandstone Di Lặc 36 m high, 45 m wide, of 6,688 blocks (S8). Ba Den Station is given as 10,959 m² on that page (S8). Those statue and station figures are one source.
- Time: a half day for one line, a full day for summit and Chùa Hang. No single visit length is printed. The wooden-boat style cap does not exist here. The buffet is served 10:00–15:00 (S10).
- Fee: two operator windows for the same round-trip cables. From 16 Feb 2026 through 3 May 2026, and again from 28 Sep 2026 through 31 Dec 2026: gate 10,000 adult and 5,000 child. Vân Sơn 450,000 adult and 350,000 child. Chùa Hang round trip 250,000 and 150,000. Tâm An 450,000 and 350,000 (S9, S11). Under 1 m free. From 1 m to under 1.4 m is the child fare. From 1.4 m is adult (S9, S11).

Buffet 300,000 and 200,000. “Ẩm thực” ticket 150,000 and 100,000. After 17:00: Vân Sơn 300,000 and 200,000, Chùa Hang round trip 150,000 and 100,000, Tâm An 300,000 and 200,000, combo Vân Sơn + Chùa Hang 400,000 and 300,000. Weekday combo Vân Sơn + buffet 650,000 and 450,000. Weekend that combo 700,000 and 500,000. Weekday Vân Sơn + Chùa Hang + buffet 800,000 and 600,000. Weekend 850,000 and 650,000 (S9).
- Fee, a closed festival window, not the October tariff: 18–26 Jun 2026, Chùa Bà cable 125,000, summit combo 450,000 adult and 350,000 child (S25). Mid-autumn prices on the management notice of Sep 2026 were another closed window (S32).
- Fee, concessions on the Sep–Dec page: students Vân Sơn 300,000, Chùa Hang round trip 250,000, Tâm An 300,000, combo 400,000, with a valid card. Tây Ninh residents, adult / child: Vân Sơn 400,000 / 300,000, Chùa Hang round trip 200,000 / 120,000, Tâm An 400,000 / 300,000, combo 600,000 / 400,000, with papers. - From the base: a little more than 10 km from the city centre (S3, S33). 11 km northeast of the old town on the biodiversity page, which still says xã Ninh Sơn (S13). About 110 km northwest of Ho Chi Minh City on the national card (S12). The operator says a first stop at the mountain from Tân Sơn Nhất can exceed 110 km, against about 90 km to more than 100 km for the city centre (S28). All of those distances stay. Minutes from Tân Ninh: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: the clock in force on 22 Oct 2026 is the 28 Sep 2026 notice, until a later notice. Monday–Friday, Vân Sơn and Chùa Hang 06:00–20:30. Tâm An does not run those days. Spiritual area 06:30–20:00. Saturday, Vân Sơn and Chùa Hang 05:30–21:00, Tâm An 06:30–19:00, spiritual area 06:00–20:30. Sunday, Vân Sơn and Chùa Hang 05:30–20:30, Tâm An 06:30–19:00, spiritual area 06:00–20:00. The gate is open one hour before the cable and one hour after. Buffet 10:00–15:00 (S10). An earlier operator article prints 6:00–20:00 on Monday and Friday and 5:30–21:00 on weekends and holidays, and does not name Tuesday–Thursday (S11). Print both clocks. Weather can stop the cable (S10).

### Thung lũng Ma Thiên Lãnh
- Vietnamese name: Thung lũng Ma Thiên Lãnh, hồ đá xanh

The ward page calls it a trek and names Suối Vàng as the first water (S30). The 2018 national article says there was no food stall, so people carried lunch (S31).

## Lifestyle

### A day between the court and the cable
- Vietnamese name: một ngày ở Tây Ninh
- Kind: lifestyle
