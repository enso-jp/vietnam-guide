### S3

### S10
- URL: https://vbpl.vn/botuphap/Pages/vbpq-toanvan.aspx?ItemID=179501
- Publisher: Bộ Tư pháp, full text of Nghị quyết 202/2025/QH15
- Accessed: 23 Oct 2026
- Supports: signed Hà Nội, 12 Jun 2025; in force 12 Jun 2025. Article 1.1 merges the whole area and population of tỉnh Hà Giang and tỉnh Tuyên Quang into a new province named Tuyên Quang: 13,795.50 km² and 1,865,270 people, bordering Cao Bằng, Lào Cai, Phú Thọ, Thái Nguyên, and China. Those figures are the new province, not the town. Article 2: new provincial governments from 1 Jul 2025. The Công báo catalog entry is https://congbao.chinhphu.vn/van-ban/nghi-quyet-so-202-2025-qh15-45050/56708.htm (signer Trần Thanh Mẫn, issued and effective 12 Jun 2025).
