Checked: 23 Oct 2026

## Why a night here

- The picture they expect: Rice terraces and karst in cloud, on the loop. The published hero is that frame. The Commons file is Núi đôi Quảng Bạ, the twin hills of Quản Bạ, with terraces in front and karst in cloud (S16). It matches the loop. It is not a picture of the town.
- Where they sleep, as an area: Town hotels in Hà Giang for the bus night. Homestays and simple inns in Đồng Văn, Mèo Vạc, and Yên Minh on the road (S1, S3, S4). Thôn Tha is a stilt-house village a few minutes outside the city (S4). No hotel brand.
- Where the evening is: In Đồng Văn, the market at night or in the early morning (S1). Sunday is the market day in Đồng Văn and Mèo Vạc (S5). In the town, the sourced evening is the arrival night before an early start (S1, S4). A bar street was not on the pages opened.
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): Cafe Cổng Trời, the veranda coffee at Quản Bạ Pass, Vietnam Tourism, https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/ha-giang-adventures (S4). The geopark office, Số 2A đường Bạch Đằng, phường Hà Giang 1, tỉnh Tuyên Quang, http://dongvangeopark.com/?lang=en (S9). They are pins, not a ranked list. No prices and no scores.
- Hero must include: The road or the town against that karst, in color, subject through the center. Terraces and cloud belong in the frame when they are the Quản Bạ picture. The twin hills stay whole.
- A crop that would fail: A pig, a market stall filling the frame, a generic mountain drawing, empty fog, or a tight crop that cuts off the road, the town, or the twin hills.

No town pin is added. GeoNames prints 22.82333, 104.98357 for the former city seat and records a 1.112 km move in 2013 (S15). That point is not adopted. A pass, a viewpoint, and a bounding-box midpoint are not the town. The published 22.823, 104.983 is the gazetteer figure rounded. It is not stored here as a pin.

Town elevation, both of them, do not pick one: about 107 m SRTM3 and 115 m ASTER on the same gazetteer page (S15). The pass at 1,200 m is Ma Pi Leng, not the town (S8).
