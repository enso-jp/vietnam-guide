## What it is

- English name: Lung Cu flag tower
- Vietnamese name: Cột cờ Lũng Cú
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Flag tower on the border
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Detour off the Đồng Văn road, climb to the flag, look over the plateau, ride back in daylight (S1, S2).
- How long on site: The detour is three hours on the four-day page (S1). That is the detour, not a measured climb.
- How long including the ride from the base: Three hours on S1.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Using the late-afternoon climb and then riding back after dark.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Allow three hours, and ride back in daylight.
