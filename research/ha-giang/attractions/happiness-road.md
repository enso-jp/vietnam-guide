Unpublished as its own page. Vietnam Tourism’s buckwheat feature calls Mã Pì Lèng “aka The Happiness Road” (S7). The name and the build history are on the published pass page `ma-pi-leng`.

## What it is

- English name: Happiness Road
- Vietnamese name: Đường Hạnh Phúc. The Commons file title is Đèo Hạnh Phúc, Mèo Vạc.
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Another name for Ma Pi Leng
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: The same ride as Mã Pì Lèng (S7). The sky path looks over the historic road (S4).
- How long on site: The pass morning (S1). The sky path is about two hours and 500 metres, a separate walk (S4).
- How long including the ride from the base: 24 km, most of a morning with stops (S1).
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Publishing a second pass page for the same road.

## Open questions

- Elevation conflict, kept off the pass page. S7 says 1,500 metres for “the Ma Pi Leng pass (aka The Happiness Road)”. S8 says the pass rises to 1,200 metres. The page keeps 1,200.
- Volunteer count, kept off the page. S4 says 1,300 volunteers and seven years. S7 says work started in the late 1950s and took over seven years, and does not print the volunteer count. The page uses the late-1950s start and more than seven years.
