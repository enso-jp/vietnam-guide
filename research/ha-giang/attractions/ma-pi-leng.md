## What it is

- English name: Ma Pi Leng Pass

- Kicker (one line, what it is): The high road above the Nho Quế
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Ride the rim from Đồng Văn toward Mèo Vạc, stop where the shoulder allows, look into the canyon (S1, S8).
- How long on site: A pass-only duration was not printed. The leg to Mèo Vạc is most of a morning with stops (S1).
- How long including the ride from the base: 24 km, most of a morning (S1).
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Stopping in the lane, or riding after dark (S1).
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Stop only where the shoulder allows.
