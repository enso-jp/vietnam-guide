## Getting around on site

The four-day route follows QL4C northeast from Hà Giang to Bảo Lâm, and QL34 southwest from Bảo Lâm back to Hà Giang (S1). The loop passes Quan Ba, Yen Minh, Dong Van, and Meo Vac (S3). Mèo Vạc is 24 km from Đồng Văn and still takes most of a morning if the stops are made (S1).

Vietnam Tourism says experienced drivers can rent a motorcycle in town, and that another option is a seat behind a rider or a private car (S2). The same publisher says not to ride alone and to take a local driver (S5). Motorbikes are rented in Hà Giang city on the four-day page, which also says to ride in daylight and stop after dark (S1). ## Season limit on the journey
