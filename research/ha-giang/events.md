- Vietnamese name: Not printed. S7 says an annual festival in the Dong Van district.
- Kind: festival
- When: Typically mid-to-late November (S7). The bloom itself is white in late September, pink as October goes into November, and deep red by the end of December (S7). - Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED

- Sources: S2, S3, S7, S8
