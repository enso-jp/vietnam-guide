- Why it is this place, not a generic label: Đồng Hới’s ordinary meal is the Quảng Bình bowl, also called bánh canh, with ram on the side. The published `banh-canh` page is a Da Nang shop bowl and is the wrong card (S16, S17).
- Fits interest key, if any: `food`

- Links to attraction, dish, or place slug: suggested `chao-canh`, `dong-hoi-market`, `le-thanh-dong`. None of those pages exist yet. Do not link the published `banh-canh` as if it were this bowl.

- Sources: S4, S16, S17
