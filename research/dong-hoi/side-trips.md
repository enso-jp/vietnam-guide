### Phong Nha
- Slug if it exists: `phong-nha`
- Travel time from this base: The park’s Vietnamese page says Phong Nha Cave is 50 km southeast of Đồng Hới (S7). The ward page says about 50 km and does not name the compass point (S1). Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Why go: The caves. This dossier does not restudy them. The park page prints its own tickets and a visit of about 2.5 hours for the ordinary boat cave (S7). Do not copy the provincial tour card that marks the caves “Miễn phí” (S5). That label disagrees with the park.
- Overnight or day: A day from Đồng Hới is the usual plan only if the unsourced minutes allow a morning start and a return before the park’s posted close. The public Phong Nha page already says to sleep in the village when the boat is the trip. An overnight there is the other choice. This pass does not pick for the reader.
- Sources: S1, S7

### Vung Chua and Yen Island
- Slug if it exists: none
- Or: Side trip placeholder: Vung Chua – Dao Yen
- Travel time from this base: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED from Đồng Hới. Two official notes disagree about the pass, not about the city. The provincial tour card puts it under Hoành Sơn, thôn Thọ Sơn, old Quảng Trạch, about 6 km southeast of Đèo Ngang and about 3 km off National Highway 1, with a sign (S5). The 7 Dec 2015 visitor article says about 7 km south of Đèo Ngang (S6). Print both. Do not print a blog’s kilometer count from the city.
- Why go: The resting place of General Võ Nguyên Giáp, on a coast with three islands the 2015 article names Hòn La, Hòn Gió, and Hòn Nồm, which it also calls Đảo Yến (S6). The tour card prints “Miễn phí” (S5). No second tariff, no gate hour, and no 2026 visitor rule were opened. Hours and any registration: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Overnight or day: Day, if the unsourced ride from Đồng Hới is accepted as a day. This pass did not open a minute count.
- Sources: S5, S6
