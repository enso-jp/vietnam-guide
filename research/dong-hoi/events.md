### Dong Hoi culture and tourism week
- Vietnamese name: Tuần Văn hóa – Du lịch Đồng Hới
- Kind: festival
- When: The provincial office calls it a yearly week around 30 April and 1 May (S11). In 2023 the office said the events ran 25 April–1 May (S11). The food-festival page says the 2023 fair itself ran 26 April–1 May (S12). A 2026 calendar was not opened. Date: Check on arrival. Do not print 25 April as this year’s date.
- Where: Split across the city in 2023, not one gate (S11, S12). Photo show at Quảng trường Hồ Chí Minh. Food festival “Hương quê” and the stage at Quảng trường biển Bảo Ninh. Bài chòi, street music, and a countryside market on the walking street. Boat races on the Nhật Lệ. A street parade. The office also names a chèo cạn and múa bông performance on the Bảo Ninh square (S11).
- What happens: The 2023 food fair had 20 stalls: seafood from the city, cháo canh, bánh lọc, bánh bèo, bánh xèo from Ba Đồn, bánh đúc, bánh khoái, snails, Lệ Thủy duck eggs, Son River carp, Ba Đồn cockles, dried squid and shrimp, fish sauce, ruốc, khoai deo, and souvenir stalls (S12). The office adds traditional boat racing on the Nhật Lệ at 07:30 on 30 April 2023, and dance clubs at Công viên Nhật Lệ at 20:00 on 27 April 2023 (S11). Those clocks are 2023, not a standing timetable.
- Fee: No ticket price was printed for the week (S11, S12). The food fair asked stalls to post prices and sell at the posted price (S12). It did not print a visitor entrance fee. Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Not the same as attraction slug: `walking-street`, `nhat-le-beach`, `bao-ninh`, `ho-chi-minh-square`. The week uses those places. It is not those places.
- Sources: S11, S12
