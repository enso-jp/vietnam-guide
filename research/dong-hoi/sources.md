Accessed 21 Oct 2026 unless a line says the page has its own date.

### S1
- URL: https://donghoi.quangtri.gov.vn/chi-tiet-tin/-/view-article/1/1404469293898/1616085529215
- Publisher: UBND Phường Đồng Hới, Quảng Trị. The article still describes thành phố Đồng Hới in Quảng Bình. The footer prints 88 Phạm Văn Đồng, phường Đồng Hới, tỉnh Quảng Trị.
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: 12 km of coast. About 50 km to Phong Nha–Kẻ Bàng and about 50 km to suối Bang, with no minutes. City history as the ward tells it, including the 16 Aug 2004 decision that created the city and Decision 1270/QĐ-TTg of 30 Jul 2014 naming a class-II city. Those two decisions were not opened as PDFs. Morning river walk, Bảo Ninh across the water, sunset on Nhật Lệ. Quang Phú dunes 8 km from Nhật Lệ along Trường Pháp, and the color change through the day. Bàu Tró. Bảo Ninh beach, whale temple, old rampart. Nhật Lệ 2: one tower, two 150 m spans, tower 98 m, lit at night. Tam Toa built 1886, bell tower left, central park, and the hedged 1912 baptism line. Ho Chi Minh square 6.8 ha on Hùng Vương in old Đồng Hải, inside the citadel area, near the provincial museum. Mother Suốt beside the river. Chợ Đồng Hới on Mẹ Suốt, with the fish landing. Quảng Bình Quan, 1631, rebuilt 1825, repaired 1961. Citadel remnants in old Hải Định, moat, east gate, arched bridge. Đại Giác in old Đức Ninh Đông, 9 m and 40 tonne marble Amitabha. Walking street 18:00–24:00 Friday, Saturday, Sunday, and holidays, about 1 km, Phan Bội Châu and Đồng Hải, opened in the 2021 culture week. Bicycle, motorbike, electric car, taxi, no fares. The shop list filed under Notes, not pages. Cháo hàu and đẻn biển named and not defined.

- vietnamtourism.gov.vn’s Nhật Lệ article timed out. Its season line was not used.
- The provincial museum, a night-market name, a bar street, a bus station, a road minute from Hue, and a 2026 festival date were not on a page this pass could cite.
