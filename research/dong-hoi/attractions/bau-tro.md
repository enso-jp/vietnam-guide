## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: A sweet-water lake in Đồng Hới, very close to the sea, with casuarina around it. The ward page says the city takes fresh water from it, and that the lake stays fresh when the sea beside it is rough (S1, re-read 23 Oct 2026). The heritage list ranks the archaeology (S2). The tour card says the city takes its fresh water from the lake and that the lake holds material of the Bàu Tró culture (S5).
