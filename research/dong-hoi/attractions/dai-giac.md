## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: A working pagoda, not the Tam Toa ruin. The ward page, re-read 23 Oct 2026, places it in old Đức Ninh Đông and says the Amitabha is made from one block of white marble, 9 m and 40 tonnes, and calls it one of the large marble Buddhas in the country (S1). Võ Văn Tường puts that figure in the front yard, with a Maitreya beside it, and a separate bronze Shakyamuni in the hall, 3.3 m and 3.5 tonnes (S30). The Di Đà tower, left of the hall, was consecrated 22 Dec 2018, nine storeys, 32 m (S30). Do not merge the marble figure and the bronze figure.
