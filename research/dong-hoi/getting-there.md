# Getting there — Dong Hoi

Slug: `dong-hoi`
Checked: 21 Oct 2026

The usual previous stop on this coast is Hue, or a flight into the airport north of town. Phong Nha is the onward day, not the way in.

### Air
- Nearest airport: Đồng Hới Airport. The civil AIP names it VVDH — QUANG BINH/DONG HOI, operated by Airports Corporation of Vietnam, 6 km north of the city center, elevation 18 m, IFR/VFR (S8). The ward office now prints a Quảng Trị address (S1). The AIP title still says Quang Binh (S8).
- IATA code VDH is the code in common use. This pass did not get a rendered ACV timetable or a rendered Vietjet route table. Which cities have a nonstop is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- From the airport to a Nhật Lệ or Bảo Ninh bed: 6 km south into town (S8). Minutes and taxi fare: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.

### Onward to the caves
- The park’s Vietnamese page puts Phong Nha Cave 50 km southeast of Đồng Hới city (S7). The ward tourism page says about 50 km and does not give a compass point (S1). Minutes, taxi fare, and a local bus: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Tickets, boat prices, and summer or winter hours stay on the park page. This dossier does not repeat them. The provincial tour cards that print “Miễn phí” on the caves disagree with the park’s own ticket list (S5, S7). Do not copy the free label onto a cave.

### Getting around on site
- The ward page says a visitor can rent a bicycle, a motorbike, an electric car, or a taxi (S1). No fare and no operator name. Fares: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Nhật Lệ beach is described as in the city center (S1). Bảo Ninh is the peninsula across Nhật Lệ bridge (S1). The citadel, the gate, the market, and Tam Toa are a walk from a riverside base if that is where the night is (S1). Minutes between them: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- The airport figure is 6 km, not a walk from the terminal with bags (S8).

### Where the night is
- Sleep as an area: the Nhật Lệ beachfront, or Bảo Ninh across the river, or a riverside room by the market and Quảng Bình Quan. Not a hotel brand (S1).
