### Đồng Bài – Cái Viềng

The city page of 14 February 2025 prints the fares and says the route is Đồng Bài–Cái Viềng. Pedestrian 10,000 đồng a crossing. Bicycle 12,000. Loaded bicycle 12,000. Motorbike 45,000 a bike. Car under 9 seats 190,000. Coach 9 to under 24 seats 210,000. Coach 24 to under 32 seats 240,000. Coach 32 seats and up 330,000. Truck fares are also on that page, from 145,000 to 970,000 depending on weight and load (S6).

From 1 April 2026, Quyết định 20/2026/QĐ-UBND: pupils and students who live or study in the special zone ride free; Vietnamese citizens resident in the special zone pay half, for a walking ticket, a bicycle, a loaded bicycle, and a motorbike. A car under 9 seats gets the reduction only if a resident owns it and it is not in the transport business. A card plus an identity paper is required (S6).

Cars and trucks heading onto Cát Bà are not taken from 09:00 to 13:00 on 25 April 2026 through 3 May 2026, and on weekend holidays from 9 May 2026 through 30 September 2026 (S6).

The 14 September 2026 notice, quoting Công ty TNHH Bayview Cát Bà, sets the clock from 1 September 2026 through 31 December 2026. The pier opens at 05:00 and the posted day runs to 18:00. Weather and the crowd can extend it to 19:00. Boats alternate. Intervals are generally 30–40 minutes depending on the boat. The pier command may change a departure, with at most 40 minutes between boats. A 20-ton boat is posted four times a day for emergencies. A boat under 100 tons carrying flammable cargo is posted twice a day. A full grid of every departure was not in the text. The February 2025 page’s own timetable is an image and was not transcribed. Outside 1 September–31 December 2026, the clock is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED (S6, S7).

## Getting around on site

Boats for the bay leave Bến Bèo. The park’s December 2022 page describes a 3–4 hour hire: Bến Bèo, vịnh Cái Bèo, a fish raft or đảo Ba Trái Đào, then đảo Khỉ, then back (S12). A boat fare is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The bay ticket is separate (S3).

The 12 August 2013 fort page says the fort is a short motorbike ride, a few kilometres from the town center, about 10 minutes on the other fort page of the same day (S10, S10b). Both stay.

The special-zone page says the zone has more than 316 lodging places and 149 tourist boats (S17). It names resort brands. Those names are in `food.md` under notes. They are not picks.
