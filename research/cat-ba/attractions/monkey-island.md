## What it is

- English name: Monkey Island

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Swim stop at the end of the 3–4 hour hire (S12); Cát Dứa called the finest satellite beach of Cát Bà (S12); rangers stocked >20 monkeys; two beaches Cát Dứa 1 and 2 (S12b). Resort names on S12 not used.
- How long on site: A swim stop on the 3–4 hour boat (S12)
