- The scene, in order: Trek from the park past Ao Ếch (pool in summer, firm springy walkable bed in the dry season) to the village (S13). ~80 houses of bamboo, wood, leaves, earth (S14). Boat from Bến Bèo or a Lan Hạ cruise (S14).
