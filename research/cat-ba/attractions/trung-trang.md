## What it is

- English name: Trung Trang Cave
- Vietnamese name: Động Trung Trang
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Limestone cave on the national park route
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the ~300 m cave through the hill; >10 parallel slabs ring when struck; cave holds hundreds of visitors; Kim Giao forest at the site; valley ~300 ha, 10–30 m below sea level (S11, re-read 23 Oct 2026).
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- How long including the ride from the base: see Getting there
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Budgeting it as a separate day; it sits on the park route
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: none

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: Route ticket Kim Giao–Ngự Lâm–Trung Trang 120,000 đồng adult (S3)
- Child, height, or senior rule: Child rule: from age 6 pays the child price, under 6 free (S3, 2025, newest). The replaced 2024 text also used a 1.2 m height line (S5). Page prints the newest rule only.
- What the basic ticket includes: The route
- What costs extra: Whether the park ticket (160,000) replaces the route ticket is not stated (S3). Page tells the reader to ask at the gate.
