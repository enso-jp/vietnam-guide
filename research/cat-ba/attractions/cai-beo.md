## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Floating houses and fish rafts off Bến Bèo (S12, S15). Households: >200 (S12, 2022) and ~300 (S15, 2013); page prints 200 to 300. Site found 1938 (S15); pottery ~7,000–7,500 years (S16) and ~7,000–4,500 years ago (S15); page prints 'about 7,000 years ago'. Ranked national Feb 2009 (S16); S15 (2013) says 'will be'. Page prints the 2009 ranking. The 3–4 h Lan Ha hire crosses vịnh Cái Bèo (S12).
- How long on site: Part of the 3–4 hour bay boat (S12)
- How long including the ride from the base: see Getting there
