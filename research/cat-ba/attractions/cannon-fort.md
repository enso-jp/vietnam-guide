## What it is

- English name: Cannon Fort
- Vietnamese name: Pháo đài Thần Công; cao điểm 177
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Hilltop fort above the town and the bay
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Ride ~10 minutes up from the center (S10b); trenches, vaulted tunnels, command area and bunkers nearly intact; large mounted viewer; Lan Hạ from above (S10b). Built 1942; displayed gun is damaged and not a gun that fought; views of Bến Bèo, Cát Cò 1 and 2, Áng Thảm; cafe at the top (S10). Sunset (S18).

- Mistake that wastes the visit: Calling the displayed gun a wartime piece
