## What it is

- English name: Lan Ha Bay
- Vietnamese name: Vịnh Lan Hạ
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Limestone bay east of Cát Bà island
- Kind: zone

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Boat from Bến Bèo → Cái Bèo bay → fish raft or Ba Trái Đào → swim at Đảo Khỉ → back (S12). Hang Luồn kayak arch; dive spots 4–5 m; Cửa Vạn >300 families (S12b). >7,000 ha, ~400 islands, 139 beaches (S12). UNESCO's whole-property counts (1,133 / ~1,600 islands) are kept off the page.
- How long on site: 3–4 hours, or an 8-hour day; overnight boats exist (S12)
- How long including the ride from the base: see Getting there
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating it as a Ha Long cruise
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: none

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: Day visit 120,000 đồng adult (S3)
- Child, height, or senior rule: Child rule: from age 6 pays the child price, under 6 free (S3, 2025, newest). The replaced 2024 text also used a 1.2 m height line (S5). Page prints the newest rule only.
- What the basic ticket includes: Entry to the Cát Bà bays
- What costs extra: Boat hire: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Overnight bay fee 1/2/3 nights 400,000/300,000, 600,000/500,000, 650,000/550,000 (S3)
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S3; retention split (S3 vs S5) not printed

## Getting there

### From Cat Ba town

- Mode: Road or walk to Bến Bèo, then boat
- Time: 1.5 km (S15) or about 2 km (S16) by road; page prints 1.5–2 km. Minutes UNSOURCED
- Fare, if any: Check on arrival
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S12, S15, S16

### From Hai Phong

- Mode: Ferry Đồng Bài–Cái Viềng, then island road
- Time: Crossing 30 minutes on the 1 Sep–31 Dec 2026 clock (sources.md S7). Road minutes UNSOURCED
- Fare, if any: Pedestrian 10,000 đồng (sources.md S6)
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S6, S7

- Coordinates, only if a source prints them: none used

## Season

- April–June calm and clear (S12b); quiet from September (S12b); autumn light preferred (S12). Page: April–June for calm water; autumn for soft light.
