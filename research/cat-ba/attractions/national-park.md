## What it is

- English name: Cat Ba National Park
- Vietnamese name: Vườn quốc gia Cát Bà
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Forested limestone peaks in the island's heart
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Trails from the park center to Kim Giao and Ngự Lâm peaks and Trung Trang cave (S3 route names). Langur endemic, critically endangered, small fragmented population, no count (S1, S17). Ao Ếch → Việt Hải trek (S13, S14).
- How long on site: A morning (dossier)
- How long including the ride from the base: see Getting there
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Expecting to see a langur; printing a count
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: none
