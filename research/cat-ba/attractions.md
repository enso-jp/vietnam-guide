## Why a night here

### The harbor, the boats, and the limestone behind the town

- The picture they expect: The town waterfront. A seawall, low boats on calm water, a row of narrow harbor buildings, and a limestone hill rising behind the roofs. That is the published hero, opened 23 Oct 2026: Ian Byrne, CC BY-SA 2.5, Commons file `Cat_Ba_Vietnam-Feb06.jpg`. The frame matches. It is not a beach and not a lobby.
- Where they sleep, as an area: Rooms along the harbor strip, above that seawall. The special-zone page says the zone has more than 316 lodging places (S17). No hotel brand. Resort names on that page and on the park’s Lan Hạ page are in `food.md` under notes, marked do not publish as a pick.
- Where the evening is: Đường 1/4 and đường Núi Ngọc, the streets on the 20 June 2013 seafood posting, including the line “phố ẩm thực, đường Núi Ngọc” (S21). After the meal, the 8 January 2016 city article sends people to a night market for sea souvenirs. It does not print the market’s street (S18).
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices):
  - Bến Bèo, the boat quay beside the town. The December 2022 park page starts the bay hire there (S12).
  - Pháo đài Thần Công, cao điểm 177. The 12 August 2013 district page: https://cathai.haiphong.gov.vn/thang-canh-cat-ba/thuong-thuc-cafe-tai-diem-cao-177-phao-dai-than-cong-di-tich-lich-su-thang-canh-cat-ba-312470 (S10). The winter article names it for sunset over the archipelago (S18).
  They are pins, not a bar list. A separate bar street was not on the pages opened.
- Hero must include: the harbor water, at least one boat, the town front, and the limestone hill behind the roofs, in one frame, subject through the center. The published hero does this.
- A crop that would fail: a beach with no town, a resort lobby, a pool, a fort tunnel, a forest interior, empty fog with no seawall, or a tight crop that cuts the hilltop off the roofs. The Lan Hạ frame and the park frame are different subjects. Do not use them as this hero.

### Lan Ha Bay
- Vietnamese name: Vịnh Lan Hạ
- Kind: zone
- Area: East of đảo Cát Bà, beside Hạ Long on the south, on the park’s 19 December 2022 page (S12).
- What the visitor does: Rides a boat among limestone islands and small beaches. That page says more than 7,000 ha, about 400 islands, and 139 small yellow beaches. It names a 3–4 hour hire: Bến Bèo, vịnh Cái Bèo, a fish raft or đảo Ba Trái Đào, then a swim at đảo Khỉ, then back. It also names an 8-hour day and overnight boats. Cruise brands on that page are not picks. The 24 July 2023 park article says dive spots on this bay are 4–5 m deep (S12b). The special-zone page says the Most Beautiful Bays of the World club recognized Lan Hạ in 2020 (S17). That club’s own list was not opened. UNESCO’s English synthesis says 65,650 ha and 1,133 islands. Other-language blurbs on the same listing say about 1,600 islands (S1). Do not merge the counts. The park’s own “about 400” is a third count (S12).
- Time: Half to full day for the hire the park page describes. An overnight is the resolution’s 1-night, 2-night, or 3-night bay ticket, which is a fee, not a boat (S3).
- Fee: Day visit to the bays of the Cát Bà archipelago, from 1 January 2026: 120,000 đồng an adult, 60,000 đồng a child from 6. Under 6 free on the 2025 article. One night on the bays 400,000 / 300,000. Two nights 600,000 / 500,000. Three nights 650,000 / 550,000. The 2025 article says the bay collector keeps 21% and pays 79% to the state budget (S3). The 2 January 2026 bay-board notice says those figures follow Nghị quyết 47/2025/NQ-HĐND of 10 December 2025 and does not reprint the numbers (S4). Boat hire is not in the resolution. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The replaced 2024 resolution used the same adult and child amounts. Its paying child column is from age 6 or from 1.2 m tall, and its free line is under 6 or under 1.2 m. It also kept 21% of the bay fee and paid 79% to the budget (S5). The 2025 article does not print the height test (S3). Both child definitions stay. Do not pick one.
- From the base: Boat from Bến Bèo (S12).
- When to go: April to June on the park’s 24 July 2023 article, for calm clear water. From September that article calls the bay quiet (S12b). The December 2022 page prefers autumn light (S12). Both stay.
- Mistake to avoid: Describing this as a Hạ Long cruise, or using the town beach as the picture.
- Local interest: The bay
- Suggested slug: lan-ha
- Suggested dot: 5 — the reason to stay the night. The published shell already uses this slug.
- Photo must show: limestone towers and the water in one frame, subject through the center. A boat may be small. The town must not fill the frame. The published file `Lan_Ha_Bay.JPG`, Binh Giang, public domain, is that bay: rock, a gap of water, no town. It matches this sight. It does not match the harbor hero.
- Sources: S1, S3, S4, S5, S12, S12b, S17

### Cat Ba National Park
- Vietnamese name: Vườn quốc gia Cát Bà
- Kind: sight
- Area: The island interior. UNESCO says the archipelago was designated a national park in 1986, a marine protected area in 2010, and a special national monument in 2013, and that the park should still be aligned with the World Heritage boundary (S1). Biosphere reserve 2004. Added to the World Heritage property in September 2023, criteria (vii) and (viii) (S1, S17).
- What the visitor does: Walks the forest. UNESCO names the Cát Bà langur, *Trachypithecus poliocephalus*, as a critically endangered endemic, a small population, hurt by tourism, few animals, and broken groups. It prints no headcount (S1). The special-zone page says the golden-headed langur lives only here and is critically endangered on the IUCN Red List (S17). Ticketed routes on the 2025 fee article: the park; the line from the park center to đỉnh Kim Giao, đỉnh Ngự Lâm, and động Trung Trang; đồng Ninh Tiếp and hang Tiền Đức; Phù Long, the mangrove, and động Thiên Long (S3). The gate hours on the park’s own fee page are 07:00 to 17:00. That page announces a fee change and does not print the amounts (S2).
- Time: A morning inside the gate. The Ao Ếch walk is longer. See Việt Hải.
- Fee: From 1 January 2026, adult / child from 6: park 160,000 / 80,000; Kim Giao–Ngự Lâm–Trung Trang 120,000 / 60,000; Ninh Tiếp–Tiền Đức 80,000 / 40,000; Phù Long–mangrove–Thiên Long 80,000 / 40,000. Under 6 free. The 2025 article says the park fee and those three routes are paid 100% to the state budget (S3). The 2024 resolution text, now replaced, said the park keeps 100% of what it collects, and it used the same prices (S5). The retention lines disagree. The prices agree. The child test disagrees, as under Lan Hạ. Hours 07:00–17:00 (S2). The park’s own fee page prints the hours and does not print the amounts.
- From the base: A road into the park. A minute was not printed. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: Inside the posted hours. The winter article names the forest walk as a winter activity (S18).
- Mistake to avoid: Printing one child rule. Printing a langur count. Using the harbor photo, or a beach, as the park.
- Local interest: The park and the langur
- Suggested slug: national-park
- Suggested dot: 4 — the other half of a two-night stay, with Lan Hạ. The published shell already uses this slug.
- Photo must show: limestone under forest, roots or a trail, subject through the center. A peak with no trees would be a different frame. The published file `Cat_Ba_National_Park,_Vietnam,_20240131_0932_4547.jpg`, Jakub Hałun, CC BY 4.0, is the forest floor: buttress roots on limestone. It matches a park frame. It does not show Ngự Lâm.
- Sources: S1, S2, S3, S5, S17, S18

### Viet Hai and Ao Ech
- Vietnamese name: Làng Việt Hải; Ao Ếch
- Kind: area

In summer Ao Ếch is a freshwater pool in the limestone. In the dry season the bed is firm, slightly springy, and damp, and the page says you can walk on it (S13). The 11 November 2023 park page says the village is under 18 km from the district center, that the walk via Ao Ếch is more than 8 km, and later on the same page that the trek is 8 km (S14). The 12 km title, the “more than 8 km” line, and the plain 8 km line all stay. Do not pick one. That page also says about 80 houses of bamboo, wood, leaves, and earth.

- From the base: Boat from Bến Bèo, or the walk (S14).
- When to go: The village page suggests April to September for an easier walk, and also allows winter (S14). Ao Ếch changes with the season (S13).
- Mistake to avoid: Promising a lake in the dry season, or one distance.
- Local interest: The park, then the village
- Suggested slug: viet-hai

### Cannon Fort

- Local interest: The wartime hill
