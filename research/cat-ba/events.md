### Ho Chi Minh visit anniversary
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội kỷ niệm ngày Chủ tịch Hồ Chí Minh về thăm Cát Hải
- Kind: festival
- When: The special-zone page prints 31/3/1959 as the visit and calls the anniversary the big festival of the special zone (S17). The 2026 fair page equates the 67th anniversary with 31/3/1959–31/3/2026 (S24). The next opening night around that day is Check on arrival. A direct fetch of the city article that search results dated to the evening of 28 March 2026 failed on 23 Oct 2026. That stage time is not used.
- Where: The fair page says Trung tâm du lịch Cát Bà, đặc khu Cát Hải (S24). The special-zone page does not print a street.
- What happens: The anniversary of the 31 March 1959 visit. The party-history page says that morning he came ashore at the fishing village, now called the fish port, on the northwest of the island, between about 09:00 and 10:00, from two navy boats, then walked to the office of Xí nghiệp đánh cá Cát Bà. The same page says Decision 317/QĐ-BVHTTDL of 22 January 2009 ranked the visit site a national historic relic (S29). From 10 November 2021 the city moved the fish-port logistics from the Cát Bà port in the town to cảng cá Trân Châu (S30). The notice moves the port work. It does not say the visit site moved.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: not the national park, not Lan Hạ
- Sources: S17, S24, S29, S30

### Cat Ba dragon-boat day
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội đua thuyền rồng trên biển Cát Bà
- Kind: festival
- When: 1 April every year, on the party-history page. It is a solar date on that page, tied to the 31 March 1959 visit, and the page also calls 1 April the fisheries tradition day (S29). This is not the 21/1 lunar race at thị trấn Cát Hải.
- Where: The old fishing-village area on Cát Bà, the visit site (S29). The 2021 notice moved the port logistics to Trân Châu and does not say the festival moved (S30).
- What happens: Dragon boats, small boats, rafts, and a tug on the water. The page says teams have come from other provinces. It does not print a 2027 start time.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: none
- Sources: S29, S30
