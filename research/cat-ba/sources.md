- Accessed: 23 Oct 2026
- Stamp: verified
- Supports: Ha Long Bay – Cat Ba Archipelago. English synthesis: 65,650 ha, 1,133 islands and islets, buffer 34,140 ha, Quang Ninh and Hai Phong. Inscription 1994. Boundary changes 2000 and 2023.
