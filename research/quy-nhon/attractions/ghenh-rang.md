## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walks the rock coast. The portal names egg-shaped stones the sea has rounded, calls the cove bãi tắm Hoàng Hậu because Nam Phương Hoàng Hậu bathed here, plus a Vọng Phu rock, a lion-shaped rock, bãi tắm Tiên Sa, and Hàn Mặc Tử’s grave on the hillside (S11). It says Bảo Đại chose Ghềnh Ráng as a royal retreat from 1927 (S11). From the grave the page says you see the east of the city and Phương Mai (S11).
- How long on site: The portal’s visit field is 60 minutes (S11).
- How long including the ride from the base: About 3 km southeast, on the church page’s figure (S13). Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Looking for a beach named Queen on an English map and missing bãi tắm Hoàng Hậu (S11).
- Notice: Bãi Xếp is 13 km south and is not this cove (S15).
