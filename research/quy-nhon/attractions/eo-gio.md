## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Stands in a curved gap between two rock ridges and looks down at the sea. The page says the name comes from wind funnelled through the saddle (S16). It says photographers and visitors have called the sunset the finest in Vietnam (S16). A 24 Feb 2021 news page on the same portal calls it the finest sunrise (S30). Both claims stay.
- How long on site: The portal’s visit field is 30 minutes (S16).
- How long including the ride from the base: On Phương Mai, east of the lagoon (S7, S16). Kilometres and minutes from the beach: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Collapsing the sunrise claim and the sunset claim into one slogan (S16, S30).
- Notice: The calm window is lunar month 3 through the end of lunar month 9. Stay away when the sea is rough (S16).
