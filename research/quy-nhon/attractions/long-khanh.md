## What it is

- English name: Long Khanh pagoda
- Vietnamese name: Chùa Long Khánh
- 141 Trần Cao Vân, phường Lê Lợi. Free. 5:00–17:00. Visit field 120 minutes (S10, reopened S40).
- More than 300 years, built by Hoa people, plan in the shape of the character Khẩu.
- 17 m Amitabha on a blue-stone lotus past the gate. Bronze Buddha in the rear hall, 1.5 m and more than 1,200 kg.
