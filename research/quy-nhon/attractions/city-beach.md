## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walks a few steps from the center and swims a long crescent of yellow sand. The portal calls the water fairly clear and blue (S3).
- How long on site: The portal’s visit field is 60 minutes (S3). A beach day is longer than that field. A clock for a full day was not printed.
- How long including the ride from the base: This is the base if you sleep on the crescent (S3).
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating Kỳ Co as this beach. Kỳ Co is nearly 25 km away (S17).
