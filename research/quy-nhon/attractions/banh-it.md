## What it is

- English name: Banh It towers
- Vietnamese name: Tháp Bánh Ít (Tháp Bạc)
- Kicker: Four Cham towers on a hill
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walks four Cham towers on a hill beside Highway 1. From the road the silhouette reads as a bánh ít cake. The French record name is Tour d'Argent (S32, reopened S40).
- How long on site: 30 minutes, the visit field (S40).
- How long including the ride from the base: About 20 km. Minutes: UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Going to Tháp Đôi, the pair inside the city, when this stop is the hill group.
- Notice: Tháp Đôi is the city pair. This is the hill about 20 km out.
