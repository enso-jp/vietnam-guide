Accessed 21 Oct 2026 unless a line says otherwise.

The Binh Dinh tourism portal (`dulichquynhon.binhdinh.gov.vn`) presented an expired TLS certificate on this date. The pages below still loaded. The portal footer says it is run by Sở Du lịch and VNPT Bình Định, contact 185–187 Phan Bội Châu, Thành phố Quy Nhơn, and `congdulichbinhdinh@gmail.com`. Many sight pages repeat the phone `02563815959`. That number is treated as the portal’s default, not as proof that each gate has its own desk. A different number is recorded only where the page prints one.

The portal still prints tỉnh Bình Định and, on some sight headers, newer ward names such as Phường Quy Nhơn, Phường Quy Nhơn Đông, and Phường Quy Nhơn Nam. Where a header and the prose disagree, both are in the entry.
