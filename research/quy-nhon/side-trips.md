### Banh It towers
- Or: Side trip placeholder: Banh It towers
- Travel time from this base: About 20 km (S32). Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Why go: Four Cham towers of the late 11th or early 12th century on a hill between two branches of the Kôn, beside quốc lộ 1A.
