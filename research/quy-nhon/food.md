## Comprehensive list of local food to try

### Fish-cake noodle soup
- Vietnamese name: Bún chả cá Quy Nhơn
- What it is: Named in the portal’s dish list (S1). The 2019 newspaper piece says this bowl’s broth is simmered from fresh fish bones, often tuna (cá thu) or sailfish (cá cờ), not from pork bone, and that the cakes are both fried and steamed, without the northern dill (S25). Herbs on that piece: lettuce, shredded cabbage, a few banana-flower strands, bean sprouts, and mint, plus a chili-sauce dip and pickled shallot (S25).
- How to eat it: Cake dipped in the sauce, herbs in the bowl, broth alongside (S25)
- When to order it: A meal. The piece does not print an hour. It is also a dish people eat through the day on Nguyễn Huệ.
- Area, not a shop: Nguyễn Huệ, on the 2019 piece (S25). The museum is at 26 Nguyễn Huệ (S9). That is the street, not a stall.
- Meal: breakfast
- Neighbor dish to link: shared `bun-cha-ca`. This bowl is the fish-bone one the 2019 piece describes. Do not reuse another city’s pork-bone caption.
- Sources: S1, S25, S9

### Jumping shrimp pancake
- Vietnamese name: Bánh xèo tôm nhảy (the 2019 piece’s full name is bánh xèo tôm nhảy rau mầm)
- What it is: The 2019 piece says a thin batter, small freshwater shrimp that turn red, bean sprouts, and scallion, eaten with fish sauce and wrapped with sprouts, cucumber, and star fruit (S25). It calls the cake yellow (S25). The 2019 night piece on the tourism portal says the menu also includes squid, beef, and egg, and that the shrimp cake is the one a visit wastes if you skip it (S24). The portal introduction does not name this dish (S1). Both descriptions stay. Do not add a “no turmeric” line. That line was not on these pages.
- How to eat it: Wrapped with the herbs, sauce on the side (S25)
- When to order it: The night piece ties it to a cool evening (S24). A shop hour was not printed.
- Area, not a shop: Diên Hồng and Đống Đa (S24)
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: shared `banh-xeo` only with this difference stated. The Quy Nhon cake is the shrimp one these two pages describe. Do not reuse a Hội An or a miền Tây caption.
- Sources: S24, S25
