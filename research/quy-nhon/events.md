## Events and to-do

### Nuoc Man pagoda festival
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Chùa Bà – Cảng thị Nước Mặn
- Kind: festival
- When: The 27 Feb 2025 article says that year’s public days were 27 Feb–1 Mar 2025, lunar 30th of the first month through the 2nd of the second month, Ất Tỵ (S28). It says “this year.” The next solar date moves. Check on arrival. The article calls the festival national intangible heritage, recognized in 2022 (S28). The decision text was not opened.
- Where: Chùa Bà, thôn An Hòa, xã Phước Quang, huyện Tuy Phước (S28). Not the city beach.
- What happens: The article’s 2025 order: a state ceremony from 15:00 on 27 Feb (the article also prints 27 Feb 2023 in one line of that same list; both years are on the page), a procession Nghinh thần and the Ngư–Tiều–Canh–Mục emblems 15:00–16:00 on 27 Feb 2025, cầu an 7:00–9:00 on 28 Feb 2025, tế Bà 5:00–7:00 on 1 Mar 2025, bài chòi, volleyball, hát lễ, tuồng, and folk games on the days printed (S28). Lanterns and open houses are the custom the lead describes (S28).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: none. Chùa Bà is not a Quy Nhon sight entry. The side-trip line is the placeholder for the commune.
- Sources: S28

### Arts street on Le Duc Tho
- Vietnamese name: Phố văn hóa – nghệ thuật
- Kind: activity
- When: The 3 Feb 2021 article says the city planned two nights a month, Friday and Saturday of the first week and the third week, after the 1–2 Jan 2021 reopening (S29). A 2026 date was not on the page. Check on arrival.
- Where: Lê Đức Thọ beside hồ sinh thái Đống Đa, from the Phan Đình Phùng corner to the Lê Lợi corner (S29)
- What happens: Two outdoor stages about 50 m apart, bài chòi, a Cham dance, modern sets, and street games (S29). The 19:30 start is the 1 Jan 2021 night, not a standing hour.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: none. This is not Đống Đa ward’s twin towers.
- Sources: S29
