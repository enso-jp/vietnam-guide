## What it is

- English name: Me Cung Cave
- Vietnamese name: Động Mê Cung
- Kicker: The maze cave on the long route
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Boat on VHL2, then walk the chambers. A limestone garden sits above the cave (S51).
- How long on site: UNSOURCED
- How long including the ride: The passenger port’s route 2, which does not list this cave, is 5–6 hours for Ti Top, Sung Sot, and Luon (S5). VHL2 is a longer stop list (S48, S52). A clock for the full VHL2 list: UNSOURCED
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Boarding the port’s shorter route 2 and expecting Mê Cung. S5 lists Ti Top, Sung Sot, and Luon. S48 and S52 add Mê Cung.
- Notice: Ask which route 2 you are on.
