## What it is

- English name: Kayaking on the bay
- Vietnamese name: Chèo kayak trên vịnh
- Kicker: A paddle from a licensed pontoon
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: The tour boat stops. You take a kayak from a licensed pontoon and paddle among the limestone.
- How long on site: About an hour where the price is hourly (Cửa Vạn, Vung Viêng, Hòn Bàn Chân). Luồn and Ba Hang are priced per trip, not per hour (S47).
- How long including the ride from the base: The boat route. Route 2 is 5–6 hours (S5). Route 1 is 3–4 hours (S6).
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Assuming one unit. Luồn is per trip for two people. Cửa Vạn is per boat per hour (S47).
- Notice: The unit changes by pontoon.

## Fee

- Basic price: Luồn kayak 50,000 đồng a trip for two. Rowboat 30,000 đồng a person. Ba Hang kayak or rowboat 40,000 đồng a person. Cửa Vạn kayak 100,000 đồng a boat for an hour; bamboo boat 60,000 a person an hour. Vung Viêng kayak or bamboo boat 50,000 a person an hour; dragon boat 100,000 a person an hour. Hòn Bàn Chân kayak 50,000 a person an hour (S47).
- Included, no separate price: Lờm Bò, Hang Cỏ, Cống Đầm, Cống Đỏ overnight (S47).
- Child rule: The kayak page does not print one. The bay entrance exemptions are S50.
- Source: S47
