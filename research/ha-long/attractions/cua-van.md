## What it is

- English name: Cua Van floating village
- Vietnamese name: Làng chài Cửa Vạn
- Kicker: Raft houses on route 3
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Route 3 boat, about 20 km from either port, then a bamboo boat or a kayak among the raft houses (S54, S47).
- How long on site: The bamboo boat and the kayak are priced per hour (S47). A minute for the whole stop: UNSOURCED
- How long including the ride: Inside route 3. A route clock in hours: UNSOURCED
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Arriving for the cultural-centre exhibits. The centre stopped receiving visitors from 25 Apr 2024 (S55). On 2 Mar 2026 the province still described a restoration project (S56). Singing resumed in March 2026 (S56). The board place page still describes the centre as open (S54). The newer provincial page is the one used for the notice.
- Notice: Centre in restoration. Singing resumed March 2026.

## Fee

- Route 3 entrance 200,000 đồng a person (S49). One night on route 3, entrance 500,000; two nights 650,000 (S49).
- Bamboo boat 60,000 đồng a person an hour. Kayak 100,000 đồng a boat an hour (S47).
- Exemptions: S50
- Source: S47, S49, S50

## Hours

- Open: Check on arrival. Day tickets 06:30–18:30 (S49).
- Closed: Centre closed to visits from 25 Apr 2024 (S55). Village boats continued (S55).
- Source: S49, S55, S56
