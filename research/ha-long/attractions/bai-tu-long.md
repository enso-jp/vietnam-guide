## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Boat from Ao Tien or Cam Pha along one of ten routes, or a connector from Ha Long route 4 (S48, S57).
- How long on site: A boat day. A printed duration for one route: UNSOURCED

- Mistake that wastes the visit: Budgeting a Ha Long cave ticket and expecting this water, or assuming the April 2025 “no entrance fee” line and the November 2024 exemption page are the same rule.
- Notice: S57 says no entrance fee, boat and port only. S50 still sets exemption bands for Bai Tu Long. Both kept.
