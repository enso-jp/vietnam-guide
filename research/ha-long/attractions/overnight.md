## What it is

- English name: Overnight on the bay
- Vietnamese name: Nghỉ đêm trên vịnh
- Kicker (one line, what it is): A night aboard after the day boats
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Board at the Ha Long passenger port or Tuan Chau. Sleep on the boat after the day routes. One night is 24 hours from departure. Two nights are 48 hours (S49).
- How long on site: One night, 24 hours from departure (S49).
- How long including the ride from the base: The 24 hours include the time on the water (S49).
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Paying the 600,000–800,000 đồng average (S7) and assuming it is the same number as the bay entrance (S49).
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Two prices. Cabin average 600,000–800,000 đồng a person, inclusions not stated (S7). Entrance: route 2 one night 550,000, two nights 750,000; routes 3 and 4 one night 500,000, two nights 650,000 (S49).

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: Average 600,000–800,000 đồng a person (S7). Entrance figures above are a different instrument (S49).
- Child, height, or senior rule: Free under 1.2 m or under 7. Half price for a Vietnamese citizen 60 or over. Twenty percent off from age 7 to under 16 (S50).
- What the basic ticket includes: S7 does not say what the average includes. S49 is the bay entrance, not the cabin.
- What costs extra: Kayak at Lờm Bò and Cống Đỏ is inside the overnight tour (S47).
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S7, S49, S50, S47
