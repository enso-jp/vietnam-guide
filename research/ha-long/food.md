## Food streets and markets

### Bai Tho walking street
- Vietnamese name: Phố đi bộ Bài Thơ
- Kind: food-street
- What the visitor does: Walks Long Tiên and Lê Quý Đôn. The opening reports say the street passes Chợ Hạ Long 1 and a tourist boat landing, and that people sell seafood and street food (S28, S29). Công luận adds OCOP stalls. Dân trí says about 50 stalls at the opening, including OCOP and street food (S28).
- When to go: 18:00–24:00 on Friday and Saturday (S28, S29). Công luận also says holidays and Tết (S29). Dân trí does not (S28). Both stay.
- Area: The 2023 reports say phường Bạch Đằng (S28, S29). Clause 42 of the 2025 resolution put Bạch Đằng into phường Hồng Gai (S4). Both the old ward and the new ward stay. Nearly 400 m, 17 tổ, more than 500 households (S29). Dân trí does not print the length.
- Suggested slug: bai-tho-walking-street
- Photo must show: Long Tiên or Lê Quý Đôn closed to cars, stalls, and the mountain or the pagoda in the same frame. A plate with no street fails.
- unpublished: no licensed photo
- Sources: S28, S29, S4
