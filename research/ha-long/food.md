## Comprehensive list of local food to try

### Cha muc Ha Long
- Vietnamese name: Chả mực Hạ Long
- What it is: The tourism pages name it as the Hạ Long squid cake, one of 50 specialties recognized by Trung tâm Sách kỷ lục Việt Nam, served on the cruise boats and at provincial banquets (S23). The Tết article calls it a dish of Quảng Ninh cooking and a gift (S24). A recipe, a species beyond mực, and a texture word were not on the pages that loaded. Do not add them.
- How to eat it: Hot from the pan, with bánh cuốn, xôi trắng, or cơm (S22). Also named as xôi chả mực (S20).
- When to order it: On the boat (S23). At Tết, on the family table and as a gift (S24). A breakfast clock was not printed.
- Area, not a shop: The boats, and the Hạ Long table. Not a named stall.
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none. No `cha-muc` slug yet.
- Photo must show: a plate of the fried squid cakes, whole pieces, with the rice sheet or the rice in the same frame. A sauce cup filling the frame fails.

- Sources: S20, S22, S23, S24

### Bun cu ky Ha Long
- Vietnamese name: Bún cù kỳ Hạ Long
- What it is: Named on the provincial food list beside chả mực (S20). Báo Quảng Ninh, 13 Mar 2019, describes the bowl: a small animal the size of a rock crab, little body meat, big claws. Claw meat is seared with onion and garlic. The cracked claws go in the bowl. The broth is the body. Shops add bề bề and shrimp. The meat is sweet and does not taste like crab (S41).
- How to eat it: As that bowl (S41).
- When to order it: Morning, with bún bề bề, and still at lunch in Bãi Cháy and Giếng Đồn (S41).

### Bun be be
- Vietnamese name: Bún bề bề
- What it is: Morning and midday bowl. Three to five bề bề, broth of the shrimp plus tôm, cua, and ghẹ. Firm, fragrant, sweet, not fishy (S41).

### Banh cuon cha muc
- Vietnamese name: Bánh cuốn chả mực
- What it is: Hot rice sheets with minced pork and wood ear, eaten with chả mực instead of chả quế or chả thịt (S40, S41). Some rolls add shrimp (S40, S41).

- Price: 30,000–55,000 đồng on 13 Mar 2019 (S41). About 40,000 đồng on 16 Mar 2021 (S40). Both stay. Current price Check on arrival.
- Area: Daytime markets (S41). Alley by rạp Bạch Đằng (S40). VuiFest, phường Bãi Cháy (S45).

### Home-style seafood meal
S43, 9 Jul 2015, one meal. Each named method is its own plate. The Hải Hà story in that article is not Hạ Long.

- Sò huyết nướng, or luộc làm lông for 2–3 minutes.
- Ghẹ hấp or luộc with a little beer or water. Ghẹ lột xào lá lốt or rang muối.
- Cù kỳ nướng. Not the noodle.
- Ruốc lỗ luộc with lá ổi, lá mít, lá dâu da xoan, or lá chùm mũm.
- Mực ống luộc or hấp. Extra plate: xào cần tây, hành, tỏi tươi.
- Tôm nướng, hấp, or luộc ít nước. No species.
- Bề bề hấp or rang muối. Not the noodle.
- Hà xào măng. Bông thùa xào su hào. Separate plates in one sentence.
- Soup slot: canh ngao ta or ngao tây nấu chua, canh cá song, or riêu hà.
- If those soups are skipped: canh rạm mướp with rau đay or mùng tơi, cà pháo muối mắm tôm, and cá thu một nắng rán giòn with the rice.
- Lẩu, broth soured with me, chay, tai chua, or cà chua. Usual seafood: mực, tôm, cá song, ngao, hà, ghẹ or cua. Ends with bánh đa or mì tôm. Mì tôm is not a Hạ Long specialty.
- Cá bớp nấu lá lốt, one fish to a bowl, at the Cái Dăm table in thành phố Hạ Long.
- Rượu ngán, three mixes, in the same article.
- No price on any of these. Check on arrival — the price is UNSOURCED. The methods are sourced.
- unpublished: no licensed photo
- Sources: S43
