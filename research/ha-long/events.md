## Events and to-do

### Ha Long Carnival
- Vietnamese name: Carnaval Hạ Long
- Kind: festival
- When: The province calls it annual, for the summer (S26). The opened years do not share one clock. 2024: 20:10 on 28 Apr, bãi tắm Công viên Đại Dương, đường Võ Nguyên Giáp, phường Bãi Cháy, theme “Bừng sáng cùng Kỳ quan” (S25). 2025 plan, written 12 Mar 2025: 01 May at Quảng trường Sun Carnival and Võ Nguyên Giáp, about 90 minutes, broadcast 20:10, theme “Kết nối Di sản, Tiên phong tỏa sáng” (S26). 2026, written the morning of 30 Apr: 18:30–22:30 that night, Trần Quốc Nghiễn from Công viên Lán Bè to Quảng trường 30/10, phường Hạ Long, theme “Kỳ quan bừng sáng – Kỷ nguyên mới”, about 80,000 people, entry vào tự do (S27). The next solar date: Check on arrival.
- Where: The shore changes. 2024 and the 2025 plan use the Bãi Cháy beach road (S25, S26). 2026 uses Trần Quốc Nghiễn and Quảng trường 30/10 in phường Hạ Long (S27).
- What happens: A street parade and a stage show. 2026 adds a free public parade at 18:30 and a stage from 20:00 to 22:30 (S27). 2024 was described as the first carnival performed on the water as well as the sand (S25).
- Fee: 2026 says vào tự do (S27). A ticket for 2024 and 2025 was not printed on the pages opened. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED for any other year.
- Not the same as attraction slug: sun-world, bai-chay, or the bay. The square is an event site.
- Sources: S25, S26, S27

### Beer and squid-cake festival
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội bia và chả mực
- Kind: festival
- When: The tourism page says 2024 was the first time, over the 30 Apr–1 May holiday, at Công viên Đại Dương (S23). A later date was not opened. Next date: Check on arrival.
- Where: Công viên Đại Dương, phường Bãi Cháy, on that page (S23).
- What happens: The page says visitors were seated, looked at a record squid cake, and were served chả mực and beer free (S23).
- Fee: Free on that 2024 account (S23). Do not extend the free line to another year.
- Not the same as attraction slug: cha-muc is a dish, not this festival. Sun World is the complex next to the ocean park, not this event.
- Sources: S23

### Long Tien pagoda festival
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội chùa Long Tiên
- Kind: festival
- When: The cluster page says the festival is held when Tết arrives (S10). A lunar day was not printed. Check on arrival.
- Where: Chùa Long Tiên, under Núi Bài Thơ, phường Hồng Gai on that page (S10).
- What happens: The page says Buddhists and visitors come to the pagoda (S10). It does not describe a procession.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: long-tien. The pagoda is the building. This is the Tết gathering.
- Sources: S10
