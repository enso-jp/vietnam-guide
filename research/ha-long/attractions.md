### Ha Long Bay
- Vietnamese name: Vịnh Hạ Long – quần đảo Cát Bà, on the UNESCO page. The English name is Ha Long Bay - Cat Ba Archipelago
- Kind: zone
- Area: The property sits in Quảng Ninh and Hải Phòng (S1). This page is the Quảng Ninh harbor and the water you enter from it. `cat-ba` keeps the Hải Phòng shore.
- What the visitor does: Rides a boat among limestone towers. UNESCO calls it the most extensive marine-invaded tower karst, fengcong clusters and fenglin towers, with three cave types (S1). The Hạ Long Bay Management Board runs the Hạ Long side. Cát Bà National Park and the Cat Ba Bays board run the other side (S1).
- Figures, all kept: English synthesis 65,650 ha, 1,133 islands and islets, buffer 34,140 ha (S1, S2). Other language blurbs on the same UNESCO page say about 1,600 islands (S1). Do not pick one count. Inscribed 1994. Boundary changes 2000 and 2023. Criteria (vii) and (viii) (S1). National scenic site 1962. Special national monument 2009 (S1).
- The property point N20 49 58 E107 9 40 is not a city pin (S2, S35).
- Bái Tử Long stays inside this water. The January 2026 patrol sentence says the bay includes it (S32). It is not a new slug.
- Time: A day boat, or a night on board. Route clocks are below.
- Fee: The opened tariff is in `getting-there.md`. Do not print one number.
- From the base: This is the base.
- When to go: A clear morning for the towers. June–September fog and rain are the airline’s note for Vân Đồn, not a boat calendar (S13). A closure list was not opened.
- Mistake to avoid: Using the pearl-farm coordinate, a cave, or a beach as the city. Using a Cát Bà frame as this harbor.
- Local interest: The boat and the towers
- Suggested slug: ha-long
- Suggested dot: 5 — the night is the bay
- Photo must show: towers and water, subject through the center. The hero file does.
- Licensed photo: yes. Commons `Halong_Bay_in_Vietnam.jpg`, Thomas Hirsch / User:Ravn, CC BY-SA 3.0 (S37)
- Sources: S1, S2, S32
