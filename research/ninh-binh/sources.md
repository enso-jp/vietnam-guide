# Sources — Ninh Binh

Accessed 21 Oct 2026 unless a line says otherwise.

### S1
- URL: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1438/
- Publisher: UNESCO World Heritage Centre
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: Trang An Landscape Complex in Ninh Binh province; towers up to 200 m; rice, water, sampans rowed by local guides; inscribed 2014; property 6,226 ha; buffer 6,026 ha; three protected areas including Hoa Lu Ancient Capital and Trang An–Tam Coc–Bich Dong.

### S2
- URL: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1438/maps/
- Publisher: UNESCO World Heritage Centre
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: Property coordinates N20 15 24 E105 53 47. These are the landscape, not the railway station. The public map pin was not moved.

### S3
- URL: https://sodulich.ninhbinh.gov.vn/vi/huong-dan-tham-quan-du-lich/huong-dan-tham-quan-du-lich-2302.html
- Publisher: Ninh Binh Department of Tourism (Sở Du lịch tỉnh Ninh Bình)
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: Page dated 1 Aug 2025. Merger of Ninh Binh, Nam Dinh, and Ha Nam. Buses from Giap Bat, Nuoc Ngam, and My Dinh, about 1.5–2 hours. Expressway Phap Van – Cau Gie – Cao Bo – Mai Son, or Highway 1A. Trains through main stations. Taxi, xe om, or motorbike from the provincial center. Season notes. Cho Rong, Cho Bau, and central streets for buying specialties. Office at 06 Trang An street, Hoa Lu ward. Phone 0229.3871.263.

### S4
- URL: https://sodulich.ninhbinh.gov.vn/vi/tai-nguyen-du-lich-tu-nhien/tam-coc-bich-dong-22.html
- Publisher: Ninh Binh Department of Tourism
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: Page dated 14 Aug 2025. Tam Coc–Bich Dong in Nam Hoa Lu ward, about 10 km from the provincial center, inside Trang An. Boat on the Ngo Dong.

### S5
- URL: https://sodulich.ninhbinh.gov.vn/en/news-events/ninh-binh-province-focuses-on-developing-eco-tourism-350.html
- Publisher: Ninh Binh Department of Tourism, citing baoninhbinh.org.vn
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: Article dated 13 Aug 2020. Homestays then concentrated in Hoa Lu and Gia Vien districts, around Trang An, Tam Coc, Hang Mua, and Van Long. District names are pre-2025.

### S6
- URL: https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/guide-boat-tours-ninh-binh
- Publisher: Vietnam Tourism (vietnam.travel), contact@vietnamtourism.gov.vn
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: About 100 km from Hanoi. The destination people mean is west of the city: Tam Coc and Trang An, UNESCO 2014. Van Lam pier. Tam Coc round trip about two hours, caves Ca, Hai, and Ba. Trang An about 20 minutes from Tam Coc. Life jacket. Avoid Tet crowds. Foot-rowing mentioned as the local technique.

### S7
- URL: https://www.vietnam.travel/places-to-go/northern-vietnam/ninh-binh
- Publisher: Vietnam Tourism
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: “Ha Long Bay on Land.” About two hours from Hanoi by train or road. Ninh Binh station. Buses daily; a van can include the transfer to Tam Coc. Cycling if staying in Tam Coc; otherwise motorcycle tour or taxi. Best time April–June; rice golden around Tam Coc in late May; autumn cool and clear. Sampans at the Tam Coc and Trang An docks, rowed with the feet.

### S11
- URL: https://sodulich.ninhbinh.gov.vn/en/travel-tips/useful-information-208.html
- Publisher: Ninh Binh Department of Tourism
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: Page dated 4 Dec 2016. Highways 1A, 10, 12A, 12B, and the north–south railway. From the city center: car, taxi, motorbike rental, self-drive, bicycle. Life jackets on cave boats. ### S16
- URL: https://www.vietnam.travel/things-to-do/10-must-do-activities-ninh-binh
