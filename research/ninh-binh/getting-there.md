# Getting there — Ninh Binh

Checked: 21 Oct 2026

### Air

- Nearest airport people actually use: Noi Bai International Airport, Cảng hàng không quốc tế Nội Bài, IATA HAN, ICAO VVNB, in Noi Bai Commune, Hanoi. The airport’s English page says it is open 24 hours (S10).
- Airports Corporation of Vietnam’s list of airports it runs does not include a Ninh Binh airport. The international list is Tan Son Nhat, Noi Bai, Da Nang, Vinh, Cat Bi, Phu Bai, Cam Ranh, Phu Quoc, Can Tho, and Lien Khuong (S9).

### Getting around on site

- From the provincial center: taxi, xe om, or a motorbike, and the department says the sights are not far (S3). The 2016 page also lists a car, a rented motorbike, a self-drive car, and bicycles (S11).
- Vietnam Tourism says a motorcycle tour or a taxi is the usual way around, and that cycling suits people staying in Tam Coc (S7).
- Tam Coc–Bich Dong is about 10 km from the provincial center (S4). Minutes from Ninh Binh station to that ward: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The 10 km figure is not a station-to-pier time.
- Trang An is about 20 minutes from Tam Coc on the national tourism boat guide only (S6).
- On the water, Vietnam Tourism says wear a life jacket (S6). The 2016 department page says the same, and says follow the rower through the caves (S11).
- Late arrivals: SE3 reaches Ninh Binh at 21:36 and SE1 at 00:03 (S8). Those nights are a city bed, then a ride out in the morning. The minute count of that ride is still unsourced.

### Season limit on the journey

- The department’s 2025 guide says the climate is tropical monsoon: hot and humid from May to October, dry and cold from November to April, and that the province can be visited any time (S3). Ideal on that page is lunar months 1–3 for spring temples and festivals, and late May to early June for ripe rice at Tam Coc, Trang An, Giao Thuy, and dam Vuc (S3).
- Vietnam Tourism’s place page says best time April to June, rice golden around Tam Coc in late May, and autumn cool and clear. It also says hot humid summers and cool drizzly winters (S7). The two pages do not name the same window. Keep both.
- The 2016 department page says rain is concentrated from May to November (S11). It also prints an annual rainfall figure this pass does not trust and does not copy.
- A flood closure, a boat stoppage, or a month when the expressway is the only sensible road: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Vietnam Tourism says avoid Tet if you do not want the boat crowds (S6). Tet’s civil dates move. Check on arrival.
