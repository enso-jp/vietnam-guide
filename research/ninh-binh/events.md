### Ninh Binh Tourism Week
- Vietnamese name: Tuần Du lịch Ninh Bình
- Kind: festival
- When: The department calls it annual (S23). On 17 Apr 2026 the organizing conference said the 2026 week was expected to run about 10 days at the end of May, at provincial scale (S24). A 21 Apr 2026 page says the same late-May window and does not print the days (S23). The English recap that search results quoted as opening on 23 May did not load on a direct fetch. The day list is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The 2027 dates are Check on arrival.
- Where: Opening planned at Dinh Tien Hoang Emperor Square, Hoa Lu ward (S23, S24). The conference also names Tam Coc–Bich Dong, Trang An, Tam Chuc, Hoa Lu ward, and Hai Thinh commune (S24).
- What happens: Sắc vàng Tam Cốc, the Trang An Forestival music program, a Yosakoi festival, a beach-tourism opening, a “Mùa vàng Tam Cốc” photo tour, an exhibition, and chèo, xẩm, and water puppetry (S23, S24). The 2024 edition’s department page puts that year’s opening at 20:00 on 1 June at Dong Gung bus station and does not, on the page opened here, print the rest of that week’s dates (S20).
- Fee: The 1 June 2024 opening was free (S20). A 2026 ticket was not on these pages. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Not the same as attraction slug: trang-an, tam-coc, hoa-lu
- Sources: S20, S23, S24

### Golden fields at Tam Coc
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Sắc vàng Tam Cốc
- Kind: festival
- When: Inside the tourism week, which the April 2026 pages put in late May (S23, S24). The department’s visitor guide separately says late May to early June is ripe-rice season for photography at Tam Coc and Trang An (S3). Vietnam Tourism says late May, and also prints a best-time line of April to June (S7). The festival day and the field day are not the same sentence. Both stay. The 2027 dates are Check on arrival.
- Where: Tam Coc, along the Ngo Dong (S23). The photo tour uses the same name (S24).
- What happens: A river festival in the ripe rice, with folk singing, plus a photography outing (S23, S24).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Not the same as the boat ticket.
- Not the same as attraction slug: tam-coc
- Sources: S3, S7, S23, S24

- Kind: festival

- What happens: Spring temple visits and festivals. The guide does not describe the rites.

### A bicycle day
- Vietnamese name: none on the page
- Kind: activity
- When: Daylight, any time the department says the province can be visited. The rice picture is the late May window above (S3, S7).
- Where: The lanes of Nam Hoa Lu ward (S7).
- What happens: Cycling between the fields if the night is in Tam Coc. This is the ordinary day, already under Lifestyle. It is listed here so the events file is not only festivals.
- Fee: Bicycle hire was not priced on these pages. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Not the same as attraction slug: tam-coc-bicycle-day
