### Hoa Lu festival
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Hoa Lư, lễ hội Trường Yên
- Kind: festival
- When: The provincial page says the 8th through the 10th of lunar month 3, every year, and a large festival every three years (S7). The heritage page says the 10th of lunar month 3 (S6). Both stay. Do not print a single day. A 1997 account on the provincial page times the water procession at about giờ Thìn on the 9th (S7). That is one year’s account on the same page, not a replacement for the 8th-through-10th line. Solar date: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Where: Xã Trường Yên, the two temples, and the ground the page lists with them: chùa Nhất Trụ, đền Phất Kim, động Am Tiên, and the caves it names (S6, S7).
- What happens: Mộc dục at giờ tý on the opening day. Rước nước from sông Hoàng Long on the morning of the opening day, usually giờ Thìn. Dâng hương at both temples. Cờ lau. Tế nữ quan. Cửu khúc at sân rồng, which the page says lapsed after 1945, was taken up again by a women’s troupe in 1994, and was restored for a men’s troupe by the culture department in 2017 (S7). The heritage page’s shorter sentence is a gathering on the 10th for Đinh Tiên Hoàng and the kings (S6).
- Fee: The ordinary temple ticket is in `attractions.md` (S5). A festival surcharge was not printed. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Not the same as attraction slug: hoa-lu. The temples are the ground. This is the lunar gathering.
- Photo: `unpublished: no licensed photo` of the procession or the yard on that day. S31 is the tomb on an ordinary day.
- Sources: S5, S6, S7
