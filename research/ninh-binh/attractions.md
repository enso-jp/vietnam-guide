# Attractions — Ninh Binh

Slug: `ninh-binh`
Province: Ninh Binh. The department’s visitor guide of 1 Aug 2025 says the new province joined the former Ninh Binh, Nam Dinh, and Ha Nam (S3). The night people book is still the karst west of the provincial center, not every town in that new province.
Kind: city
Corridor: northern-vietnam
Base a traveler sleeps in: the hamlets around Tam Coc, in Nam Hoa Lu ward, about 10 km from the provincial center (S4, S6, S7). A place slug `tam-coc` already exists. This dossier does not absorb that town.
Checked: 21 Oct 2026

## Why a night here

### The karst west of the city

- The picture they expect: Limestone towers standing over rice and water, and a sampan on that water. UNESCO describes forest-mantled towers up to 200 m, deep green forest, grey rock, blue-green water, and green and yellow rice, with visitors carried in sampans rowed by local guides (S1). Vietnam Tourism calls the same picture “Ha Long Bay on Land” and says that when people say Ninh Binh the destination, they mean the nature region west of Ninh Binh city: Tam Coc and the Trang An complex, inscribed in 2014 (S6, S7). The Trang An eco-tourism area sits in the middle of that region, Tam Coc to the south, Hoa Lu to the north (S6).
- Where they sleep, as an area: The hamlets around Tam Coc. Vietnam Tourism says cycling suits people staying in Tam Coc, and that a van from Hanoi can include the transfer there (S7). The department’s Tam Coc page of 14 Aug 2025 places Tam Coc–Bich Dong in Nam Hoa Lu ward, about 10 km from the provincial center, inside the Trang An landscape (S4). A 13 Aug 2020 department article, still on the same site, says homestays were concentrated in the old Hoa Lu and Gia Vien districts, around Trang An, Tam Coc, Hang Mua, and Van Long (S5). Those district names predate the 2025 ward. Do not print a hotel brand. The city is the practical bed only when the train arrives after dark.
- Where the evening is: Back in those hamlets, or in the city after a late train. No department or national-tourism page in this pass named a bar street or a current walking street. The 2025 guide names Cho Rong, Cho Bau, and the central streets as places to buy specialties (S3). That is a shopping note, not proof of a night walk. A 4 Dec 2016 department page says restaurants often run 07:00–22:00 and tells visitors not to stay out late in empty places (S11). The page is old, and it names no street. Boat closing time: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No park page in this pass gave an hour.
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): Van Lam pier, where Tam Coc tickets are sold, then the boat on the Ngo Dong through the caves the national site names Ca, Hai, and Ba (S4, S6). The Trang An boat country, which the same page puts about 20 minutes from Tam Coc (S6). That minute count is one publisher. Tam Coc–Bich Dong itself, Nam Hoa Lu ward (S4). Pins, not a ranked list.
- Hero must include: A limestone tower, the rice or the water, and a sampan, in one frame. The tower has to keep its top. UNESCO’s height is up to 200 m, so the frame needs sky above the rock, not a ribbon of water (S1). Subject through the center: the boat, or the base of the tower where it meets the field.
- A crop that would fail: Water with the cliff tops cut off. A cave interior used as the town hero. A portrait of a statue. Empty sky, empty paddy with no rock, or a food stall.

### Trang An boat
- Vietnamese name: Khu du lịch sinh thái Tràng An
- Kind: zone
- Area: Central part of the landscape, about 20 minutes from Tam Coc on one national-tourism page (S6)
- What the visitor does: Takes a sampan through caves and stops at temples. Vietnam Tourism describes three routes: one of 3–4 hours, one of 2–3 hours, one of about 3 hours (S6). The same publisher’s activity list says the boat rides last two to three hours (S16). UNESCO’s picture is the sampan under forested towers (S1).
- Time: 2–4 hours depending which of those lines is used (S6, S16). The ride from a Tam Coc bed is the 20-minute figure, one publisher only (S6).
- Fee: Adult over 1.3 m, 250,000 VND, child 1 m to 1.3 m 120,000 VND, guide 300,000 VND per tour. Both the 2020 department list and the 2025 center list print that adult and child pair, and both say the boat is included (S13, S14). The 2020 list adds children under 1 m free (S13). The 2025 list adds visitors over 60 at 120,000 VND (S14). A 16 Jan 2017 English department page instead says children under 1.4 m pay 100,000 VND (S17). Keep the 2020 and 2025 pair as the later department-family figures, and do not drop the 2017 child line.
- From the base: About 20 minutes from Tam Coc (S6). Minutes from Ninh Binh station: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The department says late May to early June is the ripe-rice window at Trang An (S3).
- Mistake to avoid: Treating the 250,000 VND ticket as Hoa Lu, Mua Cave, or Bai Dinh. Those are separate phones on the support list (S15).
- Local interest: The long boat
- Suggested slug: trang-an
- Suggested dot: 5 — the zone the night is for. This is a new row. The place dot stays 4.
- Photo must show: A sampan, the water, and a tower with its top in the same frame. A cave interior alone fails as the hero.
- Sources: S1, S6, S13, S14, S16, S17

### Tam Coc boat
- Vietnamese name: Tam Cốc – Bích Động
- Kind: sight
- Area: Nam Hoa Lu ward, about 10 km from the provincial center (S4). Tickets at Van Lam pier (S6).
- What the visitor does: Rows the Ngo Dong through the three caves the national site names Ca, Hai, and Ba, with rice and karst on the banks (S4, S6). The rower uses the feet (S6, S7). The 2025 center list titles the route Tam Coc – Thai Vi Temple – Tien Cave – Bich Dong Pagoda (S14).
- Time: About two hours for the round trip (S6). The activity list says two to three hours (S16).
- Fee: The pages do not match. 2020 department list: landscape 120,000 VND per adult (under 1.4 m 60,000) plus a boat of 150,000 VND per trip, four domestic passengers or two international, with surcharges for an extra person (S13). 2025 center list: 250,000 VND adult without the electric car, 350,000 VND with it; children 1–1.3 m 120,000 or 190,000 (S14). Do not publish one of these as the fare.
- From the base: The hamlets are the base. The pier is the start. Station to pier: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: Late May to early June for ripe rice (S3, S7). Morning, before the heat the department’s older tip assumes. Hour: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake to avoid: Paying a second “official” price that is only the other year’s table. Also, a life jacket is required on the cave boats (S6, S11).
- Local interest: The short boat
- Suggested slug: tam-coc
- Suggested dot: 4 — already on the Tam Coc place record. Do not change it.
- Photo must show: The sampan on the Ngo Dong, rice or water, and a karst with its top. Not the ticket booth.
- Sources: S4, S6, S7, S13, S14
- Page: do not open a second file. Place slug `tam-coc` already exists.

### Mua Cave viewpoint
- Vietnamese name: Hang Múa
- Kind: sight
- Area: The Tam Coc side. A 2020 department article places Hang Mua with Tam Coc in the old Hoa Lu district (S5). A street was not on the department fee page.
- What the visitor does: Climbs to a terrace over the valley. Vietnam Tourism says 500 steps and a view of the river, paddies, and limestone (S7, S16). The cave itself is not the picture that page sells.
- Time: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No department page gave a minute count.
- Fee: 100,000 VND per adult on the 2020 department list, and children under 10 free on that same list (S13). The 2025 center list prints 100,000 VND per person and does not repeat the child line (S14). Vietnam Tourism’s activity article prints 200,000 VND (S16). Keep both. Do not average.
- From the base: Bicycle or taxi from the Tam Coc hamlets. Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The rice window is the same late May to early June note (S3).
- Mistake to avoid: Budgeting the hour for the cave mouth. The stair and the terrace are the visit.
- Local interest: The view over the rice
- Suggested slug: mua-cave
- Suggested dot: 4 — already on the record. Do not change it.
- Photo must show: The terrace or the stair, with karst and fields behind, in one landscape frame. A tight crop that cuts the valley fails. A portrait of a dragon stair with no landscape fails.
- Sources: S5, S7, S13, S14, S16

### Hoa Lu
- Vietnamese name: Cố đô Hoa Lư
- Kind: sight
- Area: North of the Trang An eco-tourism area (S6). About 20 minutes by car from Trang An on the activity article only (S16).
- What the visitor does: Walks the old capital. UNESCO places Hoa Lu here in the 10th and 11th centuries (S1). Vietnam Tourism says two surviving structures, brick courtyards, and banyan trees (S16). The boat guide also says the 9th and 10th centuries in one sentence (S6). Use the UNESCO centuries, and do not hide the other line.
- Time: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Fee: Adult 20,000 VND. Child 6–15, and people 60 or older, 10,000 VND. The 2020 list and the 2025 list agree. The 2020 list also prices disabled visitors at 10,000 VND (S13, S14).
- From the base: Car from Tam Coc or from the city. The 20-minute figure is from Trang An, not from the station (S16).
- When to go: Lunar months 1–3 are the department’s spring-temple window, which names Bai Dinh in that list rather than Hoa Lu (S3). Hoa Lu’s own hour: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake to avoid: Assuming the Trang An boat ticket includes this gate (S15 lists a separate conservation center).
- Local interest: The old capital
- Suggested slug: hoa-lu
- Suggested dot: 3 — already on the record. Do not change it. A history stop beside the boat day, not the picture people came for.
- Photo must show: A temple gate or courtyard with the brick and a tree, in color. A cropped torso of a statue fails.
- Sources: S1, S6, S13, S14, S16

### Bai Dinh Pagoda
- Vietnamese name: Chùa Bái Đính
- Kind: sight
- Area: UNESCO’s buffer zone includes the reconstructed pagoda complex (S1). A street was not on the fee pages.
- What the visitor does: Walks a large modern complex. Vietnam Tourism’s place page calls it the largest Buddhist temple complex in the country, with cave temples reached by 300 steps and a new area of 500 hectares (S7). The activity article instead says the largest in Southeast Asia, and names thousands of statues and a 13-story tower (S16). The two superlatives disagree. Do not pick one.
- Time: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The activity article says the site needs a lot of time and does not give a number (S16).
- Fee: 2020 department list: electric car 60,000 VND per person, stupa (Bảo tháp) 50,000 VND, guide 300,000 VND per tour (S13). 2017 English department page: electric car 30,000 VND, stupa 50,000 VND, guide 300,000 VND (S17). The 2025 center list did not yield a clean adult figure in this fetch; it did print a note that children under 1.0 m are free and children over 1.3 m count as adults (S14). Do not invent the missing adult number. Hours: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- From the base: Taxi from Tam Coc or the city. Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: The department names Bai Dinh among the lunar month 1–3 temple visits (S3). Gate hour: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake to avoid: Treating the electric-car price as the whole visit, or assuming the boat ticket covers it.
- Local interest: The big pagoda
- Suggested slug: bai-dinh
- Suggested dot: 3 — already on the record. Do not change it. Scale, not the karst picture.
- Photo must show: The complex in its valley, with more than one roof, so the size reads. A single statue face, cropped, fails.
- Sources: S1, S3, S7, S13, S14, S16, S17

### Bich Dong Pagoda
- Vietnamese name: Chùa Bích Động
- Kind: sight
- Area: Inside the Tam Coc–Bich Dong visit (S4, S14)
- What the visitor does: Crosses a stone bridge over a lotus pond and climbs to temples on the mountain. Vietnam Tourism says the upper temples date to the 18th century, and that shoulders and knees should be covered (S16).
- Time: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. It sits on the Tam Coc route rather than as its own published duration (S14).
- Fee: Included in the Tam Coc route name on the 2025 list (S14). A separate gate price was not printed there. The 2020 list splits Thach Bich–Thung Nang and a cave route, which are not this pagoda’s own adult price. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED as a standalone ticket.
- From the base: With the Tam Coc visit (S4).
- When to go: Summer for the lotus the activity article describes (S16). Month of the bloom is that sentence only.
- Mistake to avoid: Giving it a full extra day when it is a stop on the Tam Coc side.
- Local interest: The pagoda in the cliff
- Suggested slug: bich-dong
- Suggested dot: 3 — a stop on the Tam Coc day, not a day by itself
- Photo must show: The gate, the bridge, and the pond in one frame. A stair with no gate fails.
- Sources: S4, S14, S16
- Page: do not give it its own page yet. Cover it on the Tam Coc place.

### Van Long
- Vietnamese name: Khu bảo tồn thiên nhiên đất ngập nước Vân Long
- Kind: sight
- Area: The old Gia Vien district, with Tam Coc and Hang Mua, on the 2020 homestay article (S5). Current ward name: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- What the visitor does: Takes a rowboat on the wetland. Vietnam Tourism names Delacour’s langur and the black-faced spoonbill as the wildlife people come for (S7). The activity article adds birdwatching and a limestone wall (S16).
- Time: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. A 2017 department tour sketch is a company’s day program, not the public visit length, so it is not used.
- Fee: 2020 department list: landscape 20,000 VND adult, boat 50,000 VND per trip (S13). 2025 center list: entrance 100,000 VND per person, and the boat line did not come through in this fetch (S14). 2017 English page: landscape 20,000 VND, boat 60,000 VND per boat for two tourists (S17). All three stand. Do not pick.
- From the base: Taxi from Tam Coc or the city. Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake to avoid: Assuming the Trang An sampan is this wetland.
- Local interest: The quiet wetland
- Suggested slug: van-long
- Suggested dot: 4 — a different morning from the two busy boats. New row.
- Photo must show: The boat, the marsh, and the limestone wall in one frame. A bird checklist with no landscape fails.
- Sources: S5, S7, S13, S14, S16, S17

### Ninh Binh city
- Vietnamese name: Ninh Bình
- Kind: hub
- Area: The provincial center, about 10 km from Tam Coc–Bich Dong (S4)
- What the visitor does: Changes from the train or the bus, then rides out. The station is the arrival (S7, S8). Cho Rong, Cho Bau, and the central streets are where the 2025 guide says people buy specialties (S3). This is not the picture they came for.
- Time: A junction, not a sightseeing day.
- Fee: None recorded for the city itself.
- From the base: The ride back from Nam Hoa Lu ward. Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: Late trains. SE3 arrives 21:36 and SE1 at 00:03 (S8).
- Mistake to avoid: Sleeping here and expecting the rice outside the window. The fields are the Tam Coc hamlets (S6, S7).
- Local interest: The arrival
- Suggested slug: ninh-binh-city
- Suggested dot: 2 — the junction. The place dot stays 4 for the landscape, not for these streets.
- Photo must show: Do not use the city as the hero. If a frame is needed, the station front with people and bags, not a hotel sign.
- Sources: S3, S4, S6, S7, S8
- Page: no separate page. The place file is this hub.
