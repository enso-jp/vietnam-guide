## Facts

- xã Gia Hưng, huyện Gia Viễn. About 15 km from the Hoa Lu capital, about 20 km by road north of the city. Open valley of about 16 mẫu, one entrance about 30 m high, đầm Cút outside. Also Thung Lau and Thung Ông. National historic relic 1996. Source: tourism page (A1).
- A promotion page (dulichninhbinh.com.vn/item/1335) describes 240 steps. Not used on the public page because A1 does not give a step count.
