## What it is

- English name: Cuc Phuong National Park
- Vietnamese name: Vườn quốc gia Cúc Phương
- Kicker: Vietnam's first national park
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Gate at xã Cúc Phương, Nho Quan, then a forest trail. A botanical loop is about 3 km. Động Người Xưa is a cave on the same grounds. Night cart is for guests staying in the park (S5).
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- How long including the ride from the base: Distances only. 45 km northwest of the city, or 15 km to Bái Đính then 26 km (S37). Minutes UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating the 60,000 đồng gate as the primate, turtle, or carnivore visit.
- Notice: Rescue programs are extra (S5).

## Fee

- Basic price: Adult 60,000 đồng. Student 20,000 đồng. Pupil or child 10,000 đồng (S5).
- Extras: Primate adult 80,000 / reduced 30,000. Turtle, carnivore, pangolin adult 60,000 / reduced 30,000. Package with gate, primates, turtles, and a cart: adult 220,000, student 100,000, child 90,000, under 6 free. Fuller package 320,000 / 170,000 / 160,000. Night cart, overnight guests: adult 150,000, child 6–10 100,000, under 6 30,000, minimum 600,000 under five people (S5).
- Hotline on the support list: 02293 848 006 (S9).
- Source: S5, S9

## Getting there

### From Ninh Binh

- Mode: Road through Tràng An to Bái Đính, about 15 km, then 26 km. Alternate: Quốc lộ 1A to Gián Khẩu, about 10 km, then Quốc lộ 12A. The Nho Quan road is under repair (S37).
- Time: UNSOURCED
- Source: S37

### From Hanoi

- Mode: About 120 km south. Bus leaves Giáp Bát for the park at 15:00 and leaves the gate for Giáp Bát at 09:00 (S37).
- Time: UNSOURCED beyond the bus clocks.
- Source: S37

## Area

- Listed area: Decision 72/TTg of 7 July 1962 sets 25,000 ha (S38). Wikipedia prints 20,000 ha for that decision and 22,200 ha for the park, and ranks it a national park in 1966 (S43). The conservation page and the park history page print 22,408 ha and date the national park to 7 July 1962 (S44, S45). Public copy uses 22,200 to 25,000 ha and both years. Botanical walk about 3 km and Động Người Xưa are on S43.
