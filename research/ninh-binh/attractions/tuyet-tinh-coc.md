## What it is

- English name: Tuyet Tinh Coc
- Vietnamese name: Tuyệt Tịnh Cốc (Động Am Tiên)
- Kicker: Cave temple beside the old capital
- Kind: sight
- One site. The ticket title is Động Am Tiên (Tuyệt Tịnh Cốc). Spelling on the ticket uses Tịnh, not Tình. Do not publish a second `am-tien` slug.

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: From the east gate of đền Đinh, about 400 m, then 205 stone steps to a mouth on the cliff. Most of the cave opens onto a flooded valley. The hall is a pagoda for the Buddha and Nguyễn Minh Không and a temple for Dương Vân Nga (S6).
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Mistake: Paying only the 20,000 đồng temple ticket and expecting the cave.
