- UNSOURCED as a separate ticket. The Hoa Lu line is 20,000 đồng and does not name this pagoda (ticket board, 23 Oct 2026).

## Hours

- UNSOURCED. Peace ceremony on the 15th of lunar month 1 on the English page (A3).

## Facts

- About 300 m north of the Le temple, thôn Yên Thành, xã Trường Yên. Pillar dated 995. Chữ Đinh hall, five front bays, three rear. National relic 1998 (A1).
- National treasure, Decision 2382/QĐ-TTg, 25 Dec 2015. Six fitted parts of the pillar (A2). Court over 3,000 m2 (A3).
