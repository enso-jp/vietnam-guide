## What it is

- English name: Hai Vong Dai
- Vietnamese name: Hải Vọng Đài (S14 also writes Vọng Hải Đài)
- Kicker: Lookout over the lagoon and the sea
- Kind: sight
- Separate visit: The park treats it as its own trail, 800 m of granite from biệt thự Morin, the easiest trail, any age (S7, re-read 23 Oct 2026). It is on the same ticket and the same summit road as the villas and the falls. It would be its own page if a licensed photo of the platform, the path, and the lagoon or the sea existed. None was found. The walk stays inside the national-park page. It is not given its own public record.

## What the visitor does

- Walk the granite and look over forest, the lagoon, and the sea (S7). A bronze bell sits here (S12, S13).
- How long on site: 800 m once you are at Morin (S7). From km 12, about 7 km on foot (S1). Gate to summit about 4–5 hours (S12).
- Mistake: Reading 1,430 m, 1,440 m, 1,450 m, and higher than 1,400 m as one surveyed point (S7, S10, S12, S13, S14).
