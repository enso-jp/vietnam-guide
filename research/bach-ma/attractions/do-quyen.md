## What it is

- English name: Do Quyen Waterfall
- Vietnamese name: Thác Đỗ Quyên
- Kicker: Waterfall off kilometre 16
- Kind: sight
- Separate visit: Yes. The park trail page is its own walk from km 16 (S8). It uses the park ticket. It is not a second gate. Ngũ Hồ can be combined with it on a 3.3 km walk (S10), and the dossier still treats the fall as its own trail. The card stops at the hero because a second frame of this cliff was not found.

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Leave km 16, walk to the lip, and go down the steps if you have the legs. Red đỗ quyên on the rocks and the stream in spring. Headwater of one of the two main branches of the Hương (S8, re-read 23 Oct 2026).
- How long on site: About 1.5 km and about 30 minutes (S8 and S13 agree). Nearly 700 steps down (S8, S13).
- How long including the ride from the base: Road minutes from the gate — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. From the viewpoint, about 3 km of road then about 2 km of path (S12). That 2 km and the 1.5 km from km 16 both stand.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: One height. Higher than 300 m (S8), about 300 m (S10), nearly 400 m (S13).
- Notice: Be back at the visitor center before 17:00 (S1). A zip-line was surveyed and not built (S15). Do not treat a 2024 rope sentence in S13 as an open ride.
