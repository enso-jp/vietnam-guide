The night on the summit. Not a second page for the viewpoint, and not a hotel name.

## What it is

The summit villas the trails use as starts. The pages name Morin, Kim Giao, Đỗ Quyên, and nhà Bảo An (S7, S9, S12). Sleep here only if the park accepted a registration at least one day ahead (S1, re-read 23 Oct 2026).

## Who built them

Print every story. Public copy states the figures and does not name the sites.

- VnExpress, 7 Mar 2025: Raoul Desmarest, March 1933, about 139 villas on the mid-slope (S14).
- VnExpress handbook, 27 Nov 2024: M. Girard, 1932. Complete in 1942 from 1,200 m to a 1,440 m summit, with nearly 139 villas (S13).
- Park-site feature: 139 villas finished in 1942 (S12).
- Vietnam Airlines: Girard in 1932, 139 villas by 1937, a forest reserve in 1937, a South Vietnam protected area in 1962, national park in 1991 (S18). The 1962 line is this page only.

## Fee and hours

- Room rate: Check on arrival. Newspaper rates are not on the May 2025 tariff and are not adopted (S13).
- Park ticket, print both: adult 65,000 VND and reduced 25,000 VND on the 1 May 2025 table (S2, re-read 23 Oct 2026). Adult 60,000 VND and reduced 20,000 VND on VnExpress, updated 4 Feb 2025 (S13).
- Day visitors return to the visitor center before 17:00 (S1). A summit night needs the day’s registration (S1).
- Campfire, re-read 23 Oct 2026 on S2: large fire 500,000 VND from 19:30 to 22:00. Small fire 300,000 VND. The dong cells did not load on 22 Oct 2026. They loaded on this pass, and they match the Vietnam Airlines table dated 1 May 2024 (S18). Prices exclude a tax invoice. The small fire has no hour in the text that loaded.
- The hour the summit goes quiet after 22:00: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
