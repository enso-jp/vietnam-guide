## What it is

The summit villas the trails use as starts. The pages name Morin, Kim Giao, Đỗ Quyên, and nhà Bảo An (S7, S9, S12). Sleep here only if the park accepted a registration at least one day ahead (S1).

## Who built them

Print every story.

- VnExpress, 7 Mar 2025: Raoul Desmarest, March 1933, about 139 villas on the mid-slope (S14).
- VnExpress handbook, 27 Nov 2024: M. Girard, 1932. Complete in 1942 from 1,200 m to a 1,440 m summit, with nearly 139 villas (S13).
- Park-site feature: 139 villas finished in 1942 (S12).
- Vietnam Airlines: Girard in 1932, 139 villas by 1937, a forest reserve in 1937, a South Vietnam protected area in 1962, national park in 1991 (S18). The 1962 line is this page only.

## Fee and hours

- Room rate: Check on arrival. Newspaper rates are not on the May 2025 tariff and are not adopted (S13).
- Park ticket, print both: adult 65,000 VND and reduced 25,000 VND on the 1 May 2025 table (S2). Adult 60,000 VND and reduced 20,000 VND on VnExpress, updated 4 Feb 2025 (S13).
- Day visitors return to the visitor center before 17:00 (S1). A summit night needs the day’s registration (S1).
- Campfire on the tariff: large fire 19:30–22:00. The dong cell did not load (S2). Price: Check on arrival.

## Getting there

- This is the summit base. From the gate, the road in `getting-there.md`.
- From 1 Jan 2026, motor vehicles stop at km 12: about 500 m across the slip, then about 7 km on foot (S1).
- 13 Jun 2026: about 600 m on a path around the slip, then a motorbike or more walking (S15).
- May 2025 shuttle table still prices a ride to the summit (S3). Confirm which rule is in force.
- From a booked night, Hải Vọng Đài is 800 m from Morin (S7).
