## What it is

- English name: Tri Sao Trail
- Vietnamese name: Đường mòn Trĩ Sao, thác Trĩ Sao
- Kicker: Lower-road walk to pools
- Kind: sight
- Separate visit: Yes, in the dossier. The 2024 trail page puts a sign about 5 km up from the gate, then 2.5 km on foot (S11). The December 2025 reopening note mentions thác Trĩ Sao at km 8 (S1, re-read 23 Oct 2026). A day from the gate can stop here without the km 12 walk. It is the next real split after the summit road. It stays unpublished because no licensed photo of the pool or the fall was found.

## What the visitor does

- Walk through open forest to falls and clear pools. Swimming is described (S11). A pheasant is the name of the trail, not a promised sighting.
- Time: about 2.5 km after the 5 km drive (S11). Minutes — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Fee: the park ticket. Check on arrival. No separate price on S11.
- Hours: the park day, back before 17:00 (S1). Weekly close — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Multi-day link from rừng Chò đen: groups of 4–6, overnight (S10). Duration — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
