## What it is

- English name: Bach Ma National Park
- Vietnamese name: Vườn quốc gia Bạch Mã
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Cloud forest on one summit road
- Kind: zone
- Separate visit: This is the ticket and the summit road. Hải Vọng Đài, thác Đỗ Quyên, Ngũ Hồ, and the villas sit on that one ticket. They are not five gates. Đỗ Quyên still has its own trail from km 16, so that walk has its own card. The viewpoint and the five pools do not, because no licensed photo of those subjects was found.

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Report at the visitor center, take the summit road, then one trail. The viewpoint is 800 m of granite from Morin. Đỗ Quyên leaves km 16. Ngũ Hồ leaves near Kim Giao. Do not budget the three as one hour.
- How long on site: A day from the gate, or a night if the viewpoint is the morning. A required minimum in hours — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- How long including the ride from the base: Gate to summit about 16 km, about 4–5 hours on foot if you start before 09:00 (S12). Drive minutes from Hue, Lang Co, and Da Nang — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Counting on a car past km 12, or turning at the Trúc Lâm sign.
- Notice: One ticket. From 1 Jan 2026 motor vehicles stop at the slip at km 12.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: Check on arrival. Park table re-read 23 Oct 2026, in force from 1 May 2025: adult 65,000 VND; child, pupil, student, and senior 25,000 VND (S2). VnExpress, updated 4 Feb 2025: adult 60,000 VND, reduced 20,000 VND (S13). Both figures. Do not average.
- Child, height, or senior rule: S2 names child, pupil, student, and senior, with a student card or an identity card for a senior. S12 adds “60 and over” on the 65,000 / 25,000 pair. S13 does not spell the reduced band in the sentence used here beyond the 20,000 VND figure.
- What the basic ticket includes: The road, the viewpoint, and the trails. No separate viewpoint or waterfall price was on the trail pages (S7, S8, S9).
- What costs extra: Vietnamese guide 500,000 VND a day, 30 percent more on a holiday. English guide 700,000 weekday and 900,000 holiday (S2, re-read 23 Oct 2026). Prices exclude a tax invoice.
- Source: S2, S12, S13.

## Hours

- Open: Visitor center before 09:00, back before 17:00 the same day (S1, re-read 23 Oct 2026). Vietnam Airlines prints 07:30–17:00 on a table dated 1 May 2024 (S18). Both clocks. Do not pick one.
- Last entry, if it differs: The 09:00 line is the report, not a last-entry clock. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED for a last-entry time.
- Closed, weekly or seasonal: Weekly close — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. A summit night needs a registration at least one day ahead (S1).
- Source: S1, S18.

## Getting there

### From the gate

- Mode: Walk, or a park shuttle if that list is the rule in force. From 1 Jan 2026, motor vehicles cannot pass the slip at km 12: about 500 m on foot, then about 7 km to Hải Vọng Đài (S1). On 13 Jun 2026 the director said about 600 m on a path around the slip, then a motorbike or more walking (S15). The May 2025 shuttle table still prices a 16-seat at 1,200,000 VND in a day and 1,500,000 VND overnight (S3).
- Time: Gate to summit about 4–5 hours on foot (S12). Drive minutes — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Fare, if any: The ticket above. Shuttle figures in S3. Which rule is in force — Check on arrival.
- Source: S1, S3, S12, S15.

### From Hue and Da Nang

- Hue: 40 km (S12, S14, S18) and 45–55 km (S13). Do not average.
- Da Nang: about 40 km to chợ Cầu Hai then about 1 km (S12); airport about 60 km (S13); 70 km (S18). Different endpoints. Do not average.
- Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Coordinates: not used. The camera positions on the trail photos are not a surveyed park pin.

## Season

- March–December, called the drier window as Huế travel advice (S12). Not a park climate table.
