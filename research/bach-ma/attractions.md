# Research dossier — Bach Ma

Slug: `bach-ma`
Province: The park’s July 2025 note says the expanded park sits on thành phố Huế and thành phố Đà Nẵng (S4). The February 2012 note still splits the same decision into Thừa Thiên Huế 34,380 ha and Quảng Nam 3,107 ha (S5). The visitor-center line on the current pages is xã Phú Lộc, thành phố Huế (S1, S2). Keep both administrative sentences. Do not file the mountain under `hue`, `da-nang`, or `lang-co`.
Kind: area
Corridor: north-central
Base a traveler sleeps in: The summit villas, if the park has accepted a registration made at least one day ahead (S1). Or thị trấn Cầu Hai, at the gate, which Thanh Niên describes as the night before the mountain (S16). Not a hotel brand. Not a Huế riverside room and not a Lăng Cô beach resort.
Checked: 22 Oct 2026

## Why a night here

- The picture they expect: Cloud forest at the viewpoint, with the granite path or the bell, and the lagoon and the sea in the same look. Hải Vọng Đài is the frame: forest, sea, and the lagoon, mountain wind and sea wind together (S7, S12). It is not the Huế citadel, not the Lăng Cô sand, and not a Đà Nẵng beach.
- Where they sleep, as an area: The summit, in the villas the park still uses, after a day’s notice (S1). The 2021 feature on the park site names biệt thự Đỗ Quyên, biệt thự Kim Giao, and nhà Bảo An, and the two camps at km 17, Thông Nàng and Kim Giao (S12). If the slip makes the summit unrealistic, the night is thị trấn Cầu Hai at the gate (S16). No hotel brand as a pick.
- Where the evening is: On a day ticket there is no evening in the park. The notice sends visitors back to the visitor center before 17:00 (S1). A registered summit night is the villa and the campfire the tariff lists from 19:30 to 22:00 (S2). A bar street was not found.
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): Hải Vọng Đài, 800 m of granite from biệt thự Morin (S7). Thác Đỗ Quyên from km 16 (S8). Ngũ Hồ from biệt thự Kim Giao (S9). Chợ Cầu Hai, the turn at the foot (S12, S16).
- Hero must include: The viewpoint in color, forest in the near ground, and either the lagoon or the sea still readable, subject through the center. Mist can stay if the shore is still there. A granite villa is allowed only if the forest is in the same frame.
- A crop that would fail: Fog with no shore. A single rhododendron that loses the mountain. A villa facade with no trees. A plate of seafood with no lagoon. A portrait of the bell that cuts off the view.

### Bach Ma National Park
- Vietnamese name: Vườn quốc gia Bạch Mã
- Kind: zone
- Area: Xã Phú Lộc, thành phố Huế, on the current service pages (S1, S2). The July 2025 note puts the expanded park on thành phố Huế and thành phố Đà Nẵng (S4). The 2012 note still says Thừa Thiên Huế and Quảng Nam (S5)
- What the visitor does: Uses the park as the whole trip: the road to the summit, the viewpoint, the falls, the five lakes, and the gate market. It is not a museum with one room. Decision 214/CT of 15 Jul 1991 established it at 22,031 ha (S4, S5). Decision 01/QĐ-TTg of 2 Jan 2008 expanded it. The 2012 page prints 37,487 ha. The 2025 page prints 37,423.1 ha for that same decision (S4, S5). VnExpress prints “more than 37,400 ha” (S13). Vietnam Airlines prints “nearly 37,500 ha” (S18). Keep every figure. ASEAN recognition is October 2022 in the director’s speech as printed, and the certificate was handed over on 3 Dec 2025 (S6). The 2021 feature calls this the only primary-forest belt in Vietnam running from the East Sea to the Laos border (S12). The airline guide says the range reaches the Cambodian border (S18). Those are not the same border. Do not pick one.
- Time: A day if the walk from km 12 is the whole point. A night if the viewpoint is the morning. A required minimum — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Fee: Check on arrival. The park table updated 1 May 2025: adult 65,000 VND; child, pupil, student, and senior 25,000 VND (S2). The same 65,000 / 25,000 is on the 2021 feature, which adds “60 and over” for the reduced ticket (S12), and on the airline guide, which dates its table 1 May 2024 (S18). VnExpress, updated 4 Feb 2025, prints adult 60,000 VND and reduced 20,000 VND (S13). Do not pick one number.
- From the base: The base is the summit or the gate. See `getting-there.md`
- When to go: The 2021 feature calls March–December the drier window, as Huế travel advice (S12). The road has been cut at km 12 since the late October 2025 flood (S1, S15). White đỗ quyên was out in early March 2025 (S14). Confirm the road the day before
- Mistake to avoid: Treating the mountain as a neighborhood of Huế, Lăng Cô, or Đà Nẵng, or turning at the Trúc Lâm sign (S12)
- Local interest: The summit view
- Suggested slug: `bach-ma-national-park`
- Suggested dot: 5 — the frame of the trip, not a side gate on another city’s page
- Photo must show: forest, a path or a viewpoint, and the sea or the lagoon in one frame. A logo or a gate sign fails
- unpublished: no licensed photo

### Hai Vong Dai
- Vietnamese name: Hải Vọng Đài (the March 2025 report also writes Vọng Hải Đài)
- Kind: sight
- Area: Summit. The trail starts at biệt thự Morin (S7)
- What the visitor does: Walks the granite path and looks out over forest, the lagoon, and the sea. The park calls it the easiest trail, for any age (S7). The 2021 feature says the view takes in biển Lăng Cô and biển Cảnh Dương, and that a bronze bell sits here, with the highest summit above the bell (S12). VnExpress also names the bell and the two bays, plus đèo Hải Vân (S13)
- Time: The paved 800 m is a short walk once you are at Morin (S7). Getting to Morin is the long part. From km 12 the park says about 7 km on foot (S1). The full gate-to-summit walk on the 2021 feature is about 4–5 hours (S12)
- Fee: Check on arrival. The ticket is the park ticket in the zone entry. No separate viewpoint price was on the trail page (S7)
- From the base: From a summit night, 800 m from Morin (S7). From the gate, the road in `getting-there.md`
- When to go: Morning, before the cloud sits on the shore. Day visitors still have to be back at the visitor center before 17:00 (S1)
- Mistake to avoid: Reading 1,430 m, 1,440 m, and 1,450 m as one surveyed point. They are three sentences
- Local interest: The summit view
- Suggested slug: `hai-vong-dai`
- Suggested dot: 5 — the picture the night is for
- Photo must show: the platform or the path through the center, forest at the edge, and the lagoon or the sea still in frame. A crop of cloud with no shore fails. A tight portrait of the bell fails
- unpublished: no licensed photo
- Sources: S1, S7, S10, S12, S13, S14

### Do Quyen Waterfall
- Vietnamese name: Thác Đỗ Quyên
- Kind: sight
- Area: Off km 16 of the Bạch Mã road (S8). The combined walk with Ngũ Hồ also returns to km 16 (S10)
- What the visitor does: Walks to the lip of the fall. The park says it is a headwater of one of the two main branches of the Hương, and that red đỗ quyên flowers on the rocks and the stream in spring (S8). Going down is nearly 700 steps (S8, S13). A zip-line over the fall was surveyed as a road repair and was not built (S15). A separate “rope across the fall” from early 2024 is one sentence in VnExpress (S13). Do not treat that sentence as an open ride
- Time: Park trail page: about 30 minutes for about 1.5 km from km 16 (S8). The combined Ngũ Hồ route is about 3.3 km (S10). VnExpress agrees the 1.5 km is about 30 minutes and then says the Ngũ Hồ leg of similar length is nearly an hour (S13). Print both times
- Fee: Check on arrival. No separate fall ticket was on the trail page (S8)
- From the base: From the summit, the road down to km 16, then the path. The 2021 feature says about 3 km by road from the viewpoint to the trailhead, then about 2 km of path (S12). The park’s own trail page says about 1.5 km from km 16 (S8)
- When to go: With the summit morning, not as a second day’s drive. Spring if the red flowers are the point (S8). The 2026 return rule still ends at 17:00 (S1)
- Mistake to avoid: One height. The pages do not agree
- Local interest: The falls and the five lakes
- Suggested slug: `do-quyen`
- Suggested dot: 4 — the walk people add to the viewpoint, not a separate park
- Photo must show: white water, the rock face, and forest in one frame. A ribbon of water with no cliff fails. A flower close-up that loses the fall fails
- unpublished: no licensed photo
- Sources: S8, S10, S12, S13, S15

Heights and lengths, print every sentence.

- Park trail page, 1 Jul 2025: higher than 300 m; about 1.5 km from km 16; nearly 700 steps (S8)
- Park walking page, 1 May 2025: about 300 m; a 500 m spur off the Ngũ Hồ path; about 3.3 km for the whole combined walk (S10)
- Feature on the park site: about 2 km of trail after 3 km of road from the viewpoint (S12)
