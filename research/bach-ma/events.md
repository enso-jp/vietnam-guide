### Rhododendron season
- Vietnamese name: Mùa hoa đỗ quyên
- Kind: activity
- When: The park trail page says red đỗ quyên on the rocks and the stream in spring (S8). The director, in the 7 Mar 2025 report, says white đỗ quyên (lá nhọn) and đỗ quyên chuông flower in spring, and that report’s own flowers were out in early March 2025 (S14). Bell rhododendron is near the viewpoint. Red is at biệt thự Đỗ Quyên and along the streams (S14). The next civil date — Check on arrival. The park page does not print a festival
- Where: Summit forest, the stream to thác Đỗ Quyên, the garden of biệt thự Đỗ Quyên (S8, S14)
- What happens: People walk the existing trails while the flowers are out. To see the white ones up close, the report says you leave the path and pass ruined villas (S14). That is not a permit. Stay on the trail the park has opened
- Fee: The park ticket. Check on arrival, with both figures in `attractions.md`. No flower surcharge was found
- Not the same as attraction slug: `do-quyen` and `hai-vong-dai` are the places. This is the season
- Sources: S8, S14

- Fee: The park ticket. Check on arrival
- Not the same as attraction slug: `summit-villas`

### Bird calling
- Vietnamese name: Gọi chim, hướng dẫn viên Trương Cảm
- Kind: activity
- When: VnExpress says early morning and late afternoon (S13). A clock — Check on arrival
- Where: On the trails. The park sells the guide by the day (S2)
- What happens: The tariff names a bird-calling guide, Trương Cảm, at 700,000 VND on a weekday and 900,000 VND on a holiday (S2). VnExpress describes the same person answering birds by voice (S13). It is a hired walk, not a show with a stage
- Fee: Check on arrival for what is included in the ticket. The day-rate above is the tariff line (S2)
- Not the same as attraction slug: `tri-sao` is the pheasant trail. This is the guide
- Sources: S2, S13
