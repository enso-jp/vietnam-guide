# Getting there — Bach Ma

Checked: 22 Oct 2026

The usual previous stop is Huế, or Đà Nẵng if the traveler is already south of the Hải Vân. The night, when there is one, is the summit or the gate town at Cầu Hai. This file is the park road. It is not a Huế arrival and not a Đà Nẵng airport guide.

- Nearest airport: VnExpress names Phú Bài for Huế, about 35 km, and Đà Nẵng airport, about 60 km (S13). A second kilometre for either airport was not opened. Door-to-door minutes — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The same page prints a taxi from Phú Bài of about 500,000 VND one way for a group under five, and a self-drive up to about 1,000,000 VND a day (S13). Those are one newspaper. Do not publish them as the fare.
- Nearest rail station: The Vietnam Railways heritage train prints Lăng Cô between Huế and Đà Nẵng, and does not print Cầu Hai (S17). A passenger timetable for a Cầu Hai stop did not load. Whether a train sets down at the gate — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Do not invent a station clock.
- From the usual previous stop:
  - Huế to the park | The feature on the park site says 40 km (S12). The March 2025 flower report says about 40 km (S14). The November 2024 handbook says 45 to 55 km, depending on CT02 or National Highway 1A (S13). Vietnam Airlines says 40 km north of the park to Huế (S18). Record all of these. A single door-to-door minute — Check on arrival.
  - Đà Nẵng to the gate | The feature on the park site: National Highway 1A, over Hải Vân, about 40 km to chợ Cầu Hai, then about 1 km, left into Trần Đình Túc (S12). VnExpress puts Đà Nẵng airport at about 60 km and does not repeat the 40 km city figure (S13). Vietnam Airlines says Đà Nẵng is 70 km south (S18). These are three endpoints and three figures. Do not average them.
  - Lăng Cô | VnExpress says the park is about 30 km from the center of thị trấn Lăng Cô (S13). One paper. Drive minutes — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
  - The last kilometre | From chợ Cầu Hai, about 1 km, then left on Trần Đình Túc, on the park-site feature (S12). VnExpress says some coaches stay on Highway 1A and do not enter the mountain foot, so the traveler changes and rides more than 3 km (S13). The 1 km and the more-than-3 km start in different places. Print both. The green round park sign on the right is the turn. The sign for Thiền viện Trúc Lâm Bạch Mã is the wrong road (S12, S13).
  - Rail Huế–Đà Nẵng | The heritage operator says the ride is about 3 hours and prints two clocks on the same site (S17). Do not pick one.
- Getting around on site: The gate-to-summit road is about 16 km on the park-site feature, a walk of about 4–5 hours if you start before 09:00, or a car of 16 seats or fewer hired at the gate (S12). From 1 Jan 2026 the park’s own notice stops motor vehicles at the slip at km 12: walk about 500 m across it, then about 7 km on foot from km 12 to Hải Vọng Đài. Be at the visitor center before 09:00. Be back before 17:00 the same day. A summit night needs a registration at least one day ahead (S1). On 13 Jun 2026 the director told Báo Văn hóa the slip was still unrepaired: walk about 600 m on a path around it, then a motorbike or more walking (S15). The May 2025 shuttle table is still on the site and still prices a 16-seat, a 7-seat, and a 4-seat to the summit (S3). Four rules are in public at once. Confirm which one the gate is using. The 7-seat and 4-seat rows that loaded do not label which figure is the day and which is the night. Do not assign them.
- Season limit on the journey: The feature on the park site calls March–December the drier window, as Huế travel advice, not as a park climate table (S12). VnExpress says the summit mean is 19°C and the park mean is 25°C, and that one day can feel like four seasons (S13). The flower report says about 20°C at Vọng Hải Đài (S14). Báo Văn hóa, quoting the director in June 2026, says the rainfall is among the highest in the country and that the October 2025 flood destroyed the roadbed at km 12 (S15). No millimetre figure was on the pages that loaded. After heavy rain the road — Check on arrival.
