# Food — Bach Ma

Checked: 23 Oct 2026. The 22 Oct 2026 list stands. This pass added bánh ép and recorded what was read and left off the public specialties.

The mountain does not have a city noodle of its own. The local list is the lagoon at the gate. Huế bowls stay Huế. Lăng Cô’s beach dishes stay `lang-co`. No stall price is repeated here.

## Comprehensive list of local food to try

### Brick crab
- Vietnamese name: Cua gạch Cầu Hai
- What it is: Lagoon crab sold at chợ Cầu Hai. Thanh Niên describes a thin shell and red roe, and says Huế restaurants advertise “cua gạch Cầu Hai” (S16)
- How to eat it: Buy it at the market. The page does not print a recipe. Do not invent one
- When to order it: A market morning before the mountain (S16)
- Area, not a shop: Chợ Cầu Hai, facing the lagoon
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: Huế’s table, not a Bach Ma restaurant. `com-hen` and `bun-bo-hue` are the city, not this market
- Sources: S16

### Land shrimp and sour shrimp
- Vietnamese name: Tôm đất, and the Huế dish tôm chua made from it
- What it is: Thanh Niên says the land shrimp at this market is the ingredient of Huế tôm chua, eaten with boiled pork and pickled bean sprouts (S16)
- How to eat it: As the Huế plate of tôm chua, thịt luộc, dưa giá. The market sells the shrimp. The plated dish is the city’s
- When to order it: With the market morning, or later in Huế
- Area, not a shop: Chợ Cầu Hai for the shrimp. The plate is Huế
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: Huế. No `tom-chua` slug on the site yet
- Sources: S16

### Lagoon fish
- Vietnamese name: Cá kình, cá dìa, cá hanh, cá đối
- What it is: The brackish fish Thanh Niên lists at chợ Cầu Hai, caught in phá Tam Giang and đầm Cầu Hai (S16)
- How to eat it: The page says people buy them to cook, often at the weekend. It does not name a grill or a soup. A second species list was not opened. Do not add a cooking method
- When to order it: Market morning (S16)
- Area, not a shop: Chợ Cầu Hai
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: None on this mountain. Lăng Cô’s lagoon is the next bay, place slug `lang-co`, and it is not this list
- Sources: S16

### What the summit kitchens actually serve
- Vietnamese name: Phở, bún, mì bò, cơm đĩa, cơm phần
- What it is: VnExpress says the kitchens inside the park, allowed by the management, serve those breakfast bowls, a set rice, and grill or seafood if you order ahead (S13). This is a canteen list, not a local dish. The dong range on that page is not repeated
- How to eat it: Order ahead, inside the park (S13)
- When to order it: Breakfast before the trail, or the meal on a summit night
- Area, not a shop: The summit kitchens. No restaurant name from that page
- Meal: breakfast
- Neighbor dish to link: `pho` and `bun-bo-hue` are other cities’ bowls. Do not rename this canteen as either
- Sources: S13

### Hue-style coffee at the gate
- Vietnamese name: Cà phê Huế, as Thanh Niên tastes it in thị trấn Cầu Hai
- What it is: A thick coffee in the town before the market (S16). One essay. Not a roastery and not a cafe street
- How to eat it: In the morning, then walk to the market (S16)
- When to order it: Before 09:00, if the night was at the gate (S1, S16)
- Area, not a shop: Thị trấn Cầu Hai. No cafe name
- Meal: drink
- Neighbor dish to link: `ca-phe` only as the drink, not as a Bach Ma brand
- Sources: S16

### Banh ep Cau Hai
- Vietnamese name: Bánh ép Cầu Hai, also bánh ép mắm nêm Cầu Hai on the CafeBiz account
- What it is: A pressed cake from the Cầu Hai lagoon in Phú Lộc. CafeBiz, Phùng Hà, 29 Jun 2022, says tapioca starch, lean pork, shrimp, and spring onion, pressed on a round iron mold over charcoal (S19). Đại Việt Tourist, Hằng Min, does not agree with that account or with itself. The 15 Aug 2024 page says the filling is pork, not the beef jerky, egg, or meat used elsewhere, and does not mention shrimp (S20). The 19 Apr 2025 page says a thin crisp cake with a pork filling (S20). Do not pick one filling
- How to eat it: CafeBiz says eat it hot, roll it like bánh cuốn with lettuce, cucumber, split water spinach, banana blossom, herbs, and bean sprouts, and dip it in mắm nêm with garlic and chili (S19). The 15 Aug 2024 tour account says cucumber, lettuce, and rau răm, with mắm nêm (S20). The 19 Apr 2025 account says nước mắm with garlic and chili (S20). Both dips stay. Do not choose one
- When to order it: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No hour was printed
- Area, not a shop: The lagoon at Phú Lộc. CafeBiz says about 45 km south of Huế. The shop profiled in that article is `do not publish as a pick`
- Meal: snack
- Neighbor dish to link: Bánh ép Thuận An is the comparison in S19, not this cake
- Sources: S19, S20

## Food streets and markets

### Cau Hai market
- Vietnamese name: Chợ Cầu Hai
- Kind: food-street
- What the visitor does: Buys lagoon seafood on the highway side of the lagoon, then goes to the gate. The park-site feature calls it the famous market and the landmark for the left turn into Trần Đình Túc (S12). Thanh Niên puts it about 40 km south of Huế, facing the lagoon, and describes the rows of fish, crab, and vegetables (S16)
- When to go: Morning. The essay’s sequence is coffee, then the market, then the mountain (S16). A clock for the market — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Area: Highway 1A at Cầu Hai, xã Phú Lộc
- Suggested slug: `cau-hai-market`
- Photo must show: the market sheds, the lagoon or the mountain behind them, and fish or crab still readable. A single plate with no place fails
- unpublished: no licensed photo
- Sources: S12, S16

The summit kitchens are not a street. They stay in the dish list above (S13).
