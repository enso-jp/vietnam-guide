# Attraction research — Ben Duoc Temple

## What it is

- English name: Ben Duoc Temple
- Vietnamese name: Đền Tưởng niệm Liệt sĩ Bến Dược
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Memorial temple on the Bến Dược ground
- Kind: sight

The operator lists the temple as its own page, and the Bến Dược tunnel page says the forest path ends at this hall (A1, A2). It is on the Bến Dược ground. It is not a stop invented from one crawl, and it is not the Cao Đài temple in Tây Ninh.

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: ticket gate at the forest edge on the Bến Dược visit, then the tunnels and bunkers, then the memorial (A2). At the memorial: three-gate entrance, stele house, main hall, nine display spaces, nine-floor tower, garden (A1, A3).
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No minute count on the temple page or the tunnel page.
- How long including the ride from the base: the Bến Dược ride is about one hour from the city centre (S5). On-site minutes for the hall: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: booking Bến Đình, on Tỉnh lộ 15, and expecting this hall. The hall is on the Bến Dược ground (A1, A2).
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: the hall is on the Bến Dược visit, after the forest path.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: the memorial has no price of its own on the temple page (A1). The Bến Dược visit is ticketed at the forest gate (A2). Foreign visitors, operator notice of 19 Jan 2026, from 1 Feb 2026: historical-site admission 35,000 đồng a person a visit, and a service-and-guide ticket 100,000 đồng a person a visit (S3, rechecked 23 Oct 2026). The notice does not add them. The guide line had been 90,000 đồng.
- Child, height, or senior rule: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED on the operator notice. Visit HCMC prints half price for students and children 7–16, and free entry under 7, for people with disabilities, for the armed forces, and for revolution contributors, beside its own 70,000 đồng international figure (S12, rechecked 23 Oct 2026). That 70,000 đồng stays in this file. It is not the 2026 operator notice. It is not printed on the public temple page.
- What the basic ticket includes: the Bến Dược tunnel page takes the visitor through the ticket gate and on to this hall (A2). A line that says the 35,000 đồng excludes the hall was not printed.
- What costs extra: incense is named, with no price (S4, A2). Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Shooting, boats, and paintball stay on the tunnel file.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S3, A1, A2, S4, S12.

Area conflict, kept here, one figure on the page: the heritage inventory gives the temple 40,299.3 m² (A3, S11). The operator temple page says the temple was built on a plot of 7 ha (A1). 7 ha is 70,000 m². Both stay. The public page uses the inventory figure. Do not average them.

Theme wording, both stay: the operator titles the nine spaces “Sài Gòn – Chợ Lớn – Gia Định kiên cường, bất khuất” (A1). The heritage page titles the displays “Sài Gòn - Gia Định kiên cường bất khuất”, also nine sections (A3). The public page uses the operator’s title.

## Hours

- Open: 7:00–17:00. The temple page prints no clock (A1). The relic gate is 7:00–17:00 every day, including Saturday, Sunday, and Tết, on the operator’s 29 Oct 2021 notice (S4, rechecked 23 Oct 2026). Visit HCMC and the 23 Apr 2024 press page print the same daily gate (S12, S13).
- Last entry, if it differs: not printed. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Closed, weekly or seasonal: no weekly close on S4. After 17:00 the grounds are quiet except on a Trăng chiến khu night.
- If a board was an image and could not be read, say so: the hours are in the 2021 notice text, not a photo of a board.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S4, A1.

## Getting there

### From the city centre

- Mode: road. Bus 13 from Bến Thành or 94 from Chợ Lớn to bến xe Củ Chi, then 79 to Bến Dược (S6, S12). Self-drive: Trường Chinh, ngã tư An Sương, Hóc Môn, then Tỉnh lộ 15 (S6).
- Time: about one hour to Bến Dược (S5). The 70 km figure does not split the gates (S13). Visit HCMC says approximately 70 km northwest and also puts Provincial Road 15 and Phú Hiệp together (S12).
- Fare, if any: taxi 600,000–700,000 đồng a trip, no gate named (S12). Bus fare: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S5, S6, S12, S13.

### From Bến Đình

- A minute between the gates was not printed. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Bus 70 is the Bến Đình bus, not this hall (S6, S12).

- Coordinates, only if a source prints them: the heritage page prints no latitude (S11). Commons camera locations on temple photos (about 11.146, 106.460) are geotags, not an official pin. They are not on the public record.
