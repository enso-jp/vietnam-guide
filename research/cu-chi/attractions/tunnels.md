## Getting there

### From the city centre

- About one hour to Bến Dược, no kilometre on that note (S5). About 70 km, gates unsplit (S13). Taxi 600,000–700,000 đồng, no gate (S12).
- Bus to Bến Dược: 13 or 94, then 79. The 2014 note names 79 “Củ Chi – Dầu Tiếng” (S6). Visit HCMC sends 79 to Bến Dược and does not use that name (S12). Both stay.
- Bus to Bến Đình: 13 or 94 to An Sương, 122 to Tân Quy, then 70 (S6, S12).
- Bus fare and bus minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Coordinates: none on the heritage page (S11). No pin.
