# Attractions — Cu Chi

Slug: `cu-chi`
Vietnamese name: Củ Chi
Province: Thành phố Hồ Chí Minh. The old unit was huyện Củ Chi. From 1 Jul 2025 the commune governments in Nghị quyết 1685 operate, and the district level does not (S14).
Kind: area
Corridor: southeast
Base a traveler sleeps in: the operator’s homestay and tents on the river at Địa đạo Bến Dược, or a return the same day to Ho Chi Minh City (`ho-chi-minh-city`). No hotel brand.
Checked: 22 Oct 2026

This is the tunnel ground and the old district around it. Ho Chi Minh City already has `ho-chi-minh-city`. Do not make a second city page. The new xã Củ Chi is clause 123 only: Tân Phú Trung, Tân Thông Hội, and Phước Vĩnh An (S14). Bến Dược is in what is now xã An Nhơn Tây. Bến Đình is in xã Nhuận Đức. They are address lines inside this place. They are not new slugs.

No opened page prints a latitude and longitude for Củ Chi the place. Do not use a tunnel entrance, a district office, or a bounding-box midpoint as the pin.

## Why a night here

### The tunnel ground

- The picture they expect: the earthen tunnel. Either the entrance in the ground, lid and path included, or the tunnel ground itself, earth on both sides and overhead, a person only if the bore stays in the frame. The published hero file `public/photos/cu-chi.jpg` is a thatched roadside stall in Phước Thạnh on Quốc lộ 22, credit Fumihiko Ueno, CC BY 3.0. The tunnels are not in that frame. It fails. Phước Thạnh is now inside xã Thái Mỹ (S14). It is not Bến Đình and not Bến Dược.
- Where they sleep, as an area: the operator’s homestay and tents on the Sài Gòn riverbank at Địa đạo Bến Dược (S5, S8). The heritage inventory also names a hotel-and-restaurant area inside the relic grounds, with no brand (S11). Many visits are a day from the city. The night that is printed is this riverbank, not a town hotel strip. No hotel brand.
- Where the evening is: the Bến Dược grounds after the 17:00 gate, on a Trăng chiến khu night, and the riverbank at the homestay (S4, S5, S7, S8). A bar street was not named on the operator pages, the tourism-center page, or the 23 Apr 2024 press page.
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices):
  - Địa đạo Bến Dược, the night ground and the memorial. Operator: http://diadaocuchi.com.vn/ (S1).
  - Địa đạo Bến Đình, the other conserved site, on Tỉnh lộ 15. Same operator homepage (S1). It is a pin for the second site, not a second copy of Bến Dược’s fee.
  - The homestay on the river at Bến Dược. Operator notice: http://diadaocuchi.com.vn/dich-vu-homestay-va-thue-leu-trai-388.html (S5).
- Hero must include: the tunnel entrance or the tunnel ground. Earth through the center. If a person is in the crawl, the bore stays around them.
- A crop that would fail: the Phước Thạnh stall, a pig, an unrelated house, empty sky, or a face with the earth cropped off. The file `public/photos/cu-chi-tunnels.jpg` shows a crawl with earth around the person. A tight crop on the face would fail. That file does not say whether the bore is Bến Đình or Bến Dược.
- unpublished: the place hero is the wrong subject. No licensed frame in the repo shows a named entrance at Bến Đình or at Bến Dược.

## Attractions (Sight, area of interests, zone, hub, etc.)

### Địa đạo Bến Dược
- Vietnamese name: Địa đạo Bến Dược. The operator also calls it the căn cứ of the Khu ủy and the Bộ Tư lệnh Quân khu Sài Gòn – Chợ Lớn – Gia Định (S1). The heritage bureau splits that ground into Khu A, the military command, and Khu B, the party committee (S11).
- Kind: sight
- Area: ấp Phú Hiệp. The operator’s Vietnamese homepage prints xã Ân Nhơn Tây (S1). The same site’s footer and the 2026 ticket notice print xã An Nhơn Tây (S3). The English homepage still prints Phu Hiep Hamlet, Phu My Hung Commune, Cu Chi District (S2). Clause 126 put the old xã Phú Mỹ Hưng into the new xã An Nhơn Tây (S14). All of those address lines stay. Do not collapse them into one line.
- What the visitor does: Walks the tunnels and the bunkers where the regional leadership worked (S1). The heritage inventory for Khu A is 66,586.4 m² and lists a meeting room, spike pit, command bunkers, a field hospital and surgery bunker, a Hoàng Cầm kitchen and canteen, a tailor bunker, a weapons workshop, a bomb crater, and a souvenir house (S11). Khu B is 16,664.8 m², with its own Hoàng Cầm kitchen, clinic, rice store, and the party secretary’s bunker (S11). A separate pair of tunnel mouths toward the Sài Gòn river is 667.9 m² (S11). Protection zone II around Khu A is about 55,947.7 m² and around Khu B is 9,530.2 m² (S11). On the same ground the visitor can enter Đền Tưởng niệm Liệt sĩ Bến Dược, the liberated-zone reconstruction, the East Sea lake with the three miniature monuments, and a live-fire range (S4, S11, S12).
- Memorial, both area figures stay: the heritage page gives the temple 40,299.3 m², started 19 May 1993, stage 1 opened 19 Dec 1995, with a basement of 9 display sections under the title of Sài Gòn – Gia Định (S11). Visit HCMC does not print that area.
- Liberated zone, both area figures stay: Visit HCMC prints 38.5 hectares for the reenactment and three periods, 1961–1964, 1965–1968, and 1969–1972 (S12). The heritage page prints 197,633.5 m² for a larger cluster: the liberated-zone reconstruction plus the East Sea mockup plus the Cedar Falls sand-table house (S11). The mockups were started 24 May 2008 and opened 19 Dec 2009 (S11). These are not the same boundary. Do not convert one into the other.
- Network length, all three stay: the heritage bureau says the tunnels are “dài hơn 200km”, with about 500 km of trenches on the surface (S11). The 23 Apr 2024 press page says “khoảng gần 250km” (S13). Visit HCMC says the system “spreads over an area of more than 250 km²” (S12). One is a length, one is a shorter length, one is an area. Do not pick a single kilometre.
- Depths, both stay: the press page says the top level is 3 m down, the middle 6 m, and the bottom 12 m (S13). Visit HCMC says 3 m, 6 m, and deeper than 12 m (S12). The heritage page says some stretches have two or three levels and does not print those metres (S11).
- Formation, both stays: the heritage page says about 1948, earliest at Tân Phú Trung and Phước Vĩnh An (S11). Visit HCMC says 1946–1948, then a rapid build from 1961 to 1965 (S12).
- Crawl: Visit HCMC says a widened visitor section of about 120 meters on 2 levels (S12). No crawl length was printed on the operator pages opened. Do not use 120 m as the length of the wartime network.
- Time: the gate day is the visit. A minute count for the walk was not printed. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Fee, foreign visitors, operator notice of 19 Jan 2026, from 1 Feb 2026: historical-site admission 35,000 đồng a person a visit, and a service-and-guide ticket 100,000 đồng a person a visit (S3). The same notice says the service ticket had been 90,000 đồng (S3). The notice does not print a combined total. Do not write 135,000.
- Fee, the single international number on two other pages: Visit HCMC prints 70,000 đồng for an international visitor, with no effective date on the page (S12). The 23 Apr 2024 press page prints the same 70,000 đồng (S13). That number is not the operator’s two-line 2026 notice. Both presentations stay.
- Fee, Vietnamese adults: Visit HCMC and the 23 Apr 2024 press page both print 35,000 đồng (S12, S13). The 2026 operator notice does not print a Vietnamese ticket (S3).
- Fee, children, one page: Visit HCMC prints 50 percent for students and children 7–16, and free entry under 7, for people with disabilities, for the armed forces, and for revolution contributors (S12). The press page prints no child band (S13).
- Fee, the 2005 decision, its own date: from 1 Jan 2006, 15,000 đồng an adult and 7,000 đồng a child (S19). This is not the 2026 notice. The 2009 decision’s fee table was not in the extract. No 2009 number.
- Fee, liberated zone: Visit HCMC prints 65,000 đồng a person (S12). The operator notice does not (S3). Both stay, which means the 65,000 is one page’s figure.
- Fee, shooting, boats, paintball, bicycles: the operator names them and prints no price (S4). Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Visit HCMC names AK-47 and AR-15 at the range and says those activities need a separate ticket, with no price (S12).
- Fee, homestay: the operator says the rate is ordinary and prints no number (S5, S8). Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- From the base: this is the night base when someone sleeps here (S5). From the city, see `getting-there.md`. The homestay page’s “khoảng 1 giờ” is this site (S5). The 70 km lines are not split to this gate alone except where a page merges Phú Hiệp into one “Cu Chi Tunnels” address (S12, S13).
- When to go: the gate on the operator’s 2021 notice and on the 2025 homestay page is 7:00–17:00, and the 2021 notice includes Saturday, Sunday, and Tết (S4, S5). Visit HCMC and the 2024 press page print the same 7:00–17:00 every day (S12, S13). The night program is later. See `events.md`.
- Mistake to avoid: using one fee, one address, or one kilometre for Bến Dược and Bến Đình. Using the Phú Nhuận line on the operator homepage. That line is 87A Trần Kế Xương, phường 7, quận Phú Nhuận, a 1955–1958 liaison office (S1). It is not this ground.
