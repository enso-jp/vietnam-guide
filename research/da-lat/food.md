# Food — Da Lat

Checked: 21 Oct 2026

## Comprehensive list of local food to try

### Vietnamese coffee
- Vietnamese name: Cà phê
- What it is: Arabica country around Cau Dat and farm tours on the slopes (S1, S23).
- How to eat it: Hot or iced at a cafe after the lake walk.
- When to order it: Mid-morning
- Area, not a shop: Cau Dat hills or in-town cafes; K'Ho named on Vietnam Tourism without a stall price (S1).
- Meal: drink
- Neighbor dish to link: shared `ca-phe` page
- Sources: S1, S23

### Avocado ice cream
- Vietnamese name: Kem bơ
- What it is: Blended avocado dessert sold around the market (S1).
- How to eat it: Eat it cold, standing at a counter.
- When to order it: Afternoon snack
- Area, not a shop: Market slope and night market (S5, S25)
- Meal: sweet
- Neighbor dish to link: none
- Sources: S1, S25

### Grilled rice paper
- Vietnamese name: Bánh tráng nướng
- What it is: Crisp rice sheet grilled with toppings, a Da Lat street snack (S25).
- How to eat it: Fold and eat from the paper.
- When to order it: Night market evening
- Area, not a shop: Night market lanes (S25)
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none
- Sources: S25

### Hot soy milk
- Vietnamese name: Sữa đậu nành nóng
- What it is: Sweet hot soy milk on cool evenings (S1).
- How to eat it: Cup in hand while walking.
- When to order it: After dark when the air cools
- Area, not a shop: Market and night market (S25)
- Meal: drink
- Neighbor dish to link: none
- Sources: S1, S25

### Strawberry with condensed milk
- Vietnamese name: Dâu sữa
- What it is: Fresh strawberries with sweet milk, a market souvenir (S1, S5).
- How to eat it: Cup or bag; wash fruit if you are cautious.
- When to order it: Daytime at the market
- Area, not a shop: Da Lat Market (S5)
- Meal: sweet
- Neighbor dish to link: none
- Sources: S1, S5

### Artichoke tea
- Vietnamese name: Trà atiso
- What it is: Herbal tea from local artichoke, sold as a Lam Dong specialty on provincial desks.
- How to eat it: Hot or bottled to take home.
- When to order it: Any time; souvenir shops cluster near the market.
- Area, not a shop: Market area
- Meal: drink
- Neighbor dish to link: none
- Sources: S3

### Banh mi
- Vietnamese name: Bánh mì
- What it is: Baguette sandwiches in the morning like any Vietnamese city; no Da Lat-only filling list on Vietnam Tourism (S1).
- How to eat it: In the hand at breakfast.
- When to order it: Morning
- Area, not a shop: Street counters near the market
- Meal: breakfast
- Neighbor dish to link: `banh-mi`
- Sources: S1

## Food streets and markets

### Da Lat Market
- Vietnamese name: Chợ Đà Lạt
- Kind: food-street
- What the visitor does: Morning produce, flowers, strawberries, and snacks on the slope (S5).
- When to go: Morning; hours Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Area: Above Xuan Huong Lake
- Suggested slug: market

- Sources: S5

### Da Lat Night Market
- Vietnamese name: Chợ đêm Đà Lạt
- Kind: food-street
- What the visitor does: Grilled rice paper, soy milk, and skewers under lights (S25).
- When to go: About 18:00–23:00 on portal (S25)
- Area: Centre
- Suggested slug: night-market
- Photo must show: Lit food counters.
- Sources: S25
