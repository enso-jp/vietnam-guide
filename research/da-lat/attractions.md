# Attractions — Da Lat

Checked: 21 Oct 2026

## Why a night here

### Cool lake and pine town

- The picture they expect: Cool air, pine ridges, and Xuan Huong Lake in the middle of town, with mist on the water at dawn (S1, S4).
- Where they sleep, as an area: Around Xuan Huong Lake and the market slope, or on a pine ridge above the lake. Not a hotel brand (S1).
- Where the evening is: The night market lanes and the lit walk along the lake and Lam Vien Square (S19, S25).
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): Lam Vien Square for the 2026 flower festival opening and closing programmes (S16). The tourist railway station as the art-deco departure point toward Trai Mat (S8). Dalat Palace Golf Club is named on Vietnam Tourism as a 1933 course; do not publish as a stay pick (S1).
- Hero must include: The lake, the pines, and a colonial roofline or the railway station peaks in one frame. The existing place photo is the station; keep it if the crop still reads.
- A crop that would fail: A portrait of Crazy House that cuts off the roof, or a food stall with no lake or pine context.

### Xuan Huong Lake
- Vietnamese name: Hồ Xuân Hương
- Kind: sight
- Area: City centre
- What the visitor does: Walk or jog the shore, rent a pedal boat, stop for tea on the bank (S1, S4).
- Time: 45–90 minutes for a loop
- Fee: Free on Visit Lâm Đồng (S4)
- From the base: Central
- When to go: Late afternoon for the light; bring a layer November–January (S1)
- Mistake to avoid: Treating the lake as the whole trip. The waterfalls and Lang Biang are half-day rides out.
- Local interest: Lakes
- Suggested slug: xuan-huong
- Suggested dot: 4 — already on record
- Photo must show: The lake surface with pines or city edge in frame.
- Sources: S1, S4
