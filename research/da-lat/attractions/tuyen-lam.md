## What it is

- English name: Tuyen Lam Lake
- Vietnamese name: Hồ Tuyền Lâm
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Pine reservoir south of town
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Reach the pine shore south of town, walk the bank, or take a boat on the water. The monastery on the hill above is Truc Lam, a different page (A1, A2).
- How long on site: 60 minutes on the provincial page (A1). A travel page gives 45–90 minutes for a look only, and a half day when the cable car, monastery, and a boat are combined (A4). The public time is the 60-minute shore stop. Do not invent a drive time.
- How long including the ride from the base: UNSOURCED. Do not print a minute figure for the taxi.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating this as Xuan Huong, or as the monastery. A boat is not included in a monastery visit (A1).
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Truc Lam is the monastery. This page is the lake. Boat hire is a separate fare.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: Provincial price field reads “Đang cập nhật” (A1). Travel pages say shore viewing is free and that there is no single gate for the whole lake (A3, A4). Public fee: free to view; boats Check on arrival.
- Child, height, or senior rule: UNSOURCED for the shore. Do not copy cable-car child bands onto this page.
- What the basic ticket includes: Shore viewing, where the travel pages say free (A3, A4).
- What costs extra: Boat and kayak. Figures disagree and sit on single secondary pages, so the fare is Check on arrival. Examples left off the public page: A4 prints boat 400,000–800,000 VND by group size and kayak 100,000–200,000 VND an hour; A3 prints kayak tours about 340,000–500,000 VND a person. A resort page prints its own boat timetable and no public fare (A5). A 2024 news report says the Tuyen Lam boat cooperative was told to stop (A6). One report is not enough to say every boat has stopped. Public line stays Check on arrival.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: A1, A3, A4. Boat numbers UNSOURCED for the page.
