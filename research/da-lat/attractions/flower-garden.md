## What it is

- English name: Da Lat Flower Garden
- Vietnamese name: Vườn hoa thành phố
- Kicker (one line, what it is): City garden of flower beds
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the paths between planted beds. Hydrangea, roses, mimosa, newer imports, and an orchid section are named on the provincial page (A1).
- How long on site: 90 minutes on the provincial page (A1). Travel pages say 60–90 minutes (A2) and about 1.5–2 hours (A3). 90 minutes sits inside both, so the public time is about 90 minutes.
- How long including the ride from the base: UNSOURCED. The provincial widget prints 850 m, 880 m, and 1.54 km next to other place names. Those distances are not a clear door-to-door time, so they stay off the page.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating the December lake displays as this garden, or paying the wrong figure because the prices disagree.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Adult prices in circulation are 30,000 VND and 100,000 VND. Confirm at the gate.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: Provincial page prints 30,000–30,000 VND (A1). The same portal’s sample tour also prints 30,000 đ for this garden (A5). Travel pages print adult 100,000 VND and child 50,000 VND (A2, A3, A4). Do not average. Public fee prints both adult figures.
- Child, height, or senior rule: 50,000 VND child on A2, A3, and A4, stated with the 100,000 VND adult price. A4 also says under 1.2 m pays 50,000 VND and under 5 is free. That height and age split is one page, so it stays off the public page.
- What the basic ticket includes: Entry to the garden. No inclusion list beyond the visit.
- What costs extra: UNSOURCED. Horse rides and photo stalls are not priced here.
