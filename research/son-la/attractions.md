## Why a night here

- The picture they expect: A mountain town in a bowl, the prison walls on Khau Cả above the Nậm La, and the ridges around the valley. Báo Sơn La puts the hill over that stream (S16). The same paper’s temple piece says the view from the Lê temple takes in the city and suối Nặm La (S14). It is not the Mộc Châu tea morning.
- Where they sleep, as an area: Small hotels and guesthouses in the town center, phường Tô Hiệu as of the 2025 wording, which is the old Chiềng Lề market street (S21). The other bed is a Thái stilt house at Bản Mòng, xã Hua La, on the edge of the city (S17, S18). No hotel brand. Names in the 2020 handbook are pins for the streets only, under Notes in `food.md`.
- Where the evening is: Two Saturday nights a month on Hoàng Quốc Việt, from the National Highway 6 junction to the turn into bản Mé Ban, 19:30–23:00 (S7, S8, S9). On the other nights a bar street was not named. The ordinary evening address is Check on arrival.
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): The walking street, Báo Sơn La, 28 May 2025, https://baosonla.org.vn/thanh-pho-son-la/to-chuc-tuyen-pho-di-bo-tai-thanh-pho-son-la-UWqhCpBHR.html (S7). The prison hill, GoVietnam, https://govietnam.travel/destination/nha-tu-son-la/ (S3). - Hero must include: The stone walls on Khau Cả, the town and the Nậm La in the valley below, and the ridges behind, in color, with the hill through the center. If the Tô Hiệu peach is in bloom, it stays inside that same frame, not as a branch that fills the picture.

## Attractions (Sight, area of interests, zone, hub, etc.)

### Son La Prison
- Vietnamese name: Nhà tù Sơn La
- Kind: sight
- Area: Khau Cả, phường Tô Hiệu (the ward name after 1 Jul 2025). Older pages still print tổ 9, phường Tô Hiệu, thành phố Sơn La (S3, S4, S21)
- What the visitor does: Walks the French prison on the hill above town. Stone walls, four watchtowers, ground-floor and underground cells, a cistern, the cemetery and Tô Hiệu’s grave, and the peach tree he planted. Much of the original building is a ruin after the 1952 and 1965 bombings, with some rebuilt sections and labels (S3). Built 1908, enlarged through the 1930s and 1940s, from more than 1,200 m² to about 2,184 m², walls up to 4.5 m (S3). Ranked a special national relic by Quyết định 2408/QĐ-TTg on 31 Dec 2014 (S15, S16). Báo Sơn La prints more than 260,000 visits a year (S16).
- Time: About one hour for the prison, on the GoVietnam page, plus about one hour if the museum next door is included (S3)
- Fee: GoVietnam and Mytour both print 30,000 VND for an adult, 15,000 VND for a person over 60, and 5,000 VND for a student. GoVietnam also puts disabled visitors on the 15,000 VND line. Mytour puts wounded veterans and people over 60 on that line, and limits the 30,000 VND ticket to adults under 60 (S3, S4). A travel page that describes the museum, not the relic gate, prints 10,000 VND from age 16, 5,000 VND from 10 to under 16, and free under 10 (S5). Keep every ladder. Do not pick one. The gate on the day — Check on arrival
- From the base: In the town center, a short climb on the hill. A taxi fare inside town — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- When to go: Morning, before the stone yard is hot. Around Tết if the peach tree is the picture. The blossom week moves — Check on arrival
- Mistake to avoid: Treating the 10,000 VND museum line and the 30,000 VND relic line as one ticket, or budgeting a whole day for a hill that sits in town
- Local interest: The prison hill
- Suggested slug: son-la-prison
- Suggested dot: 5 — the hill over the town is why the night is here
- Photo must show: The walls on the hill, the valley town below, and the ridges, in one color frame. A cell door alone fails. A peach-blossom portrait fails if it cuts the walls
- Unpublished: no licensed photo
- Sources: S3, S4, S5, S15, S16, S21

### Ban Mong

### Son La dam and the reservoir
- Vietnamese name: Thủy điện Sơn La, lòng hồ sông Đà
- Kind: sight
- Area: Ít Ong, Mường La, on the Đà River. Not the town center. The city is the bed, and this is the morning drive north on QL279 (S6, S12, S13)
- What the visitor does: Looks at the dam from the public side. EVN inaugurated the plant on 23 Dec 2012. Installed capacity 2,400 MW, six units of 400 MW. Reservoir 9.26 billion m³. Normal level 215 m, surcharge 217.83 m, dead water 175 m. Gravity dam 138.1 m high, crest 961.6 m. Mean output 10.246 billion kWh. Catchment 43,760 km² (S10). A second EVN page adds crest elevation 228.1 m, base width 105 m, and crest width 10 m, and calls it the largest hydropower plant in Vietnam and Southeast Asia at that time (S11). GoVietnam prints a lake surface of 224 km² at design level. The EVN release does not print that surface. Keep 224 km² as that one page’s figure (S12). The plant below the dam is closed on the GoVietnam page. The same page says viewing from outside is free, for about 30–45 minutes, and that boat tours run 7:00–17:00 (S12). A 2026 travel note says visitors should register before a plant visit, that a visit takes about 2–3 hours, and that the cost is free or very low. Those two visit rules disagree. The distance also disagrees: the museum handbook and GoVietnam print about 40 km and about one hour (S6, S12); sonlatour.vn prints about 60 km from the city center (S13). Keep 40 km and 60 km. Do not pick one.
- Time: A half day from the city for the viewpoint. A boat is extra. GoVietnam says 2–3 hours on the water and prices a boat at 400,000–1,000,000 VND, or a group seat at 350,000–650,000 VND (S12). Those prices are one commercial page. The plant’s own visitor desk was not read. Fee for a boat — Check on arrival
- Fee: Outside view described as free, with the plant below the dam closed (S12). A 2026 Mytour note says register ahead, about 2–3 hours, free or very low, and also prints about 40 km (S31). Those visit rules disagree. A current gate tariff — Check on arrival
- From the base: QL6 into town, then QL279 north to Mường La (S13). 40 km or 60 km, as above. Minutes for the 60 km figure — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The one-hour figure belongs to the 40 km account (S12)
- When to go: GoVietnam names the dry months, October–April, and suggests early morning or late afternoon for the viewpoint (S12). That season is one page
- Mistake to avoid: Promising a tour inside the powerhouse, or printing one distance. Also do not make the dam the hero of the city night. The hero is Khau Cả over the town
- Local interest: The Đà River dam
- Suggested slug: son-la-dam
- Suggested dot: 4 — the big half-day from this base, not the bed
- Photo must show: The dam wall and the reservoir in one frame, with a ridge or the near bank so the picture is not only concrete. A turbine hall is the wrong subject
- Unpublished: no licensed photo
- Sources: S6, S10, S11, S12, S13, S31
