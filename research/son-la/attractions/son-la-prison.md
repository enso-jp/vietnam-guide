## What it is

- English name: Son La Prison
- Vietnamese name: Nhà tù Sơn La
- Kicker (one line, what it is): French prison on the hill over town
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Stone walls, four watchtowers, ground-floor and underground cells, a cistern, the cemetery and Tô Hiệu’s grave, and the peach tree he planted. Much of the original building is a ruin after the 1952 and 1965 bombings, with some rebuilt sections and labels (S3).
- How long on site: About one hour for the prison, plus about one hour if the museum next door is included (S3 only). Public `timeNeeded` is Check on arrival. One secondary source is not enough for the time field.
- How long including the ride from the base: The hill is in town. Walking minutes — UNSOURCED — Check on arrival
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating the 10,000 VND museum ladder and the 30,000 VND relic ladder as one ticket, or budgeting a whole day for a hill in town
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The two ladders are not one ticket

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: 30,000 VND adult, 15,000 VND over 60, 5,000 VND student (S3, S4). S3 also puts disabled visitors on 15,000 VND. S4 puts wounded veterans and people over 60 on 15,000 VND and limits 30,000 VND to adults under 60. S5, for the museum rather than the relic gate, prints 10,000 VND from age 16, 5,000 VND from 10 to under 16, free under 10. Keep every ladder. Public fee field: Check on arrival
- Child, height, or senior rule: As above. Do not merge the ladders
- What the basic ticket includes: Whether one ticket covers the prison and the museum — Check on arrival — the sources disagree
