## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: A Tết and first-month incense stop. Đền Nàng Han and Đền Linh Sơn Thủy Từ were rebuilt from shrines of the 17th century. A Quan Âm statue, finished at the end of 2020, is 32 m, back to the limestone and face to the Đà. The ground is still being finished, on a plan of nearly 20 ha (S14)
