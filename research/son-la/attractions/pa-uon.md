## Measurements (do not average, do not call them one height)

- Main bridge 918 m, width 9 m, two abutments and 11 piers (S45). S44 says length over 918 m and width 9 m. S12 says 918 m
- Total with approaches 1,418 m, of which the main bridge is 918 m and the approaches are 500 m. Roadway 8 m (S45). This is a longer total, not a rival length for the 918 m bridge
- Main pier 98 m at the 18 Apr 2010 closure (S44). Main pier 98.6 m on 28 Feb 2015 (S45). Print both
- River bed to deck 103.8 m (S45). Handbook: 105 m from the ground to the deck, middle span 120 m (S6). Print both bed-to-deck figures and keep 120 m as the span
- Deck 100 m above the lake (S12). That is the lake clearance, not the pier and not the span
- Started 28 May 2007 (S44). Closure ceremony 18 Apr 2010 (S44). S44 said it would open in July 2010. That sentence is a plan on the ceremony day, not a confirmed opening. S12 says built in 2010. A technical opening in August 2010 is on a page that was not opened. Do not print August
- Skip the “tallest” and record-book lines (S12, S44, S45)
