## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Looks at the wall from the public side. The plant is at Ít Ong, Mường La, not in the town center
- How long on site: About 30–45 minutes for the outside look (S12). About 2–3 hours if the visit is the registered plant visit (S31). Print both. Do not average
- How long including the ride from the base: About 40 km and about one hour (S6, S12). About 60 km from the city center (S13). Minutes for 60 km — UNSOURCED — Check on arrival
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Promising a tour inside the powerhouse from the page that says the plant is closed, or printing one distance
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The visit rules disagree
