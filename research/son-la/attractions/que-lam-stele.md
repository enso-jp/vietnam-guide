## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Reads the poem King Lê Thái Tông cut into the rock at the cave mouth in the fifth lunar month of 1440. The handbook calls the cave động La, local name Thẩm Ké (S6). Báo Sơn La in 2025 calls the same rock hang Thẳm Báo Ké and says the temple above it looks over the city and suối Nặm La (S14). A festival page says the temple beside the stele was built in 2001 (S27)
- How long on site: Under an hour in the dossier. No timed visit was printed. Public time would be Check on arrival

- Mistake that wastes the visit: Filing this as a Mộc Châu pagoda. Vặt Hồng is the plateau. Hang Miếng is Vân Hồ (S14)
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The festival window is 15–17 or 22–24 of the first lunar month (S27, S28). Fee UNSOURCED
