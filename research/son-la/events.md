### Le Thai Tong temple festival
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội đền thờ vua Lê Thái Tông
- Kind: festival
- When: Two festival pages disagree. lehoi.info prints 15–17 of the first lunar month, at phường Chiềng Lề (S27). A second festival page prints 15–17 in the header and 22–24 of the first lunar month in the body, and says the first city edition fell on 15–17 of the first lunar month in 2016 (S28). Keep both windows. The civil dates for 2027 — Check on arrival. Báo Sơn La on 17 Feb 2025 describes Tết incense at the temple and does not print a three-day span (S14)
- Where: The temple and the Quế Lâm stele in town. The 2001 building date is one festival page (S27). The ward is Chiềng Lề on those pages, and part of phường Tô Hiệu from 1 Jul 2025 (S21)
- What happens: Incense and offerings, then drum, dance, a food contest, and folk games, on the festival pages (S27, S28). A 2026 program was not opened
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: `que-lam-stele` is the place. This is the first-month gathering
- Sources: S14, S21, S27, S28

### Tet at the temple and the peach tree
- Vietnamese name: Tết ở đền và cây đào Tô Hiệu
- Kind: activity
- When: Tết Nguyên đán. The civil dates move — Check on arrival. GoVietnam says the peach tree flowers around Tết and that many people choose that week (S3). Báo Sơn La describes first-days incense at the Lê temple, with the view over the city (S14). A museum news page counts provincial Tết visits from 14–22 Feb 2026 and names the prison and the temple as open. That span is a visitor count, not a rite with its own hours (S33)
- Where: Khau Cả and the Lê temple
- What happens: Incense, and a look at the peach tree if it is in flower. It is not a ticket separate from the prison
- Fee: The prison ladders in `attractions.md` still disagree. A Tết surcharge — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: `son-la-prison` and `que-lam-stele` are the places. This is only the Tết week
- Sources: S3, S14, S33
