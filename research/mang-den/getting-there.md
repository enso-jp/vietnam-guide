# Getting there — Mang Den

Checked: 21 Oct 2026

- Nearest airport: Pleiku (PXU), in Gia Lai. Vietnam Tourism puts it roughly 100 km from Mang Den (S1).
- Nearest rail: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. This pass did not read a Vietnam Railways timetable for Kon Tum or Pleiku.
- Road hub: National Highway 24 through Kon Plông district. Vietnam’s national tourism board puts Mang Den about 60 km northeast of Kon Tum city (S2). Go Vietnam Travel puts Kon Pring about 57 km from old Kon Tum city along Highway 24 (S8).
- Usual previous stops: Ho Chi Minh City or Da Lat by air or long-distance bus, then road; Pleiku airport then taxi or hired car; Kon Tum city as an overnight break on the same highway.

Getting around on site:
- Waterfalls and lakes sit outside the town strip. Pa Sy is about 9 km from Mang Den town center on several tourism pages (S3, S4). Kon Pring community village is about 3–5 km from central Mang Den along Highway 24 (S8, S9).
- No public bus network was named on the primary pages read. Hire a car, use a hotel shuttle, or ride your own motorbike. Minutes between sights are Check on arrival — UNSOURCED unless a source below prints them.

Season limit on the journey:
- Blog-style pages cite October–December or a broader dry window for Pa Sy access (S4, S5). Those months disagree. Treat road risk in heavy rain as Check on arrival.
