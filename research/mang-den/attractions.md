## Why a night here

- The picture they expect: Cool air near 1,200 m, pine forest, mist on the pass, and water — Pa Sy in the trees or Dak Ke lake at dawn — not a beach or a city skyline (S1, S2).
- Where they sleep, as an area: Small hotels and homestays in Mang Den town and along the pine roads. Vietnam Tourism also names forest camping and ethnic homestays as options, without a hotel brand (S1).
- Where the evening is: A quiet town after waterfall runs. No sourced bar street. Eat grilled chicken or lam rice at a local restaurant or homestay meal (S1, S8).
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): Kon Pring communal house as a culture pin (S8). Mang Den church and Virgin Mary statue on the Vietnam Tourism Kon Plông list (S2). They orient the day, not a ranked nightlife list.
- Hero must include: Pine forest and either mist or lake water in one frame, with enough town or path in the center that a card crop still reads “cool highlands.”
- A crop that would fail: Only sky and fog with no trees; a borrowed Da Lat flower field; a coast photo.

## Attractions (Sight, area of interests, zone, hub, etc.)

### Pa Sy Waterfall
- Vietnamese name: Thác Pa Sỹ (thác Đăk Ne)
- Kind: sight
- Area: Mang Canh commune, Kon Plông — about 9 km from Mang Den town center (S3, S5)
- What the visitor does: Walk forest stairs to a fall formed where three streams meet. Vietnam Tourism and TITC describe the eco-cultural park and Ro Mam / Mơ Nâm culture space (S1, S3).
- Time: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED for a fixed visit length
- Fee: 50,000 VND adult and 25,000 VND child from 30 May 2026 (Resolution 52/2026/NQ-HĐND). Older 20,000 VND listings are outdated (S4, S5).
- From the base: Road from Mang Den town, then footpath. Minutes Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: Dry-season roads are cited as easier in Oct–Dec on secondary pages; months disagree with other blogs (S5). Check on arrival.
- Mistake to avoid: Budgeting only the old 20,000 VND gate. Confirm the posted price at the counter.
- Local interest: Seven lakes, three falls
- Suggested slug: `pa-sy-waterfall`
- Suggested dot: 5 — the headline fall for the town
- Photo must show: Waterfall and green forest in one color frame, subject through the center
- Sources: S1, S3, S4, S5
