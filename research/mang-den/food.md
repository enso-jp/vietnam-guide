# Food — Mang Den

Checked: 21 Oct 2026

## Comprehensive list of local food to try

### Grilled chicken with lam rice
- Vietnamese name: Gà nướng cơm lam
- What it is: Free-range chicken grilled in mountain style, often eaten with sticky rice cooked in bamboo tubes (S1, S10).
- How to eat it: Shared plates at a homestay or local restaurant. No stall address on the primary pages.
- When to order it: Lunch or dinner
- Area, not a shop: Kon Pring village and Mang Den town restaurants — areas only (S8, S10)
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: `com-lam` when published
- Sources: S1, S10

### Lam rice
- Vietnamese name: Cơm lam
- What it is: Sticky rice grilled inside young bamboo, served with peanut salt (S1).
- How to eat it: Break open the tube. Sold in restaurants and by street vendors per Vietnam Tourism, without a named street (S1).
- When to order it: Lunch or dinner
- Area, not a shop: Mang Den town
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: —
- Sources: S1

### Grilled pork skewers
- Vietnamese name: Thịt heo xiên nướng
- What it is: Chunks of locally raised pork grilled on skewers (S1, S10).
- How to eat it: With rice or beer. No shop list.
- When to order it: Evening
- Area, not a shop: Mang Den town and village homestays
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: —
- Sources: S1, S10

### Forest banana flower salad
- Vietnamese name: Gỏi hoa chuối rừng
- What it is: Wild banana flower salad with herbs and meat (S1).
- How to eat it: As a shared salad plate.
- When to order it: Lunch
- Area, not a shop: Mang Den town

### Highland vegetable tray

- How to eat it: With grilled meat or hot pot.
- When to order it: Lunch or dinner

### Arabica coffee

- What it is: Cool-climate coffee on the plateau. Link the site-wide `ca-phe` dish when the food pass runs.
