Checked: 23 Oct 2026

## Comprehensive list of local food to try

### Grilled chicken with lam rice
- Vietnamese name: Gà nướng cơm lam
- What it is: Free-range chicken grilled in mountain style, often eaten with sticky rice cooked in bamboo tubes (S1, S10).
- How to eat it: Vietnam Tourism does not name a street (S1, re-checked 23 Oct 2026). The rural portal sells it at the weekend market (S15). Báo Kon Tum serves gà nướng at the cultural night (S18). The earlier homestay line was not reconfirmed on S1.
- When to order it: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED for a clock
- Area, not a shop: Mang Den town and Chợ phiên Măng Đen
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: local dish `mang-den--com-lam`. The shared `com-lam` record was not edited.
- Sources: S1, S15, S18. S10 was the earlier Kon Pring citation; the 23 Oct 2026 pass did not re-open S10 for this plate.

### Lam rice
- Vietnamese name: Cơm lam
- What it is: Sticky rice grilled inside young bamboo, served with peanut salt (S1).
- How to eat it: Break open the tube. Sold in restaurants and by street vendors per Vietnam Tourism, without a named street (S1).
- When to order it: Lunch or dinner
- Area, not a shop: Mang Den town
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: —
- Sources: S1, S18

## Food streets and markets

### Mang Den weekend market
- Vietnamese name: Chợ phiên Măng Đen
- Kind: food-street
- What it is: A weekend market of local specialties, farm goods, and medicinal goods.
- When: Saturday and Sunday (S15, S16). Weekends (S17). Opened 7 October 2023 (S16, S17). The rural portal says since October 2023 (S15). The opening hour is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Where: Pine hill on Hùng Vương, xã Măng Đen, Quảng Ngãi (S15). Pine hill on Hùng Vương, thị trấn Măng Đen, Kon Plông (S16). Vườn thông, district central square (S17). All three stay.
- Stalls: 24, of which 9 are stilt houses (S15). 24, later 32 (S16). More than 30 at the start, more than 40 later (S17).
- Goods that are not plates: cà phê, mắc ca, gạo đỏ, organic vegetables, cherry tomatoes, pomelo, oranges, cucumber, strawberries (S15); cà phê, mắc ca, gạo đỏ (S16); wine products and specialty salt at a center stall (S17); medicinal names hồng đẳng sâm, đương quy, sơn tra, chè dây, tinh bột nghệ, chuối hột (S16), nấm linh chi cổ cò, sâm dây, giảo cổ lam, nấm lim xanh, mật ong rừng (S15).
- Sources: S15, S16, S17
