## What it is

- English name: Pa Sy Waterfall
- Vietnamese name: Thác Pa Sỹ (thác Đăk Ne)
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Forest fall where three streams meet
- Kind: sight
- Area: Outside the Mang Den town strip. Việt Úc Tourist and AnHang place it in Mang Canh commune, Kon Plông (A2, A4). The June 2025 rearrangement folded Mang Canh into Mang Den commune (S14). District on the public record: The falls

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Three streams meet and drop in one column into a pool in the forest (A1, A6). Việt Úc Tourist describes a walk down mossy stone steps through the trees (A2). The same page and Nam Thiên Travel both print a 25-hectare park (A2, A3).
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No visit length was printed on the fee notice or the national tourism page.
- How long including the ride from the base: Minutes Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Distances disagree. See Getting there.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Budgeting the older 20,000 VND gate. The provincial fee from 30 May 2026 is 50,000 VND for an adult and 25,000 VND for a child (A5). Việt Úc Tourist and Nam Thiên Travel still print 20,000 VND (A2, A3).
- Notice: Two prices are still in circulation. Pay the figure at the gate.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: 50,000 VND a visit for an adult and 25,000 VND for a child, from 30 May 2026, for Di tích lịch sử và thắng cảnh Măng Đen — địa điểm thác Đăk Ne (thác Pa Sỹ), under Resolution 52/2026/NQ-HĐND (A5). AnHang repeats those figures and the same start date (A4).
- Older figure, not averaged: 20,000 VND a person on Việt Úc Tourist and on Nam Thiên Travel (A2, A3). Nam Thiên’s own 2026 heading still prints 20,000 VND in the price table.
- Child rule: A child pays 25,000 VND. Under 6 is free. The resolution does not print an upper age for “trẻ em” (A5).
- Free: children under 6, disabled visitors, people in poor or near-poor households, and people recognized for service to the revolution (A5, A4).
- Half price: older visitors, students, people with the culture-access concession, residents of specially difficult villages, people’s artisans, and elite artisans (A5).
- What the basic ticket includes: The visit. Tent, meal, and chair prices on A2 and A3 are a rental menu, not the gate, and are left off the public page.
- Source: A5 official. A2 and A3 for the older 20,000 VND only.
