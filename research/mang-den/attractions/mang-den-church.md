# Attraction research — Mang Den church and the Virgin Mary statue

Place: `mang-den`
Slug: `mang-den-church`
Status: stored

One visit. The pilgrimage center holds the Virgin Mary shrine (linh đài) and the central church (nhà thờ trung tâm). Vietnamese Wikipedia lists Đức Mẹ Măng Đen as its own sight (A7). The national Kon Plông page names the church and the Virgin Mary statue together (S2, stored 21 Oct 2026; the English page did not load on 23 Oct 2026).

## What it is

- English name: Mang Den church and the Virgin Mary statue
- Vietnamese name: Đức Mẹ Măng Đen / Nhà thờ Măng Đen
- Kicker: Pilgrimage center on Highway 24
- Kind: sight
- Area: Highway 24, Mang Den commune, Quảng Ngãi (A1, A3). District on the public record: The town

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Stop at the statue among the trees, and at the church for Mass. The center asks visitors to come to pray, give thanks, and visit (A1).
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The dossier’s 30 minutes was approximate and stays off the page.
- How long including the ride from the base: From the town strip, minutes Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The center does not print a town-strip distance.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Expecting a finished campus. The center says the buildings are being repaired and upgraded (A1).
- Notice: Work on the buildings is still underway.

## Fee

- Basic price: The gate, the parking, and the toilets are free (A1).
- Child rule: No ticket bands. The gate is free.
- What costs extra: UNSOURCED. A donation box is not a fee.

## History used

- Father Giuse Phạm Minh Công set the statue in Mang Den after Christmas 1971 (A4). The introduction also dates the statue’s presence to 1971 (A1).
- From 1974 the outpost was abandoned, the statue was damaged, and it was forgotten in the forest (A4).
- In 1983 two people from the north found it without its head and both hands. They tried to fit the hands. The hands fell off again (A4).
- 9 December 2006: a Catholic was led to the statue and reported it to the Kon Tum diocese. 28 December 2006: Bishop Michael Hoàng Đức Oanh visited. 9 December 2007: Mass of Our Lady of Sorrows, more than 2,000 people, marks the start of the pilgrimage center (A4).
- The current photographs show a statue with hands. The page does not claim the broken statue is what you see now.
- A tour-blog story that names a different priest and a helicopter was not used. The center’s own history is the one on the page.
