## What it is

- English name: Kon Pring village
- Vietnamese name: Làng du lịch cộng đồng Kon Pring (Kon Bring)
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Mơ Nâm village on Highway 24
- Kind: area
- Area: About 3–5 km from Mang Den on Highway 24. District on the public record: The village road

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: See the nhà rông, stilt houses, gong or xoang, weaving, and rice wine. An optional homestay is named. Ask before photographs inside the communal house (S8, S9, S10).
- How long on site: About 60 minutes minimum on Mang Den Tourism (S9).
- How long including the ride from the base: Short drive. Minutes Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Photographing inside the communal house without asking (S8).
- Notice: Ask before you photograph inside the nhà rông.
