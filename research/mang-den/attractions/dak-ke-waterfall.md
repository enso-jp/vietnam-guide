## What it is

- English name: Dak Ke Waterfall
- Vietnamese name: Thác Đắk Ke
- Kind: sight
- Separate from Dak Ke lake: yes. The national things-to-do page gives the fall its own chapter, after Pa Sy and before the lake. It calls the fall smaller than Pa Sy and more remote, with a natural pool you can swim in once you reach it. The lake chapter is the shore path, canoeing, fishing, and carriage rides (A1). Vietnamese Wikipedia lists Thác Đăk Ke and does not fold it into the lake (A7).
- It stays off the public list because no licensed photo of this fall was found, not because it is the same stop as the lake.
