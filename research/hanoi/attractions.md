# Research dossier — Hanoi

Slug: `hanoi`
Province: Hanoi, the capital. The national tourism board’s attractions page says Emperor Ly Thai To named it Thang Long in 1010, on the Red River, and calls it the capital (S2). The 2025 rearrangement of provinces was not opened in this lane.
Kind: city

Checked: 21 Oct 2026

## Why a night here

- The picture they expect: Dawn on Hoan Kiem Lake. The national tourism board says people in Hanoi invite each other “đi một vòng hồ”, and that the lake is Hoàn Kiếm, a green body of water in the core of the city. Before sunrise, joggers, tai chi, and games gather on the leafy banks. The same page tells a visitor to walk to the red Húc Bridge, Ngọc Sơn Temple, and the Turtle Tower (S1). The attractions page tells the sword legend: a turtle in the lake takes back the sword after the victory, which is why the lake is called Hoàn Kiếm, the returned sword (S2). The lake is the picture. The airport is not.
- Where they sleep, as an area: A room in the Old Quarter, the 36 guild streets the tourism board places north of the lake (S1, S2). The other walkable base is the south rim. The same board’s walking-tour page says the streets just south of the Old Quarter are the spacious, tree-lined French Quarter, and it ends that walk at the Opera House (S3). No official page names a hotel street or a brand. A hotel terrace named on that walking tour is not a pick. Which block faces the water is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED as a lodging map.
- Where the evening is: The streets Decision 72/2024/QĐ-UBND closes into the Hoan Kiem walking zone: Đinh Tiên Hoàng, Lê Lai from Đinh Tiên Hoàng to Lý Thái Tổ, Lê Thạch, Đinh Lễ, Nguyễn Xí, Tràng Tiền from Ngô Quyền to Đinh Tiên Hoàng, Hàng Khay, Lê Thái Tổ, Lò Sũ from Hàng Dầu to Đinh Tiên Hoàng, Hàng Dầu from Lò Sũ to Đinh Tiên Hoàng, Đông Kinh Nghĩa Thục square, Trần Nguyên Hãn from Đinh Tiên Hoàng to Lý Thái Tổ, phố hồ Hoàn Kiếm, Lương Văn Can from Hàng Hành to Lê Thái Tổ, Bảo Khánh from the Bảo Khánh lane to Lê Thái Tổ, and Hàng Bài from Hai Bà Trưng to Tràng Tiền (S4). The tourism board says that on weekends the lake is closed to vehicles (S1). The decision does not print a clock. A standing hour is Check on arrival. What is on record is narrower: the government portal, writing a ward adjustment for 7–9 Mar 2025, puts the old-quarter walking streets at 19:00–24:00 that weekend and starts the lake zone at 13:00 on the Friday (S5). Dân trí, 13 Mar 2026, reports a Hoan Kiem ward pause from 13 Mar until 19:00 on 20 Mar 2026, and states the ordinary weekend as 19:00 Friday to 24:00 Sunday, with summer from 18:00 (S6). That summer line is the newspaper’s sentence. The ward notice itself was not opened. Do not treat either adjusted weekend as the rule for every Friday.
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): Cầu Thê Húc, the red bridge, and Đền Ngọc Sơn on the island at the north side of the lake (S1, S2). Tháp Rùa, the Turtle Tower, standing in the water (S1). Nhà hát Lớn Hà Nội, the Opera House, which the tourism board places at 1 Tràng Tiền, Hoàn Kiếm, and calls the largest theatre in the country, built at the turn of the 20th century and refurbished in 1997 (S2). The same page says the house is currently under renovation. No second page was opened, so whether a performance is on is Check on arrival. These are pins. They are not a ranked bar list. The tourism board also names a beer corner at Tạ Hiện and Lương Ngọc Quyến (S2). That corner is a pin for the nightlife lane. This pass records no drink price and no restaurant.
- Hero must include: the lake water, the scarlet bridge, and Ngọc Sơn on the island, with the lakeside trees, subject through the center. The Turtle Tower belongs in the water if the frame can hold it (S1). Not the airport, not a document, not a cafe interior.
- A crop that would fail: a bridge cut off from the temple, a panorama whose center is only green water, a fog bank with no island, a portrait of the Lý Thái Tổ statue cropped through the chest. Decision 72 names the garden in front of that statue, on Đinh Tiên Hoàng between Lê Thạch and Trần Nguyên Hãn, as a place where ordinary events are not held (S4). The statue is not the hero.

### Hoan Kiem Lake and the Old Quarter
- Vietnamese name: Hồ Hoàn Kiếm and Phố cổ
- Kind: area
- Area: The lake, the walking streets named in Decision 72, and the 36 guild streets north of the water
- What the visitor does: Wakes to the lake and sleeps within a walk of it. The Old Quarter is the tight commercial grid north of the water. The south rim, toward the Opera House, is the wider walk. This is the scene, not a ticket.
- Time: A loop of the lake is the morning and the evening. A minute count is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Fee: Walking the bank is described as free by the tourism board (S2). A temple door on the island is a different question and is not recorded in this lane.
- From the base: On foot if the room is in the Old Quarter or on the south rim. Which hotel street faces the water is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- When to go: Before sunrise for the exercise on the bank, and Friday to Sunday evening if the walking zone is open (S1, S5, S6). Confirm that weekend’s clock.
- Mistake to avoid: Treating the metro map as a way onto the lake. The open lines do not reach this water. See getting-there.md.
- Local interest: The lake evening
- Suggested slug: hoan-kiem
- Suggested dot: 5 already on the published lake record. Do not change it. Do not add a second record for the scene.
- Photo must show: the lake, the scarlet bridge, and the temple on the island in one frame, trees on the bank, subject through the center
- Sources: S1, S2, S3, S4, S5, S6
