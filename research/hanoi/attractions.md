## Why a night here

### The lake in the morning
- The picture they expect: Turtle Tower on Hoan Kiem, with the water in front of it and the willows on the shore. The walk is the lake path, then the lanes north of the lake.
- Where they sleep, as an area: the lanes north of the lake, or the streets south of the lake. Not a hotel brand.
- Where the evening is: the bia hơi corner of Tạ Hiện and Lương Ngọc Quyến (S10). The lake path is free and open all day (S10). A weekend pedestrian zone around the lake is one newspaper report only. Do not publish a clock for it.
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): Tạ Hiện / Lương Ngọc Quyến bia hơi corner, https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/11-must-see-attractions-ha-noi (S10). Café Giảng, alley 39 Nguyễn Hữu Huân, is the egg-coffee pin on https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/10-must-try-hanoi-dishes (S18). Pin, not a ranked cafe list.
- Hero must include: Turtle Tower, the lake, and the near shore in one color frame, tower through the center. A close tower and a wider lake are two files. Do not use the same file for the city banner and the sight card.

### Hoan Kiem Lake
- Vietnamese name: Hồ Hoàn Kiếm
- Kind: sight
- Area: Hoàn Kiếm
- What the visitor does: Walk the lake. Turtle Tower stands in the water. Ngọc Sơn Temple sits on a small island, reached by the Thê Húc bridge. Nguyễn Văn Siêu’s renovation of the temple is dated 1864. The bridge name is given as “where the morning sun rests.” The temple worships Trần Hưng Đạo and Văn Xương (S16).
- Time: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Fee: The lake path is free and open all day (S10). The temple ticket is separate. The management board: Monday–Thursday 7:00–19:00; Friday–Sunday and holidays 7:00–22:00; adult 50,000 VND from 1 Jan 2024; half 25,000 VND; under 16 free (S16). Vietnam Tourism: Monday–Friday 7:00–18:00, Saturday–Sunday 7:00–22:00, 50,000 VND (S10). Both hour tables stay.
- From the base: Walk. The minutes from a named hotel are Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: Morning, for the tower and the walk. Dawn exercise is on the lifestyle entry.
- Mistake to avoid: Budgeting the temple ticket as the price of the lake path.
- Local interest: The lake and the lanes
- Suggested slug: hoan-kiem
- Suggested dot: 4 — already published. Do not change it.
- Photo must show: Turtle Tower centered, with water in front. A second frame is the willow shore, not the same file.

### Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum
- Vietnamese name: Lăng Chủ tịch Hồ Chí Minh
- Kind: sight
- Area: Ba Đình

- Hours: Operator regulation (S7). Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Saturday, Sunday mornings. Closed Monday and Friday, except 19 May, 2 September, and the first day of Tết when those fall on a closed day. Hot season 1 Apr–31 Oct: weekdays 7:30–10:30, weekends and holidays 7:30–11:00. Cold season 1 Nov–31 Mar: weekdays 8:00–11:00, weekends and holidays 8:00–11:30. Vietnam Tourism compresses the hours and adds a last entry of 10:15 that S7 does not state (S10). Print the operator hours and say the tourism page adds 10:15.
- Closure: Government portal, 4 Sep 2026–2 Nov 2026, reopens 3 Nov 2026 (S9). A relic page says about two months in October and November each year. Both stay.
- From the base: Ride from the lake to 17 Ngọc Hà. Minutes Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: An open morning. Not Monday or Friday unless that morning is one of the exceptions.
- Mistake to avoid: Arriving in the afternoon, or during the 2026 closure window, or treating one fee as the only fee.
- Local interest: War history
- Suggested slug: mausoleum
- Suggested dot: 4 — a timed morning, often the reason for the Ba Đình walk
