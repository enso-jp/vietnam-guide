# Sources

Accessed 21 Oct 2026 unless a line says the page has its own date.

### S1
- URL: https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/explore-old-quarter-your-way
- Publisher: Vietnam National Authority of Tourism, vietnam.travel
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: The Old Quarter is north of Hoan Kiem Lake. The 36 guild streets supplied the citadel and are walked. Before sunrise and in the evening, people exercise and sit on the banks. The red Húc Bridge, Ngọc Sơn Temple, and the Turtle Tower are the lakeside landmarks. On weekends the lake is closed to vehicles. The page also names shops, cafes, and bars. Those names are not picks in this lane.

### S2
- URL: https://vietnam.travel/things-to-do/11-must-see-attractions-ha-noi
- Publisher: Vietnam National Authority of Tourism, vietnam.travel
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: Ha Noi was named Thang Long by Ly Thai To in 1010, on the Red River, and the page calls it the capital. The Old Quarter is one square kilometre of 36 streets north of the lake. The page says the quarter is free and prints “7am to midnight”; that hour was not confirmed on a second page. Hoan Kiem is free and open all day on this page, with the returned-sword legend. Ngoc Son is on an island on the north side, reached by a scarlet bridge. The Opera House is at 1 Trang Tien, Hoan Kiem; the page says it is currently under renovation, which this pass does not treat as confirmed. The page also prints admission fees for other sights. Those fees are not used in this lane. It names the beer corner at Ta Hien and Luong Ngoc Quyen.
