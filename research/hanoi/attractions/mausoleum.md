## What you see
The mausoleum on Ba Dinh Square. Assembly at 17 Ngoc Ha (S7).

## Hours and fees
Fee, both: no ticket for the mausoleum or the square (S8). Vietnam Tourism prints 40,000 VND (S10).
Hours from the operator (S7): mornings Tue, Wed, Thu, Sat, Sun. Closed Mon and Fri except 19 May, 2 Sep, and mung 1 Tet when those fall on a closed day. Hot season 1 Apr–31 Oct: weekdays 7:30–10:30, weekends and holidays 7:30–11:00. Cold season 1 Nov–31 Mar: weekdays 8:00–11:00, weekends and holidays 8:00–11:30.
S10 adds a last entry of 10:15 that S7 does not state.
Closure: 4 Sep 2026–2 Nov 2026, reopens 3 Nov 2026 (S9). A relic page says about two months in October and November each year. Both stay.
