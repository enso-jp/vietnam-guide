# One Pillar Pagoda
Status: stored
Checked: 22 Oct 2026

## What you see
A wooden pagoda on a single stone pillar. Also called Dien Huu and Lien Hoa Dai. Address Chua Mot Cot, Doi Can, Ba Dinh (S22).

## Hours and fees
Fee: 25,000 VND (S10 and S22).
Hours, both: S10 summer 7:30–10:30, winter 8:00–11:00, Tue–Thu and Sat–Sun. S22 8:00–11:30 daily, and 14:00–16:00 Tue–Thu and Sat–Sun.
Emperor, both: Ly Thai To, 1049 (S10, S22). Ly Thai Tong, 1049 (Hanoi culture department).
