# Hoan Kiem Lake
Status: stored
Checked: 22 Oct 2026
Slug: hoan-kiem

## What you see
Walk the lake path. Turtle Tower stands in the water. Ngoc Son Temple is on an island, reached by the The Huc bridge. Nguyen Van Sieu’s renovation is dated 1864. The bridge name is given as where the morning sun rests. The temple worships Tran Hung Dao and Van Xuong (S16).

## Hours and fees
- Lake path: free, open all day (S10).
- Temple ticket is separate. Management board: Mon–Thu 7:00–19:00; Fri–Sun and holidays 7:00–22:00; adult 50,000 VND from 1 Jan 2024; half 25,000; under 16 free (S16).
- Vietnam Tourism: Mon–Fri 7:00–18:00; Sat–Sun 7:00–22:00; 50,000 VND (S10). Both hour tables stay.
- Time on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED

## Getting there
Walk from the lanes north of the lake or from the streets south of the lake. Minutes from a named hotel: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
