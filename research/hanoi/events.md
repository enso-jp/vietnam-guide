### Tet
- Vietnamese name: Tết Nguyên Đán
- Kind: festival
- When: The public holiday is about a week, and the city feels it for longer. The calendar date moves. Check on arrival. In the days before, seats and rooms sell out. Many shops close for at least the first days (S20).
- Where: Hang Luoc flower market, from the 23rd to the 30th day of the last lunar month. Quang Ba flower market, Tay Ho, is the large daily flower market and is busier at Tet (S20). Tet Eve offerings in Old Quarter lanes (S20).
- What happens: Families buy peach boughs and other flowers. The first days of the city are quieter than a normal week (S20).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No gate fee was printed for the flower streets.
- Not the same as attraction slug: hoan-kiem
- Sources: S20
- Photo: unpublished: no licensed photo of the flower market. Do not use the city banner as the event.
