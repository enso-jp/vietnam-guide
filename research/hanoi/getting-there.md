- Getting around on site: Walk the Old Quarter and the lake. The tourism board says the 36 streets are one square kilometre north of the lake and are walked, and that motorcycles move through those narrow streets (S1, S2). Line 2A, Cát Linh – Hà Đông, is the operator’s open east-west metro. The operator homepage prints a timetable of 05:30–22:00 and lists twelve stations from Cát Linh to Yên Nghĩa (S12). The Hà Đông station page on the same site prints 05:30–22:30 and about 25.5 minutes from Cát Linh to Yên Nghĩa (S13). Those two clocks both stand. The line does not serve Hoàn Kiếm. Line 3 is only partly open. The city railway-project office says the elevated section, Nhổn to Cầu Giấy, about 8.5 km and eight stations, has carried passengers since 08:00 on 8 Aug 2024, and that the tunnel and the underground stations are still being built (S14). The government portal confirms commercial service on that elevated section from 08:00 on 8 Aug 2024 (S15). The operator homepage nevertheless lists Line 3 through to Ga Hà Nội, including Kim Mã, Cát Linh, Văn Miếu, and Hà Nội Station, with a 05:30–22:00 timetable (S12). Prefer the project office: the metro does not yet reach the railway station or the lake. A current daily clock for the elevated section alone was not separated from that longer list.
