# Food — Hanoi

Checked: 22 Oct 2026
Source for the dish list: Vietnam Tourism, 10 must-try Hanoi dishes (S18). Shop names on that page are notes, not picks.

## Comprehensive list of local food to try

### Pho
- Vietnamese name: Phở
- What it is: A beef or chicken noodle soup. S18 does not give a founding year, a texture essay, or a history. Leave those off the dish page.
- How to eat it: Start with a bit of chili. Garlic vinegar is for the beef bowl. Lime is for the chicken bowl (S18).
- When to order it: Morning, in the Hoàn Kiếm lanes. The clock is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Area, not a shop: Hàng Vải and the lanes around Hoàn Kiếm. The shop named on S18 is under Notes, not pages.
- Meal: breakfast
- Neighbor dish to link: the shared `pho` record also lists Ho Chi Minh City. Add only the Hanoi lines above.
- Sources: S18
- Photo: a chicken bowl in a blue bowl, cropped tight so the fruit plate is not the subject. Licensed. No second frame yet.

### Bun cha
- Vietnamese name: Bún chả
- What it is: Chargrilled pork belly and pork patties with rice noodles. S18 does not call the sauce sweet-sour. It says a diluted vinegar fish sauce. Do not keep “sweet-sour” on the public summary.
- How to eat it: Put the noodles into the bowl with the herbs. Nem is optional (S18).
- When to order it: Almost exclusively lunch (S18).
- Area, not a shop: Cửa Đông. The shop at 40 Cửa Đông is under Notes, not pages.
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: `bun-cha` already lists Hanoi and the Old Quarter.
- Sources: S18
- Photo: overhead pork, noodles, herbs, and nem. A second frame of herbs, rolls, and pork exists.

## Specialties added 23 Oct 2026

Each line is a dish. Shop names stay doors the source prints, not a ranked pick. Hours are Check on arrival — UNSOURCED unless a clock is quoted. No new plate photograph was filed. Text line, not a card.

### Mien
- Vietnamese name: Miến
- What it is: Chewy glass noodle from mung bean or sweet potato, mild and savory, with chicken, crab, or freshwater eel, in broth or stir-fried (S18).
- How to eat it: Quẩy in the broth (S18).
- Area: 6 Phùng Hưng, Hoàn Kiếm (S18).
- Sources: S18

### Nom bo kho
- Vietnamese name: Nộm bò khô
- What it is: Sweet and sour green papaya, carrot, and beef jerky. S18 offers spleen and tripe. S29 adds peanuts. S35 adds lemon balm and fish sauce.
- When: Afternoon snack (S18).
- Area: 23 Hoàn Kiếm (S18).
- Sources: S18, S29, S35

### Bun ca
- Vietnamese name: Bún cá
- What it is: S18: sweet and sour summer bowl, fried tilapia, Vietnamese celery, pineapple and tomato, herbs and pickled bamboo shoots. S34: lunch, fried fish, a green vegetable compared to morning glory, cilantro and dill. Both stay.
- Area: 14 Trung Yên alley, Hàng Bạc (S18). S34 names no second door.
- Sources: S18, S34

### Bun oc
- Vietnamese name: Bún ốc
- What it is: Snail rice vermicelli with beef, crab meat, herbs, tomatoes, wine vinegar, and pepper (S34).
- Area: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. S34 names no street.
- Sources: S34

### Lau
- Vietnamese name: Lẩu
- What it is: Hot pot. Vegetables, meat, and seafood in a bubbling pot (S34). No broth was named.
- Area: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Sources: S34

### Banh da tron
- Vietnamese name: Bánh đa trộn
- What it is: Thick flat noodles with sausage, fried fish cakes, tofu, peanuts, and leafy vegetables. Miến is the other noodle in the baskets (S34).
- Area: Old Quarter, from a carrying pole (S34). No house number.
- Sources: S34

### Banh goi
- Vietnamese name: Bánh gối
- What it is: Pillow pastry. S34: minced pork, black fungus, vermicelli, quail egg. S35: pork, wood ear, glass noodles, fish sauce, mint. No quail egg on S35. Both stay.
- When: Afternoon on S34.
- Area: Old Quarter walk. No house number.
- Sources: S34, S35

### My ga tan
- Vietnamese name: Mỳ gà tần
- What it is: Braised chicken and egg noodles. Regular chicken or black-skinned chicken, simmered in an empty drink can with herbs including mugwort (S34).
- Area: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Sources: S34

### Banh duc nong
- Vietnamese name: Bánh đúc nóng
- What it is: Hot steamed cake of rice and tapioca flour, black fungus, meat, fried shallots, chilli-garlic fish sauce, herbs (S34). Not the Yên Mô bánh đúc record.
- When: Afternoon snack on a cold day; winter (S34).
- Area: 45 Châu Long, Quán Thánh, Ba Đình, the door S39 names. do not publish as a ranked pick.
- Sources: S34, S39

### Ha cao

### Pho mai que

- Sources: S34

### Nem chua ran
- Vietnamese name: Nem chua rán

- Sources: S34

### Banh khuc
- Vietnamese name: Bánh khúc
- What it is: S35 calls it a Hanoi specialty. Roasted pork and mung-bean paste in sticky rice with khúc leaves from the Red River, steamed in banana leaves, with salty peanuts and pork-patty slices.
- Area: Sidewalk on the Old Quarter walk. No house number.
- Sources: S35

### Ca cuon thit
- Vietnamese name: Cá cuộn thịt
- What it is: Fried fish ball stuffed with pork, greens, light fish sauce, calamansi (S35). The stall is described as twenty years old.
- Area: Old Quarter market corner. No street name.
- Sources: S35

### Bia hoi
- Vietnamese name: Bia hơi
- What it is: Fresh draft beer, unpasteurized, drunk soon after it is made, called as light as water (S35). The corner at Tạ Hiện is the street and does not count as this dish.
- Price: The 15,000 VND figure on S31 stays off the public dish.
- Sources: S35, S29. S31 price not published.

### Pho cuon
- Vietnamese name: Phở cuốn

- Sources: S31, S37

### Com Vong
- Vietnamese name: Cốm Vòng
- What it is: Young green rice from Vòng, Cầu Giấy. Gently sweet, soft, lotus scent, eaten with ripe banana. Wrapped in lotus leaves and rice straw. The craft is believed to date back several centuries (S36).
- When: Autumn, as S36 states visitors come then. No clock.
- Sources: S36
