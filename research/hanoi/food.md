## Comprehensive list of local food to try

### Pho
- Vietnamese name: Phở
- What it is: A beef or chicken noodle soup. S18 does not give a founding year, a texture essay, or a history. Leave those off the dish page.
- How to eat it: Start with a bit of chili. Garlic vinegar is for the beef bowl. Lime is for the chicken bowl (S18).
- When to order it: Morning, in the Hoàn Kiếm lanes. The clock is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Area, not a shop: Hàng Vải and the lanes around Hoàn Kiếm. The shop named on S18 is under Notes, not pages.
- Meal: breakfast
- Neighbor dish to link: the shared `pho` record also lists Ho Chi Minh City. Add only the Hanoi lines above.
- Sources: S18
- Photo: a chicken bowl in a blue bowl, cropped tight so the fruit plate is not the subject. Licensed. No second frame yet.

### Bun cha
- Vietnamese name: Bún chả
- What it is: Chargrilled pork belly and pork patties with rice noodles. S18 does not call the sauce sweet-sour. It says a diluted vinegar fish sauce. Do not keep “sweet-sour” on the public summary.
- How to eat it: Put the noodles into the bowl with the herbs. Nem is optional (S18).
- When to order it: Almost exclusively lunch (S18).
- Area, not a shop: Cửa Đông. The shop at 40 Cửa Đông is under Notes, not pages.
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: `bun-cha` already lists Hanoi and the Old Quarter.
- Sources: S18
- Photo: overhead pork, noodles, herbs, and nem. A second frame of herbs, rolls, and pork exists.

- When to order it: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Area, not a shop: Đường Thành. 6B Đường Thành is under Notes, not pages.
- Meal: main

- Vietnamese name: Cà phê trứng
- What it is: Strong robusta under a sweet egg foam (S18). Do not add a 1946 milk-shortage story. It is not on S18.
- How to eat it: In the afternoon, sitting down. The morning pho shop is a different stop.
- When to order it: Afternoon (lifestyle note). The clock is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Area, not a shop: The Nguyễn Hữu Huân alley. Café Giảng, alley 39, is the scene pin S18 names. Pin, not a ranked pick. No logo photo.
- Meal: drink
- Neighbor dish to link: `ca-phe`, which already has a licensed iced-coffee photo. Do not make a separate egg-coffee file. The cup with the café logo was rejected.
- Sources: S18
- Photo: the existing iced coffee with condensed milk stays the `ca-phe` photo. unpublished: no licensed photo of the egg cup without a logo.
