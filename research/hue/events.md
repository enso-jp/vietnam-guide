### Hue Festival
- Vietnamese name: Festival Huế
- Kind: festival
- When: Plan 500/KH-UBND of 31 Dec 2025 sets Festival Huế 2026 as a year-long chain. It opens with the announcement and the reconstructed Ban Sóc rite on 1 Jan 2026 at the Ngọ Môn square, and it ends with a countdown on 31 Dec 2026. The highlight inside that year is the opening with Hoàng cung Huyền Dạ and the International Arts Festival week, 13 Jun 2026 through 18 Jun 2026. The theme line is “Di sản văn hóa với hội nhập và phát triển.” The year is split into four seasons: spring (months 1–3), summer (months 4–6), autumn (months 7–9), winter (months 10–12) (S25, S26). The culture department’s 5 Jan 2026 note confirms the 1 Jan opening at Ngọ Môn and the four-season model (S27). Dates after 2026 are Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The plan is a 2026 document. Do not copy 13–18 Jun onto 2027.
- Where: Opening at Quảng trường Ngọ Môn, Đại Nội. The conservation centre is the standing office of the festival (S25, S26). Other venues sit in the appendix and were not each copied here.
- What happens: Court and folk rites, the summer arts week, a mid-autumn lantern programme, and a winter music programme named for Trịnh Công Sơn, then the new-year countdown (S25). This is not the citadel visit. The citadel is open on ordinary days under its own ticket (S11).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The plan gives organizing budgets, not a visitor ticket.
- Not the same as attraction slug: citadel
- Sources: S11, S25, S26, S27
