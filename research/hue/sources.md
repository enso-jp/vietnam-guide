# Sources

Accessed 21 Oct 2026 unless a line says the page has its own date.

### S1
- URL: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/678/
- Publisher: UNESCO World Heritage Centre, Complex of Huế Monuments
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: Capital of unified Vietnam from 1802 to 1945 under the Nguyen dynasty. The Perfume River runs through the Capital City, the Imperial City, the Forbidden Purple City, and the Inner City. Ngu Binh is the Royal Screen. The citadel is the central structure. Thien Mu and the emperors’ tombs lie outside the Capital City; the tombs are upstream along the river. Recurrent floods are a management problem. The Hue Monuments Conservation Centre manages the property.

### S2
- URL: https://svhttdl.hue.gov.vn/tin-trong-tinh/hue-duong-dua-di-san-giua-long-co-do.html
- Publisher: Hue Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism. Issued 9 Apr 2026 (DC.Date.Issued 2026-04-09T10:29)
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: Phu Xuan Bridge joins the two banks. The south bank is the modern city. The north bank is the old imperial city. Kỳ Đài was built in 1807 under Gia Long, three tiers, and the page calls it the city’s zero marker. Nghinh Lương Đình stands on the river bank, a summer stop for the king and a landing for the royal boats. The article is about a planned 2026 bicycle stage, not a hotel map.

### S4
- URL: https://hue.gov.vn/Portals/0/Uploads/VBPL/Nam2026/Thang2/00.00.H57-66-KH-UBND-2026-PL1_signed.pdf
- Publisher: Hue People’s Committee, Plan 66/KH-UBND, 3 Feb 2026, implementing the 2026 tourism-services program
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: Night product. The plan tells wards to keep completing services on the night streets Hoàng Thành, phố “Tây”, and Hai Bà Trưng. It aims to shape one or two night anchors at Hoàng Thành – Chi Lăng – Bạch Đằng – Bao Vinh, with the Perfume River, the Ngu Ha, and An Cuu. Ca Huế on the river and chamber Ca Huế are products to improve in 2026–2030; a chamber theatre is to be built on Bui Thi Xuan. The plan also drafts a free-bus resolution for visitors, talks about electric boats on the Perfume River and the Ngu Ha, and says the city will work with airlines on more flights. It gives no hotel street, no phố Tây address, no night-street hour, no boat wharf, no bus fare, and no flight time. The city’s target for average stay in 2026 is 2.1 days. That figure is a target, not a recommended itinerary.

### S5
- URL: https://admin.hue.gov.vn/Portals/0/Uploads/VBPL/Nam2026/Thang10/00.00.H57-3279-QD-UBND-2026-PL3_signed.pdf
- Publisher: Hue People’s Committee, disaster-prevention plan 2026–2030, attached to Decision 3279/QĐ-UBND of 6 Oct 2026
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: Hot season May–September, 35°C to 40°C, southwest foehn. Cool season October–March, 20°C to 22°C on the plain, northeast monsoon. Annual rain above 2,700 mm, over 4,000 mm at Bach Ma and Thua Luu, concentrated September–February, with November about 70 percent. Extreme flood and erosion follow from that concentration. Perfume River system about 104 km; early flood May–June; main flood September–December, especially October–November; the downstream plain floods in the rainy season. The coast is affected directly or indirectly by about 3–5 storms or tropical depressions a year. Northeast monsoon October–March brings cold damp weather, drizzle, fog, and rough seas on the lagoon.
