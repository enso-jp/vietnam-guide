Kind: city

## Why a night here

### The night on the south bank

- The picture they expect: the Perfume River through the old capital, with the citadel on the north bank and Ngu Binh, the Royal Screen, behind the monuments (S1). The morning frame is that river and those walls. It is not the airport.
- Where they sleep, as an area: a room on the south bank, in Phu Hoi, the ward that holds the weekend walking streets (S3). Toa Kham pier, where Ca Hue tickets are sold, is the river edge of that same evening (S4). No hotel brand. A room on the citadel bank is the other base, across Truong Tien Bridge. Whether that bank has its own night street is still open.
- Where the evening is: Pham Ngu Lao, Chu Van An, and Vo Thi Sau, a walking street a little over 1 km long on the south bank. The city opened it on 29 Sep 2017. Published hours are Friday and Saturday 18:00–02:00, and Sunday 18:00–24:00. Vehicles are banned while it is on (S2). Phu Hoi ward, replying on 9 Apr 2025, said the street still runs under Decision 5960/QĐ-UBND of 20 Sep 2017 (S3). The same ward, replying on 11 Mar 2025, said that on the night of 1 Mar 2025 the walking-street barrier opened earlier than usual because the urban-order shift was pulled to another incident (S11). The posted hour is the rule. It is not a promise that the street stays closed to traffic until 02:00. Weeknights are not covered by those hours. The 2017 opening said a later link, by 2018, would use Le Loi, Nguyen Dinh Chieu, and Truong Tien Bridge toward the north-bank parks and Dong Ba market (S2). This pass did not find a notice that the link is open. Do not send someone there as a walking street until that notice exists.
- Scene landmarks: Ca Hue on the Perfume River. It is the pin for the evening, not a ranked bar list, and this dossier has no drink price. Decision 21/2024/QĐ-UBND, in force 13 May 2024, requires a performance of at least 50 minutes, allows performances from 08:00 to 24:00, and puts ticket sales at Ben Toa Kham, with the registered price posted there (S4). The number is not in that summary, so the fare is Check on arrival. Boats moor between Truong Tien Bridge and Da Vien Bridge, at least 50 m apart. Pickup is at Toa Kham and other licensed piers. A walk-up guest is not to be folded into a group booking (S4). Which boat leaves at which hour is Check on arrival. The Imperial City is not the ordinary night. The city portal dates the free program “Hoàng cung huyền ảo” to 20:00 on 25–28 Apr 2026 only, inside Hue Festival 2026 (S5). Do not tell a traveler the citadel is open every evening.
- Hero must include: the Perfume River, Truong Tien Bridge, and the citadel wall or the flag tower in one frame, subject through the center. Ngu Binh can sit in the background. Not the airport, and not a tomb door.
- A crop that would fail: a panorama whose center is only water, the Noon Gate cropped so the flag tower is cut off, a statue cut through the chest, a fogged Hai Van road with no pass in the frame.

Sights, the nightlife entries, and the ordinary day are later lanes. This file stops at the scene.
