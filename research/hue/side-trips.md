### Da Nang
- Slug if it exists: da-nang
- Travel time from this base: Rail, about 2 hours 30 minutes to 3 hours 20 minutes on the SE board (S7). The heritage train’s own page says about 3 hours and then prints two different clocks (S8). Bus from Bến xe phía Nam: the station company, replying on the city desk, says the Hue–Da Nang buses run 05:00–19:00, about every 15 minutes, and about 2 hours to 2 hours 15 minutes depending on traffic (S22). No second timetable was found. The Hai Van road is about 22 km and nearly an hour if nothing goes wrong; the tunnel is more than 12 km and 10–15 minutes (S9). The door-to-door city drive is still Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Why go: The beach city on the other side of the pass. Not researched again in this dossier.
- Overnight or day: Day by rail or bus. Overnight if the beach is the stay. That choice is Da Nang’s, not this file’s.
- Sources: S7, S8, S9, S22
