## What the visitor does

- 100B Bạch Đằng, phường Phú Cát, near Gia Hội bridge, on the Hộ Thành river (also called Gia Hội or Đông Ba). One of three Nguyễn national pagodas still in Hue.
- Built up in the third month of Thiệu Trị 4 (1844) on the garden of the future Thiệu Trị. Follow the Vietnamese city page.
- The English page’s 1807 date and 5,000 m² line conflict. Do not use them.
