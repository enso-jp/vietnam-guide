## What it is

- English name: Tomb of Gia Long
- Vietnamese name: Lăng Gia Long (Thiên Thọ Lăng)
- Kicker: Pine-hill tomb of the dynasty’s founder
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- A family complex, not one courtyard. Built 1814–1820. More than 28 km², 42 named hills, from Trường Sơn to the Tả Trạch. Three zones: burial (honor court, seven terraces, Bửu Thành with two stone tombs), Minh Thành temple to the right, stele pavilion to the left with Minh Mạng’s “Thánh đức thần công”. No outer wall; pines are the boundary. Marker columns: 85 once, 42 in 1859, two left.
- Address conflict, print both: address field xã Hương Thọ, huyện Hương Trà; description xã Hương Thọ, thành phố Huế.
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Approach: boat about 18 km, or road about 16 km then Kim Ngọc ferry and a few km of forest track. Minutes — UNSOURCED
- Mistake: treating it as included in the four-site ticket.
