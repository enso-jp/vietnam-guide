## What it is

- English name: Tu Duc Tomb
- Vietnamese name: Lăng Tự Đức (Khiêm Lăng)
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Garden tomb with a lake and pavilions
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Garden tomb, Lưu Khiêm lake, almost 50 Khiêm buildings, two axes. Started as Vạn Niên Cơ, renamed after the Chày Vôi revolt (S14).
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- How long including the ride from the base: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Photographing the incense stalls and leaving. Also the old line “south of the citadel.”
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Two ward names, and the tomb is upstream.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: Adult 150,000 VND (S11).
- Child, height, or senior rule: Child 7–12 is 30,000 VND. Under 7 free. Half price for the groups in the resolution, including Hue residents (S11).
- What the basic ticket includes: This tomb.
- What costs extra: The four-site ticket is a different line, 530,000 / 100,000, up to two days (S11).
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S11
