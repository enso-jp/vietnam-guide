## What it is

- English name: Hue Citadel
- Vietnamese name: Đại Nội / Hoàng thành Huế
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Walled imperial court on the north bank
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Ticketed Imperial City. Ngọ Môn, courts to Thái Hòa, ancestral temples, Forbidden Purple City from 1804, Duyệt Thị Đường. Outer citadel about 10 km around, walls about 6.6 m, Imperial City about 600 m a side (S13).
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- How long including the ride from the base: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Buying only the Imperial City ticket and expecting the tombs on it.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: 200,000 VND is the Imperial City only.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: Adult 200,000 VND. Same price for an international visitor and a Vietnamese visitor (S11).
- Child, height, or senior rule: Child 7–12 is 40,000 VND. Under 7 free. Hue residents and the other groups named in the resolution pay half (S11).
- What the basic ticket includes: The Imperial City. Not the tombs, the royal antiquities museum, An Dinh, Hon Chen, or Nam Giao (S11).
- What costs extra: Each tomb is its own line. Four-site ticket (Đại Nội, Minh Mạng, Tự Đức, Khải Định) 530,000 adult / 100,000 child, up to two days (S11). Audio guide and electric cart are named with phone numbers and no prices (S13). Check on arrival — UNSOURCED as prices.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S11, S13
