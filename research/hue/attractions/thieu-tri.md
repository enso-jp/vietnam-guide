## What it is

- English name: Tomb of Thieu Tri
- Vietnamese name: Lăng Thiệu Trị (Xương Lăng)
- Kicker: The only tomb that faces northwest
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- Only Nguyễn tomb facing northwest. Làng Cư Chánh, xã Thủy Bằng, huyện Hương Thủy, about 8 km from the citadel. Perfume River in front. No outer wall; rice fields are the wall. Built in about three months. Tự Đức checked it 14 Jun 1848. Tomb and temple are parallel, not one axis. Đức Hinh pavilion; temple area about 100 m to the left.
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Minutes from the south bank: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
