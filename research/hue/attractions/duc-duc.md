## What it is

- English name: Tomb of Duc Duc
- Vietnamese name: Lăng Dục Đức (An Lăng on the Vietnamese Wikipedia article)
- Kicker: Duc Duc’s worship hall, on its own ticket
- Kind: sight
- Separate visit: yes. Its own line on Resolution 55, not inside the citadel ticket and not on the four-site ticket.

## What the visitor does

- Walks the worship hall, Điện Long Ân, in the court.
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- How long including the ride from the south bank: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Mistake: treating the four-site ticket as covering this tomb.

## Fee

- Adult 50,000 VND. Child 7–12: miễn phí. Under 7 free. Same adult price for international and Vietnamese visitors. Half price for the groups in the resolution, including Hue residents. (S11, PDF read 23 Oct 2026, row 5 of the per-site table.)
- Four-site ticket (Đại Nội, Minh Mạng, Tự Đức, Khải Định) 530,000 / 100,000, up to two days. Does not name Dục Đức.
- All-site ticket 600,000 / 120,000, up to five days. Includes this tomb.
- Source: S11

## Hours

- Open: 16 Mar–15 Oct 06:30–18:00, tickets until 17:30. 16 Oct–15 Mar 07:00–17:30, tickets until 17:00.
- Closed: no weekly closing day on the 12 Mar 2024 notice.
- The notice URL timed out again on 23 Oct 2026 (HTTP 504). The clocks are the text retrieved 21 Oct 2026 (S12). Public line says confirm the board.
- The notice does not name Dục Đức. It is the centre’s visiting-point notice, and this tomb is a centre ticket site.
