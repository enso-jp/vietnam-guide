## What it is

## What the visitor does

- Beside Tu Duc, uncle and adoptive father. Died at 25. Hall began as Truy Tư (second month of 1888) and was renamed Ngưng Hy. Burial on Hộ Thuận Sơn, 30 m west. Khải Định rebuilt from August 1916; the hall continued to 1923.
- Address conflict, print all: Đoàn Nhữ Hải, Thủy Xuân; thôn Thượng Hai, phường Thủy Xuân; thôn Thượng Hai, xã Thủy Xuân.
- How long: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Mistake: treating the Tu Duc pair (adult 200,000) as the single 100,000 price.
