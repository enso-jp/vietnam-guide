## What it is

- Vietnamese name: Nhà thờ Phủ Cam
- Kicker: The cathedral on Phuoc Qua hill
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- Main cathedral of the Archdiocese of Hue, on Phước Quả hill, design credited to Ngô Viết Thụ. Grounds printed as 10,804 m².
- The same English city page disagrees with itself: 01 Đoàn Hữu Trưng, Phước Vĩnh ward, and 06 Nguyễn Trường Tộ, Phúc Vĩnh ward. Print both.
- Hours on that page only: grounds “24 hours” for a visit outside; weekday mass 05:00–18:00; Sunday 05:30, 08:00, 15:00, 18:30. Confirm on arrival. No second hours source.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Coordinates: none. Do not geocode.
- Route on the page: Đống Đa, right on Hai Bà Trưng, Phan Đình Phùng, left on Nguyễn Trường Tộ, then Hàm Nghi–Đoàn Hữu Trưng.
