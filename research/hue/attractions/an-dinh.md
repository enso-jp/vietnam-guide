## What the visitor does

- Khải Định’s private palace on the An Cựu, facing south. Residence 1902; rebuilt 1917–early 1919 with his own money. Crown prince’s house from 28 Feb 1922. Grounds 23,463 m². Three buildings remain: gate, octagonal Trung Lập (bronze of 1920), Khải Tường tower, 745 m².
- Address conflict: 179 Phan Đình Phùng, Phú Nhuận, and số 97 Phan Đình Phùng.
