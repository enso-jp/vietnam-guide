## What the visitor does

- Dương Xuân Thượng III, Thủy Xuân. Pine hillside, about 8 acres, curved ditch. About 5 km from the centre. Route: Điện Biên Phủ, then Lê Ngô Cát, then the sign. Tự Đức gave the name. Resting place of Nguyễn eunuchs.
- Vietnam Tourism (S17) names it as the monastery where Thích Nhất Hạnh was a novice.
