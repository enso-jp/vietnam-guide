- Why it is this place, not a generic label: Ta Dung reservoir and its island skyline are the “plateau Ha Long” image on provincial pages (S10).

### Volcanic caves and craters
- Why it is this place, not a generic label: Krong No’s lava tubes and Nam Kar crater are UNESCO geopark highlights; self-entry to many caves is banned (S5, S11, S12).
- Fits interest key, if any: caves
- Icon: view
- Links to attraction, dish, or place slug: attractions `volcanic-cave-c9`, `nam-kar-volcano`
- Food guide card: no
- Photo must show: a lawful cave mouth or basalt crater rim, not an illegal C7 descent
- Sources: S5, S11, S12
