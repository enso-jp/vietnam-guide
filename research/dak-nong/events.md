### Hoi Xuan Lieng Nung
- Vietnamese name: Hội Xuân Liêng Nung
- Kind: festival
- When: Annual when spring returns — Check on arrival. The provincial tourism blog describes it as a yearly spring event without a fixed Gregorian date (S6).
- Where: Gia Nghia area, Dak Nong province
- What happens: M'Nong and Ma ritual re-enactments, folk games, gong culture, and specialty foods such as com lam and grilled fish (S6).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: yes
- Sources: S6

### Long Tong festival
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Lồng tông
- Kind: festival
- When: Lunar calendar — example given as 10th day of the first lunar month (31 Jan 2023 solar in one report) — Check on arrival (S7).
- Where: Nam Tan village, Nam Da commune, Krong No district
- What happens: Tay and Nung spring rice festival with a tall neu tree, offerings, and folk games (S7).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: yes
- Sources: S7
