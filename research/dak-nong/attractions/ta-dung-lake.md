## What it is

- English name: Ta Dung Lake
- Vietnamese name: Hồ Tà Đùng (also Hồ Đồng Nai 3)
- Kicker: Island reservoir in Ta Dung National Park
- Kind: sight. The park forests, the boat, and Granite waterfall are the same visit (S1, S15).

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: The park’s centre is the lake, the Dong Nai 3 reservoir at Dak Som, about 45 km southeast of Gia Nghia on QL28. You take a boat among the islands, then the forest and Granite waterfall in the same park (S1, S15). Mnong, Ma, and Kinh villages sit around the shore (S10).

## Season

- Handbook favours January–April after the rains for the wider province (S1). UNESCO wet season May–October fills waterfalls, including Granite (S5). Leave a lake-only month chip unset until a boat page names one.

## Size, where sources disagree

- Handbook: the high reservoir is about 22,000 ha, with more than 40 islands and peninsulas (S1).
- Vietnamese Wikipedia, Hồ Đồng Nai 3, opened 23 Oct 2026: water area 3,632 ha and 47 islands. Do not average 22,000 and 3,632. The handbook figure may be the park, not the water. S15 says the park is more than 20,000 ha, founded 8 Feb 2018 by Decision 185/QĐ-TTg, from the former reserve. Public copy, if a photo is ever filed, must print both area figures.
- Peak of the range: 1,982 m, ancient tea trees (S15). Granite waterfall is a geopark point in the same park (S15).
