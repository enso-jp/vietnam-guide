## What it is

- English name: Lieng Nung Waterfall
- Vietnamese name: Thác Liêng Nung
- Kicker: Basalt-vault fall near Gia Nghia
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: A short drive from Gia Nghia to a fall inside a hexagonal basalt cave vault, over the Dak Nia stream, in bon N’Jriêng, Dak Nia (S1, S29).
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- How long including the ride: About 9 km from Gia Nghia on QL28 (S1, S29). Minutes Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake: Confusing the fall with the Lieng Nung spring festival, or with Thác Gia Vĩ, a different fall in the same commune.

## Fee

- Basic price: Free on the tourism page header, “Miễn phí” (S29). The handbook does not print a ticket (S1).
- Child rule and what the ticket includes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Source: S29 for free entry.

## Hours

- Open, last entry, closed: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Blogs that say “all day” are secondary and are not printed.

## Getting there

### From Gia Nghia

- Mode: Car or motorbike, then a walk to the vault
- Distance: About 9 km on QL28 (S1, S29)
- Time and fare: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Coordinates: none on S1 or S29. Leave unset.

## Height, where the same visit prints two figures

- Handbook: the rock wall is 35 m, over a large cave vault of hexagonal blocks, above the Dak Nia stream (S1).
- Tourism page: the rock wall is 30 m on a large cave vault, and the water falls from about 35 m (S29). Print both 30 m and 35 m. Do not average them.
- The eco-site is planned at more than 84 ha (S29).
