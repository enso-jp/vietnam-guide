## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Look at a young scoria complex. The handbook describes three cones: one main cone and two secondary cones. The main cone is 60 m high, 220 m across, and the crater is about 20 m deep from the summit, oval. The peak is 660 m above sea level. The rock is scoria, each piece a few centimetres across (S1). UNESCO calls it a young complex of scoria cones, spatter cones, and lava flows, once mined for cement additives, now protected (S5).
