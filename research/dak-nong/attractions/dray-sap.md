## What it is

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Viewpoints over a wide sheet on the Serepok, in primary forest. UNESCO describes about 100 metres across and about 12 metres down (S5). The provincial handbook names Gia Long and Trinh Nu in the same cluster and puts the eco-site in Dak Sor, Krong No (S1).
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The city dossier suggested two to three hours and marked that figure unsourced.
- How long including the ride from the base: About 110 km from Gia Nghia (S1). Minutes Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating it as a short hop after a Ta Dung morning (S1).
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The handbook distance is about 110 km from Gia Nghia, so the falls are their own day.
