# Side trips — Dak Nong

Checked: 21 Oct 2026

### Da Lat
- Slug if it exists: `da-lat`
- Travel time from this base: About 180 km by road — Check on arrival for minutes (S4).
- Why go: Cooler hills, lake walks, and the usual pairing with Ta Dung in travel articles (S2).
- Overnight or day: Either. Many travelers fly to Lien Khuong and loop down to Ta Dung.
- Sources: S2, S4

### Buon Ma Thuot
- Or: Side trip placeholder: Buon Ma Thuot
- Travel time from this base: Handbook about 125 km; operators quote about two hours by bus from the airport area — Check on arrival (S1, S3).
- Why go: Coffee city, airport gateway, and the Dray Nur–Gia Long waterfall cluster on the Dak Lak side of the Serepok (S1).
- Overnight or day: Day or overnight if flying out of BMV.
- Sources: S1, S3

### Buon Don
- Or: Side trip placeholder: Buon Don
- Travel time from this base: Handbook puts Buon Buor village about 90 km toward Dak Lak — Check on arrival (S1).
- Why go: Historic Ede longhouse village on the Serepok, recognized as an ancient Ede buon in 2008 (S1).
- Overnight or day: Day from Gia Nghia or combined with Buon Ma Thuot.
- Sources: S1

### Yok Don National Park
- Or: Side trip placeholder: Yok Don
- Travel time from this base: Handbook notes part of the park lies in Dak Nong south of the Serepok but the main gate is in Buon Don, Dak Lak — Check on arrival (S1).
- Why go: Forest trekking and the Seven-branch waterfall on the Dak Lak access.
- Overnight or day: Day with a Buon Ma Thuot base is more common than from Gia Nghia.
- Sources: S1
