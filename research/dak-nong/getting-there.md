# Getting there — Dak Nong

Checked: 21 Oct 2026

- Nearest airport: Buon Ma Thuot (BMV), Dak Lak. The provincial handbook puts Gia Nghia about 125 km from Buon Ma Thuot and says Dak Nong has no airport of its own (S1). A Lam Dong tourism article puts the road distance from Buon Ma Thuot to Gia Nghia at about 135 km (S2). Lien Khuong (DLI), Da Lat, is an alternate; the same article describes combining Da Lat with Ta Dung (S2).
- Nearest rail station: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. This pass did not read a Vietnam Railways timetable for Gia Nghia.
- From the usual previous stop:
  - Ho Chi Minh City | About 250 km by road | Provincial handbook quotes intercity bus fares of about 150,000–200,000 VND and a run of about six hours (S1). Minutes vary by operator — Check on arrival.
  - Buon Ma Thuot (air or bus hub) | About 120–135 km | Handbook 125 km (S1). Vexere lists about 120 km and about two hours for airport-to-Gia Nghia limousine service (S3). Hoang Kim quotes 150,000 VND on one route list in a Lam Dong article (S2). Other operators quote higher night fares — Check on arrival.
  - Da Lat | About 180 km | A bus-guide article puts Gia Nghia–Da Lat at about 180 km and four to four and a half hours (S4). Lien Khuong is farther by road than straight-line distance — Check on arrival.
- Getting around on site: Gia Nghia sits on National Highway 14 and 28. Ta Dung is about 45 km southeast on QL28 (S1). Krong No and the Dray Sap cluster are a long day from Gia Nghia; the handbook puts Dray Sap about 110 km from Gia Nghia (S1). Hire a car or a motorbike for multi-stop days. Grab may work in Gia Nghia — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Season limit on the journey: The handbook calls January through early April strong for waterfalls and calls summer rainy with quick showers (S1). UNESCO notes May–October as wet season with fuller waterfalls (S5). Road closures in storms — Check on arrival.
