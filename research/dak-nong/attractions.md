# Research dossier — Dak Nong

Slug: `dak-nong`
Province: Dak Nong (visitor pages still use this name). National Assembly Resolution 202/2025/QH15 merged Dak Nong, Binh Thuan, and Lam Dong into a new Lam Dong province from 12 Jun 2025, with administrative center at Da Lat (S14). Record both for copy.
Kind: area
Corridor: highlands-coast
Base a traveler sleeps in: Gia Nghia town for Ta Dung and Lieng Nung days; farmstays near Ta Dung if the lake is the picture; Krong No side for waterfall-and-cave days with a long drive.
Checked: 21 Oct 2026

## Why a night here

- The picture they expect: islands on Ta Dung reservoir under cool plateau light, or basalt waterfalls smoking on the Serepok — not a beach and not a single city square (S1, S5, S10).
- Where they sleep, as an area: Gia Nghia hotels and guesthouses on the QL14/28 crossroads for a two- or three-day loop; Ta Dung eco-lodge belt in Dak Som for the lake morning. Never a hotel brand as a pick (S1).
- Where the evening is: Quiet town streets after a long drive — no sourced nightlife district. Festival nights and shared ruou can jars in buons, not a bar row (S9).
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): Ta Dung boat pier; EXPLORASOUND on Dak Nur hill, Gia Nghia — pins only (S5, S1).
- Hero must include: Ta Dung lake surface with at least one island silhouette, or Dray Sap’s wide white cascade with forest — subject through the center, color, daylight.
- A crop that would fail: wind turbines alone with no landscape; a cave interior with no scale; empty red dirt road with no water feature.

## Attractions (Sight, area of interests, zone, hub, etc.)

### Ta Dung Lake
- Vietnamese name: Hồ Tà Đùng
- Kind: sight
- Area: Dak Som and Dak Plao, Dak Glong
- What the visitor does: Boat on the Dong Nai 3 reservoir, called the plateau’s Ha Long Bay on provincial pages, with dozens of islands and cool air (S1, S10). National park forests and Granite waterfall lie in the same park unit (S15).
- Time: Half day to a full day with a boat — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Fee: Provincial park page prints “Miễn phí” for the park listing; boat and resort tickets — Check on arrival (S15)
- From the base: About 45 km from Gia Nghia on QL28 per handbook (S1)
- When to go: Handbook favors January–April after rains; UNESCO wet season May–October for fuller falls elsewhere (S1, S5)
- Mistake to avoid: Treating it as a same-day add-on from Da Lat without a night — drives are long (S2, S4)
- Local interest: mountains, photography
- Suggested slug: `ta-dung-lake`
- Suggested dot: 5 — the reason many travelers add Dak Nong
- Photo must show: islands on the reservoir from boat or viewpoint
- Sources: S1, S10, S15

### Dray Sap waterfall
- Vietnamese name: Thác Đray Sáp
- Kind: sight
- Area: Dak Sor, Krong No
- What the visitor does: Walk viewpoints over a wide Serepok cascade; UNESCO describes a 100 m wide, 12 m drop in primary forest (S5). Handbook puts the eco-site about 110 km from Gia Nghia and names linked falls Gia Long and Trinh Nu in the same cluster (S1).
- Time: Two to three hours on site — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED on an official gate page. Foody lists 07:30–18:00 hours secondary (S16). User reviews mention 30,000–40,000 VND historically — not confirmed here.
- From the base: Long day from Gia Nghia; often paired with Buon Ma Thuot approach (S1, S3)
- When to go: Wet season for volume — Check on arrival
- Mistake to avoid: Assuming a short hop from Gia Nghia after a Ta Dung morning
- Local interest: photography
- Suggested slug: `dray-sap`
- Suggested dot: 4
- Photo must show: the full width of the falls and forest
- Sources: S1, S5, S16
