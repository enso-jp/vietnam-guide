# getting there

Checked: 21 Oct 2026

My Son is a closed valley, not a station and not a town you change planes in. The sanctuary homepage prints these distances and no drive times: 20 km from Trà Kiệu (the old citadel Simhapura), 45 km from Hoi An Ancient Town, 68 km from Da Nang, 145 km from Hue (S2). UNESCO’s English text gives no kilometer figure. The Japanese summary on the same UNESCO page says the site is about 15 km west of Trà Kiệu (S1). Use 20 km as the figure the management site publishes, and do not pretend the two Tra Kieu numbers are one number.

The inscription coordinates are N15 46 25.835 E108 6 33.008, Duy Phu Commune, Duy Xuyen District, Quang Nam Province (S1). The homepage prints “Vị độ Bắc: 15 o 505”, later “15 o 515”, and “Kinh độ Đông: 108 o 573” (S2). Those strings were not used. They do not match the inscription, and a reading of 108°57' would not sit west of Hoi An. A map pin stays Check on arrival until someone reconciles them.

- Nearest airport: Da Nang International. The aviation AIP names it VVDN — DA NANG/Da Nang International, and places the aerodrome 3.2 km southwest of the city. The ILS is coded DAD (S12). The city aviation page calls it a branch of Airports Corporation of Vietnam, in Hai Chau, 1 km east of the center (S13). Those two official lines disagree about the airport’s place in the city. Neither line is the ride to the sanctuary. The sanctuary’s own distance is 68 km from Da Nang city, with no airport-to-gate duration (S2). Door to door from the terminal is approximate — UNSOURCED.
- Nearest rail station: no train enters the basin, and the timetable does not list a station named My Son (S14). The southbound board (Saigon toward Hanoi) does list Ga Trà Kiệu, between Tam Kỳ and Đà Nẵng. On the board checked this pass, only SE10 prints a time there: 07:50, marked ngày +1, with Đà Nẵng at 08:59 on that same train. The other southbound SE columns are blank at Trà Kiệu. The northbound board (Hanoi toward Saigon) lists Ga Đà Nẵng at km 791 and Ga Tam Kỳ at km 865 and does not list Trà Kiệu at all (S14). The sanctuary’s 20 km is to Trà Kiệu the citadel, Simhapura, not “from the station door” (S2). Whether Ga Trà Kiệu is that 20 km is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Da Nang remains the stop on every SE train in both directions. From there the sanctuary’s city distance is 68 km, and the road minutes are still UNSOURCED (S2).
- From the usual previous stop: Hoi An, 45 km on the sanctuary’s distance line (S2). Da Nang is the airport and the rail city, 68 km on the same line (S2). Hue is 145 km on that line and is not the usual morning start (S2).

- Getting around on site: the last stretch is an electric shuttle, not a city street. The 29 Jun 2020 ticket page says the entrance ticket includes xe điện trung chuyển (S4). The 10 Mar 2025 board note says those carts were carrying about 1,000 to 2,000 guests a day from the museum toward the Nhà Đôi area, and that a rail shuttle had been requested from the Japanese side. The note does not describe a train as running (S6). The homepage puts the museum about 2 km northwest of the monuments (S2). Whether a visitor may walk that 2 km instead of the cart is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. UNESCO says unidentified unexploded ordnance may remain in the buffer zone and has limited access off the presented monuments (S1). Stay with the route the site opens. Do not treat the forest as a walking trail.
- On-site hours that change the ride home: leave time depends on which close is in force. S3 says 17:00. S4 says visiting until 18:00, ticket sales until 17:00, museum until 17:00. S5 says 06:00–18:00. A bus or a driver timed to one of those clocks can miss the other. Confirm that morning.
- Season limit on the journey: UNESCO says the property is at risk from flooding and high humidity (S1). The Da Nang climate page says the dry season is January–September and the rainy season October–December, with rain concentrated in September–December, floods usual in September–November, and storms mainly September–December. It also says the mountains get more rain than the plain, and that the northeast monsoon brings flash floods and landslides in the midlands and mountains (S15). The sanctuary footer now places the valley in Da Nang (S2). A 7 Apr 2026 bus-center note refers to the administrative merger that tied the old Quang Nam area to the city (S10). This pass did not read the merger resolution itself. Do not send someone in on a flood morning without checking that morning. No source on this pass publishes a standing closure date for the valley road.
