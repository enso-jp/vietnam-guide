# getting there

Checked: 21 Oct 2026

My Son is a closed valley, not a station and not a town you change planes in. The sanctuary homepage prints these distances and no drive times: 20 km from Trà Kiệu (the old citadel Simhapura), 45 km from Hoi An Ancient Town, 68 km from Da Nang, 145 km from Hue (S2). UNESCO’s English text gives no kilometer figure. The Japanese summary on the same UNESCO page says the site is about 15 km west of Trà Kiệu (S1). Use 20 km as the figure the management site publishes for the citadel, and do not pretend the two Tra Kieu numbers are one number. The citadel is not the same sentence as the railway station.

The inscription coordinates are N15 46 25.835 E108 6 33.008 (S1). In decimals that is 15.773843 north and 108.109169 east: 15 + 46/60 + 25.835/3600, and 108 + 6/60 + 33.008/3600. The homepage prints “Vị độ Bắc: 15 o 505”, later “15 o 515”, and “Kinh độ Đông: 108 o 573” (S2). Those strings were not used. They do not match the inscription. The map pin uses the inscription conversion only.

- Nearest airport: Da Nang International, a branch of Airports Corporation of Vietnam. The city aviation page, dated 8 Jun 2020, places it in Hai Chau, 1 km east of the center, and calls it IATA class 4E, category C (S12). That page’s route list is from 2019 and is not a 2026 timetable. It is also not the ride to the sanctuary. The sanctuary’s own distance is 68 km from Da Nang city, with no airport-to-gate duration (S2). Door to door from the terminal is approximate — UNSOURCED. No published block time from Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City was taken from that 2020 page.
- Nearest rail station: no train enters the basin. The Vietnam Railways board checked this pass does not list a station named My Son (S13). The northbound board (Hanoi toward Saigon) has Ga Đà Nẵng at km 791 and Ga Tam Kỳ at km 865, and no Trà Kiệu row. Example on that board: SE3 passes Huế at 07:55 and Đà Nẵng at 10:43, both marked ngày +1 against a Hanoi departure. The southbound board (Saigon toward Hanoi) lists Ga Tam Kỳ at km 861, Ga Trà Kiệu at km 901, and Ga Đà Nẵng at km 935. Only SE10 prints a time at Trà Kiệu: 07:50, marked ngày +1, then Đà Nẵng at 08:59 on that same train. The other southbound columns are blank at Trà Kiệu. Kilometer posts are measured from the origin of each direction, so the two boards do not share one km number for Da Nang. The management site’s 20 km is to the citadel of Trà Kiệu, not a measured ride from Ga Trà Kiệu (S2). Station to gate is approximate — UNSOURCED. Reseller timetables were not used.

- Getting around on site: Cars stop at the gateway. An electric cart covers the last stretch. The 5 Jun 2019 service page says 12 carts of 6 to 14 seats, about 2 km beside the Khe Thẻ stream, and that the fee is inside the entrance ticket (S11). The 29 Jun 2020 guide says the same inclusion (S4). The 10 Mar 2025 board note says those carts were still the daily vehicle, about 1,000 to 2,000 guests a day, from the museum toward the Nhà Đôi area, and that a rail shuttle had been requested. It does not say a train is running (S8). The services index also lists a green bicycle, with no price (S17). Walk only after the cart, on the paths the board keeps open. UNESCO still records unidentified unexploded ordnance in the buffer zone (S1).
- Season limit on the journey: The city climate page dated 4 May 2026 says a dry season from January to September and a rainy season from October to December, with rain concentrated in September–December and heavier in the mountains than on the plain. Floods are usual in September–November. Storms are mainly September–December, with flash floods and landslides in the midlands and mountains (S14). This pass is 21 Oct 2026, inside that rainy season. UNESCO names flooding and high humidity at the sanctuary itself (S1). The 25 Feb 2025 history essay says the long rains run from the 9th lunar month toward the end of the year (S15). That lunar window is not the same sentence as the city page’s October–December. Both stay. Do not treat a dry-season drive time as a promise in a flood week. There is no My Son closure notice in the pages opened here. If the road is shut that morning, Check on arrival.
