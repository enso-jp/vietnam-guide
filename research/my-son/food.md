# Food

Checked: 23 Oct 2026

My Son is a sanctuary in a closed valley, not a town with a food street. The ticket rules still forbid eating inside the monument area (S5). The services index still has no market (S17). The 25 Feb 2025 history essay still names restaurants and local food as infrastructure the management board wants to build (S15).

Sixteen foods are sourced. Four short of twenty. Left out: a stall, an hour, and a price for each of them. Neighbor plates that Vietnam Tourism ties to Hoi An or Tam Kỳ, not to the valley, were not copied in as My Son specialties: cao lầu, bánh bao bánh vạc, cơm gà Tam Kỳ (S30). Bánh tổ in the 7 Feb 2025 ministry article is a Hoi An Tet cake (S31).

## Comprehensive list of local food to try

### Mì Quảng
- Vietnamese name: Mì Quảng
- What it is: Wide turmeric noodles and a small ladle of broth. Vietnam Tourism calls it the dish most often associated with Quảng Nam, with pork, chicken, shrimp, or a mix, and sometimes quail eggs or pork sausage, herbs, rice crackers, and peanuts (S30).
- How to eat it: Toss the noodles with the herbs and the ladle. Chili, lime, and soy sauce are the seasonings Vietnam Tourism names.
- When: VOV2 names it at a program that began at 16:00 on 24 Mar 2022 (S29). Outside that night, Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Area: Thôn Mỹ Sơn for that one program. No stall. The 24 Feb 2025 essay names the brand Mỳ Quảng Mỹ Sơn with no street (S22). do not publish as a pick.
- Price: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Sources: S29, S30, S22
