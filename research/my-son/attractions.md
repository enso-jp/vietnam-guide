## Why a night here

- The picture they expect: brick Cham tower-temples in a closed mountain basin, forest at the foot of the brick, a ring of hills behind. UNESCO describes an elevated basin in the Thu Bon watershed, towers of fired brick with sandstone bas-reliefs, from the 4th to the 13th century, the religious center of the Champa kingdom for most of that span (S1). The management homepage says more than 70 structures in a closed valley, and that time and war damaged the site heavily (S2). The 25 Feb 2025 history essay on that site says French surveys exposed 72 structures, divided into 13 lettered groups, and that a 1969 bombing brought down many of them, including tower A1, which it calls 24 m high. It says about 20 towers remain, mostly in groups B, C, and D (S15). UNESCO’s English integrity line says eight groups of 71 standing monuments. The Japanese summary on the same UNESCO page says 67 monument sites (S1). Those counts are not one number. Use the valley and the standing brick, and do not print a single monument total on the page.
- Where they sleep, as an area: Hoi An, which the sanctuary’s distance line puts at 45 km, or Da Nang, which that line puts at 68 km (S2). Not a hotel brand. The contact page and the services index do not list a lodging area inside the hamlet (S2, S17). The 25 Feb 2025 history essay treats hotels and restaurants as infrastructure the board still needs to build (S15). A night in Thôn Mỹ Sơn is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Where the evening is: not among the towers on an ordinary day. The 1 Jan 2019 hours page closes the site at 17:00 in both summer and winter, every day including holidays (S3). The 29 Jun 2020 visitor guide ends visiting at 18:00, ticket sales at 17:00, and the museum at 17:00 (S4). The live ticket site ends ticket sales at 17:30 and visiting at 18:00 (S5). The 2026 city tourism list also prints 06:00–18:00 (S6). Those close times disagree. Either way the valley is a morning and an afternoon. After the gate, the evening is back in Hoi An or Da Nang. There is no night street on the pages checked. Đêm Mỹ Sơn huyền thoại is not a night you can put on a calendar. See Nightlife.
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): the tower valley itself, and the My Son Museum at the gate. They are pins for the visit, not a ranked list. No bar and no restaurant on the management pages checked.
- Hero must include: one brick tower group, the forest at its foot, and the mountain ring, in one frame, subject through the center, in daylight. War-damaged brick may be in the frame if a tower still reads. Not the ticket booth, not a military photograph, not the night show standing in for the morning valley.
- A crop that would fail: a sandstone figure cropped through the chest or losing the head; a frame of forest, fog, or sky with no tower; the museum roof with no valley; a panorama whose center is only empty ground.

## Attractions (Sight, area of interests, zone, hub, etc.)

The visit is one ticket and one valley. The lettered groups are parts of that walk, not separate gates. A planning trip needs the groups named. It does not need eight pages.

### Main tower groups
- Vietnamese name: Các nhóm tháp
- Kind: sight
- Area: the basin, reached by the cart from the museum. The 10 Mar 2025 board note says the carts run from the museum toward the Nhà Đôi area (S8).
- What the visitor does: Walks the brick groups that are open. French lettering, from the management history essay of 25 Feb 2025: 13 groups, A through N. B–C–D had 27 structures and is where that essay says most of the remaining towers stand. A and A' had 17. E and F had 12. G had 5. H had 4. K, L, and N had one each (S15). The same essay says tower A1, 24 m, was brought down in 1969, and that work in 2017–2022 restored groups H, K, and A. Group G was the Italian restoration project. Tower E7, a boat-roofed kosagrha, was restored in 2011–2015 (S15). The architecture page says a group is a cluster: a main kalan, usually with its door to the east, a gopura in front, a long mandapa, and smaller shrines inside a brick wall (S16). Styles named on that page, dated 17 May 2019, include Mỹ Sơn E1, Hòa Lai, Đồng Dương, Mỹ Sơn A1, Ponaga, and Bình Định, on fired brick and sandstone (S16). UNESCO says the towers are fired brick with sandstone bas-reliefs, built for Hindu divinities and above all Shiva (S1).
- What is on the clock, not a second ticket: Group G performances at 09:00 and 14:45, and the hall slots at 09:45, 10:30, 11:15, 14:00, and 16:00, on the 2019 hours page and the live ticket site (S3, S5). The 2020 guide puts one morning slot at the foot of group G at 09:00 and one afternoon slot there at 14:45, with the other slots in the hall, and says bad weather can stop the group G show (S4). Echoes of My Son (Âm vang Mỹ Sơn) is the 15th of each month at groups B, C, and D, 09:00 and 14:45 (S3, S5). A Da Nang newspaper page that would have been a 2026 confirmation did not load on this pass, so the clock stays on those two sanctuary pages. Cham folk singing is Monday, Wednesday, and Friday at the group H slope, 09:00 and 14:45 (S3, S5).
- Time: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No management page opened here prints how long the paths take. The cart ride is about 2 km (S11).
- Fee: S4 (29 Jun 2020) prints 100,000 VND domestic, 150,000 VND international, 30,000 VND for children 5 to 15, and says the ticket includes the museum, the electric shuttle, a Cham folk performance, and the monuments. An on-site explanation is 100,000 VND a ticket, one ticket for 1–5 guests, two for 6–15, three for 16–30, sold at the shuttle stop, only on S4. S5 (live ticket site) prints foreign 150,000 VND, Vietnamese 100,000 VND, and a service ticket of 30,000 VND, prices said to include VAT. It says children 5 to 15 buy that shuttle service ticket and children under 5 are free. It does not repeat the explanation ticket. S9 (1 Jan 2019) prints only the two adult bands and says each includes the visit and the services. S6 (2026 city list) prints the same 100,000 / 150,000 adult bands and does not repeat a child band. Do not collapse the child line. Confirm the category on the day.
- From the base: road to the gateway, then the cart. Distances in `getting-there.md`. You cannot walk from Hoi An.
- When to go: at opening, if the heat is the problem. No hourly temperature was published. Be at groups B, C, and D on the 15th if the monthly show is the point, and at group G at 09:00 or 14:45 if that show is the point. Bad weather can cancel the group G show (S4, S5).
- Mistake to avoid: treating the lettered groups as separate ticketed sights, or arriving after the earlier of the two published close times. Also climbing the brick, eating in the monument area, and using flash on objects (S5). Stay on the open paths. UNESCO still names unexploded ordnance in the buffer zone (S1).
- Local interest: Cham towers, and the brick as a photograph
- Suggested slug: `towers` (already published)
- Suggested dot: already 3 on the published record — do not change
- Photo must show: standing brick towers of the valley, in color, with enough of the group that a center crop still shows a tower. A second frame should be a different moment: a doorway, carved brick, or the pedestal of a ruined group, not the same file and not the place banner.
- Sources: S1, S3, S4, S5, S6, S8, S9, S11, S15, S16, S20

### My Son Museum, the gateway
- Vietnamese name: Bảo tàng Mỹ Sơn
- Kind: area
- Area: beside the Khe Thẻ stream, about 2 km northwest of the monument groups (S2). This is where cars stop and the cart starts.
- What the visitor does: reads the site before the towers. The homepage says the exhibition gives the general picture before the visitor goes in (S2). The museum was built from June 2004 to March 2005 (S2). The 10 Mar 2025 board note calls it the gateway, in use since 2005, and the start of the electric shuttle (S8).
- Time: the 2020 visitor guide prints museum hours 06:00–17:00 (S4). No second hours page was found for the museum alone.

## Nightlife and evening

None recorded.

The ordinary gate is a daytime gate. Close times in print are 17:00 (S3), ticket sales to 17:00 and visiting to 18:00 (S4), ticket sales to 17:30 and visiting to 18:00 (S5), and 06:00–18:00 (S6). The services index lists daytime craft demonstrations, Cham singing, a green bicycle, a virtual-reality visit, and an audio guide. It does not list a bar, a night street, or an evening district (S17).

Đêm Mỹ Sơn huyền thoại is a show inside the monument grounds, not a street. A 2022 management notice set it on the 16th of the lunar month and gave one performance date, 14 Jun 2022 (S21). On 1 Dec 2025 Tuổi Trẻ quoted Nguyễn Công Khiết, then described as phó giám đốc phụ trách, saying the calendar is not fixed and follows dates when large groups are present (S7). Those two dates do not agree, and the later quote says there is no fixed calendar. Check on arrival. Do not publish the 2022 lunar date as the current night.

## Lifestyle

### A morning in the valley, and out again
- Vietnamese name: Một buổi sáng ở thung lũng
- Kind: lifestyle
- What a day here actually is: You do not wake up here. You leave Hoi An or Da Nang in the morning, stop at the museum, take the electric cart about 2 km along the Khe Thẻ stream, walk the tower groups, and catch a daytime Cham performance if the clock matches your hour. The hall slots run through 16:00. Group G is 09:00 and 14:45, and weather can cancel it (S3, S4, S5). You eat before you come or after you leave. The ticket rules forbid eating in the monument area (S5). The services index offers a green bicycle, pottery, hat-weaving, an audio guide, and a virtual-reality visit, and prints no price for them (S17). Fee for those extras: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The valley goes quiet when the gate closes. The evening is Hoi An or Da Nang, not a cafe street in the basin. No source names a breakfast street, a swim, or a cafe street on the site.
- When: opening until the earlier close you are willing to trust. See the hours disagreement.
- Area: gateway, then the basin
- Suggested slug: do not publish a lifestyle page. The place intro carries the day.
- Photo must show: the same daylight valley as the hero, not a night show and not a cafe
- Sources: S3, S4, S5, S11, S17
