## Why a night here

- The picture they expect: brick Cham tower-temples in a closed mountain basin, forest at the foot of the brick, a ring of hills behind. UNESCO describes an elevated basin in the Thu Bon watershed, towers of fired brick with sandstone bas-reliefs, from the 4th to the 13th century, the religious center of the Champa kingdom for most of that span (S1). The management homepage says more than 70 structures in a closed valley, and that time and war damaged the site heavily (S2). UNESCO says many towers were damaged in the Second World War and the Indochina wars, especially the Second Indochina War, and that the towers which remain have been maintained (S1). The English inscription counts eight groups of 71 standing monuments. The Japanese summary on the same page says 67 monument sites. Use “a standing tower in that valley,” not one of those counts, until the attractions lane catalogs the groups. The picture is not a city skyline and it is not a military frame.
- Where they sleep, as an area: Hoi An, the world-heritage town the sanctuary’s own distance line puts at 45 km, or Da Nang, which that line puts at 68 km (S2). Not a hotel brand. No management page opened on this pass lists a lodging area inside the hamlet or inside the property. A night in Thôn Mỹ Sơn is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Where the evening is: not among the towers on an ordinary day. The 1 Jan 2019 hours page closes the site at 17:00 in both summer and winter, every day including holidays (S3). The 29 Jun 2020 visitor guide ends visiting at 18:00, ticket sales at 17:00, and the museum at 17:00 (S4). The live ticket site ends ticket sales at 17:30 and visiting at 18:00 (S5). The 2026 city tourism list also prints 06:00–18:00 (S6). Those close times disagree. Either way the valley is a morning and an afternoon. The published performance clock on S3 and S5 is daytime (hall slots through 16:00, Group G at 09:00 and 14:45). After the gate, the evening is back in Hoi An or Da Nang. This lane does not map those streets.
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): the tower valley itself, and the My Son Museum at the gate. They are pins for the visit, not a ranked list. No bar and no restaurant turned up on the management pages checked. Details are the two entries under this heading. Đêm Mỹ Sơn huyền thoại is not a landmark you can plan. On 1 Dec 2025 Tuổi Trẻ quoted Nguyễn Công Khiết, then described as phó giám đốc phụ trách of the management board, saying the calendar is not fixed and follows dates when large groups are present (S7). Check on arrival.
- Hero must include: one brick tower group, the forest at its foot, and the mountain ring, in one frame, subject through the center, in daylight. War-damaged brick may be in the frame if a tower still reads. Not the ticket booth, not a military photograph, not the night show standing in for the morning valley.
- A crop that would fail: a sandstone figure or a dancer cropped through the chest or losing the head; a frame of forest, fog, or sky with no tower; the museum roof with no valley; a panorama whose center is only empty ground.

### The valley in the morning
- Vietnamese name: Khu đền tháp Mỹ Sơn
- Kind: scene pin for this lane, not the sight entry. The attractions lane still writes the tower groups. The published shell `towers` was not researched here.
- Area: closed basin, Thôn Mỹ Sơn
- What the visitor does: Comes for the brick towers in the forested basin, then leaves the same day. The homepage asks the visitor to use the museum, about 2 km northwest of the monuments beside the Khe Thẻ stream, before going in to the towers (S2). The ticket rules forbid climbing the brick, eating inside the monument area, and flash on objects (S5). UNESCO still records unidentified unexploded ordnance in the buffer zone (S1). Stay on the paths the board keeps open.
- Time: a morning out of Hoi An or Da Nang. Hours on site disagree: close at 17:00 (S3), ticket sales to 17:00 and visiting to 18:00 (S4), ticket sales to 17:30 and visiting to 18:00 (S5), or 06:00–18:00 on the city list (S6).
- Fee: not one line. S4 (29 Jun 2020) prints 100,000 VND domestic, 150,000 VND international, 30,000 VND for children 5 to 15, and says the ticket includes the museum, the electric shuttle, a Cham folk performance, and the monuments. S5 (the live ticket site) prints the same three figures, calls the 30,000 VND line a service ticket, and says children 5 to 15 buy that shuttle ticket and children under 5 are free. S9 (1 Jan 2019) prints only the two adult bands and says each includes the visit and the services. S6 (2026 city list) prints the same 100,000 / 150,000 adult bands and does not repeat a child band. An on-site explanation ticket of 100,000 VND, one ticket for 1–5 guests, is only on S4. Confirm the category on the day.
- From the base: not on foot from Hoi An. Distances and the cart are in `getting-there.md`.
- When to go: when the gate is open, and early enough that the valley is not only the hottest hour. No source on this pass gives a temperature by hour. Do not invent one. October sits in the city’s rainy season (S14).
- Mistake to avoid: booking a night in the valley because a show once ran after dark. S7 says that calendar was not fixed as of 1 Dec 2025. Also, leaving the maintained paths. S1 still names unexploded ordnance in the buffer zone.
- Local interest: Cham towers. The interest cards are a later lane.
- Suggested slug: already published as the place `my-son`. Do not open a second place.
- Suggested dot: already published on the place — do not change
- Photo must show: the brick towers, the forest, and the mountain ring in one daylight frame
- Sources: S1, S2, S3, S4, S5, S6, S7, S9, S14

### My Son Museum, at the gate
- Vietnamese name: Bảo tàng Mỹ Sơn
- Kind: scene pin. Not a finished sight page. The attractions lane decides whether it is its own row.
- Area: the gateway, beside the Khe Thẻ stream, about 2 km northwest of the monument groups (S2)
- What the visitor does: reads the site before the towers. The homepage says the exhibition gives the general picture of the monuments before the visitor goes in (S2). The board’s 10 Mar 2025 note calls the museum the gateway building, in use since 2005, and the start of the electric shuttle toward the Nhà Đôi area (S8).
- Time: the 2020 visitor guide prints museum hours 06:00–17:00 (S4). No second hours page was found for the museum alone.
- Fee: included in the entrance ticket on S4. S5 and S6 do not say so in those words. Check on arrival if the window splits the museum from the towers.
- From the base: this is the arrival building, not a second destination across town.
- When to go: before the cart, on the way in
- Mistake to avoid: treating the museum as the ruins. It is the gateway. The towers are the valley.
- Scene landmarks, if any, with a primary URL: https://mysonsanctuary.com.vn/ and the 10 Mar 2025 board note https://mysonsanctuary.com.vn/tin-tuc/ban-quan-ly-lam-viec-voi-doan-nhat-ban-vao-chieu-ngay-632025.html
- Suggested slug: do not publish from this lane
- Suggested dot: do not add a record in this pass
- Photo must show: the gateway building with the valley or the stream, not a document and not a statue cropped through the chest
- Sources: S2, S4, S8
