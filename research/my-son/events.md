### Echoes of My Son
- Vietnamese name: Âm vang Mỹ Sơn
- Kind: festival
- When: The 15th of each calendar month, 09:00 and 14:45, at groups B, C, and D (S3, S5). The day of the month does not move on those pages. A 2026 newspaper confirmation did not load on this pass, so nothing newer than the live ticket site was added. The pages do not print a cancellation rule specific to this show.
- Where: Groups B, C, and D, inside the sanctuary
- What happens: A 13 Jul 2026 note by Đức Nhân, from the Culture and Arts office, names the ginang and paranung drums, the saranai, a fan dance (múa quạt), a water-carrying dance (múa đội nước), and Cham folk song, staged by Phòng Văn hóa và Nghệ thuật (S24). Do not treat a 1 Jan 2026 New Year chapter list as this monthly program. That list was not the source used here.
- Fee: No separate price on S3 or S5. The 2020 guide says the entrance ticket includes a Cham folk performance (S4). Whether this monthly show is that inclusion is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Not the same as attraction slug: `performances`. The monthly show is the festival. The ordinary daytime clock is the attraction.
- Sources: S3, S4, S5, S24

### Cham performances on an ordinary day
- Vietnamese name: Biểu diễn văn hóa dân gian Chăm
- Kind: activity
- When: Hall slots 09:45, 10:30, 11:15, 14:00, and 16:00, every day except when the board cancels for operations (S3, S5). Group G at 09:00 and 14:45 (S3, S5). The 2020 guide places the 09:00 and 14:45 slots at the foot of group G and says bad weather can stop that show (S4). Cham folk singing: Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, 09:00 and 14:45, at the group H slope (S3, S5).
- Where: the performance hall, the foot of group G, and the group H slope
- What happens: The daytime Cham program included, on the 2020 guide, in the entrance ticket (S4). The live ticket page prints the clock and does not repeat the word “included” (S5).
- Fee: Included on S4. Not restated on S5. Check on arrival if the window sells it separately.
- Not the same as attraction slug: `towers`
- Sources: S3, S4, S5

### Đêm Mỹ Sơn huyền thoại
- Vietnamese name: Đêm Mỹ Sơn huyền thoại
- Kind: activity
- When: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED as a current calendar. A management notice set the show on the 16th of the lunar month and named one night, 14 Jun 2022 (S21). On 1 Dec 2025 Tuổi Trẻ quoted the deputy in charge saying the calendar is not fixed and follows dates when large groups are present (S7). Do not publish the 2022 date as 2026.
- Where: inside the monument grounds (S7, S21)
- What happens: An evening show with sound and light, inside the grounds. S7 names three parts: Thung lũng câu chuyện thời gian, Đêm Mỹ Sơn huyền thoại, and Ánh sáng chan hòa. Not a street and not a weekly gate extension. No licensed night photograph, so no festival record. The date stays unstated on the public page. The 21 Nov 2025 night in S7 is a past performance, not a date to attend.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The 2022 notice said tickets would be issued at the end of April 2022. That is not a 2026 price. Whether it sits inside the daytime ticket is also Check on arrival.
- Not the same as attraction slug: `performances`. The show is a paragraph on that page, not its own festival.
- Sources: S7, S21
