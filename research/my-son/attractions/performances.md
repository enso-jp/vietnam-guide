## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: After the cart, the hall runs at 09:45, 10:30, 11:15, 14:00, and 16:00 unless the board cancels for operations. Group G is at 09:00 and 14:45, and bad weather can stop it. Group G is the Italian restoration, funded with UNESCO’s backing after a 1997–2000 survey, and a 24 Feb 2025 conservation note says that group also uses laterite (S15, S28). Cham folk singing is Monday, Wednesday, and Friday at the group H slope, at those two hours (S3, S4, S5). On the 15th, Echoes of My Son is in groups B, C, and D at the same two hours. A 13 Jul 2026 note’s own text puts Echoes on the 15th and names ginang and paranung drums, the saranai, a fan dance, a water-carrying dance, and Cham folk song. It does not print 09:00 or 14:45, and it does not say the show is inside the ticket (S24). The evening show is a separate paragraph. Its date stays unstated (S7). No 2026 night was on the ticket information page or the 2019 hours page.
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No show length is printed.
- How long including the ride from the base: Same unsourced road minutes as the towers.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Staying the night for a date the deputy said is not fixed. Also treating the dance photograph as group G.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Đêm Mỹ Sơn huyền thoại has no fixed calendar. S7 quotes Nguyễn Công Khiết on 1 Dec 2025. Do not publish 21 Nov 2025, 14 Jun 2022, or the 16th lunar day as a date to attend.
