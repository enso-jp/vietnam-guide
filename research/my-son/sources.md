### S1
- URL: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/949/
- Publisher: UNESCO World Heritage Centre, My Son Sanctuary, inscription 1999, criteria (ii)(iii)
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: Tower-temples of the 4th to the 13th century in a mountain basin on the Thu Bon, fired brick and sandstone, religious center of Champa. Inscription location: Duy Phu Commune, Duy Xuyen District, Quang Nam Province, N15 46 25.835 E108 6 33.008. Property 142 ha, buffer zone 920 ha. Eight groups of 71 standing monuments in the English text. The Japanese summary on the same page says about 15 km west of Tra Kieu and 67 monument sites. War damage in the Second World War and the Indochina wars, especially the Second Indochina War. Flooding, humidity, and unidentified unexploded ordnance in the buffer zone.
