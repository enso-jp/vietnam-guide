# Research dossier — Dien Bien Phu

Slug: `dien-bien-phu`
Province: Điện Biên. Resolution 202/2025/QH15 left the province in place. The statistics office catalog prints “Giữ nguyên, không sắp xếp” for tỉnh Điện Biên (S23, S24). Resolution 1661/NQ-UBTVQH15, 16 Jun 2025, in force 1 Jul 2025, merged phường Him Lam, Tân Thanh, Mường Thanh, Thanh Bình, Thanh Trường and xã Thanh Minh into phường Điện Biên Phủ, and merged xã Nà Nhạn, Pá Khoang, and Mường Phăng into xã Mường Phăng (S22). The national tourism desk still prints tỉnh lỵ Thành phố Điện Biên Phủ (S35). The museum and the relic board now print phường Điện Biên Phủ, tỉnh Điện Biên (S1, S6). Both the city wording and the 2025 ward wording stay. This pass did not redraw the commune map.
Kind: city
Corridor: northern-midlands
Base a traveler sleeps in: A hotel or guesthouse in the Mường Thanh basin, phường Điện Biên Phủ, near the victory monument and the Nậm Rốm. Not a Sơn La prison-hill night. Not a Mộc Châu tea-farm night. No hotel brand.
Checked: 22 Oct 2026

This is the city of the 1954 battle, in the Mường Thanh basin. Sơn La (`son-la`) is the valley on the other side of Pha Đin. Mộc Châu (`moc-chau`) is the tea plateau further east. A tea-row photograph does not show this city. A prison-wall photograph does not show this city.

## Why a night here

- The picture they expect: The victory monument on Đồi D1, the basin and the rice under it, and the ring of hills that held the 1954 siege. The 2021 handbook says the monument sits high enough to see the city, the yellow rice fields, and the ridges (S8). It is not the Mộc Châu tea morning and not the walls on Khau Cả.
- Where they sleep, as an area: Small hotels and guesthouses in the basin ward, phường Điện Biên Phủ, which from 1 Jul 2025 includes the old wards Him Lam, Tân Thanh, Mường Thanh, Thanh Bình, and Thanh Trường and the old xã Thanh Minh (S22). The national tourism desk still says thành phố Điện Biên Phủ (S35). No hotel brand.
- Where the evening is: Phố đi bộ Mường Thanh, beside the Nậm Rốm, in the new urban block the city paper calls Khu đô thị mới – Chợ Du lịch Mường Thanh (S19). The relic handbook also puts a cultural program on the monument yard on Saturday evenings and on holidays (S8). Đồi A1 has a separate night opening on the relic board’s standing hours page (S6).
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): The monument on Đồi D1, Ban Quản lý di tích, the 2021 handbook, http://bqldt.svhttdl.dienbien.gov.vn/portal/DinhKemTinBai/2022-03/Tập%20gấp%20DT%20ĐBP%2021.10.2021_S9yoVRoMZUaQGDYs.pdf (S8). The walking street, Báo Điện Biên Phủ, 23 Mar 2026, https://baodienbienphu.vn/tin-bai/van-hoa/pho-di-bo-muong-thanh-lam-gi-de-giu-chan-du-khach (S19). Đồi A1 at night, the relic board’s hours page, http://bqldt.svhttdl.dienbien.gov.vn/portal/KenhTin/Gio-mo-cua.aspx (S6). They are pins, not a ranked bar list.
- Hero must include: The monument, the basin and the rice, and the hills around the valley, in color, with the statue through the center so the flag and the valley both stay in the frame.
- A crop that would fail: A tight portrait of the statue that cuts the hills, the blast crater with no hill, empty sky, a tea contour, or the Sơn La prison walls.

### Victory monument and Hill D1
- Vietnamese name: Tượng đài Chiến thắng Điện Biên Phủ — Đồi D1
- Kind: sight
- Area: Center of the basin. The handbook treats Đồi D1 as the eastern outpost of the Dominique group (S8). The 2025 ward is phường Điện Biên Phủ (S22). Older pages still say thành phố Điện Biên Phủ
- What the visitor does: Walks the copper monument on Đồi D1 and the ceremony yard under it. The handbook says the group is three soldiers and one Thái child, the flag reads “Quyết chiến, quyết thắng,” the sculptor is Nguyễn Hải, and the yard is about 3,000 m², used for cultural programs on Saturday evenings and on holidays (S8). The 2022 route page says this is the copper monument in the 50th-anniversary works and that the group weighs 217 tons (S9). The handbook does not print 217 tons. Keep the weight on the route page and the yard size on the handbook. Do not merge them into one specification. The same route page calls the hill a peace park with a view over the city
- Time: About an hour on the hill — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The handbook does not print a visit length
- Fee: The relic board charges 20,000 VND a person a visit a point for visitors 18 and over. Ethnic residents of the province, older people, and people with severe disability pay half. Veterans, people with a revolutionary-service paper, organized students and soldiers with a plan, people under 18, and people with especially severe disability are free (S7). A 2024 provincial television note says the electronic receipt runs at four points, and the monument is one of them (S33). Whether the yard and the hill are one point or two — Check on arrival
- From the base: In the basin. A walk from a central hotel. A taxi fare inside the ward — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- When to go: Morning, or Saturday evening when the handbook’s cultural program is on (S8). Day hours are the relic calendar in the A1 entry
- Mistake to avoid: Cropping the statue so the basin disappears, or reading 217 tons and 3,000 m² as one measurement
- Local interest: The monument over the basin
- Suggested slug: victory-monument
- Suggested dot: 5 — the picture the night is for

Half price for ethnic residents of the province, older people, people named in the cultural-access decision, and people with severe disability.
