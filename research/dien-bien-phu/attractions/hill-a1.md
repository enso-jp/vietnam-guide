## What it is

- English name: Hill A1
- Vietnamese name: Đồi A1 — Eliane 2 — Pú Lạng Chượng
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Hill with the blast crater
- Kind: sight

The night opening is the same hill. It is not a second page. Hill F sits immediately east and is its own catalog point (S8, S31). It is not folded into this page. The dossier does not call A1, the De Castries bunker, and Hill F one walk.

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the hill, the blast crater, the trenches, and the French bunker on the crest. S8 says the fighting lasted 39 days and nights and more than 2,500 soldiers died. French name Eliane 2. Local names Pú Lạng Chượng and Đồi Đồn Tây. S9 repeats 39 days and 2,500, and adds a tank it calls 18 tons, the crest bunker, the trenches, and a crater it calls 960 kg, restored in 2004. S8’s poem-page names the crater’s shape and does not print 960 kg or 18 tons. Keep both pages.
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The night window is 19:30–21:30, which is the gate, not a walk length (S6).
- How long including the ride from the base: Inside the basin, not the Mường Phăng road. Minutes — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Arriving 11:00–13:30, or going for the night opening on a weeknight.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Midday close, and the day ticket does not say it covers the night.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: 20,000 VND a person a visit a point from age 18 (S7, re-read 23 Oct 2026). Night adult ticket 20,000 VND (S32). The two lines agree. They do not say a day ticket covers the night.
- Child, height, or senior rule: Same half-price and free lines as the other relic points (S7).
- What the basic ticket includes: One point, one visit. No combined ticket (S7).
- What costs extra: Night entry if the day ticket does not cover it — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S7, S32.
