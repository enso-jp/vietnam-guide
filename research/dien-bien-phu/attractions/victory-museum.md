## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the campaign museum. S36 says the building opened in 1984 for the 30th anniversary, was reworked at the end of 2003, and then had five galleries, 274 objects, and 122 paintings. S3 does not repeat those counts. They are not a 2026 inventory. A panorama length was not opened. Do not publish one.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: 100,000 VND a visit a person from age 18, Vietnamese or foreign (S1, re-read 23 Oct 2026, citing Nghị quyết 13/2024/NQ-HĐND of 4 Nov 2024 and Công văn 5076/UBND-KT of 11 Nov 2024). S36 prints 5,000 VND a person for one of five sites at the museum. Keep both. Do not average.
- Child, height, or senior rule: Half price for ethnic residents of the province, older people, people named in the cultural-access decision, and people with severe disability. Free under 18, veterans, organized students and soldiers with a plan, and people with especially severe disability (S1). Papers must be shown (S1).
- What the basic ticket includes: That day’s visit. Not refunded (S1). S36’s 5,000 VND is one of five sites, not a band of the 100,000 VND ticket.
- What costs extra: Guide registration is named at the ticket desk (S1). A guide price — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S1 and S36. The gate on the day — Check on arrival.
