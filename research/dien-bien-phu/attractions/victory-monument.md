## What it is

- English name: Victory monument and Hill D1
- Vietnamese name: Tượng đài Chiến thắng Điện Biên Phủ — Đồi D1
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Copper monument on Hill D1
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the copper group on Đồi D1 and the ceremony yard under it. The group is three soldiers and one Thái child. The flag reads “Quyết chiến, quyết thắng.” The sculptor is Nguyễn Hải (S8). The yard is about 3,000 m² and is used for cultural programs on Saturday evenings and on holidays (S8). The copper group weighs 217 tons on the 2022 route page (S9). The handbook does not print 217 tons. Do not merge the yard size and the weight.
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. S8 does not print a visit length.
- How long including the ride from the base: A walk in the ward. Minutes — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. From the airport to the monument, S26 prints about 4 km and 10–15 minutes. S37 prints about 4.1 km and 8–10 minutes. Keep both.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Arriving 11:00–13:30, when the relic gates are shut (S5, S6), or treating 3,000 m² and 217 tons as one measurement.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The 20,000 VND charge is per point (S7). Whether the yard and the hill are one point or two — Check on arrival. S33 names the monument as one of four electronic-receipt points and does not split the yard from the statue.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: 20,000 VND a person a visit a point from age 18 (S7, re-read 23 Oct 2026).
- Child, height, or senior rule: Half price for ethnic residents of the province, older people, and people with severe disability. Free for veterans with a card, people with a revolutionary-service paper, organized students and soldiers with a plan, people under 18, and people with especially severe disability (S7).
- What the basic ticket includes: One point, one visit. No combined ticket is printed (S7).
- What costs extra: The museum is a separate ticket, 100,000 VND (S1) and 5,000 VND for one of five museum sites (S36).
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S7. Point count for yard versus statue — UNSOURCED.

## Getting there

### From the basin ward

- Mode: Walk
- Time: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Fare, if any: Taxi inside the ward — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED

### From the airport

- Mode: Taxi or motorbike taxi
- Time: About 4 km and 10–15 minutes (S26). About 4.1 km and 8–10 minutes (S37). Both stay.
- Fare, if any: Taxi 70,000–80,000 VND and motorbike taxi 50,000 VND (S26). Taxi about 80,000 VND and motorbike taxi 40,000–60,000 VND (S37). Not a city tariff.
