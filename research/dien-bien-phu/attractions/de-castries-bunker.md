## What it is

- English name: De Castries bunker
- Vietnamese name: Hầm De Castries — Sở chỉ huy Tập đoàn cứ điểm Điện Biên Phủ
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Command bunker in the basin
- Kind: sight

Separate from Hill A1 and from Hill F. S8 calls this the heart of the French command in the center of the old strongpoint. The guns and tanks seen from the yard do not get their own pages.

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Go down into the command bunker. S8 prints the flag on the roof at 17:30 on 7 May 1954. S9 uses the “heart” line and does not repeat 17:30. S10’s 15:00 on 7 May is the assault order at Mường Phăng, not this flag minute. From this stop S8 says visitors can also see the Mường Thanh bridge, burned-out tanks left in place, and French 105 mm and 155 mm gun positions.
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- How long including the ride from the base: In town, near the bridge. Minutes — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Using the museum’s 100,000 VND ticket, or the Mường Phăng 15:00 order, as this bunker’s clock.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: 15:00 and 17:30 are two moments.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: 20,000 VND a person a visit a point if charged as its own point (S7). S33 names Hầm Đờ Cát as one of the four electronic-receipt points, so the gate does charge it as a point.
- Child, height, or senior rule: Same half-price and free lines as the other relic points (S7).
- What the basic ticket includes: One point, one visit (S7). The bridge is not in the four-point list (S33).
- What costs extra: Museum ticket is separate (S1, S36).
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S7, S33.
