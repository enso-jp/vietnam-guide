# Getting there — Dien Bien Phu

Checked: 22 Oct 2026

The usual previous stop is Hanoi (`hanoi`), by air into the basin or by road over Pha Đin. Sơn La (`son-la`) is the overnight on that road if the traveler did not fly. This city is the basin after the pass. It is not the pass, and it is not Sơn La.

- Nearest airport: Cảng hàng không Điện Biên, IATA DIN. Vietnam Airlines’ Hanoi-to-Điện Biên page calls it Cảng hàng không quốc tế Điện Biên, in phường Thanh Trường, thành phố Điện Biên Phủ, about 4 km from the center (S25). The same airline’s return page calls it Cảng hàng không Điện Biên Phủ, at Tổ dân phố 10, phường Thanh Trường, thành phố Điện Biên Phủ, also about 4 km (S26). Keep both names and both street lines. Thanh Trường is inside phường Điện Biên Phủ from 1 Jul 2025 (S22). The national tourism desk still says sân bay Mường Thanh in thành phố Điện Biên Phủ (S35). The relic board’s pass article also says sân bay Mường Thanh (S12). Vietnam Airlines’ own note of 4 Mar 2026 says the airline was flying Hanoi–Điện Biên twice a day and added flights for Ban flower week (S27). Do not publish a second airline from an older commercial page.
- Nearest rail station: None named on the tourism desk’s transport lines, the relic board, or the airline pages (S25, S35). This pass did not open a Vietnam Railways timetable. Do not publish a station in the city.
