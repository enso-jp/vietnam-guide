### OCOP, cloth, and Him Lam Plaza
- Why it is this place, not a generic label: The flower-festival plan puts an OCOP market and a craft ground on the walking street (S14). The city paper says the street’s booths were built for local goods, OCOP, and highland cloth, and that most were not yet trading on 23 Mar 2026 (S19). The 2018 television survey names Trung tâm thương mại Him Lam Plaza in the old phường Tân Thanh as one of two rank-III trade centers in the province (S29). That is a mall the survey names, not a shop pick
