### Ban flower festival
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Hoa Ban
- Kind: festival
- When: The culture desk says it is yearly, in the middle of March, tied to the opening of the attack on 13 Mar 1954 (S14). For 2026 the province first planned 20–26 Mar (S16), then moved it to 6–12 Mar, with the opening at 20:10 on Sunday 8 Mar 2026 at the provincial stadium in phường Điện Biên Phủ (S15). The press note of 26 Feb 2026 prints the moved week, 6–12 Mar, and says the main events sit in phường Điện Biên Phủ (S14). Keep the standing “middle of March,” the first 2026 window, and the moved 2026 window. The civil dates for 2027 — Check on arrival. The blossom, not a fixed civil day, is why 2026 moved (S15)
- Where: Provincial stadium, the walking street, and the Mường Thanh bridge for the 2026 layout (S14, S15). A Thái food stop at bản Phiêng Lơi, and a separate white-Thái house day at Mường Lay, were inside the 2026 plan (S14). Mường Lay is not this ward. It stays a placeholder in `side-trips.md`
- What happens: Incense at the martyrs’ temple, an opening show, a xòe circle, street carnival, the staged piece “Huyền tích U Va,” folk games that include pushing loaded bicycles and pulling artillery, a food ground called “Hương sắc Điện Biên,” an OCOP market, and the walking-street opening on 7 Mar 2026 (S14, S15). Him Lam’s phase-two works were inaugurated on 9 Mar 2026 inside that week (S15). A ticket for the opening — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: `him-lam` and `muong-thanh-walking-street` are the places. This is the March week
- Sources: S14, S15, S16

### Victory anniversary, 7 May
- Vietnamese name: Kỷ niệm Chiến thắng Điện Biên Phủ
- Kind: festival
- When: 7 May. The handbook prints the flag on the De Castries bunker at 17:30 on 7 May 1954, and the Mường Phăng page prints the general-attack order at 15:00 the same day (S8, S10). Those are two 1954 moments, not two festival clocks. A 2027 program of ceremonies — Check on arrival. The relic board’s 4 May 2026 note is a staffing plan for the long holiday then, and it names the points that were receiving visitors. It is not a published run-of-show for 7 May (S31)
- Where: The basin relics and the Mường Phăng headquarters. The 2026 note lists A1, Đồi D1 and the monument, the De Castries bunker, Đồi F and the memorial, Đồi E, Him Lam, and the Mường Phăng headquarters among the points staffed for that holiday (S31)
- What happens: Visits and ceremonies. A parade script for a future year was not opened
- Fee: The relic point fee is 20,000 VND unless a waiver applies (S7). A 2018 culture-desk note waived fees for ethnic residents of the province on Tết (3 days), 2 Sep, 30 Apr, 1 May, and 7 May, at seven named sites, until the council amended an older resolution (S28). The current fee page does not repeat that waiver (S7). Whether 7 May is still free for those residents — Check on arrival. Do not publish one answer
- Not the same as attraction slug: `victory-monument`, `hill-a1`, `de-castries-bunker`, and `muong-phang` are the places. This is the 7 May date
- Sources: S7, S8, S10, S28, S31

### Soldier’s meal and the loaded bicycle
- Vietnamese name: Bếp Hoàng Cầm và xe đạp thồ
- Kind: activity
- When: When a group books it. A public weekly slot — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The flower-festival plan also staged bicycle, ammunition, and artillery games in March 2026 (S14). That week is the festival entry. This line is the relic board’s booked activity
- Where: At the relic points. The handbook names A1 and Đồi D1 for the bicycle, and the field kitchen at the relics (S8). The 2022 route page adds listening to a veteran and digging a practice trench (S9)
- What happens: A group cooks on a Hoàng Cầm stove, pushes a loaded bicycle, and hears a veteran if one is scheduled. Registration on the handbook goes to the relic board (S8)
