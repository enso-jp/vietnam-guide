Checked: 21 Oct 2026

### Trung Khanh chestnut
- Vietnamese name: Hạt dẻ Trùng Khánh
- What it is: A round chestnut, dark glossy shell, yellow kernel. Bao Cao Bang says the roasted nut is béo, sweet, and aromatic, and that the same nut goes into ham hock and into chestnut wine (S9)
- How to eat it: Roasted or boiled, or mixed into com. Score the tip before roasting so the shell splits — that step is on an indexed tourism note this pass could not reload, so treat the knife nick as Check on arrival
- When to order it: Autumn, on the eastern route through Trung Khanh (S9). Month numbers — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED on the page that loaded
- Area, not a shop: Trung Khanh / the eastern route (S9)
- Meal: sweet
- Neighbor dish to link: none
- Sources: S9

### Chestnut green rice
- Vietnamese name: Cốm hạt dẻ
- What it is: Young Pi Pat glutinous rice, boiled, dried, and pounded to a translucent green, then rubbed with pounded chestnut (S9)
- How to eat it: By the handful, as a seasonal sweet
- When to order it: Autumn, when the rice is still milky and the chestnut is in (S9)
- Area, not a shop: Eastern route, Trung Khanh (S9)
- Meal: sweet
- Neighbor dish to link: none
- Sources: S9

### Five-colour sticky rice

- When to order it: Festivals and ordinary highland meals — Check on arrival for a daily stall

### Roast duck with mac mat
- Vietnamese name: Vịt quay tẩm lá mác mật

### Corn wine
- Vietnamese name: Rượu ngô
- What it is: Corn distilled with a yeast cake of more than 20 forest leaves, rested about a month, described by Bao Cao Bang as the most famous drink of the northern kitchen (S9)
- How to eat it: A small cup with a meal. The paper calls a modest pour a tonic. Do not publish a drinking dare
- When to order it: Evening at a village meal
- Area, not a shop: Northern route (S9)
- Meal: drink
- Neighbor dish to link: none
- Sources: S9
