### Sour pho and the highland table
- Why it is this place, not a generic label: Pho chua is the city bowl Vietnam Tourism names, and Bao Cao Bang hangs chestnut, mac mat duck, khau sli, and corn wine on the geopark routes (S7, S9)
- Fits interest key, if any: food
- Icon: food
- Links to attraction, dish, or place slug: dishes `pho-chua`, `trung-khanh-chestnut`, `vit-quay-mac-mat`; food street `kim-dong-food`; neighbor dish `pho`
- Food guide card: yes
- Photo must show: a cold sour pho with herbs and fried threads, and a second frame of chestnut or five-colour rice. Not a generic beef pho
- Sources: S7, S9
