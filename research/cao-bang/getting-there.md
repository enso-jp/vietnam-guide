# Getting there — Cao Bang

Checked: 21 Oct 2026

- Nearest airport: Noi Bai (HAN), Hanoi. Vietnam Tourism sends travelers by road from Hanoi and does not name an airport in Cao Bang (S7). A booking desk states that no flight is operated on the Hanoi–Cao Bang route (S21). There is no sourced Cao Bang airfield on the geopark or UNESCO pages (S1, S2).
- Nearest rail station: None named. The same booking desk states that no train is operated Hanoi–Cao Bang (S21). Vietnam Tourism’s Cao Bang page describes bus and road only (S7). This pass did not read a Vietnam Railways timetable. Do not publish a station name.
- From the usual previous stop:
  - Hanoi to Cao Bang city | About 280 km, about eight hours (S7); redBus says about 282 km via the Bac Giang–Lang Son expressway and National Highway 4A, with operator times on that page from 4 hours 30 minutes to 8 hours 7 minutes and a listed average of 5 hours 49 minutes (S18). Easybook quotes about 240 km and 6–7 hours (S21). Record all three. Road time — Check on arrival.
  - Hanoi buses | Day and overnight buses from My Dinh bus station (S7). An operator yard in Cao Bang lists night departures from My Dinh and Gia Lam and its own address as QL3, Xom Khau Sa, De Tham ward (S19). That address is an operator page, not a transport-department notice — Check on arrival.
  - Hanoi to Ban Gioc | Vietnam Tourism puts the falls about 340 km north of Hanoi by paved national highway (S8). The city is the usual overnight break on the way (S7).
  - Cao Bang city to Ban Gioc | More than 80 km, Trung Khanh in the December 2024 management-board notice (S17). The geopark eastern route follows National Highway 3 east, and Nguom Ngao sits beside provincial road 206 (S3). Drive minutes — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
  - Cao Bang city to Pac Bo | About 55 km on provincial road 203 (S12). A provincial three-day feature indexed on the tourism site said about 52 km; that page was not reloaded. Use 55 km from the relic monograph and treat minutes as Check on arrival.
- Getting around on site: The city is the hub for the northern and western days; Dam Thuy is the overnight base for the falls (S7, S3). Hire a car or a motorbike in the city for the geopark roads. A local bus to the falls or to Pac Bo — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Grab or taxi inside the city — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Season limit on the journey: Vietnam Tourism calls October–April the dry season and the better time to travel, November–February cold, and May–September rainy, hot, and humid (S7). The falls are a separate problem: the management board says water is very low from the end of November to the beginning of April and asks visitors to come between 10:30 and 15:30 (S17). Vietnam Tourism describes a dam release on the Quay Son at the end of the dry season between 10:30 and 13:00, and fuller water from June to September (S7, S8). Mountain roads in storms — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
