## What it is

- English name: Ban Gioc Waterfall
- Vietnamese name: Thác Bản Giốc
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Border cascade on the Quay Son
- Kind: sight
- Area: Dam Thuy. The 10 Dec 2024 management-board letter, carried by Vietnam.vn on 12 Dec 2024, still says Trung Khanh (A2).

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Stand on the Vietnam bank and look across the cascade. A bamboo raft toward the foot is optional (A1, A3). A Ban Gioc–Detian cross-border scenic route is named (A1). Whether a visitor may cross: UNSOURCED. No visa rule in this file.
- How long on site: UNSOURCED. Check on arrival.
- How long including the ride from the base: Same day as Nguom Ngao and the pagoda (A1, A4). More than 80 km east of the city (A2). Drive minutes UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: A late arrival from the end of November to the beginning of April, when the water is very low (A2). Pairing it with Pac Bo or Phia Oac.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: In those low-water months, come between 10:30 and 15:30 (A2).

## Season

- Sight-specific months: Fuller water June–September (A3). Drier travel months October–April (A4) and October–May (A3). Print both.
- One concrete climate fact: Very low water from the end of November to the beginning of April (A2).
- Width and height: About 300 m wide and 35 m high, three tiers (A2). About 300 m wide is also on A4. The main fall is over 30 m (A3). Print both heights. An indexed provincial width of about 208 m was not reloaded. It stays off the page.
