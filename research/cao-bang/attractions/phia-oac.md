## What it is

- English name: Phia Oac–Phia Den National Park
- Vietnamese name: Vườn quốc gia Phja Oắc – Phja Đén
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Moss forest on a 1,935 m summit
- Kind: sight
- Area: Western road. The park decision names Nguyen Binh for the older Phia Oac forest (A2).

- The scene, in order: Drive from evergreen forest up into moss and elfin forest as the air cools (A1, A2). The summit named on the western route is 1,935 m (A1).
