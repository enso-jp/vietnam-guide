## What it is

- English name: Nguom Ngao Cave
- Vietnamese name: Động Ngườm Ngao
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Lit cave near the falls
- Kind: sight
- Area: Ban Gun, Dam Thuy. The 2023 article still says Trung Khanh (A2).

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk an electrically lit passage. Formations are named as a golden falls, a silver falls, an upside-down lotus, and terraces (A1, A2). The Tay name is tiger cave (A2).
- How long on site: About an hour or more for a one-kilometre walk is on A3 only. One secondary source. Public time field: Check on arrival.
- How long including the ride from the base: Same Dam Thuy day as Ban Gioc (A1). About 4 km from the falls (A1). Drive minutes from the city UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Budgeting the Ban Thuon branch as the short lit walk. From 2021 that branch is nearly 3 km, with climbing and wading (A1).
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The long branch is not the lit walk.

## Season

- Sight-specific months: Leave to the place. Do the cave with the falls, morning or early afternoon. The floor is wet (A1).
- Length: More than 2,000 m and three mouths, Nguom Ngao, Nguom Lom, and Ban Thuon (A1, A2). A3 says 2,144 m, with 948 m open. Print both. Ban Thuon branch nearly 3 km (A1).
