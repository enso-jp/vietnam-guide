## What it is

- English name: Truc Lam Ban Gioc Pagoda
- Vietnamese name: Chùa Phật Tích Trúc Lâm Bản Giốc
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Pagoda viewpoint above the valley
- Kind: sight
- Area: Phia Nhan, Dam Thuy (A1).

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Climb to the pagoda and look down on the falls, the Quay Son, and the fields (A1). A1 calls it the first pagoda of this line built on this northern border.
- How long on site: A2 calls the walk short and steep. A3 calls it a short climb. No minute count. Public time field stays a description of the walk, and the clock is Check on arrival.
- How long including the ride from the base: Same valley day as the falls (A1, A2). Minutes from the falls UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: A frame of the hall with no valley, or saving the climb until after the falls’ low-water window.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The viewpoint is the visit. In the low-water months the falls are still a 10:30–15:30 visit (falls letter, see ban-gioc.md A2).
