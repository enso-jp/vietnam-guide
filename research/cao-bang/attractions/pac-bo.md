## What it is

- English name: Pac Bo
- Vietnamese name: Pác Bó
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Special national relic at km 0
- Kind: sight
- Area: Truong Ha, Ha Quang (A1).

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk Coc Bo and the Lenin stream. Pac Bo means the mouth of the spring. It is km 0 of the Ho Chi Minh Road (A1). Coc Bo is about 80 m², and the mouth fits one person. He stayed there from 8 February 1941 to the middle of March 1941 (A1). He named the stream in front of the cave Lenin, formerly Giang, and the hill beside it Karl Marx, formerly Phja Tao (A1).
- How long on site: UNSOURCED. Check on arrival.
- How long including the ride from the base: A half day from the city, including the drive. About 55 km. Minutes UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Pairing it with Ban Gioc. The roads leave town in different directions.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Not the same afternoon as the falls.

## Other sourced details

- Special national relic on 10 May 2012, Decision 548/QĐ-TTg (A1). The period tied to the site is 1941–1945 (A1).
- A restored stone table, about 80 by 50 cm and about 60 cm high, stands outdoors where he worked. The original set has been at the Ho Chi Minh Museum since 1978 (A1).
- A2 describes the cave where he lived and the river where he fished. Fishing is one secondary sentence. The public page uses the stream naming from A1.
