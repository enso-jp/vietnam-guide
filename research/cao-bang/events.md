# Events and to-do — Cao Bang

Checked: 21 Oct 2026

These are not copies of the sight entries. Ban Gioc, the cave, and Pac Bo stay in `attractions.md`.

### Long Tong
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Lồng Tồng
- Kind: festival
- When: Early spring, often from the 5th to the full moon of the first lunar month (S13). Village dates differ. A 2023 VOV report remembers the 15th in Nguyen Binh and describes Ha Quang villages choosing their own day (S14). The year ahead — Check on arrival
- Where: Tay and Nung fields. S13 names Cao Bang among the provinces. S14’s Cao Bang examples are Ha Quang and a memory of Nguyen Binh. Do not pin one commune as the only site
- What happens: A field offering at sunrise (five-colour sticky rice, boiled chicken, banh chung, glutinous wine), a ceremonial first ploughing, then con throwing, tug of war, then singing, dan tinh, sli and luon (S13). VOV adds local games such as “mac cau” (S14)
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: yes
- Sources: S13, S14

### Ban Gioc Waterfall Tourism Festival 2023
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội du lịch thác Bản Giốc 2023
- Kind: festival
- When: 5 Oct 2023–9 Oct 2023 on the national tourism calendar (S15). A later year is not on that page — Check on arrival
- Where: Ban Gioc, inside the geopark (S15)
- What happens: The 2023 page says local culture, festivals, and Cao Bang specialties under the theme of coming to the Non Nuoc region (S15). It does not print a timetable
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: yes — the falls are `ban-gioc`. This is a dated festival, and only 2023 is sourced
- Sources: S15

### Then singing
- Vietnamese name: Hát Then, đàn tính
- Kind: activity
- When: No fixed public clock on the pages read — Check on arrival. It is part of Long Tong (S13) and part of what the geopark says a visitor may hear on the eastern route (S3). Vietnam Tourism says residents have been trained to perform it for guests (S22)
- Where: Villages on the eastern route, and at spring festivals (S3, S13)
- What happens: Then songs with the dan tinh lute (S3, S22)
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: yes
- Sources: S3, S13, S22
