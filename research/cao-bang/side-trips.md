### Ha Giang
- Slug if it exists: `ha-giang`
- Travel time from this base: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The relic monograph says Cao Bang could “advance west” toward Ha Giang (S12). It does not print a modern drive time. After Resolution 202/2025/QH15 the former Ha Giang province sits inside Tuyen Quang; the loop is still the `ha-giang` place (S10). Do not rewrite that dossier
- Why go: The limestone loop is a different road from Ban Gioc
- Overnight or day: Overnight. A day return is not supported by any duration this pass found
- Sources: S10, S12
