# Attractions — Ben Tre

Slug: `ben-tre`
Corridor: `mekong-delta`
Kind: city
Province on the page: the traveler still says Bến Tre. Clause 20 of Nghị quyết 202/2025/QH15 puts the old province into the new Vĩnh Long. Do not draw that border here. See `getting-there.md`.
Base a traveler sleeps in: the town bank of the river, or a garden homestay on a near islet. Not a hotel brand.
Checked: 22 Oct 2026

## Why a night here

- The picture they expect: Coconut water and the Tiền at the Ben Tre bank. The town sits on cù lao Bảo, one of three strips the rural page names (Bảo, Minh, An Hóa), cut by the Tiền, Hàm Luông, Ba Lai, and Cổ Chiên (S7, S12, S14). Boats leave the town pier into nipa creeks. The palms are the foreground, not an airport.
- Where they sleep, as an area: A river hotel on the town bank, or a garden homestay on the near islets the portal sends boats to (Mỹ Thạnh An and Phú Nhuận are the old commune names on that page) (S8). Cồn Phụng has houses on the islet, at the gate toward Mỹ Tho, if the night is the orchard rather than the town (S6). No brand.
- Where the evening is: After dark the portal walks Hồ Trúc Giang, công viên Bến Tre, công viên Trần Văn Ơn, and công viên Đồng Khởi, then chợ đêm Bến Tre and khu phố chợ Phú Khương (S8).
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices):
  - Hồ Trúc Giang and công viên Đồng Khởi, the town pins on that walk. Primary URL: https://bentretourism.vn/vi/detailnews/?id=news_3258&t=48-gio-an-va-choi-o-thanh-pho-ben-tre (S8).
  - Chợ đêm Bến Tre and khu phố chợ Phú Khương, the night market street on the same page (S8).
  - Cồn Phụng, the orchard gate on the Tiền. Primary URL: https://nongthon.vietnamtourism.gov.vn/ve-ben-tre-tham-con-phung-lang-que-yen-binh-xu-dua/ (S6).
- Hero must include: the river, the coconut palms, and the town bank in one frame, subject through the center. A boat or the embankment can sit in the lower third. The palms have to stay in the frame.
- A crop that would fail: a tight crop that cuts the water or the palms, a portrait of a candy or a statue that loses the head, a frame that is mostly sky, or a workshop table with no river and no tree.

### Con Phung
- Vietnamese name: Cồn Phụng
- Kind: sight
- Area: The Tiền, at the old Châu Thành gate toward Mỹ Tho. The rural page places it in xã Tân Thạch, huyện Châu Thành (S6). Clause 59 of Nghị quyết 1687 merges thị trấn Châu Thành and the communes Tân Thạch, Tường Đa, and Phú Túc into xã Phú Túc (S2). The operator page prints ấp 10 (Ấp Tân Vinh), xã Phú Túc, tỉnh Vĩnh Long (S23). Keep both addresses. Do not invent which map sheet is the boundary.
- What the visitor does: Boats the Tiền, then walks the Đạo Dừa ground of Nguyễn Thành Nam: Tòa Bảo tháp, Sân Rồng with nine dragons for the nine Mekong mouths, and Cửu Trùng đài (S7). Watches coconut candy cooked, rice paper on the griddle, and coconut-shell craft. The rural page says more than 500 craft objects (S7). Sits for honey tea, đờn ca tài tử, a horse cart under the palms, and a sampan in a nipa creek (S6). The islet is one of four the pages nickname Long, Lân, Quy, and Phụng (S6). Vietnam Tourism places Cù lao Long on this side of the river, by a short ferry (S29). Lân is still not assigned a bank.
- Time: A river day. The national page does not print a minute count. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED for a timed ticket.
- Fee: Check on arrival. The operator page prints a gate of 50,000 VND for an adult and 30,000 VND for a child, hours 7:30–18:00, and an ecology tour at 149,000 VND (S23). The national rural page prints no tariff (S6). One operator figure. Do not pick it for the page.
- From the base: 12 km by road and 25 km by river from the city center (S6). The operator page prints about 10 km (S23). Record both road figures. The town orchard boat on the portal is a different landing, bến tàu du lịch thành phố Bến Tre, for the near-city creeks (S8). Do not treat that pier as the Cồn Phụng wharf unless a page says so. None did.
- When to go: Morning, so the boat is not the last hour of a same-day return to Ho Chi Minh City. The road figures from that city are 1.5 hours, about 2 hours, or 1.5–2 hours (S14, S25, S26).
- Mistake to avoid: Calling the four islets one island, or calling this cù lao Thới Sơn. Thới Sơn is the Mỹ Tho side and stays on `my-tho`. Also booking the Friday town cart as the way onto the islet (S8).
- Local interest: Xứ dừa, the river day
- Suggested slug: con-phung
- Suggested dot: 5 — the picture the river day is booked for
- Photo must show: a sampan or a small boat, nipa or coconut palms, and the river in one frame, subject through the center. A plate of candy alone fails.

- Sources: S2, S6, S7, S8, S23
