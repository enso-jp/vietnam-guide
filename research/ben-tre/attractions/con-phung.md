## What it is

- Kicker (one line, what it is): Orchard islet of the Coconut Monk on the Tiền
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Tòa Bảo tháp, Sân Rồng with nine dragons for the nine Mekong mouths, Cửu Trùng đài, then coconut candy, rice paper, more than 500 shell-craft objects, honey tea, đờn ca tài tử, a horse cart, and a sampan in a nipa creek (S6).
- How long on site: UNSOURCED. No minute count. Treat it as a river day.
- How long including the ride from the base: UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating this as cù lao Thới Sơn, or taking the Friday town cart, or using bến tàu du lịch thành phố Bến Tre. Those are different landings (S6, S8).
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Not Thới Sơn. The town pier is different. The Friday cart does not reach it.
