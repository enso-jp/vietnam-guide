## Events and to-do

### Coconut festival
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Dừa, Festival Dừa
- Kind: festival
- When: The national tourism extract says the coconut festival was raised to a national festival, held every two years around 30/04 (S12). Báo Đồng Khởi, 27 Mar 2019, says the first was 2009, then 2010, 2012, 2015, and a fifth edition then planned for November 2019 (S22). Those lists do not match a clean two-year gap or a fixed 30/04. Record both. A date after 2019 was not opened. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED for the next edition.
- Where: The national extract says the city (S12). A ground inside the city was not in that extract.
- What happens: Village coconut fairs, a product show, a trade fair, talks on the coconut chain, and orchard boat routes (S12). The 2009 edition, on the newspaper extract, had a craft contest and a “largest coconut object” show (S22).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: `con-phung` is the islet on an ordinary day. This is the festival.
- Sources: S12, S22

### Culture day
- Vietnamese name: Ngày hội truyền thống văn hóa 01/7
- Kind: festival
- When: 01/07 dương lịch, the birth date printed as 01/07/1822, with the program from 30/06 to 02/07 (S10). The national extract names the same 01/07 at An Đức (S12). The next year’s poster was not opened. The date itself is the annual day on the culture site (S10).
- Where: The tomb and memorial at old xã An Đức, Ba Tri (S10). Clause 83 merges that commune into xã Ba Tri (S2).
- What happens: Đờn ca tài tử, costume play of Lục Vân Tiên and Kiều Nguyệt Nga, recited verse, drum, traditional martial arts, a feast, and yard games (S10).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: `nguyen-dinh-chieu` is the ground on an ordinary day.
- Sources: S2, S10, S12
