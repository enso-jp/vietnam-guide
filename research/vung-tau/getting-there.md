# Getting there — Vung Tau

Slug: `vung-tau`
Checked: 21 Oct 2026

- Nearest airport: No airport is named on the Vietnam Tourism Vung Tau pages (S1, S2). The usual previous stop is Ho Chi Minh City. Vietnam Tourism names Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN) as that city’s airport, 7 km from the centre on the city page and 8 km on the airports guide (S16, S17). Minutes from Tan Son Nhat to Vung Tau: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The airports guide’s “50 minutes or more” is the ride into Ho Chi Minh City centre, not to Vung Tau (S16).
- Nearest rail station: None is named for Vung Tau. Vietnam Tourism’s Vung Tau transport note lists a bus and a hydrofoil only (S1). The Ho Chi Minh City page says travellers also arrive in that city by train (S17). The Saigon station name, and the onward road or boat, were not on the pages opened here. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- From the usual previous stop: Ho Chi Minh City. The provincial catalogue prints about 100 km by the Long Thanh–Ho Chi Minh City expressway, and about 95 km from Bien Hoa (S4). Vietnam Tourism prints “just 100 kilometers” and “just two hours” (S1, S2). A separate driving-minute table was not on those pages. Treat “about two hours” as the tourism-board figure, and traffic as Check on arrival.

- Getting around on site: Vietnam Tourism says a motorbike taxi can reach the Christ statue, and a taxi covers the about 40-minute ride to the Monkey Temple and Long Hai (S1, S2). The provincial one-day sketch says a private vehicle or a hired motorbike for a morning loop (S18). Fares: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Walks that the sources describe are the Back Beach sand, the seafront pavement, and the hill paths to Villa Blanche and the statue (S1, S2).
- Season limit on the journey: GreenlinesDP says it does not compensate when weather, tide, a natural disaster, or a technical or security hold delays or cancels a boat (S15).
