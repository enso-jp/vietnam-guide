Not the same site as `thich-ca` (Thích Ca Phật Đài, 610 Trần Phú, Big Mountain). Niết Bàn Tịnh Xá is on Small Mountain above Pineapple Beach, 60/7 Hạ Long (A1, A2). Both have a 12 m reclining Buddha; the photographs are different buildings.

## What it is

- English name: Niet Ban Tinh Xa
- Vietnamese name: Niết Bàn Tịnh Xá
- Kicker: Reclining Buddha temple on the Small Mountain shore
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Climb 37 steps to the main hall, where a 12 m reclining Buddha lies at the centre, head on the right hand to the north, feet to the south, the soles carved with marks (A1). Then go up to the upper level cut into the hillside, a terrace with flowers and a view over the sea and Pineapple Beach bay (A1). Behind it is the Prajna boat (thuyền Bát Nhã), shaped as a large dragon and covered in porcelain mosaic, holding fresh water and fish (A1). The bell tower beside the boat court holds a bronze bell 2.8 m tall and 3,500 kg (A1).
- How long on site: UNSOURCED. Check on arrival.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Mixing it up with Thích Ca Phật Đài on Big Mountain, which also has a 12 m reclining figure (dossier S8). Budgeting the Christ stair count; here it is 37 steps to the main hall (A1).
- Notice: None needed.

## Facts

- Mid Pineapple Beach (Bãi Dứa), on the Small Mountain slope facing the East Sea (A1)
- Built 1969, finished 1974, on nearly 10,000 m2 (A1)
- Ranked relic 1989, 60/7 Hạ Long (A2)
- Gilded seated Buddhas on the upper altar glow in afternoon sun (A1, “rực rỡ trong nắng chiều”)
