## History (for the body)

- Built by the French in 1862 at 149 m above the sea; rebuilt by the French in 1913 and moved up to 170 m (A2).
- Tower: cylindrical, 18 m, about 3 m across, painted white; a stair inside leads near the top, with a balcony (A2).
- Vietnam Tourism calls it the early-twentieth-century lighthouse and puts it not far from the Christ statue (A1). The provincial one-day text calls it the oldest lighthouse in Vietnam (dossier S18). A2 says one of the oldest in Vietnam and Southeast Asia. Page wording: “one of the oldest”, not “the oldest”.
- 1862 (first build) and “early twentieth century” (Vietnam Tourism) agree once the 1913 rebuild is read. Both kept; the page prints both dates.
