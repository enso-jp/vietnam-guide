# Attractions — Vung Tau

Slug: `vung-tau`
Checked: 21 Oct 2026

Province on the pages: the tourism catalogue and the museum still print Ba Ria–Vung Tau (S4, S5, S6). The Ho May operator and Tuoi Tre’s 2026 festival report print Phường Vũng Tàu, Thành phố Hồ Chí Minh (S9, S14). The merger decision was not opened.

## Why a night here

### The beach, then the white figure

- The picture they expect: Wake up to Back Beach. Sand, a low surf, and the hotel line along the strand, with Small Mountain closing the bay (S1, S2). The other picture, and the one a tight crop ruins, is the white Christ figure on that headland, arms out, sea below (S1, S2). A second white figure, Mary holding a child on Big Mountain, is the same height in the provincial text and is not the same statue (S4).
- Where they sleep, as an area: Beachfront hotels along Back Beach, where Vietnam Tourism puts the high-end rooms, restaurants, and beach clubs (S1). Front Beach is the other seafront, with buildings along the arc between the two mountains (S2, S4). No hotel brand.
- Where the evening is: Front Beach for a drink after dark (S2). Back Beach for the night seafood market, where you choose the fish and the cooking (S2). The 2026 whale-festival procession used Quang Trung, Truong Cong Dinh, and Hoang Hoa Tham between Front Beach and the whale hall (S14).
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): Christ the King on Small Mountain, free on the Vietnam Tourism page (S1), https://www.vietnam.travel/things-to-do/essential-vung-tau-guide . Bach Dinh on Big Mountain, a late-1800s villa that is now a museum (S1, S2, S4), catalogue https://diemden.baria-vungtau.gov.vn/places . Ho May cable-car station, 1A Tran Phu, the operator’s own address (S9), https://homaypark.com/san-pham/ve-tham-quan-vui-choi-tron-goi-ho-may-park/ . Pins, not a bar list.
- Hero must include: The surf, the sand, and the hotel line in one frame, subject through the center, with Small Mountain and the full Christ figure still in the frame if the lens can hold them. If the hero is the statue, both arms, the head, and the sea, subject through the center. A landscape card of a standing figure fails when the crop takes the head or an arm.
- A crop that would fail: Empty sky, empty sand, a slice of the torso, fog with no shoreline, or only the ships offshore. Mary on Big Mountain is the wrong statue for a Christ crop.

- Vietnamese name: Bãi Sau
- Kind: area
- Area: Southeast side of the peninsula, the sunrise side (S4)
- What the visitor does: Walks the sand at the start of the day, swims, rents an umbrella, eats seafood in sight of the water, or tries surf, stand-up paddle, or kite surf (S1, S2). Vietnam Tourism calls the waves fairly flat and suitable for children (S1).
- Time: A morning to a full beach day (S1). A clock time was not printed.
- Fee: The sand is not given a ticket on these pages. Umbrella hire: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Water-sport hire: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- From the base: This is the base if you sleep on the beachfront (S1).
- When to go: Early morning for the walk (S1). Midday is the umbrella hours on that same page.
- Mistake to avoid: Using this as a short photo stop and missing that it is the swim. Length disagrees and was not copied as one number: Vietnam Tourism prints 3 km (S1); a 2015 national-tourism news page that named nearly 10 km was not opened. The province extract also lists Thuy Van and Chi Linh beside Back Beach, without saying they are the same sand (S20).

### Bach Dinh
- Vietnamese name: Bạch Dinh (Villa Blanche)
- Kind: sight
- Area: Big Mountain, above the Front Beach side. Relic list: 6 Tran Phu (S6). The museum building is printed at 4 Tran Phu (S5).
- What the visitor does: Stops on the walk toward the Big Mountain summit. Vietnam Tourism calls it a late-1800s house for the French governor, now a quiet museum of local artefacts, with gardens and verandas toward the sea (S1, S2). The catalogue says work started in 1898, in the same years as the Hanoi Opera House, Notre-Dame, and the Saigon Opera House, and cites decision 983VH/QĐ of 4 Aug 1992 (S4).
- Time: A short tour on the way up the hill. The summit trek is printed as 30 minutes (S1). Time inside the house: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The hours page prints no price (S5).
- From the base: The hill path from the Front Beach side (S1)
- When to go: Afternoon, on Vietnam Tourism’s line for the garden walk (S2). Gate hours disagree on the museum’s own page: Tue–Fri 7:30–11:30 and 13:30–17:00 with Sat–Sun 7:30–17:00, and another line Tue–Sun 8:00–17:00 (S5). Closed Monday is implied by both lines. Confirm at the door.
- Mistake to avoid: Assuming 4 Tran Phu and 6 Tran Phu are one ticket window. The pages print both (S5, S6).
- Local interest: Museum, war history
- Suggested slug: bach-dinh
