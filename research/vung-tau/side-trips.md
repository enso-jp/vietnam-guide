### Long Hai and the Monkey Temple
- Or: Side trip placeholder: Long Hai
- Slug if it exists: none. Do not create `long-hai`.
- Travel time from this base: About 30 km from Back Beach, about 40 minutes by taxi, on the Vietnam Tourism page that also sends you to the Monkey Temple (S1)
- Why go: A beach stop, and Chua Khi, a pagoda with nearly 200 long-tailed macaques a few hundred metres from the sea (S1). Dinh Co at Long Hai is a national relic, ranked 1995 (S6). The catalogue puts the Dinh Co festival on the 10th of the 2nd lunar month at the tomb hill, with the shrine in Long Hai town (S4). That festival is not a Vung Tau city rite.
- Overnight or day: Day
- Sources: S1, S4, S6
