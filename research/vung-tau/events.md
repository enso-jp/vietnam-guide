## Events and to-do

### Nghinh Ong Thang Tam
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Nghinh Ông Thắng Tam
- Kind: festival
- When: Tuoi Tre says the rite falls on the 16th of the 8th lunar month, and that the 2026 public days were 26 and 27 Sep, opening on 26 Sep at Front Beach park (S14). The next solar date moves. Check on arrival. A provincial page that, in search, mentioned a three-day window and a February 2023 heritage decision did not load. It is not used. Tuoi Tre reports the ward chairman calling the festival national intangible heritage (S14). The decision text was not opened.
- Where: Front Beach for the opening and the boats; the boats go along the shore to Hon Ba on Back Beach; the land procession uses Quang Trung, Truong Cong Dinh, and Hoang Hoa Tham, and ends at the whale hall. The communal house holds the indoor rites (S14).
- What happens: Boats leave at 5:30. About 20 fishing boats in the 2026 report. The sea rite greets Nam Hai and Thuy Long Than Nu and remembers people lost at sea. Around 8:30 the palanquin goes inland. On both days the hall holds offerings, the sac-than rite, the whale offering, hat boi, and a classical opera (S14). Sailing and a children’s drawing contest sat beside the 2026 rite (S14).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: thang-tam. That slug is the hall on an ordinary day. This is the festival.
- Sources: S14

### Hon Ba tide festivals
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Hòn Bà
- Kind: festival
- When: The 15th of the 1st, 4th, 7th, and 10th lunar months. The catalogue says the low-water walk that morning is 5:00–9:00 (S4). Solar dates: Check on arrival.
- Where: Hon Ba, Mui Nghinh Phong, Back Beach (S4)
- What happens: People walk out at low tide for the shrine festival (S4). Vietnam Tourism describes the same crossing without the calendar (S1).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: hon-ba. The sight is the rock on any low tide. This is the four festival mornings.
- Sources: S1, S4

- Vietnamese name: None printed
- Kind: activity

- Where: Back Beach (S2)

- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
