Checked: 22 Oct 2026

Public fields stay “Check on arrival” until a source is opened.

- A single gate fee, a gate hour, and a visitor-permit rule for the strict zone.

- A rail timetable into the valley.
- A minute count beside the 160 km and the 210 km from Hanoi.
- Whether homestays still lend bicycles without a charge, and any guide fee.
- A road-closure calendar. The airline guide’s July–August warning has no second source.
- 2026 dates for Căm Mương, the new-rice rite, the culture day, and the Vietnam Jungle Marathon.

- Whether gà nướng at the summit camp is gà đồi.
- What Vietnam Airlines means by xáo.
- Which leaves are in nộm rau rừng, and which leaf is in canh lá đắng.
- A method for xôi tím, rau bí, lá sắng, sâu măng, and xôi hấp gà Kho Mường.
- How long măng chua sours.
