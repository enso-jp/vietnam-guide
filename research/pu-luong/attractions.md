# Research dossier — Pu Luong

Slug: `pu-luong`
Province: Thanh Hóa. The reserve sits on the old districts Bá Thước and Quan Hóa (S1, S2, S3). From 1 July 2025 those districts are not the address line. Resolution 1686 puts old Cành Nàng, Ban Công, and Hạ Trung into xã Bá Thước (clause 85), old Lũng Cao and Cổ Lũng into xã Cổ Lũng (clause 91), and old Thành Sơn of Bá Thước, Lũng Niêm, and Thành Lâm into xã Pù Luông (clause 92). On the Quan Hóa side, old Hồi Xuân town and Phú Nghiêm become xã Hồi Xuân (clause 72), old Phú Sơn, Phú Thanh, and Phú Lệ become xã Phú Lệ (clause 76), and Phú Xuân of Quan Hóa is one of the communes left unmerged (clause 146) (S14). The new xã Pù Luông is not the whole reserve. Bản Hiêu and Son–Bá–Mười sit in the new xã Cổ Lũng, not in that commune.
Kind: area
Corridor: North Central Coast (`north-central`). Inland limestone, not the Hue–Phong Nha coast.
Base a traveler sleeps in: A stilt house in the valley, above all at Bản Đôn (old Thành Lâm, now inside xã Pù Luông) or around Bản Hiêu (old Cổ Lũng, now xã Cổ Lũng). S6 and S7. Not a hotel brand.
Checked: 21 Oct 2026

## Why a night here

- The picture they expect: Terraced rice on a limestone valley, forest above the fields, stilt houses, and mist in the morning. S1 and S6. Late May to early June is the green crop on those two pages. September and October are the gold crop. S20 calls both windows golden. This is not Lac village and not the Ninh Binh rivers. S13 lists Bản Lác inside a Pu Luong village roll. S1 and S2 put Làng Lác in Mai Chau. Use S1.
- Where they sleep, as an area: Stilt-house homestays in the hamlets. S7 describes Bản Đôn that way. S13 says the same for a night in a traditional house. Resort brands named on S7 are in Notes in `food.md`. They are not the reason to come.
- Where the evening is: The hamlet floor. S7: a campfire at Bản Đôn, rượu cần, xòe Thái, and khắp. S17: xòe for guests on ordinary nights, not only on a festival date, and the forest around it is quiet. No bar street was named on the pages opened.
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): The terraces and the houses at Bản Đôn (S7, https://thanhhoa.travel/ban-%C4%91on---ban-du-lich-cong-%C4%91ong.html). The roadside market at Phố Đoàn on Thursday and Sunday (S8, S9). Thác Hiêu (S10). No bar is a pin.
- Hero must include: Terrace walls, one stilt house with its posts, and the limestone ridge, in color, the valley through the center so a card crop still reads as a place to wake up.
- A crop that would fail: Empty fog. A plate of food. A resort pool. A statue-tight portrait. A Mai Chau longhouse or a Ninh Binh boat reused for this valley. A frame that cuts the house roof or the ridge.

- S4 cites 16,986.16 ha and then prints strict 12,561.60 ha, restoration 4,300.4 ha, service 216.03 ha, and other land 93.0 ha. Those four figures add to the 2013 total, not to 16,986.16.

- When to go: Year-round for water (S6, S10). After rain the water is white (S10). A swim has no posted hour.
