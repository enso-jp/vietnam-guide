## Comprehensive list of local food to try

### Co Lung duck
- Vietnamese name: Vịt Cổ Lũng
- What it is: S13 calls it a local duck breed of this valley, low in fat, eaten boiled, grilled, or in a sour soup. S8’s writer names vịt cổ lũng luộc as the thing to dip in chẩm chéo. S7’s Bản Đôn list says vịt cỏ and does not say it is the same bird.
- How to eat it: Boiled, with chẩm chéo, on S8. Grilled or boiled or in soup, on S13. S7 does not print a method for vịt cỏ.
- When to order it: With a village meal. A clock was not printed. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Area, not a shop: Bản Đôn and the Phố Đoàn market. Not a restaurant name.
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none on this site
- Sources: S8, S7, S13

- When to order it: At the homestay meal, or buy the dried shoot on Thursday or Sunday at the market (S7, S9).
- Area, not a shop: Bản Đôn and Phố Đoàn
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none

### Dipping sauce
- Vietnamese name: Chẩm chéo
- What it is: The sauce S8’s writer builds from rang mắc khén, fresh chili, ginger, salt, and herbs, then more mắc khén stirred in. That is one writer on the provincial page, not a certified recipe. S8 says it goes with boiled, roasted, and grilled meat, and especially with boiled Cổ Lũng duck.

- When to order it: With the meat. Not a dish on its own on that page.
- Area, not a shop: Phố Đoàn is where the writer buys the seed. The sauce is eaten at the meal.

## Food streets and markets

### Pho Doan market
- Vietnamese name: Chợ phiên Phố Đoàn. S8 also prints Phố Đòn.
- Kind: food-street
- What the visitor does: Stands at a roadside market. Sellers lay cloth, jar wine, forest greens, fruit, and snacks on the verge. S8 says some trades are still in kind. S9 says Thai and Muong people from xã Pù Luông bring thổ cẩm, hạt dổi, rau dớn, hoa chuối, dried bamboo shoot, and honey. Kinh traders from the lowlands sell clothes and tools (S8).
- When to go: Thursday and Sunday (S8, S9). S9 describes a Thursday morning in the mist. S13 prints a photo window of 06:30–09:00. The provincial articles do not print that clock. S8 says sellers from old Cổ Lũng leave at 05:00. Use the two weekdays. Treat the clock as one commercial guide.
- Area: S8 places it in old xã Lũng Niêm, huyện Bá Thước. S6 says the same old commune, and adds Kinh, Muong, and Thai traders, including people from Tân Lạc and Lạc Sơn in Hoa Binh. S9 places the same market in xã Pù Luông. Clause 92 of Resolution 1686 put old Lũng Niêm into xã Pù Luông from 1 Jul 2025 (S14). Both addresses are the rename, not two markets.
- Suggested slug: `pho-doan`
- Photo must show: Roadside stalls, thổ cẩm or baskets of greens, and the hillside behind the road, in one color frame. A tight shot of one jar loses the market.
- Sources: S6, S8, S9, S14

No second eating street was named on S7, S8, S9, or S13. Meals in the hamlets are in the house, not along a cafe row.
