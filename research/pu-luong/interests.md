- Why it is this place, not a generic label: The meal is Cổ Lũng duck, forest bamboo, hill chicken, stream fish, cơm lam, and rượu cần at a stilt house, plus Thursday and Sunday at Phố Đoàn (S7, S8, S9, S13). It is not a city noodle street.
