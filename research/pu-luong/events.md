### Folk games in Co Lung
- Vietnamese name: None printed beyond a cultural exchange and folk-games day
- Kind: activity
- When: S19 says “last April” in a 2023 note. The day of the month was not printed. Not a 2026 date. Check on arrival.
- Where: Organized by the old Cổ Lũng People’s Committee, 11 villages (S19)
- What happens: One day, about 800 people, Thai songs and dances, khen and gongs, 11 village teams, and stalls of cloth, bamboo, rattan, and produce (S19)
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: `ban-hieu`
- Sources: S19

### Vietnam Jungle Marathon
- Vietnamese name: Giải chạy Marathon băng rừng Việt Nam (VJM)
- Kind: activity
- When: The provincial article is dated 19 Oct 2026. It does not print the race morning’s calendar date. Check on arrival. Do not treat the article date as the gun time.
- Where: Start and finish at Làng Leo. The article also names a check-in at Kho Mường and a view at Đồi Trời (S21). Làng Leo is in the 2021 buffer list under old Thành Lâm (S3).
- What happens: Trail run through forest, hamlets, terraces, and bamboo bridges. Printed distances: 70 km, 50 km, 25 km, and 10 km. The 70 km start on the page is 04:00, then the shorter distances (S21).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: `pu-luong-peak`
- Sources: S21, S3
