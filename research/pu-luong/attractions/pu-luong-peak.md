## What it is

- English name: Pu Luong peak
- Vietnamese name: Đỉnh Pù Luông
- Kicker: Controlled two-day trek in special-use forest
- Kind: sight
- Separate visit: Yes. A two-day walk from bản Pù Luông to bản Báng. It is not the breakfast view from the valley.

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: A guided trek inside special-use forest. S12, re-read 23 Oct 2026: about 11 km from bản Pù Luông to bản Báng, nearly 2 km steeper than 35 degrees, two days, group size limited, no litter. The same article’s clock, which a second timetable does not confirm: day one leave bản Pù Luông at 07:00, rest at hang Khoai at 09:40, lunch at hang Thắm Mớ at 11:30, Lưỡi Hổ rock in the afternoon, summit about 15:30, camp, then cloud and sunrise from 06:00 the next morning. The article prints the height as 1,700 m in the lede and in a quote, and as higher than 1,700 m in another sentence. S6 prints 1,700 m. S3 names the peak as a line and does not print metres. Both height wordings stand. Do not average them.
- How long on site: Two days in the S12 plan. The clock has no second timetable. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED for a published duration beyond “two days in that one plan”.
- How long including the ride from the base: The S12 start is bản Pù Luông, not Cành Nàng and not Bản Đôn. Minutes from Bản Đôn were not printed. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Walking the strict forest without the controlled group S12 describes, or printing one height when the article uses two.
- Notice, if a page is later published: The walk is a limited group inside special-use forest. Decision 4513 of 29 Nov 2023 was cited and not opened.
