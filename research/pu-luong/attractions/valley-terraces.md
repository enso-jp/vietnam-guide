## Getting there

### From Bản Đôn

- Mode: On foot from the house. The fields are the view (S7). A bicycle on the valley road is what S6 says visitors are offered. Homestays often lend one without a charge. That “often” is one page. A hire sheet was not opened. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.

### From Hanoi

- Mode: Road. S6 says a car or a motorbike hired in Hanoi is the usual way. A limousine of about 4.5 hours from Giáp Bát or Mỹ Đình is also on S6.
- Time: No minute count beside the kilometers. S1 and S2 print about 210 km. S6 prints about 160 km southwest. Both stand.

## Season

- Late May–early June green, September–October gold (S1, S6). S20 calls both windows ripe and golden. S7 says Bản Đôn is busiest in February, March, September, and October. Morning, for the mist S9 describes.
- S6 also says any month, and that summer stays cooler than the lowland. That climate line stays on the place `season`, not in this body as a weather report.
