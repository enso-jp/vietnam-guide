## What it is

- English name: Ban Hieu and Hieu waterfall
- Vietnamese name: Bản Hiêu và Thác Hiêu
- Kicker: Limestone waterfall in xã Cổ Lũng
- Kind: sight
- Separate visit: Yes. A walk to the fall, not the terrace loop and not Thác Mơ in Hoa Binh.

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walks to a limestone waterfall and looks. S6 says five small falls, blue-green water, year-round, and that people sometimes swim. S10, re-read 23 Oct 2026, says the stream is about 800 m, splits into two branches, and meets again above a natural pool. S10 also says objects in the flow pick up a limestone crust (“hóa đá”), the source is a cave in the Pù Luông–Cúc Phương limestone, and the water runs all year, milky in rain and clear when the weather is calm. The “five falls” and the “two branches / 800 m” both stand. S11’s lede puts the fall at Bản Hiêu, in the core zone, on the Hoa Binh border. The body of S11 did not decode.
- How long on site: S13 prints 2–4 hours and 6–8 km from Bản Đôn, including the fall. No second source. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- How long including the ride from the base: From Cành Nàng, about 25 km on S10 only. That kilometer has no second source. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating the swim as a staffed pool, or using a photo of another fall.
- Notice: none until a page exists. A swim has no posted hour.
