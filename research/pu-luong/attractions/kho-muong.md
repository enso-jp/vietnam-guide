## What it is

- English name: Kho Muong and the bat cave

- Kind: sight
- Separate visit: Yes, if the cave mouth or the hamlet can be shown. It is not the valley loop. S1 names Kho Muong bat cave. S3’s lines pass Hang Dơi at Bản Kho Mường.

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walks to the hamlet and into the cave mouth the pages describe. S13 suggests a photo from inside looking out, and says bring a lamp. A quoted visitor on S25 says the cave has no coloured lights, no cement, and has not been turned into a show cave. S13’s founding story past 300 years stays S13 only.
