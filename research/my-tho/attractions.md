# Research dossier — My Tho

Slug: `my-tho`
Province: Đồng Tháp, the province formed in 2025 from Tiền Giang and the former Đồng Tháp. National Assembly Resolution 202/2025/QH15, clause 21, gives the new province 5,938.64 km² and 4,370,046 people (S10). A scheme quoted from Decision 759 prints 5,938.7 km² and 3,397,200 people, and a draft news page prints 4,203,948 people (S19). Those are not the enacted resolution. Do not pick one of the draft counts. The political-administrative center in that scheme is the old Mỹ Tho city (S19). The government directory and the provincial portal both print the working address as 23 đường 30/4, phường Mỹ Tho, tỉnh Đồng Tháp (S20, S21). Verified 21 Oct 2026 on the government directory.
This dossier is the Mỹ Tho base only. It does not research Cao Lãnh, Sa Đéc, or the rest of the merged province. The search slug stays `my-tho`.
The old city is no longer a district. Resolution 1663/NQ-UBTVQH15, clauses 83–87, forms five wards: phường Mỹ Tho (old Phường 1, Phường 2, and Tân Long), phường Đạo Thạnh (old Phường 4, Phường 5, and xã Đạo Thạnh), phường Mỹ Phong (old Phường 9, xã Tân Mỹ Chánh, and xã Mỹ Phong), phường Thới Sơn (old Phường 6 and xã Thới Sơn), and phường Trung An (Phường 10, xã Phước Thạnh, and xã Trung An) (S11). The clauses opened do not name old Phường 3, Phường 7, or Phường 8. An address that still uses those numbers stays Check on arrival for the new ward.
Kind: city
Corridor: mekong-delta
Base a traveler sleeps in: A river room on đường 30 Tháng 4 in phường Mỹ Tho, facing the Tiền and vườn hoa Lạc Hồng, or a garden homestay on cù lao Thới Sơn. Never a hotel brand.
Checked: 21 Oct 2026

## Why a night here

- The picture they expect: The Tiền at the town bank. Boats leave the pier under Rạch Miễu, and the orchards sit on Thới Sơn, not at an airport (S7, S8). The national place page puts the city on the north bank of the lower Tiền, south of the river only as the Ben Tre bank (S1).
- Where they sleep, as an area: River rooms along đường 30 Tháng 4, phường Mỹ Tho. That ward absorbed old Phường 1 and Phường 2 (S11). The provincial office at 23 đường 30/4 is the administrative pin for this street, not a hotel (S20). On the islet, TITC describes simple garden stays: thatch roofs, wooden houses, garden paths, and a riverside wharf (S8).
- Where the evening is: Đường 30 Tháng 4 and the food court inside the tourist-boat pier, beside the Tiền. A 5 Jan 2019 provincial article prints the court as 3,000 m² and open 18:30–02:00 (S22). A 2026 confirmation of those hours was not opened.
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): Chùa Vĩnh Tràng on Nguyễn Trung Trực in phường Mỹ Phong (S2). Cảng du thuyền Mỹ Tho at số 01 Hoàng Sa, with the cart stand on đường dọc kè sông Tiền (S7). The Thủ Khoa Huân statue and vườn hoa Lạc Hồng, which Báo Xây dựng places at the old railway station on 30/4 and Trưng Trắc (S15). They orient the stay. They are not a ranked bar list.
- Hero must include: The Tiền, a boat or the pier, and the far bank or Rạch Miễu in one color frame, with enough of the town embankment that a card crop still reads as a river town. If the frame is the pagoda, the five-tower front and the ceramic gate must both stay in the picture.
- A crop that would fail: Empty sky. A tight portrait of a Buddha that cuts off the head or the gate. A Cần Thơ market boat standing in for Mỹ Tho. A resort pool with no river.

## Attractions (Sight, area of interests, zone, hub, etc.)

### Vinh Trang Pagoda
- Vietnamese name: Chùa Vĩnh Tràng
- Kind: sight
- Area: Nguyễn Trung Trực, phường Mỹ Phong, tỉnh Đồng Tháp (S2). Verified 21 Oct 2026. An older copy of the same record, seen in search on the previous tourism domain, printed “Nguyễn Trung Trực Phường 8” on the address line and “xã Mỹ Phong” in the body. The current page prints phường Mỹ Phong only. Do not revive Phường 8.
- What the visitor does: Walks a large southern pagoda. The tourism portal calls it the largest and most beautiful Vietnamese pagoda in the south, built in the 19th century and heavily repaired in 1849, with an Asian-European mix. The main hall reads as one stupa and two side peaks, five towers in all, which the page compares to an Angkor outline. The two triple gates are mosaics of broken ceramics telling Buddhist stories. Inside: a relief of eight immortals on animals, a life-size bronze Tam Tôn set, and the wooden Eighteen Arhats carved in 1907, which the page calls unique in the delta. National historical relic in 1984 (S2). The national place page says the Buddha hall holds 60 precious wood statues, and singles out those 1907 arhats (S1). Record both counts: 60 on the national page, the arhat set and the bronze triad on the portal.
- Time: The portal prints 120 minutes for a visit (S2).
- Fee: Free on the portal (S2). Verified 21 Oct 2026. No second tariff was opened. Confirm at the gate.
- Hours: 06:00–23:00 on the portal (S2). Verified 21 Oct 2026.
- From the base: The electric-cart route 1 reaches it along Nguyễn Trung Trực (S7). The portal’s own distance list puts the pier 1.96 km from the pagoda (S2). Minutes on foot: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: Morning, before the heat. A clock for tour groups was not printed.
- Mistake to avoid: Budgeting the whole day here. The portal’s own visit line is 120 minutes, and the boat leaves from the pier, not from the pagoda gate (S2, S7).
- Local interest: Town pagodas
- Suggested slug: `vinh-trang`
- Suggested dot: 5 — the pagoda the town is known for
- Photo must show: The five-tower front and one ceramic gate in one color frame, so a landscape crop does not cut the roof off the statues
- unpublished: no licensed photo
- Sources: S1, S2, S7

### Buu Lam Pagoda
- Vietnamese name: Chùa Bửu Lâm (Bửu Lâm Cổ Tự)
- Kind: sight
- Area: 162 Nguyễn Văn Giác, phường Mỹ Tho, tỉnh Đồng Tháp (S3). Verified 21 Oct 2026.
- What the visitor does: Walks an older town pagoda in the trees. The portal says it was built in 1742, kiểu Nội công ngoại quốc, 987 m², still in three parts after more than 200 years of repairs: Tiền đường, Chánh điện, and Hậu tổ. In the main hall: three carved dragon columns, five bao lam, gold-leaf boards, a Cửu long tranh châu carving, and a peony-and-pheasant panel the page dates to 19th-century hands (S3). The national place page names Bửu Lâm with Vĩnh Tràng and says the old carving, the boards, and the plaques are still there (S1).
- Time: 120 minutes on the portal (S3).
- Fee: Free on the portal (S3). Verified 21 Oct 2026. No second tariff was opened.
- Hours: 07:00–18:00 on the portal (S3). Verified 21 Oct 2026. A tour page prints 07:30–11:30 and 13:30–17:30, and also prints two addresses on one page, 22 Nguyễn Trung Trực and 162B Nguyễn Văn Giác, and the year 1803 (S23). Two travel pages print 1802, the address 162B, and 07:00–18:00 (S24). Record 1742 against 1802 and 1803. Record 162 against 162B. Record a full day against a midday close. Do not pick one.
- From the base: The portal lists it 320 m from Vĩnh Tràng and 1.64 km from the pier (S3). The cart route uses Nguyễn Văn Giác (S7).
- When to go: With Vĩnh Tràng, morning. If the midday close on the tour page is in force, do not arrive at noon (S23).
- Mistake to avoid: Looking for this pagoda in Gò Công. The travel headlines say “Bửu Lâm Gò Công,” but the portal and those same pages place the building in Mỹ Tho (S3, S24).
- Local interest: Town pagodas
- Suggested slug: `buu-lam`
- Suggested dot: 4 — the older town pagoda, inside a morning that also has Vĩnh Tràng
- Photo must show: The pagoda front under the trees, whole roof in the frame
- unpublished: no licensed photo
- Sources: S1, S3, S7, S23, S24

### Tien Giang Museum
- Vietnamese name: Bảo tàng Tiền Giang
- Kind: sight
- Area: 2A Trương Vĩnh Ký, phường Mỹ Tho. From đại lộ Hùng Vương, turn right at the Trương Vĩnh Ký crossing and continue about 100 m (S4). Verified 21 Oct 2026.
- What the visitor does: Walks a French-style building the portal says opened in September 2000, ranked II/IV among Vietnamese museums. The page says it holds more than 50,000 objects, images, and documents, including old coins, lamps, and bronze trays, plus war objects. Two national treasures: a Vishnu statue, and a gold-leaf set carved with elephants, excavated at the Gò Thành archaeological site. Display house 1 is a provincial architectural relic. House 1 is the society before the Communist Party, including Óc Eo culture (4th–8th centuries), the Rạch Gầm – Xoài Mút battle of January 1785, and the later-19th-century scholars Trương Định, Nguyễn Hữu Huân, and the Tứ Kiệt at Cai Lậy. House 2 is the two resistance wars of Mỹ Tho and Gò Công, plus the displays titled “Chương trình Ngọt Hóa Gò Công” and “Cải Tạo Đồng Tháp Mười.” House Khu 8 is the history of that resistance zone (S4). Gò Thành is the find-spot named on this page. It is not given a visitor address here, so it is not a second sight.
- Time: 120 minutes on the portal (S4).
- Fee: Free on the portal (S4). Verified 21 Oct 2026. No second tariff was opened.
- Hours: 07:00–18:00 on the portal (S4). Verified 21 Oct 2026.
- From the base: The cart route names the museum on Nguyễn Tri Phương and Trương Vĩnh Ký (S7). The Vĩnh Tràng page lists the museum 900 m from the pagoda (S2).
- When to go: Midday, when the pagoda courts are hot and a boat is not running.
- Mistake to avoid: Expecting the Rạch Gầm battlefield inside this building. The battle is a display. The ground is a side trip (S4, S25).
- Local interest: Museum of the river province
- Suggested slug: `tien-giang-museum`
- Suggested dot: 3 — a rain hour, not the reason for the night
- Photo must show: The French-style front from the street, whole facade, not a dark case of coins
- unpublished: no licensed photo
- Sources: S2, S4, S7

### Thoi Son Islet
- Vietnamese name: Cù lao Thới Sơn (cồn Lân)
- Kind: area
- Area: Phường Thới Sơn, between two branches of the Tiền. Resolution 1663 forms that ward from old Phường 6 and xã Thới Sơn (S11). TITC says the islet is now in the merged Đồng Tháp – Tiền Giang region, 6 km from Mỹ Tho city, more than 1,200 hectares, more than 1,500 households (S8). Verified 21 Oct 2026.
- What the visitor does: Takes a boat on the Tiền, passes under Rạch Miễu, then a small sampan into nipa canals. Walks orchards. TITC names longan, rambutan, Hòa Lộc mango, and An Phước plum, and says guests pick fruit. Garden houses play đờn ca tài tử, including Dạ cổ hoài lang and Tình anh bán chiếu. Honey is tasted under the trees. Since the 1990s the islet has been one of the early community-ecotourism areas; gardeners also row, host, play, and cook (S8). The national tourism article on the four islets calls this one Lân (S9).
- Time: A half day from the pier. A fixed ticket length: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Fee: A gate or boat price was not on the TITC page. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- From the base: Boat from Cảng du thuyền Mỹ Tho (S7, S8). The 6 km is TITC’s figure (S8). The tourism directory also lists a point called Điểm du lịch Xanh Thới Sơn at 3.29 km from Vĩnh Tràng (S2). That is a named yard, not the same measurement as the islet. Record both and do not average them.
- When to go: Morning boat. Fruit follows the orchard calendar. A month-by-month chart was not on the page.
- Mistake to avoid: Calling this Cồn Phụng. Phụng is Tân Thạch, on the Ben Tre side of the river (S9).
- Local interest: Thới Sơn orchards
- Suggested slug: `thoi-son`
- Suggested dot: 5 — the orchard half-day the town is visited for
- Photo must show: A small boat, nipa or orchard, and the river or canal in one frame, subject through the center
- Published 23 Oct 2026. Hero: sampan in the nipa, Commons panoramio (1), jetsun, CC BY-SA 3.0, GPS 10.341252, 106.355008. Second: tour boats at the cồn Lân landing, Bùi Thụy Đào Nguyên, CC BY-SA 3.0.
- Sources: S2, S7, S8, S9, S11

### Noodle craft village
- Vietnamese name: Làng nghề bánh, bún, hủ tiếu Mỹ Tho
- Kind: area
- Area: Ấp Hội Gia, phường Mỹ Phong (S6). Verified 21 Oct 2026.
- What the visitor does: Watches the sheets rolled, dried, and cut. The portal says the village is over 100 years old and that real Mỹ Tho hủ tiếu had to be made from fragrant Gò Cát rice grown in phường Mỹ Phong. The same rice was also the local material for bún, bánh tráng, and bánh nghệ for more than half a century (S6). A provincial tourism article places workshops in ấp Mỹ Hòa and ấp Hội Gia, and in old phường 9: 22 workshops and about 100 workers in the village account, against a cooperative of 8 members in 2005. The province recognized the village in 2007. The city portal later describes the cooperative as 6 workshops and nearly 10 tons a day (S16, S27). Record the village count and the cooperative count as different groups, not as one corrected number.
- Time: 120 minutes on the portal (S6).
- Fee: Free on the portal (S6). Verified 21 Oct 2026. No second tariff was opened.
- Hours: 07:00–18:00 on the portal (S6). Verified 21 Oct 2026.
- From the base: The Vĩnh Tràng page lists the village 1.95 km from the pagoda (S2).
- When to go: Morning, while the sheets are being handled. A drying hour was not printed.
- Mistake to avoid: Treating a bowl of hủ tiếu in town as this visit. This is the workshop (S6).
- Local interest: Hủ tiếu Mỹ Tho
- Suggested slug: `noodle-village`
- Suggested dot: 3 — how the noodle is made, after a bowl in town
- Photo must show: Noodle sheets or cut strands in a workshop yard, hands in the frame, not a restaurant bowl
