## Events and to-do

### Hu tieu My Tho festival
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Hủ tiếu Mỹ Tho
- Kind: festival
- When: The provincial portal says the 2024 festival, theme “Tinh hoa từng sợi gạo,” ran from 27 Dec to 31 Dec 2024 at Công viên Tết Mậu Thân, old Phường 4 (S33). Báo Đồng Tháp’s interview with the city’s culture chief says the opening was the evening of 27 Dec and that it ran continuously until the solar New Year of 2025 (S34). Tuổi Trẻ and VOV print the same park and the same opening (S35, S36). Record 31 Dec 2024 and “until Tết Dương lịch 2025” as the two end lines. Phường 4 is now inside phường Đạo Thạnh (S11). The provincial page calls it part of the 345th year of the Mỹ Tho town, 1679–2024 (S33). The national place page starts the settlement story in 1623 (S1). Record both years. A 2025, 2026, or 2027 date was not opened. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED for the next date.
- Where: Công viên Tết Mậu Thân, old Phường 4, thành phố Mỹ Tho (S33)
- What happens: A craft-village space where guests watch the strand made, a cutting contest, a startup noodle cart given away, and a 2024 list of “reliable, quality” hủ tiếu shops. VOV says cooks from the Vietnam Chefs Association scored the shops (S34, S36). The shop list is not a pick for this guide.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No ticket was on the pages opened.
- Not the same as attraction slug: `noodle-village` is the working yard at Hội Gia. This is the festival in the park.
- Sources: S1, S11, S33, S34, S35, S36

### Provincial culture festival, second edition, 2025
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Văn hóa, Thể thao và Du lịch tỉnh Tiền Giang lần thứ 2 năm 2025
- Kind: festival
- When: The tourism-portal event page prints a river program from 08:00 to 11:30 on 29 Apr 2025 (S25). The rest of the festival calendar was not in the extract opened. A later year was not opened. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Where: The page’s main ground is Quảng trường Hùng Vương, then xã Đạo Thạnh (S25). That commune is now inside phường Đạo Thạnh (S11).
- What happens: An electric cart around the city and the display at the square, then a boat from Rạch Gầm – Xoài Mút back toward the Mỹ Tho pier, for about 50 official guests (S25). This is a 2025 program, not a daily boat.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: `tourist-pier` is the daily hub. This is one dated program.
- Sources: S11, S25

### Electric cart loops
- Vietnamese name: Du lịch xe điện vòng quanh Mỹ Tho
- Kind: activity
- When: 07:00–22:00 every day on the tourism portal (S7). Verified 21 Oct 2026.
- Where: Starts at Cảng du thuyền Mỹ Tho, số 01 Hoàng Sa, đường dọc kè sông Tiền (S7)
- What happens: Two loops of 7,000 m, each 20–30 minutes. Route 1 is the pagoda and the museum. Route 2 is the statue street and Hùng Vương. Tickets on that page: route 1 is 40,000 VND a guest, route 2 is 30,000 VND a guest (S7). One source. No second tariff was opened.
- Fee: 40,000 VND and 30,000 VND, as the two routes, not as a conflict (S7)
- Not the same as attraction slug: `tourist-pier` is the dock. This is the ride.
- Sources: S7
