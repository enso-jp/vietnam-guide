### Ben Tre
- Slug if it exists: none
- Or: Side trip placeholder: Ben Tre
- Travel time from this base: The road is Cầu Rạch Miễu on National Route 60, from the Mỹ Tho bank to the Ben Tre bank (S17, S18). Quân đội Nhân dân prints the whole project as 8,331 m, from the K120 bridge on Route 60 in Tiền Giang to km 08+331 at thị trấn Châu Thành, and the bridge itself as 2,878 m (S18). A construction-library page prints the main bridge as 2,868 m and the same 8,331 m overall, and says the bridge crosses Thới Sơn (S37). Record 2,878 m and 2,868 m. Do not pick one. Minutes from the pier to a Ben Tre landing: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The national tourism article puts Cồn Phụng 25 km by river from the center of Bến Tre, not from Mỹ Tho (S9).
- Why go: This is the usual river day. The four islets are Long, Lân, Quy, and Phụng (S9). Lân is Thới Sơn, inside this base (S8). Phụng is xã Tân Thạch, old huyện Châu Thành, tỉnh Bến Tre, about 50 hectares, also called cù lao Đạo Dừa after Nguyễn Thành Nam (S9). A 2020 Zing account places Quy in xã Quới Sơn and xã Tân Thạch of that same Ben Tre district (S26). After Resolution 202, clause 20, Bến Tre, Trà Vinh, and Vĩnh Long are the new Vĩnh Long province, 6,296.20 km² and 4,257,581 people (S10). The day is still called Ben Tre. Do not research Vĩnh Long here.
- Overnight or day: day
- Sources: S8, S9, S10, S17, S18, S26, S37
