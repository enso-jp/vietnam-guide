## What it is

- English name: Vinh Trang Pagoda

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: The ceramic gate and the seated Buddha, then the hall. Five towers on the portal’s description. Inside, the arhats of 1907 and the bronze triad. The national page says 60 wood statues.
- How long on site: 120 minutes (S2)
