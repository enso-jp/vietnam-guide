## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Gate, snake pens, venom demonstration, snake museum. Other animals on the grounds include crocodile, bear, and ostrich (S54).
- How long on site: 120 minutes (S54).
- How long including the ride from the base: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED for minutes. Distance about 9 km west of phường Mỹ Tho (S54) or about 8 km from TP Mỹ Tho (S55). Both stay. Do not average.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Approaching a venomous pen alone. The operator page says stay with the guidance and come in the morning (S55).
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Stay with the staff at the pens.
