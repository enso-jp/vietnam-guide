## Nearest rail

- No passenger station is open. Tuổi Trẻ, 10 Oct 2025, says the Sài Gòn – Mỹ Tho railway, the first in Indochina, ran from 1885 and stopped in 1958, about 70 km, built by contractor Joret from 1881 (S14). Báo Xây dựng places the old Mỹ Tho station near the Tiền, in front of what is now vườn hoa Lạc Hồng and the Thủ Khoa Huân statue, on đường 30/4 and đường Trưng Trắc, then phường 1 (S15).
- The same Tuổi Trẻ article says the Mekong Delta has had no railway since that closure. A Ho Chi Minh City – Cần Thơ line is in pre-feasibility: 175.2 km, from ga An Bình to ga Cần Thơ, through Ho Chi Minh City, Tây Ninh, Đồng Tháp, Vĩnh Long, and Cần Thơ. The Prime Minister asked for study and a hoped start in 2027. The printed schedule has phase 1 basically finished in 2035 (S14). A Mỹ Tho station is not named. An economist in that article suggests an extra stop at Cai Lậy. That is a suggestion, not a decision.
- Do not tell a traveler to take a train.
