## What it is

- English name: Ba Be Lake
- Vietnamese name: Hồ Ba Bể. Tay: Slam Pe, the widenings Pé Lèng, Pé Lù, and Pé Lầm
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Mountain lake with three Tay widenings
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: A boat on the open water, with landings the boatman sells at An Ma, Fairy Pond, Puong, and Dau Dang
- How long on site: VnExpress speaks of trips of 2–8 hours. A park duration was not found — UNSOURCED
- How long including the ride from the base: From Pac Ngoi the landing is in the village. From Buoc Lom the cave is about 1 km down the Nang
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Budgeting the cave, the fall, Hua Ma, and the Keo Siu walk as one hour. Swimming in Fairy Pond
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: A park post forbids hands or feet in Fairy Pond
