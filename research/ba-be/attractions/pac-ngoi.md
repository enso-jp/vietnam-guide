## What it is

- English name: Pac Ngoi
- Vietnamese name: Thôn Pác Ngòi
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Tay stilt village on the Leng
- Kind: area

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Sleep in a stilt house, eat the Tay table, and take the morning boat
- How long on site: The night, plus the early walk before the boat
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Expecting a shop street
- Notice: The hamlet has few stores, so the meal is booked and water is carried

## Fee
