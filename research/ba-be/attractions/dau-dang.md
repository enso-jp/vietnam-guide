## What it is

- Kicker (one line, what it is): Stacked fall west of the lake
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Land from the boat and look at the Nang dropping through stacked limestone
- How long on site: A stop on the long boat, the same boat as Puong when the circuit is the long one. Minutes ashore — UNSOURCED. Time field: Check on arrival
- How long including the ride from the base: By boat toward the west end. About 3 km west of the lake on the park location page
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Reading 500 m as a vertical drop
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The slope is estimated at 500 m. The height on that page is about 53 m
