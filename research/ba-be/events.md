These are not copies of the sight entries. The lake, Puông, Đầu Đẳng, and the villages stay in `attractions.md`.

### Long Tong
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Lồng Tồng (also Lồng tồng; “xuống đồng”)
- Kind: festival
- When: The 10th day of the first lunar month. The national tourism note prints “10/1 âm lịch” at Nam Mẫu (S16). The park’s own recap says the 2024 opening was the morning of 19 Feb, that lunar day, at xã Nam Mẫu (S9). VietnamPlus says the 2026 opening was 26 Feb at thôn Bó Lù, xã Ba Bể, tỉnh Thái Nguyên, and that it shared the morning with a spring press fair (S15). The press fair is a 2026 pairing. The next civil date moves — Check on arrival
- Where: The lake shore. Older pages say xã Nam Mẫu (S9, S16, S17). The 2026 report says thôn Bó Lù, xã Ba Bể (S15). Báo Xây dựng puts the main day at thôn Bó Lù, xã Nam Mẫu, and a procession that starts at đền An Mạ (S17)
- What happens: Incense and an offering for a good crop, then a procession of trays (S9, S17). The park recap names múa lân, a bowl dance (múa bát / múa pát) by 250 performers, tung còn, and volleyball (S9). The national note names đẩy gậy, kéo co, ném còn, courtship singing, and a singing contest between a Tay hamlet and a Dao hamlet (S16). VietnamPlus adds five-colour sticky rice, glutinous wine, boat racing, bull fighting, and fireworks at the 2026 opening (S15). Báo Xây dựng says grilled lake fish is served (S17). Which games run in a given year — Check on arrival
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No ticket was on the festival pages
- Not the same as attraction slug: yes. The lake is `ba-be-lake`. The temple stop is `an-ma`. Bó Lù is `bo-lu`
- Sources: S9, S15, S16, S17

### Keo Siu to An Ma on foot
- Vietnamese name: Tuyến trekking Kéo Siu – An Mạ
- Kind: activity
- When: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No season limit was on the trek page
- Where: From Kéo Siu, through forest the page describes as primary, to đảo An Mạ (S7)
- What happens: A walk the park clocks at 5–6 hours, ending at the temple island. The page names old trees, wire bamboo, streams, and small falls. It says the walk is not a gentle outing (S7)
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: yes — the island and temple are `an-ma`. This is the path
- Sources: S7

### Dugout, kayak, and the motorboat
- Vietnamese name: Thuyền độc mộc, kayak, xuồng máy
- Kind: activity
- When: The lake is visited year round on the pages read. Which craft is out in a given month — Check on arrival
- Where: Two starts: the lake, and the Năng (S18). Buốc Lốm is the Năng pier the park names for Puông (S3)
- What happens: The motorboat is the usual circuit. The dugout is the older Tay boat (S18). The district page shows kayak on the lake and says families in Nam Mẫu have bought boats (S14). VnExpress also names stand-up paddle boards as a newer hire (S18)
- Fee: Check on arrival. VnExpress prints motorboat hire from 300,000 to 1,000,000 VND by time, and kayak hire about 1.2–1.5 million VND for three boats in a day (S18). No park tariff was found. Do not publish those figures as the fee
- Not the same as attraction slug: yes — the water itself is `ba-be-lake`
- Sources: S3, S14, S18

### Camping on the shore
- Vietnamese name: Cắm trại ven hồ
- Kind: activity
- When: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Where: VnExpress names a silt bank at the end of the Lèng in Pác Ngòi, bãi Hội Xuân, vườn Lạc Ngà, and Puông (S18). The park navigation has a camping label. This pass did not open a park camping tariff
- What happens: A night on the shore rather than in a stilt house. The newspaper calls it a newer habit
- Fee: Check on arrival. VnExpress prints about 1–2 million VND a person depending on what is included (S18). That is not a park notice
- Not the same as attraction slug: yes
- Sources: S18

### Then singing for guests
- Vietnamese name: Hát then, đàn tính
- Kind: activity
- When: When a homestay asks the village troupe. No public clock was found — Check on arrival
- Where: Pác Ngòi. VOV describes then and đàn tính with leaf-yeast wine on the stilt-house floor, and a hamlet troupe coached by the district culture centre (S13)
- What happens: Listening, not a ticketed theatre. The park also lists then and lượn among living Tay practice, and khèn among Mong practice (S1)
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: yes — the village is `pac-ngoi`
- Sources: S1, S13
