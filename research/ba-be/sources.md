### S12
- URL: https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/6262/
- Publisher: UNESCO World Heritage Centre, tentative list “Ba Be – Na Hang Natural Heritage Area”. Submitted 31 May 2017 by the Vietnam National Commission for UNESCO. Criteria (vii)(x). Provinces named: Bac Kan and Tuyen Quang
- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: Not on the World Heritage List. A 2006 nomination was deferred in 2007 (Decision 31 COM 8B.13). Four parts: the park, Nam Xuan Lac, Na Hang, Lam Binh. Park 68 km northwest of Bac Kan city and 250 km north of Hanoi; 10,048 ha; five old communes. Lake 150 m, 450 ha, formed about 10,000 years ago, average depth 17–23 m, deepest 35 m, about 90 million m³. Đầu Đẳng “53 meters” and, in the same sentence, “from the height of 85 meters”. ASEAN year printed as 2004; Ramsar site 1938, third in Viet Nam, 2011; national landscape 2012; tentative-list date printed as 15 Nov 1997
