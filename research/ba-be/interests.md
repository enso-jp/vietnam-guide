# Interests — Ba Be

Checked: 21 Oct 2026

Local titles. Not Cao Bang’s falls, not Sapa’s ridge, not Ha Long’s junks. One card is the food guide.

### The lake at breakfast
- Why it is this place, not a generic label: Hồ Ba Bể is the mountain lake the night is for. Tay speakers call the three widenings Slam Pe: Pé Lèng, Pé Lù, and Pé Lầm (S10). The water is one lake (S1, S10)
- Fits interest key, if any: photography
- Icon: view
- Links to attraction, dish, or place slug: `ba-be-lake`, `widow-island`, `an-ma`, `pac-ngoi`
- Food guide card: no
- Photo must show: water, limestone, and a boat or stilt roofs in one frame
- Sources: S1, S10, S13

### The river cave and the fall
- Why it is this place, not a generic label: The Năng goes through Puông and then drops at Đầu Đẳng. They are one boat, not two provinces (S3, S4)

### Tay houses on the Leng
- Why it is this place, not a generic label: Pác Ngòi is the Tay village on the Lèng where the stilt houses face the water (S2, S13). The park counts Tay at 57 percent of people living in the park, Mong at 37 percent, Dao and Kinh a small share (S1)
- Fits interest key, if any: family
- Icon: view
- Links to attraction, dish, or place slug: `pac-ngoi`, `bo-lu`
- Food guide card: no
- Photo must show: stilt houses, water, and the cliff. Not a hotel facade
- Sources: S1, S2, S13

### Limestone forest
- Why it is this place, not a generic label: The park is karst forest around the only large natural mountain lake the biodiversity desk describes in these terms (S10). The walk that is actually clocked is Kéo Siu to An Mạ, 5–6 hours (S7)
- Fits interest key, if any: mountains
- Icon: view
- Links to attraction, dish, or place slug: `ba-be-national-park`, `hua-ma`, the Kéo Siu activity
- Food guide card: no
- Photo must show: forest on limestone above the lake, a path or a person for scale. Empty mist fails
- Sources: S7, S10

### Islands in the water
- Why it is this place, not a generic label: Bà Góa sits in Pé Lèng. An Mạ is the temple island, about 30 m out of the water (S2, S6). They are lake islands, not a sea crossing
