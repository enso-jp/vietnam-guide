### Hanoi

- Travel time from this base: The return of the arrival ride. Distances disagree: about 240 km on the locations index (S2), 250 km on the about page and on UNESCO (S1, S12), and a split of 165 km to Bac Kan city plus 60 or about 70 km on VnExpress (S18). Minutes — Check on arrival
- Why go: It is the usual previous stop, not a second landscape to add after the lake

- Sources: S1, S2, S12, S18

### Cao Bang
- Slug if it exists: `cao-bang`
- Travel time from this base: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No drive minutes were on the park pages
- Why go: Ban Gioc and the geopark are a different road. The park was under the Cao Bang People’s Committee only from 1992 to 1997 (S1). Resolution 202/2025/QH15 left Cao Bang unmerged and folded Bac Kan into Thai Nguyen (S11). That history does not make the lake a section of the Cao Bang page
- Overnight or day: Overnight. A day return is not supported by any duration this pass found
- Sources: S1, S11

### Na Hang
- Or: Side trip placeholder: Na Hang
- Slug if it exists: none
- Travel time from this base: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The district says a Bac Kan–Ba Be–Na Hang road is being invested in (S14). It does not print kilometres or minutes
- Why go: The park’s west edge is xã Na Hang, tỉnh Tuyên Quang (S1). UNESCO’s tentative list pairs Ba Be National Park with Na Hang Nature Reserve, and puts that reserve 110 km northeast of Tuyen Quang city and 270 km northwest of Hanoi (S12). Those Hanoi figures are not a drive from the lake. Hà Giang province was merged into Tuyên Quang (S11). This pass does not research Na Hang
- Overnight or day: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Sources: S1, S11, S12, S14
