### Flower program
- Vietnamese name: Mộc Châu - Hẹn ước mùa hoa
- Kind: festival
- When: From 17 Jan 2026. Opening night 17 Jan 2026 at Quảng trường 8.5, show “Đắm say mùa hoa” (S10). Bao Van Hoa on 8 Jan 2026 called the plum blossom at its best. The next blossom week — Check on arrival
- Where: Square 8.5, with satellite grounds named in that article: Moc Chau Island, Happy Land, Mu Nau on 18 Jan 2026, and strawberry gardens (S10)
- What happens: A three-part show (cherry blossom, white plum, the story of Miss Ban), street flower trucks, a market-style space and adventure sports at the island, a snow-town and old Tet set at Happy Land, and a fashion-and-tea show at Mu Nau (S10)

### Hau-plum harvest festival
- Vietnamese name: Ngày hội hái quả mận hậu Mộc Châu
- Kind: festival
- When: 23–24 May 2026, the 10th holding, phường Thảo Nguyên (S11, S12). Next year — Check on arrival
- Where: Thung lũng mận Nà Ka, tổ dân phố Pa Khen (S12)
- What happens: Hand picking, a picking contest, an eating contest, fruit displays, folk games, and an evening show. Dan Viet says pickers were not to shake the tree or break branches (S11).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: `na-ka-plum-valley`
- Sources: S11, S12

### Culture and Tourism Week
- Vietnamese name: Tuần Văn hóa - Du lịch Mộc Châu
- Kind: festival
- When: 27 Aug–5 Sep 2026. VietnamPlus calls it the first holding at provincial scale (S9). Next year — Check on arrival
- Where: Khu du lịch quốc gia Mộc Châu. Brocade night “Dệt di sản hòa giao sắc màu” on the evening of 31 Aug 2026 at Quảng trường 08-5. Art show “Mộc Châu - Tiếng gọi mùa yêu” on the evening of 1 Sep 2026 at the same square, with fireworks planned for about 10 minutes (S9). The week closes at Cửa khẩu quốc tế Lóng Sập.
- What happens: Brocade fashion, about 14 culture camps, Thai, Hmong, Dao, and Muong food, a border highland market, pao throwing, bánh dày pounding, and programs at the sites named in the attractions file (S9)
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: `square-08-5` and `long-sap-gate`
- Sources: S9

### Xen ban at Ban Vat
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Xên bản, bản Vặt
- Kind: festival
- When: The heritage monograph says it is usually held from 29 Aug to 1 Sep (S7). A 2026 confirmation of those exact days, separate from the culture week, was not found. Check on arrival
- Where: Procession from Chùa Vặt Hồng to Thác Dải Yếm. The monograph sets the start at 8:00 (S7).
- What happens: A Thai rite for the village spirit, then contests, a communal meal, and a xoe circle (S7). The monograph’s legend text is not copied here.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Not the same as attraction slug: `dai-yem-waterfall` and `vat-hong-pagoda`
- Sources: S7
