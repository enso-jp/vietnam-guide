## What it is

- English name: Dai Yem Waterfall
- Vietnamese name: Thác Dải Yếm. Monograph also: Thác Ta Lét, thác Bản Vặt (S7). National page also: thác Nàng (S4).
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Two limestone drops you hear before you see
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk down to a limestone fall in two parts, with a pool under the lower water. Vietnam Tourism says you hear it before you see it. A red Love Glass Bridge crosses above it on that page (S1). The 2011 monograph describes stair paths and does not mention the bridge (S7).
