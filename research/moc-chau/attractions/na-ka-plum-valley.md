## Season

- Blossom called at its best on 8 Jan 2026 (S10). Harvest festival 23–24 May 2026 (S11, S12). S25, a Vietnam Tourism note read on the food pass, puts blossom from the middle of January to the end of February and fruit from March to May. The next year moves. Do not set a public bestMonths line that collapses those into one season.
