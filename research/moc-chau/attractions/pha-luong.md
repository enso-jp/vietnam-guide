## What it is

- English name: Pha Luong

- Kicker: Ridge walk toward the Laos border

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Hikes to the ridge called the roof of Moc Chau, with rice terraces on the way, forest, and a rock ledge over the border (S1). The reprint splits the approach into about 20 km from Moc Chau to the foot, about 6 km to the border post, and about 10 km from the post to the summit (S15).
