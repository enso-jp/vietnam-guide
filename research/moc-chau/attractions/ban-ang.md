## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walks Thai stilt houses under pine, a lake the 2012 feature puts at 5 ha, and the tea hills beyond. Horseback is offered (S1, S14).
- How long on site: A half day if the meal is included is approximate only. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- How long including the ride from the base: About 2 km on National Highway 43 (S14). Minutes — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Using a Mai Chau rice-valley frame for this pine.
- Notice: The village and the provincial resort zone share the pine. The company is not a pick.
