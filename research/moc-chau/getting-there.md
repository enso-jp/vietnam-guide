# Getting there — Moc Chau

Checked: 21 Oct 2026

The usual previous stop is Hanoi (`hanoi`). The road is National Highway 6. Mai Chau (`mai-chau`) is the valley before the pass, and it is a different place.

- Nearest airport: Noi Bai (HAN) in Hanoi. Operator pages pick up there and in the city (S16). Vietnam Tourism’s district page names the road and does not name an airport in Moc Chau (S2). An older Thanh Nien report of a transport-ministry letter said Na San was the airport in the national plan and that a Moc Chau airport was not added, though a specialized field could be studied (S23). The article date was not on the extract. This pass found no 2026 timetable of commercial flights into Moc Chau or Na San. Do not publish a local flight.
- Nearest rail station: None named. The district page’s transport lines are National Highway 6 and National Highway 43 (S2). This pass did not read a Vietnam Railways timetable. Do not publish a station name.

The October 2026 construction note says winter mornings and evenings are foggy on this alignment (S18).
