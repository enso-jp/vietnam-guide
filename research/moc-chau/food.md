# Food — Moc Chau

Checked: 21 Oct 2026

Plateau dairy, tea, hau plum, and a Thai hearth. No stall prices. This is not Mai Chau’s valley menu. The site dish slug `che` is a sweet soup. Do not reuse it for Moc Chau tea.

## Comprehensive list of local food to try

### Fresh yogurt and fresh milk
- Vietnamese name: Sữa chua, sữa tươi
- What it is: The plateau’s dairy snack. TITC names the Moc Chau Milk dairy farm as a place where visitors make dairy products, and names dairy goods among the souvenirs (S5). A 3 May 2024 provincial announcement ranked an Amifarm set at 3-star OCOP: plain yogurt, black sticky-rice yogurt (sữa chua nếp cẩm), and passion-fruit yogurt (S13). That household is not a restaurant pick.
- How to eat it: Cold, from a cup, in the farm town. The OCOP line is three named cups, not a recipe.
- When to order it: Daytime in the farm town, including as the morning cup before the hills. Exact stall hours — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Area, not a shop: Farm town (old nông trường; 2026 news prints phường Thảo Nguyên and phường Mộc Châu)
- Meal: breakfast
- Neighbor dish to link: none on this site. Do not link `che` or a Hanoi bowl.
- Unpublished: no licensed photo
- Sources: S5, S13

### Plateau tea
- Vietnamese name: Chè / trà Mộc Châu
- What it is: Tea from the hills. Vietnam Tourism sends visitors to Moc Suong for an old tea ceremony and to watch the leaf being processed (S1). The 2019 provincial feature says the hills include older plantings from the 1960s state farm and newer oolong, and that Moc Chau tea is less famous than Thai Nguyen tea (S19). Drink the cup. Do not treat “largest tea region” as settled: S1 says that, S19 does not.
- How to eat it: As a drink at the tea ground. A recorded ceremony script beyond S1’s line — Check on arrival — UNSOURCED

- Area, not a shop: Tân Lập tea hills and Mộc Sương
- Meal: drink
- Neighbor dish to link: none. The slug `che` is a different sweet.

## Food streets and markets

### Moc Chau walking street and night market
- Vietnamese name: Phố đi bộ và chợ đêm Mộc Châu
- Kind: food-street
- What the visitor does: Walks a ground of more than 2 ha around Công viên ánh sáng Pha Luông, in the guesthouse quarter of the old farm town. Nearly 60 stalls split into a trade side and a food side: Thai display house, brocade, snacks, and farm goods. The food side serves dried buffalo and hearth sausage with rice wine (S8). VietnamPlus still names Phố đi bộ Mộc Châu as a site for the 2026 culture week (S9).
- When to go: The 2023 feature says weekends are the fuller trading days (S8). Clock hours — Check on arrival. A tour page’s 16:00–23:00 window is not used. The provincial article does not print a clock.
- Area: Old thị trấn Nông trường, the guesthouse quarter. 2026 news no longer uses that town name for the ward.
- Suggested slug: `moc-chau-night-market`
- Photo must show: The lit path, several open stalls, and people in one frame, subject through the center. A single skewer, or a dark frame with no stalls, fails.
- Unpublished: no licensed photo
- Sources: S8, S9

A separate morning market was not named on the provincial night-market feature, the national tourism pages, or the 2026 culture-week notice. Do not invent one.
