- Accessed: 21 Oct 2026
- Supports: Phong Nha Cave address (Phong Nha Commune, Quang Tri), about 50 km from Dong Hoi, length 7,729 m, summer hours 07:15–16:30, winter hours 07:30–15:45, open daily, visit about 2.5 hours, boat about 30 minutes on the Son River then about 1,200 m rowed inside, return walk through Tien Cave and Royal Palace Cave. Vietnamese guide 300,000 VND for one cave or 400,000 for two. English guide 400,000 for one cave or 500,000 for two. Operator: Phong Nha-Ke Bang Tourism Center. Also names a 4,500 m adventure and a night tour without prices on this page. Dry-season water described as jade-green and rainy-season water as reddish.
