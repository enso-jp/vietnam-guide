### Mooc Spring platter
- Vietnamese name: Mẹt ăn at Suối Nước Moọc
- What it is: The spring page names mountain-stream shrimp, freshwater fish, and free-range chicken (S7).
- How to eat it: At the spring, after the walk or the swim. Not a town crawl.
- When to order it: While the spring is open. Hours follow the summer and winter boards (S7).
- Area, not a shop: Mooc Spring. The “from 130,000 VND” chicken set is a site price. Do not print it on a public food page.
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none. Hue dishes are a different city.
- Sources: S7
