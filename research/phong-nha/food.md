# Food — Phong Nha

Checked: 23 Oct 2026

There is no sourced town food street. The park names community dishes. The Quang Binh visitor portal names Son River dishes. Do not invent a market, a stall price, or a restaurant pick. The 23 Oct 2026 pass publishes every dish those two publishers name for Phong Nha or the buffer. Streets and rooms are not in that count. Mooc Spring’s chicken-set price stays off the public food page.

Left out of the public list, on purpose: cá chép and lươn (named as cage fish on 20 May 2025, no plate), bột báng, củ nâu, and củ mài (staples, not dishes), rau rừng (greens, not one dish), chè xanh (named as a drink, not a local recipe), and Quảng Bình cakes the portal does not place on the Son. Restaurant names below are not picks.

## Comprehensive list of local food to try

### Cơm pồi
- Vietnamese name: Cơm pồi
- What it is: A Chứt community dish named with ốc lèn and rượu đoác (S10). Also a Nguồn dish (S10, S31). The visitor portal disagrees on the method and one article spells it cơm bồi (S34, S35, S36). Do not pick one account.
- How to eat it: See S34, S35, and S36. They do not match. A shop door is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to order it: S34 says evening meals in homes in làng Tân Háa, Minh Hóa. An hour is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Area, not a shop: Chứt communities in the park buffer, and Minh Hóa for the Nguồn accounts. Not a town street.
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none
- Sources: S10, S31, S34, S35, S36

### Cá trắm sông Son
- Vietnamese name: Cá trắm sông Son
- What it is: The 20 May 2025 visitor-portal article calls it the food emblem of the park. Cages at xã Sơn Trạch, huyện Bố Trạch. Flesh firm, not fishy. Preparations: hấp, kho, nướng, and especially cá trắm kho tiêu with rau dưa muối (S33). The park names Hội thi Cá trắm sông Son and does not give a date (S10, re-read 23 Oct 2026).
- How to eat it: Steamed, kho, grilled, or pepper kho with pickled vegetables (S33).
- When to order it: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Area, not a shop: Sông Son at Sơn Trạch. Not a street.
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none
- Sources: S33, S10

### Bánh xèo gạo lứt

- Neighbor dish to link: none. Do not use the shared bánh xèo crepe record. That cake is pork and shrimp.
