# Getting there — Phong Nha

Checked: 21 Oct 2026

### Dong Hoi Airport to the village
- Nearest airport: Dong Hoi Airport, Cảng hàng không Đồng Hới, IATA VDH, managed by Airports Corporation of Vietnam (S15, S16).
- Vietjet lists domestic flying with Hanoi (HAN) and Ho Chi Minh City (SGN) (S15). Do not treat that as a full schedule.
- From Dong Hoi city the park puts Phong Nha Cave at about 50 km (S1, S2). The English and Vietnamese pages both say “southeast.” No second source confirmed that compass point, so the public page should keep the distance and drop the direction.
- Drive minutes, taxi fare, and any local bus: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Blog fares were not copied.

### Getting around on site
- Phong Nha Cave tickets are sold at the Tourism Center. The boat pier is the one to the left of the counter (S1).
- Paradise Cave’s operator address is Km 16 on the west branch of the Ho Chi Minh road (S3). The minute count from the village is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mooc Spring is about a 20-minute drive from Phong Nha Cave (S7). That figure is from the cave, not from every guesthouse.
- Bicycles and town taxis: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Do not repeat the old “45–60 minutes” line unless a source is added.

### Season limit on the journey
- The Son River at Phong Nha Cave is described as jade-green in the dry season and reddish in the rainy season (S1). The page does not name the months.
- Chay River–Dark Cave may close in heavy rain, flooding, or storms (S5).
- Summer and winter opening hours are both published. Which calendar months use which board is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Son Doong expeditions on the Oxalis pack run January to August (S13). That is one product, not the park’s dry season.

### Where the night is
- Oxalis gives its address as Phong Nha village, Phong Nha Commune, Quang Tri (S14). Sleep in the village, not in a named hotel.
- The boat cave closes at 16:30 in the published summer hours and 15:45 in the winter hours (S1). The evening is back in the village. No bar street was on the park pages.
