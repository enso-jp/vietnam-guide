## Fee and hours, re-read 23 Oct 2026

- Oxalis page https://oxalisadventure.com/tour/hang-en-adventure-cave-camp/ accessed 23 Oct 2026.
- Price block: 9,500,000 VND a person and about 358 USD. Age 16–70. Join-in group of 16. Operation December to mid-September.
- FAQ on the same page: the cave tour operates from December until the end of August. Both season lines stay. Do not collapse them.
- Arrive in Phong Nha before 07:00. Pickup 07:30–08:00. Return around 18:00 the next day.
- The itinerary describes the last climb as 450 m of elevation. The terrain list on the same page says 650 m of elevation gain. Both stay.
- The park product list names Oxalis for Hang En and does not print this price (S26). No second publisher confirmed the dong figure on 23 Oct 2026.
