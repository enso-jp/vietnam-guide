## What it is

- English name: Phong Nha Cave
- Vietnamese name: Động Phong Nha
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Boat ride into a river cave
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Ticket counter at the Tourism Center, pier on the left, boat downstream on the Son River for about 30 minutes, engine off, row about 1,200 m of a cave measured at 7,729 m (S1). On the water the park names a Goddess of Mercy, Tien Sa sandbank, and Bi Ky dry chamber (S1). The return uses a composite walkway through two dry branches of this cave, Tien Cave and Royal Palace Cave, where it names a Golden Bell and a King's Bed (S1). Those branches are not Tien Son.
- How long on site: About 2.5 hours (S1).
- How long including the ride from the base: The 2.5 hours starts at the counter. Village-to-counter minutes are Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Paying 150,000 VND and expecting that to include the boat, Paradise Cave, or Son Doong (S1, S3, S12).
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The entrance ticket is not the boat. The boat is 700,000 VND per round trip for up to 12 people (S1).

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: 150,000 VND per person for the entrance (S1). No resident or senior band is printed on that page.
- Child, height, or senior rule: Under 1.3 m, entrance free (S1). Senior rule not printed.
- What the basic ticket includes: The entrance. Not the boat. Not a guide.
- What costs extra: Boat 700,000 VND per trip, or 750,000 VND if the trip includes Tien Son (S1, S18). Guide 300,000–500,000 VND depending on language and whether it is one cave or two (S1, S18). The Vietnamese Tien Son page also prints an entrance of 80,000 VND a person, children under 1.3 m free (S18). The English boat page does not print that 80,000 line (S1). Do not add the two entrances into one figure. The 4,500 m adventure is priced on S21, not on S1. The night tour is named and not priced (S1).
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S1. S2 is the same office in Vietnamese. No second publisher confirmed the dong figures.

## Getting there

- From the Tourism Center: Walk to the boat pier on the left after you buy the ticket (S1).
- From Dong Hoi: The cave is about 50 km from Dong Hoi city (S1). Minutes Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- From Hue: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED (S19). Do not print a hour.
- Tien Son: Same pier, then nearly 600 steps. About 100 m vertically above Phong Nha Cave. The cave is 980 m. About 400 m is open. The page then describes a drop of about 10 m and nearly 500 m that stays closed (S18). Not a second departure, and not the dry branches inside Phong Nha Cave. The Tien Son page also says the visit is about 2.5 hours. That matches the boat-cave duration. Do not add a second 2.5 hours.
