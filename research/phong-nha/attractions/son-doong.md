## What it is

- English name: Son Doong
- Vietnamese name: Hang Sơn Đoòng
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Booked six-day cave expedition
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Reach Dong Hoi by 15:00 for the operator transfer to Phong Nha. The safety briefing that evening is compulsory. The trek leaves before 07:00 along Road 20 and the west Ho Chi Minh road. The operator’s route camps in the cave and passes Hang En on the way in (S12, S20).
- How long on site: 6 days, 5 nights (S12, S13, S20).
- How long including the ride from the base: The trailhead minutes are Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The sourced start is Dong Hoi by 15:00, not the village pier (S12).
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating it as a half-day beside Phong Nha Cave, or arriving in Dong Hoi after 15:00 and missing the briefing (S12).
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: There is no walk-up ticket at the Tourism Center. Oxalis runs it (S12, S20).

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: Join-in group of 10. The pack generated 17 Oct 2026 prints 3,000 USD and 80,000,000 VND per person (S13). Age 18–70 (S13).
- Child, height, or senior rule: Age 18–70 on that pack (S13). Not a child gate ticket.
- What the basic ticket includes: The expedition, not a cave entrance you buy at the pier (S12, S13).
- What costs extra: A private departure is a different price and was not copied (S13).
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S12, S13. The park page confirms Oxalis, January to August, a maximum of 10 guests, and about 1,000 visitors a year, and does not print the dollar figure (S20).

## Hours

- Open: January to August on the Oxalis pack (S13) and on the park page (S20). Departures are the operator calendar.
- Last entry, if it differs: Be in Dong Hoi by 15:00 the day the transfer starts (S12).
- Closed, weekly or seasonal: No walk-up entry. Outside January to August the pack does not run (S13, S20).
- If a board was an image and could not be read, say so: Not an image board.
