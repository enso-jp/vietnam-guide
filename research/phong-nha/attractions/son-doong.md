## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Reach Dong Hoi by 15:00 for the operator transfer to Phong Nha (S12). At 17:00 the group visits the Phong Nha Caves Exploration Museum, then the compulsory briefing (S12). Pack the porter bag before 07:00. The group leaves at 08:00 along Road 20 and the west Ho Chi Minh road to the trailhead at kilometre 35 (S12). That day is about 14 km, with about 30 knee-deep river crossings, a stop in Doong village, and a walk through Hang En (S12). The park’s day list camps that night at Hang En and enters Son Doong the next day (S20). The operator’s current itinerary camps near the Son Doong entrance after Hang En, then enters the next day (S12). Print both. Do not name Commander Lodge, Oxalis Home, or Chay Lap Farmstay.
- How long on site: 6 days, 5 nights (S12, S13, S20).
- How long including the ride from the base: The trailhead minutes are Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The sourced start is Dong Hoi by 15:00, not the village pier (S12).
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating it as a half-day beside Phong Nha Cave, or arriving in Dong Hoi after 15:00 and missing the briefing (S12).
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: There is no walk-up ticket at the Tourism Center. Oxalis runs it (S12, S20).

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: Join-in group of 10. The park page now prints 3,000 USD a person (S20, re-read 21 Oct 2026). The Oxalis page prints 3,000 USD and 80,000,000 VND and says the price includes VAT (S12). The pack generated 17 Oct 2026 printed the same two figures (S13). Age 18–70 (S12, S13).
- Child, height, or senior rule: Age 18–70 on that pack (S13). Not a child gate ticket.
- What the basic ticket includes: The expedition, not a cave entrance you buy at the pier (S12, S13).
- What costs extra: A private departure is a different price and was not copied (S13).
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S12, S13, S20. The park page now prints 3,000 USD. It still does not print 80,000,000 VND. Keep both. S20 also says Oxalis has run it since 2013, a jungle 252 m down in a doline, and a 90 m wall. S12 splits that wall into an 18 m ladder and a 72 m ascent, and describes an 80 m descent into the main chamber and an upper passage of about 700 m. Inside temperature on S12 is 18–24°C. Ho Khanh, 1990, and the 2009 British-Vietnamese survey are on S20. Skip the world-record lines.
