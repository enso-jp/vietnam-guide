## What it is

- English name: Paradise Cave
- Vietnamese name: Động Thiên Đường
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Dry cave on a wooden walkway
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Ticket at the site, then 1.6 km by electric buggy or on foot under the trees, about 500 m up to the entrance, then 1 km on the wooden walk (S3). The park gives the cave a length of 31.4 km, a height of 60 m, and a width from 30 m to 100 m, with some sections to 150 m. The same page describes chambers on the 1 km walk as wide as 150 m and as high as 100 m. Both height lines stay. A British cave expedition found the entrance in 2005 (S3). Record claims on that page are not copied.
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED for the 1 km ticket. Do not borrow the 7 km timetable.
- How long including the ride from the base: Address is Km 16, west Ho Chi Minh road (S3). Minutes from the village are Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Using the Phong Nha boat ticket, or buying the 2,000,000 VND trek when you wanted the boardwalk (S1, S3).
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Vietjet prints 250,000 VND and 06:30–17:00 (S4). The park prints 270,000 VND and does not state the 1 km gate hours (S3). Confirm the price and the clock on arrival.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: Adult 270,000 VND (S3).
- Child, height, or senior rule: 1.1 m to 1.3 m, 145,000 VND. Under 1.1 m free (S3). No senior band on S3.
- What the basic ticket includes: Entrance and the electric buggy (S3).
- What costs extra: The 7 km one-day tour, 2,000,000 VND, minimum 2 people. The park’s timetable starts at 08:00 and ends about 16:00. It kayaks a river described as 60–100 m across and finishes at Heaven’s Well (S3). The overnight, 4,000,000 VND, minimum 5, not for children under 16, and not for people the page lists with heart disease or high blood pressure (S3). Not the boardwalk ticket.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S3, with S4 disagreeing at 250,000 VND. Both stay visible.
