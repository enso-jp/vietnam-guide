## What it is

- English name: Tien Son Cave
- Vietnamese name: Động Tiên Sơn
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Dry cave above the boat
- Kind: sight
- Area: Same pier as Phong Nha Cave. District on the page: The pier.

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Buy a ticket at the counter, take a boat number at the pier on the left, ride downstream on the Son River about 30 minutes to the Phong Nha mouth, then climb nearly 600 steps. The open walk is about 400 metres from the entrance of a cave measured at 980 metres. The page then describes a drop of about 10 metres and nearly 500 metres that stays closed (A1). The cave sits about 100 metres vertically above Phong Nha Cave (A1).
- How long on site: About 2.5 hours for that route, boat included (A1). The English boat page uses the same 2.5 hours for Phong Nha Cave (A2). Do not add a second 2.5 hours when the visitor does both.
- How long including the ride from the base: The 2.5 hours starts at the counter. Village-to-counter minutes are Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating 80,000 VND as the whole price, or budgeting a second day for a stair you climb on the boat morning.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The Vietnamese page prints an 80,000 VND entrance. The English boat page prints a 150,000 VND Phong Nha entrance and does not print 80,000. Neither page says whether a visitor who does both pays both entrances (A1, A2).

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: Entrance 80,000 VND a person on the Vietnamese page (A1). The English boat page does not print that line (A2).
- Child, height, or senior rule: Children under 1.3 m free of the Tien Son entrance (A1). No senior band is printed.
- What the basic ticket includes: The entrance named on the Vietnamese page. The boat is separate.
- What costs extra: Boat service 700,000 VND a round trip, maximum 12, on both pages. Boat for Phong Nha and Tien Son together 750,000 VND a round trip, maximum 12, on both pages (A1, A2). Vietnamese guide 300,000 VND for one cave or 400,000 for two. English guide 400,000 for one cave or 500,000 for two (A1, A2). Whether the 150,000 VND Phong Nha entrance is added to the 80,000 VND line is Check on arrival — the two pages do not say. Do not add them into one figure.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: A1, A2. Same park office. No second publisher confirmed the dong figures.
