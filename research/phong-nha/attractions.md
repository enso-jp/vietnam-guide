## Why a night here

### The river and the village

- The picture they expect: The Son River against a limestone wall, and a boat going into a cave mouth. That is the visit the Tourism Center sells as Phong Nha Cave (S1).
- Where they sleep, as an area: Phong Nha village, Phong Nha Commune, Quang Tri. Oxalis prints that as its address (S14). Not a hotel brand. Dong Hoi is the airport city, about 50 km from the cave (S1).
- Where the evening is: Back in the village. The boat cave’s summer board ends at 16:30 and the winter board at 15:45 (S1). No park page names a bar street.
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): The Tourism Center boat pier, to the left of the ticket counter (S1). Paradise Cave’s gate is at Km 16 on the west Ho Chi Minh road (S3). Oxalis Home and Commander Lodge are operator stops for the Son Doong briefing (S12). Do not publish those as picks.
- Hero must include: The river in front, the cave mouth, and the rock wall behind it, in one frame. Boats help, but the mouth has to read.
- A crop that would fail: The old banner is 960 by 137. A center cover keeps a ribbon of water and cuts the wall. A food stall, a selfie, or a cave interior used as the town hero also fails.

## Attractions (Sight, area of interests, zone, hub, etc.)

### Phong Nha Cave
- Vietnamese name: Động Phong Nha
- Kind: sight
- Area: Phong Nha village, at the Tourism Center
- What the visitor does: Buy a ticket, take a boat downstream on the Son River for about 30 minutes, then row about 1,200 m into the water cave. On the way back the same trip can walk the dry branches called Tien Cave and Royal Palace Cave (S1).
- Time: About 2.5 hours for the visit (S1). That is the half-day. The ride from the village to the counter is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Fee: Entrance 150,000 VND per person. Children under 1.3 m free (S1). Boat 700,000 VND per round trip, maximum 12 people, not per person (S1). Adding Tien Son on that boat is 750,000 VND per trip (S1). A guide is extra: Vietnamese 300,000 VND for one cave or 400,000 for two; English 400,000 or 500,000 (S1). No second publisher repeats these 2026 figures. S2 is the same park in Vietnamese.
- From the base: Walk from the Tourism Center counter to the pier on the left (S1). Minutes from a village bed are Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: Morning. Summer board 07:15–16:30. Winter board 07:30–15:45. Open daily (S1). The park does not say which months are summer. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake to avoid: Treating 150,000 VND as the whole price. The boat is separate. Paradise Cave and Son Doong are not on this ticket (S1, S3, S12).
- Local interest: The boat cave
- Suggested slug: phong-nha-cave
- Suggested dot: 4 — already on the record. Do not change it. One line: the half-day the village is built around, not the whole park.
- Photo must show: Tourist boats at the mouth, or one boat on the water inside the mouth. A center crop still needs the mouth.
- Sources: S1, S2

### Tien Son Cave
- Vietnamese name: Động Tiên Sơn
- Kind: sight
- Area: Same pier as Phong Nha Cave
- What the visitor does: Leave the boat and climb nearly 600 steps. The open section is about 400 m of dry cave. The rest is closed (S18).
- Time: The Phong Nha visit is published as about 2.5 hours, and this is an add-on on that boat, not a separate published duration (S1, S18).
- Fee: Same entrance logic as the boat cave. The shared boat becomes 750,000 VND per trip when Tien Son is included (S1). Step-only price: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- From the base: Same pier (S18).
- When to go: Same summer and winter boards as Phong Nha Cave (S18).
- Mistake to avoid: Budgeting a second day for a cave you reach on the same boat.
- Local interest: The boat cave
- Suggested slug: tien-son-cave
- Suggested dot: 3 — a branch of the boat visit, not its own day
- Photo must show: The dry cave or the stair, not the river mouth again. None accepted in this pass.
- Sources: S1, S18
- Page: do not give it its own page. Cover it on Phong Nha Cave.

### Paradise Cave
- Vietnamese name: Động Thiên Đường
- Kind: sight
- Area: Km 16, west branch of the Ho Chi Minh road (S3)
- What the visitor does: On the 1 km ticket, walk or take an electric buggy 1.6 km through forest, walk about 500 m to the entrance, then 1 km on a wooden walkway (S3). The 7 km tour to Heaven’s Well is a different product.
- Time: The park does not publish a minute count for the 1 km walk. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The one-day 7 km tour is timed 08:00 to about 16:00 (S3). Do not use that timetable for the boardwalk ticket.
- Fee: Adult 270,000 VND, including entrance and the electric buggy. Child from 1.1 m to 1.3 m 145,000 VND with the buggy. Under 1.1 m free (S3). Vietjet prints 250,000 VND and 06:30–17:00 (S4). Keep both. Do not average them. The 7 km one-day tour is 2,000,000 VND, minimum 2 people. The overnight is 4,000,000 VND, minimum 5, and not for children under 16 (S3).
- From the base: By road to Km 16 (S3). Minutes from the village: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: Gate hours for the 1 km walk are Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. S3 does not state them. S4 states 06:30–17:00, and other unofficial pages disagree, so none of those hours goes on the page as settled.
- Mistake to avoid: Assuming the Phong Nha boat ticket includes this cave, or that the 2,000,000 VND trek is the ordinary walk.
- Local interest: The dry boardwalk
- Suggested slug: paradise-cave
- Suggested dot: 5 — already on the record. Do not change it. One line: the other half-day, a dry cave rather than a second boat.
- Photo must show: The wooden walk and the stalactites together. A second frame should be a different chamber, not the same crowd shot.
- Sources: S3, S4

### Chay River and Dark Cave
- Vietnamese name: Sông Chày – Hang Tối
- Kind: sight
- Area: Phong Nha Commune. A street address was not on the page (S5).
- What the visitor does: The exploration package crosses the river on a 400 m zipline, goes into Dark Cave, and uses the mud bath, then kayaks and swims. The standard package skips the cave and the mud. The recreation package also skips the long zipline (S5).
- Time: About 4 hours, depending on the package (S5).
- Fee: Exploration adult 450,000 VND; child 1.1–1.3 m 150,000. Standard adult 220,000; child 110,000. Recreation adult 150,000; child 110,000 (S5). A May 2024 park notice instead cut a “standard” package from 270,000 to 250,000 and added river play at 150,000 (S6). The numbers disagree. Use S5 as the current visitor page and say the older notice does not match.
- From the base: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No minute count from the village.
- When to go: Summer 07:45–16:30. Winter 08:00–15:30. Open daily (S5). Months for summer and winter are Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The site may close for heavy rain, flooding, or storms (S5).
- Mistake to avoid: Buying the 220,000 or 150,000 package and expecting the cave. Mud bath requires height of at least 1.1 m. The long zipline requires weight over 40 kg (S5).
- Local interest: The river afternoon
- Suggested slug: dark-cave
- Suggested dot: 4 — a half-day on the river, not the picture people mean by Phong Nha Cave
- Photo must show: The river, the zipline, or the cave mouth at this site. No Commons frame in this pass showed that subject. Do not borrow the Phong Nha Cave mouth.
- Sources: S5, S6

### Mooc Spring
- Vietnamese name: Suối Nước Moọc
- Kind: sight
- Area: About a 20-minute drive from Phong Nha Cave (S7)
- What the visitor does: Walk the forest to the spring, or swim at Ghenh Mooc, Ghenh Chum, or both (S7).
- Time: About 4 hours, depending on the package (S7). The drive figure is from the cave, not from every bed.
- Fee: Sightseeing adult 80,000 VND is the only price in that table row (S7). It does not print a second seasonal cell, and it does not print a child price. Ghenh Mooc and Ghenh Chum are each 160,000 VND from 1 Apr to 30 Sep 2026 and 130,000 VND from 1 Jan to 31 Mar and from 1 Oct to 31 Dec 2026. Both areas together are 220,000 and 180,000 VND on those bands (S7). S6, from May 2024, prints older and lower service prices. Do not mix them into one number.
- From the base: About 20 minutes by car from Phong Nha Cave (S7).
- When to go: Same summer and winter clock as Dark Cave (S7). March and April are when the page says the apricot flowers are out.
- Mistake to avoid: Reading 80,000 VND as the swim. That row is sightseeing without the recreational package.
- Local interest: The river afternoon
- Suggested slug: mooc-spring
- Suggested dot: 3 — a swim after a cave, not the reason to stay
- Photo must show: The spring pool or the forest path. None accepted. Do not use the cave mouth.
- Sources: S7, S6
- Page: left out until there is a photograph of the spring.

### Son Doong
- Vietnamese name: Hang Sơn Đoòng
- Kind: sight
- Area: Inside the park, reached by a trek from the west Ho Chi Minh road. Not the village pier (S12).
- What the visitor does: A guided expedition. Oxalis describes 6 days and 5 nights. You cannot walk up and buy a ticket (S12, S13).
- Time: 6 days, 5 nights (S12, S13).
- Fee: The operator pack generated 17 Oct 2026 prints 3,000 USD and 80,000,000 VND per person for the join-in group of 10. Age 18–70. Runs January to August (S13). A private departure is a different price and was not copied. Confirm on the operator page. This is not a gate fee.
- From the base: Be in Dong Hoi by 15:00 for the operator transfer. The safety briefing that evening is compulsory (S12).
- When to go: January to August on that pack (S13).
- Mistake to avoid: Planning it as a half-day beside Phong Nha Cave.
- Local interest: The expedition
- Suggested slug: son-doong
- Suggested dot: 5 — the famous cave, and still not the base-layer day
- Photo must show: The doline or the passage at a scale that is not Paradise Cave’s boardwalk. None accepted.
- Sources: S12, S13
- Page: written 21 Oct 2026. A doline photograph was accepted. It is still not a half-day.
