# Attractions — Can Gio

Slug: `can-gio`
Vietnamese name: Cần Giờ
Province: Thành phố Hồ Chí Minh. The biosphere sits on the old huyện Cần Giờ. From 1 Jul 2025 the address is the new communes, not a district (S5).
Kind: area
Corridor: southeast
Base a traveler sleeps in: the beach town in xã Cần Giờ, or a room at the Vàm Sát forest centre. No hotel brand.
Checked: 22 Oct 2026

This is the mangrove biosphere. Ho Chi Minh City already has `ho-chi-minh-city`. Cu Chi already has `cu-chi`. Vung Tau already has `vung-tau`. Do not make a second city page. Xã Cần Giờ, xã An Thới Đông, xã Bình Khánh, and xã Thạnh An are address lines inside this place. They are not new slugs.

## Why a night here

### The creek at first light

- The picture they expect: a mangrove channel. Dark water in the foreground, đước trunks and roots through the middle, the canopy closing over the boat. The city skyline is not in this frame. The beach is the second picture, and it is a separate hour.
- Where they sleep, as an area: the seafront town in xã Cần Giờ, which is the old thị trấn Cần Thạnh and xã Long Hòa joined on 1 Jul 2025 (S5). Some people sleep at the Vàm Sát centre, where the operator prints family rooms and dorms (S4). No hotel brand.
- Where the evening is: chợ hải sản Hàng Dương on đường Thạnh Thới, beside bãi biển 30 tháng 4. The 2019 story puts the sand about 50 m from the market (S14). A bar street was not named on the pages opened.
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices):
  - Vàm Sát, the forest centre. Operator tariff: https://vamsat.vn/bang-gia-dich-vu/ (S4). It is a pin for the boats and the animals, not a ranked tour list.
  - Lăng Ông Thủy Tướng, the whale temple in the beach town. The culture department’s festival page is the pin: https://svhtt.hochiminhcity.gov.vn/tin-chi-tiet/-/chi-tiet/le-hoi-nghinh-ong-huyen-can-gio-thanh-pho-ho-chi-minh-22374-1.html (S6).
  - Chợ Hàng Dương, the evening market by the sand. Report: https://thanhnien.vn/nguoi-sai-gon-lac-buoc-trong-thien-duong-hai-san-co-khung-gia-binh-dan-185832450.htm (S14).
- Hero must include: the water, the mangrove trunks, and the boat, in one frame. Subject through the center. Water is the foreground. Canopy stays in the frame.
- A crop that would fail: a monkey’s face with no forest, a crab on a plate, empty sky, empty mud, or the Ho Chi Minh City skyline. A portrait of a statue at the whale temple will lose the roof.

## Attractions (Sight, area of interests, zone, hub, etc.)

### Khu dự trữ sinh quyển rừng ngập mặn Cần Giờ
- Vietnamese name: Khu dự trữ sinh quyển rừng ngập mặn Cần Giờ, also Rừng Sác
- Kind: zone
- Area: the whole former coastal district, southeast of the city
- What the visitor does: Rides the forest road and the creeks through mangrove that UNESCO calls the city’s green lung. Habitats on the UNESCO page: mangroves, wetlands, salt marshes, mudflats, seagrasses. The soil is river silt from the Sài Gòn and Đồng Nai, plus a brackish water table (S1). The board says people live in the transition zone. The core and the buffer are forest-guard households, staff, and a few traditional shrimp traps (S1, S2).
- Time: a day, or the nights of a weekend
- Fee: there is no single gate for the whole reserve on the pages opened. The tickets are the Vàm Sát gate and the Rừng Sác relic, below.
- From the base: this is the place
- When to go: a dry morning for the creek. Visit HCMC says to skip a storm day and does not name the wet months (S10).
- Mistake to avoid: treating it as a district of the city centre, or as a second Vũng Tàu. The 2025 resolution split the old district into communes and left Thạnh An alone (S5).
- Local interest: The mangrove
- Suggested slug: can-gio-biosphere
- Suggested dot: 5 — the night is the forest and the water
- Photo must show: a creek, mangrove trunks, and a boat, one frame, subject through the center
- unpublished: no licensed photo
- Area figures, all kept: UNESCO surface 75,740 ha, population 71,157 with no year on the page, coordinates latitude 10.52 and longitude 106.89, nomination year 2000 (S1). The board: 70,445.34 ha, more than 75,000 people in 2018, density 108 per km², recognized 21 Jan 2000 (S2). The biodiversity agency: “more than 75,000 hectares”, core 4,721 ha, buffer 41,139 ha, transition 29,880 ha, recognized 21 Jan 2000 (S3). Those three zone figures add to 75,740, which matches UNESCO and not the board. The board’s own zones are different: core 6,134.43 ha; buffer 29,152.10 ha of forest land and 12,763.56 ha of water; transition 13,227.79 ha of forest land and 7,267.47 ha of water (S2). A 2012 IUCN evaluation said 71,964 ha and 67,272 people in November 2011 (S20). Do not pick one hectare.
- Species, all kept: the board’s reserve-wide counts are 318 higher plants (37 true mangrove, 56 mangrove associates, 225 introduced), 89 insects, 282 fish, 36 amphibians, 36 reptiles, 164 birds, 35 mammals, and 66 animal plus 66 plant plankton species (S2). Visit HCMC prints over 150 plant species, over 700 non-bony aquatic animals, 130 fish, 9 amphibians, 34 reptiles, 4 mammals, and around 130 bird species (S10). Do not average them.
- Sources: S1, S2, S3, S10, S20

### Vàm Sát
- Vietnamese name: Khu du lịch sinh thái Vàm Sát
- Kind: sight
- Area: tiểu khu 15A. The tariff still says xã Lý Nhơn, huyện Cần Giờ (S4). Clause 121 put Lý Nhơn into the rearranged xã An Thới Đông (S5).
- What the visitor does: Pays the road gate, rides the shuttle, and chooses a boat from cầu Dần Xây. The operator’s circuit includes the wild-monkey reserve, a crocodile swamp and a caged fishing boat, Tràm Chim, Đầm Dơi, a forest walk, and tháp Tang Bồng (S4). Visit HCMC describes the same centre: bats at Đầm Dơi, birds at Tràm Chim, and the tower (S10).
- Time: a half day on the road gate, longer if the boat package includes Đầm Dơi. The wooden boat to Đầm Dơi is capped at 2 hours on the tariff (S4).
- Fee: operator, road gate 60,000 VND, shuttle included. Under 6 free, 6–11 half, over 11 an adult ticket (S4). Visit HCMC prints the same 60,000 road gate and the same child bands, and says the prices are for reference (S10).
- Fee, boats from cầu Dần Xây, operator, per person: canoe 1,075,000 for one, 650,000 for two, 440,000 for three, 320,000 for four to seven, 300,000 for eight to eighteen, 270,000 from nineteen. Wooden boat 165,000 from nineteen. Smaller wooden-boat cells were blank on the table (S4). Visit HCMC prints “from 270,000 VND to 895,000 VND” for that canoe leg (S10). Both bands stay.
- Fee, add-ons, operator: crocodile swamp shuttle 20,000; caged crocodile boat 60,000, minimum two people; Tràm Chim shuttle 20,000, minimum two; bird reserve 55,000 from May through October; rowing boat at Đầm Dơi 65,000; wooden boat to Đầm Dơi 85,000 for 9–16, 75,000 for 17–24, 55,000 for 25–30; canoe to Đầm Dơi 185,000, priced as three passengers even if one or two go (S4). Visit HCMC repeats the 20,000, 60,000, 55,000, 65,000, and 185,000 lines (S10).
- Fee, full water package, operator: canoe 1,335,000 for one down to 550,000 from nineteen; wooden boat 445,000 from nineteen. A package without Tràm Chim is lower, from 1,260,000 for one canoe to 370,000 for a wooden boat from nineteen (S4). Visit HCMC prints a full-service band of 550,000–1,175,000 (S10). Both stay.
- Fee, a night at the centre, operator: family room for two, weekday 400,000 day or 600,000 night, weekend 500,000 day or 750,000 night, third person extra 200,000 weekday or 250,000 weekend, breakfast included. Dorm bed, weekday 150,000 day or 200,000 night, weekend 200,000 day or 250,000 night (S4). These are the operator’s rooms, not a hotel pick.
- From the base: along đường Rừng Sác to cầu Dần Xây, or the road gate. Minutes from the beach town: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: the gate is 8:00–17:00 every day (S4). The bird ticket is May–October on that tariff. Visit HCMC’s Tràm Chim breeding line is April–October (S10). Both months stay.
- Mistake to avoid: using the tourism page’s canoe band when the operator’s table is the tariff, or the reverse. Print both.
- Local interest: The mangrove
- Suggested slug: vam-sat
- Suggested dot: 4 — the boat day inside the reserve, not the whole night
- Photo must show: a boat on the creek, mangrove on both sides, the passenger in the boat rather than a face filling the frame
- unpublished: no licensed photo
- Counts that sit here and disagree: the board says Sân Chim Vàm Sát holds about 2,000 birds of 33 species, Đầm Dơi holds more than 500 bats, mostly Pteropus lylei, and the monkey reserve holds more than 1,000 long-tailed macaques (S2). Visit HCMC says Tràm Chim is about 200 ha in the centre and 500 ha outside, with over 30 bird species and about 20,000 birds, and that Monkey Island has over 2,000 monkeys (S10). The tower is 28 m only on the tourism page (S10). Do not merge the bird counts. Do not merge the monkey counts.
- Sources: S2, S4, S5, S10

### Căn cứ Rừng Sác
- Vietnamese name: Di tích lịch sử Căn cứ Rừng Sác
- Kind: sight
- Area: đường Rừng Sác, old xã Long Hòa on the tourism page (S10). That commune is now part of xã Cần Giờ (S5).
- What the visitor does: Walks or takes a canoe into the rebuilt special-forces camp. Visit HCMC names observation posts, a military workshop, an information house, hideouts, a medical station, a supply house, and figures of Unit 10, including the raid on the Nhà Bè oil depot (S10). The board dates the national historic listing to 2004 (S2). UNESCO notes revolutionary use of the region in the wars against France and the United States, without a battle count (S1). The biodiversity agency says defoliant was sprayed many times from 1964 to 1972 and that more than 80% of the old mangrove died (S3).
- Time: a few hours once you are in the forest. Door to door from the city is the road-and-ferry problem in getting there.
- Fee: Resolution 09/2020/NQ-HĐND, effective 1 Jan 2021, adult 35,000 VND a visit, domestic and foreign. Free: older people, disabled people, the armed forces, people with revolutionary merit, the veterans’ association, poor households, children under 7. Half price: children from 7 to under 16, pupils, and students. Collection is assigned to the Củ Chi tunnels site (S18). The open reprint did not show whether that resolution was still in force in 2026. Visit HCMC prints 35,000 VND for the combat zone and says its prices are for reference (S10). The two adult figures match. The child bands do not match Vàm Sát’s bands. Do not mix them.
- Fee, canoe: Visit HCMC prints 600,000 VND a turn, round trip, into the base (S10). The resolution does not mention a canoe (S18). A second publisher for 600,000 was not opened. Print it as the tourism page’s reference figure.
- Hours: Visit HCMC prints 7:30–17:30 for Sac Forest (S10). Vàm Sát’s own clock is 8:00–17:00 (S4). They are different gates. Do not average them.
- From the base: đường Rừng Sác. The 2019 story calls that road about 30 km of trees (S14). Minutes from the beach town: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: morning, before the heat. The tourism page gives no wet-season month (S10).
- Mistake to avoid: calling this gate “Đảo Khỉ” and also calling Vàm Sát “Đảo Khỉ”, then charging one ticket twice. The monkeys at a forest gate and the monkey reserve are both described below.
- Battle figures, both from Visit HCMC: nearly 400 battles and 860 dead in the history section; “thousands of large and small battles” in the combat-zone section (S10). Keep both. Unit 10 and the years 1966 to 30 Apr 1975 are in the history section (S10).
- Local interest: The Rừng Sác base
- Suggested slug: rung-sac-base
- Suggested dot: 4 — the historic walk inside a forest day
- Photo must show: a rebuilt shelter or trench with mangrove behind it, wide enough to see both
- unpublished: no licensed photo
- Sources: S1, S2, S3, S4, S5, S10, S14, S18

### Ruộng muối
- Vietnamese name: ruộng muối Cần Giờ; làng nghề muối Cần Thạnh
- Kind: area
- Area: đường Duyên Hải in the old town, the fields at Lý Nhơn (now inside xã An Thới Đông), and đảo Thiềng Liềng in xã Thạnh An (S5, S11)
- What the visitor does: Walks the pans in the dry season and watches salt raked into piles. Phụ Nữ says most pans now crystallize on tarpaulin (S11). Dân trí says the city recognized the Cần Thạnh village as a traditional craft village, and that visitors join a salt morning (S15).
- Time: a morning in the season
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No ticket was printed.
- Area figures, both kept: about 1,500 ha, at Lý Nhơn, Cần Thạnh, and Thạnh An (S11, 10 Mar 2024). About 1,200 ha in production, more than 1,000 ha on tarpaulin, yield 80 tons a hectare (S15, 14 Jan 2020).
- Season: December–May on both stories. Crystals in 7–10 days of hard sun (S11). From first crystal to harvest, 7–9 days (S15). Both day counts stay.
- From the base: đường Duyên Hải is in the town. Minutes to Lý Nhơn or Thiềng Liềng: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Dân trí’s distances are in getting there and disagree with each other (S15).
- When to go: after sunrise on a hard sunny day in the season. Dân trí says the picture is at dawn (S15).
- Mistake to avoid: going in the rains. Both stories tie the harvest to the dry months.
- Local interest: Salt pans
- Suggested slug: can-gio-salt
- Suggested dot: 3 — a morning in season, not the reason for the night
- Photo must show: white pans, a person raking, and the mangrove or the sky’s edge still in the frame. A frame that is only white salt fails.
- unpublished: no licensed photo
- Sources: S5, S11, S15

### The salt morning
- Vietnamese name: một buổi làm muối
- Kind: lifestyle
- What a day here actually is: Diêm dân are on the pans from sunrise while the sun is hard. The visitor who comes for this walks the pans and leaves before the worst of the noon rake, which is the hour Dân trí describes as the heavy part of the job (S15). It is not the ordinary forest day.
- When: December–May (S11, S15)
- Area: Duyên Hải, Lý Nhơn, or Thiềng Liềng
- Suggested slug: salt-morning
- Photo must show: pans, a rake, and a person, with the horizon still in the frame
- unpublished: no licensed photo
- Sources: S11, S15
