## Comprehensive list of local food to try

### Lẩu cá dứa
- Vietnamese name: lẩu cá dứa nấu măng chua
- What it is: A hotpot of cá dứa, the fish the paper calls a Cần Giờ specialty, with a smooth fatty skin. The pot is a southern sour-sweet broth with bamboo shoot. You add bean sprouts, eggplant, pineapple, spring onion, and ngổ, then eat the fish with nước mắm and ớt and a little bún (S12).
- How to eat it: Shared, at a table, not from a stall box.
- When to order it: Lunch or dinner in the town.
- Area, not a shop: đường Tắc Xuất and đường Duyên Hải, old thị trấn Cần Thạnh, now xã Cần Giờ
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none on this site
- Sources: S12

### Cá dứa kho
- Vietnamese name: cá dứa kho
- What it is: The same fish, simmered. The paper quotes a diner who ordered it with rice and calls the flesh firm and a little fatty (S12).
- How to eat it: With rice, as a home-style plate.
- When to order it: Lunch.
- Area, not a shop: the town streets above
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none
- Sources: S12

### Tôm tích
- Vietnamese name: tôm tích; tôm tít
- What it is: Mantis shrimp. The paper calls it a Cần Giờ specialty and describes large animals cooked with burnt garlic. Other cooks on that page: steamed with ginger or lemongrass, grilled plain, with cheese, or with mỡ hành, or tossed with tamarind or salt and chili (S12). Thanh Niên names tôm tích among the three things sellers said visitors ask for at Hàng Dương, with ghẹ and ốc (S14).
- How to eat it: Cracked at the table. The paper notes that a small one leaves little meat once the shell is off.
- When to order it: Lunch or dinner.
- Area, not a shop: the town, or chợ Hàng Dương
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none
- Sources: S12, S14

### Ốc len
- Vietnamese name: ốc len
- What it is: A mangrove snail. Named on the town menu (S12) and among the ốc at Hàng Dương (S14). A recipe was not printed in either story. Ốc len xào dừa appears as a dish name in the 2019 market piece, without a method (S14).
- How to eat it: As ordered, steamed or stir-fried. Do not invent a coconut ratio.
- When to order it: With the seafood lunch.
- Area, not a shop: Hàng Dương, or the town streets
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none
- Sources: S12, S14

### Sò huyết
- Vietnamese name: sò huyết
- What it is: Blood cockles, named on the town menu. One cook on that page is “sò huyết lá cót” (S12). They are also among the live shellfish at Hàng Dương (S14).
- How to eat it: Cooked to order.
- When to order it: Lunch or dinner.
- Area, not a shop: the town, or Hàng Dương
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none
- Sources: S12, S14

### Vọp
- Vietnamese name: vọp; the paper also writes dọp
- What it is: A shellfish the diner calls large, not too chewy, eaten with mỡ hành and peanuts, and also grilled (S12).
- How to eat it: Grilled or with scallion oil, at the table.
- When to order it: With the seafood lunch.
- Area, not a shop: the town streets
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none
- Sources: S12

### Hàu nướng
- Vietnamese name: hàu nướng
- What it is: Oysters. A diner in the town story lists hàu among the plates (S12). The market story shows grilled oysters with cheese and with mỡ hành (S14). A recipe was not printed.
- How to eat it: Hot, off the shell.
- When to order it: At the market in the evening.
- Area, not a shop: chợ Hàng Dương
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none
- Sources: S12, S14

### Cá đuối hấp
- Vietnamese name: cá đuối hấp hành
- What it is: Stingray steamed with onion. The paper says diners also eat the fatty liver (S12). Lẩu cá đuối is named in the same piece.
- How to eat it: Shared, with rice or as a hotpot.
- When to order it: Lunch or dinner.
- Area, not a shop: the town streets
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none
- Sources: S12

### Other pots on the same menu
- Vietnamese name: lẩu cá ngát, cá bóp, cá mao, cá chẽm; also cá đuối
- What it is: The paper lists these as the other hotpots beside cá dứa, cooked with vegetables, sour bamboo, cần cà, or ngót (S12). They are town seafood, not separate specialties with their own method on the page.
- How to eat it: As a shared pot.
- When to order it: When cá dứa is not the pot.
- Area, not a shop: the town streets
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none
- Sources: S12

### Ghẹ and the market pan
- Vietnamese name: ghẹ; hải sản chọn sống rồi chế biến
- What it is: Swimmer crab and the mixed live seafood at Hàng Dương: sò, ốc, cua, ghẹ, tôm, mực. You point at a live animal and the stall cooks it, or you take a pan that is already on the fire (S14). The town story also names cua hai da and cua um chua ngọt (S12).
- How to eat it: At the market, then walk to the sand. Processing fees printed in 2019 are not copied.
- When to order it: From morning through the evening the 2019 seller described.
- Area, not a shop: chợ Hàng Dương
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none
- Sources: S12, S14

## Food streets and markets

### Chợ hải sản Hàng Dương
- Vietnamese name: chợ hải sản Hàng Dương
- Kind: food-street
- What the visitor does: Walks đường Thạnh Thới and chooses live seafood or a pan that is already cooking. The market sits beside bãi 30 tháng 4. The 2019 story says the sand is about 50 m away (S14).
- When to go: A seller in that story said she sold from about 7:00 to about 21:00, and that the seafood had come in that morning at Cầu Cảng. A market bylaw was not opened. On a busy day the story says a cook-to-order wait of about 30–40 minutes.
- Area: old xã Long Hòa, now xã Cần Giờ
- Suggested slug: hang-duong
- Photo must show: the market aisle, live seafood in tanks, and a cooking pan, wide enough to see it is a market
- unpublished: no licensed photo
- Sources: S5, S14

### Duyên Hải and Tắc Xuất
- Vietnamese name: đường Duyên Hải; đường Tắc Xuất
- Kind: food-street
- What the visitor does: Eats in the old town centre. Dân Việt sends the reader along Duyên Hải and into Tắc Xuất toward the sea for the cá dứa and the tôm tích (S12). This is the sit-down street. Hàng Dương is the market by the sand.
- When to go: lunch and dinner. A closing hour was not printed. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Area: old thị trấn Cần Thạnh, now xã Cần Giờ
- Suggested slug: duyen-hai
- Photo must show: the street, tables, and a hotpot, not a single bowl filling the frame
- unpublished: no licensed photo
- Sources: S5, S12
