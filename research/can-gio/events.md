# Events — Can Gio

The festival is not the whale temple on an ordinary day. The temple is an attraction. This file is the rite and the hội around it.

## Events and to-do

### Lễ hội Nghinh Ông Cần Giờ
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Nghinh Ông; also Tết biển on the 2025 city story
- Kind: festival
- When: The culture department, on a page stamped 7 Nov 2018, says the 15th through the 17th of lunar month 8. The main sea rite is 10:00 on the 16th. It says the rite used to fall in the middle of lunar month 3 and moved in 1914 to the full moon of month 8, so it sits on Tết Trung thu (S6). The 2025 city story says 5–7 Oct 2025, lunar 14, 15, and 16 of month 8, Ất Tỵ, and puts the sea rite at 9:00 on 7 Oct (S16). The 2026 paper says 23–27 Sep 2026, and the sea procession on 26 Sep, with no lunar date printed (S13). The next solar dates: Check on arrival. Do not pick 9:00 or 10:00. Do not collapse a three-day lunar window and a five-day 2026 window.
- Where: Lăng Ông Thủy Tướng in the beach town. The culture page also names the Rừng Sác historic park, đình Cần Thạnh, and the streets of the old thị trấn Cần Thạnh, then the sea (S6). The 2025 story names Bến đò Cơ Khí and Bến đò Tắc Xuất for the boats, and Công viên Cần Thạnh for the flag and the closing show (S16). Sài Gòn Giải Phóng in 2026 says xã Cần Giờ (S13).
- What happens: The palanquin of Nam Hải Đại tướng quân leaves the temple, goes to the boats, and the rite is held at tam giang khẩu. The culture page puts that point about 4 nautical miles out and writes 1 nautical mile as 1.8 km (S6). Offerings are livestock and rice cakes, not seafood, because the fish is the spirit’s soldier (S6). The boats return and the temple holds the túc yết, xây chầu, and hát bội (S6). In 2026 more than 100 boats went, with Hội Vạn lạch Cần Thạnh on the water (S13). In 2025 the hội added a visit to the Rừng Sác cemetery and the martyrs’ temple, a flag raising, a fishers’ thanksgiving, and a fair. Streets Lê Thương and Đặng Văn Kiều carried an exhibition. The closing show was 19:30 at Công viên Cần Thạnh. The story also lists hát bội, đờn ca tài tử, water puppets, a green marathon, and beach games inside that week (S16). Those are the hội, not separate attractions.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No ticket was printed.

The 2025 story says the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism recognized it in 2013, and that the exhibition marked 12 years of that listing and “more than 112 years” of the festival (S16). The culture page’s move-in-1914 line and the “more than 112 years” line both stay.
