# Getting there — Can Gio

Slug: `can-gio`
Checked: 22 Oct 2026

Cần Giờ is the mangrove biosphere on the southeast edge of Thành phố Hồ Chí Minh. It is not a second city page. From 1 Jul 2025 the old huyện Cần Giờ is no longer the address line. Resolution 1685 puts the beach town into xã Cần Giờ (old xã Long Hòa plus thị trấn Cần Thạnh), the forest office’s commune into the rearranged xã An Thới Đông, the ferry’s far bank into xã Bình Khánh, and leaves xã Thạnh An as it was (S5).

The crossing in use on 30 Apr 2026 and still the subject of a 13 Sep 2026 bridge story is phà Bình Khánh. The bridge is not a road you can drive (S7, S19).

Bridge figures, both kept:

- 30 Apr 2026: about 6.3 km, six lanes, investment more than 13,200 billion VND, groundbreaking January 2026, hoped construction start June 2026, main span closed end of 2028, operation June 2029 if the work goes well (S7).
- 13 Sep 2026: the lead says the whole line is about 7.3 km, and later the same story says about 7 km. The bridge portion is about 3 km. Component 2 is about 10,668 billion VND including interest during construction. Groundbreaking had happened in January 2026. A start on 25 Aug 2026 and a BT contract on 20 Sep 2026 had not been met. The feasibility study was going back for appraisal in September 2026 (S19).

Do not pick 6.3, 7.3, or 7. Do not add the investments together. They may be different slices of the project. The ferry is the crossing.

Getting around on site:

- Sleep in xã Cần Giờ, the beach town, or in the rooms the Vàm Sát tariff lists at the forest centre (S4, S5). No hotel brand.
- After the ferry, Visit HCMC sends you along the forest road. The 2019 market story calls đường Rừng Sác about 30 km of trees (S14). That is the forest road, not the whole trip from the city.
- Bus 90’s ends, on the 2025 list, are the ferry and Cần Thạnh (S17). Cần Thạnh is now inside xã Cần Giờ (S5).
- Vàm Sát’s own boats leave from cầu Dần Xây (S4). The management board is at 1541 đường Rừng Sác (S2).
- A motorbike is the mode Visit HCMC and the 2019 story both describe. A hire price was not printed. Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
