## What it is

- English name: Rung Sac base
- Vietnamese name: Di tích lịch sử Căn cứ Rừng Sác
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Rebuilt special-forces camp in the mangrove
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk or take a canoe into the rebuilt camp. Visit HCMC names observation posts, a military workshop, an information house, hideouts, a medical station, a supply house, and figures of Unit 10, including the raid on the Nhà Bè oil depot (S10).
- How long on site: a few hours once you are in the forest. No minute count was printed.
- How long including the ride from the base: the road-and-ferry problem. Minutes from the beach town: Check on arrival.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: treating this desk as the Vàm Sát gate, or mixing the two child bands.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The Sac Forest gate is a different desk from Vàm Sát, with its own fee and its own clock.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: Resolution 09/2020/NQ-HĐND, effective 1 January 2021, adult 35,000 VND a visit, domestic and foreign (S18). Visit HCMC prints the same 35,000 VND and says prices are for reference (S10). The open reprint did not show whether that resolution was still in force in 2026.
- Child, height, or senior rule: Free: older people, disabled people, the armed forces, people with revolutionary merit, the veterans’ association, poor households, children under 7. Half: children from 7 to under 16, pupils, and students (S18). These bands are not Vàm Sát’s under-6 and 6–11 bands.
- What the basic ticket includes: a visit. Collection is assigned to the Củ Chi tunnels historic site (S18).
- What costs extra: Visit HCMC prints a canoe at 600,000 VND a turn, round trip (S10). The resolution does not mention a canoe (S18). A second publisher for 600,000 was not opened.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S10, S18

## Getting there

### From Ho Chi Minh City

- Mode: the same ferry road, then đường Rừng Sác. Old address xã Long Hòa, now part of xã Cần Giờ (S5, S10).
- Time: about 1 hour 30 minutes and about 50 km (S10), or about 2 hours (S7). The 2019 story calls đường Rừng Sác about 30 km of trees (S14). That is the forest road, not the whole trip.
- Fare, if any: ferry fares as in getting-there. The relic fee is separate.
