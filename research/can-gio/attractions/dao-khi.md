## What it is

- English name: Monkey Island
- Vietnamese name: Khu Đảo Khỉ; Khỉ đuôi dài
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Long-tailed macaques in the reserve
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Watches long-tailed macaques in the trees. The tourism page also describes weekend circus turns and warns that the monkeys take bags and phones (S10). That circus line was not on the operator tariff or the board.
- How long on site: a stop on the forest circuit, not a separate day
- How long including the ride from the base: through Vàm Sát or the Rừng Sác road. Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Mistake that wastes the visit: feeding them, or planning a second full day because the nickname sounds like another island
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: the 35,000 VND line and the 60,000 VND road gate are different desks. Do not collapse them.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: Visit HCMC prints a Monkey Island entrance of 35,000 VND a person, marked for reference (S10). The operator folds the wild-monkey stop into Vàm Sát combos that start at the 60,000 road gate (S4). The board does not print a monkey ticket (S2).
- Child, height, or senior rule: UNSOURCED for a separate monkey gate
- What the basic ticket includes: UNSOURCED as a separate gate
- What costs extra: the Vàm Sát boats, on that tariff
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S2, S4, S10. Keep 35,000 and 60,000 apart.
