## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the pans in the dry season and watch salt raked into piles. Phụ Nữ says most pans now crystallize on tarpaulin (S11). Dân trí says the city recognized the Cần Thạnh village as a traditional craft village, and that visitors join a salt morning (S15).
- How long on site: a morning in the season. No minute count was printed.
- How long including the ride from the base: đường Duyên Hải is in the town. Minutes to Lý Nhơn or Thiềng Liềng: Check on arrival.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: going in the rains. Both stories tie the harvest to December–May.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The harvest is December–May. Outside those months the pans are the wrong reason to come.

- Mode: đường Duyên Hải in the old town. The fields at Lý Nhơn are now inside xã An Thới Đông. Đảo Thiềng Liềng is in xã Thạnh An (S5, S11).

### From Ho Chi Minh City

- Mode: the ferry, then the town or the Lý Nhơn road
- Time: Visit HCMC about 1 hour 30 minutes and about 50 km to the forest (S10). Tuổi Trẻ about 2 hours (S7). For the Cần Thạnh salt village, Dân trí prints about 30 km in the lead, about 60 km in the body, and about 30 km south from the old district centre (S15). All three stay.
