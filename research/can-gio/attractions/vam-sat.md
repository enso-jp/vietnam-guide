## What it is

- English name: Vam Sat
- Vietnamese name: Khu du lịch sinh thái Vàm Sát
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Forest centre for the creek boats
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Pay the road gate, ride the shuttle, and choose a boat from cầu Dần Xây. The operator’s circuit includes the wild-monkey reserve, a crocodile swamp and a caged fishing boat, Tràm Chim, Đầm Dơi, a forest walk, and tháp Tang Bồng (S4). Visit HCMC describes bats at Đầm Dơi, birds at Tràm Chim, and the tower (S10).
- How long on site: a half day on the road gate. The wooden boat to Đầm Dơi is capped at 2 hours (S4). A minute count for the whole gate was not printed.
- How long including the ride from the base: Check on arrival. Minutes from the beach town were not printed.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: using only the tourism page’s canoe band, or only the operator table. Print both.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: The 60,000 VND road gate includes the shuttle. The canoe and the wooden boat are separate.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: 60,000 VND road gate, shuttle included (S4 and S10). Over 11 is an adult ticket.
- Child, height, or senior rule: Under 6 free, ages 6–11 half (S4 and S10).
- What the basic ticket includes: the road gate and the shuttle.
- What costs extra: Operator canoe from cầu Dần Xây, per person: 1,075,000 for one, 650,000 for two, 440,000 for three, 320,000 for four to seven, 300,000 for eight to eighteen, 270,000 from nineteen. Wooden boat 165,000 from nineteen. Smaller wooden-boat cells were blank (S4). Visit HCMC prints that canoe leg as from 270,000 VND to 895,000 VND (S10). Add-ons both pages print: crocodile swamp shuttle 20,000; caged crocodile boat 60,000; bird reserve 55,000 from May through October; rowing boat at Đầm Dơi 65,000; canoe to Đầm Dơi 185,000. Wooden boat to Đầm Dơi: 85,000 for 9–16, 75,000 for 17–24, 55,000 for 25–30, capped at 2 hours (S4). Full water package, operator: canoe 1,335,000 for one down to 550,000 from nineteen; wooden boat 445,000 from nineteen. Without Tràm Chim, from 1,260,000 for one canoe to 370,000 for a wooden boat from nineteen (S4). Visit HCMC full-service band 550,000–1,175,000 (S10). Family room and dorm rates are operator rooms (S4), not a hotel pick.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S4, S10

## Getting there

### From Ho Chi Minh City

- Mode: Nguyễn Văn Linh, right onto Huỳnh Tấn Phát, phà Bình Khánh, then đường Rừng Sác (S10).
- Time: Visit HCMC about 1 hour 30 minutes and about 50 km (S10). Tuổi Trẻ about 2 hours (S7). Ferry about 10–15 minutes, about 1.2 km (S7).
- Fare, if any: From 18 March 2026, walk or bicycle 3,000 VND, motorbike 8,000, motorbike with a passenger 11,000 (S8). Car fare Check on arrival.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: S7, S8, S10
