## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: From central Pleiku, follow Quốc lộ 19 about 10 km to An Phú. Stone elephants stand at the gate. The grounds hold rông and longhouse models, a lotus pond, a fossil tree from the Chư A Thai crater in Ayun Pa, bungalows by a fish pond, and a water park with the first wave pool in the Central Highlands. Boats, pedal boats, and fishing are named (A1). The licensed frames show the one-pillar pavilion in the grounds.
