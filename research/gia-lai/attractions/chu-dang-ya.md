# Attraction research — Chu Đăng Ya

Place: `gia-lai`
Slug: `chu-dang-ya`
Status: unpublished: no licensed photo

Inactive crater about 30 km northeast of Pleiku and about 500 m above sea level on the national tourism article. Farmers plant the bowl. Wild sunflowers: Vietnam Tourism names November to December; the national tourism article names late October to December. Fee, hours, and drive minutes are Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Steep, slippery sections are named on the Vietnamese walk-up.
