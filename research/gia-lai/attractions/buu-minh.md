## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the courtyard and the hall in the tea hills of Nghĩa Hưng, Chư Păh. The west front looks toward Biển Hồ. Tiên Sơn is behind the hall. The last stretch is a pine avenue (A1).
- How long on site: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- How long including the ride from the base: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED minutes. Distance is 15 km north of Pleiku (A2) or more than 15 km (A1).
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Confusing it with Minh Thanh, the gold tower above town.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Buu Minh and Minh Thanh are different pagodas. This one is in the tea hills, not on the hill above Pleiku.
