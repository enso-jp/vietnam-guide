## What it is

- English name: Biển Hồ
- Vietnamese name: Biển Hồ (Hồ T’Nưng)
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Volcanic crater lake north of Pleiku

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Turn from Phạm Văn Đồng (Quốc lộ 14) at the Biển Hồ junction onto Tôn Đức Thắng and walk the shore of the crater lake. The Gia Lai provincial portal calls it a trace of three extinct volcanoes (S5).
- How long on site: Half a morning for the shore and viewpoints. A full loop with the tea hills needs longer.
- How long including the ride from the base: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED minutes. The lake is about 7 km north of Pleiku center (S5).
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating Biển Hồ as a beach. It is a freshwater volcanic lake.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: It is not a sea swim. Boat hire and tickets were not printed on the pages in the dossier.
