## What it is

- English name: K50
- Vietnamese name: Thác K50 (Thác Hang Én)
- Kicker (one line, what it is): Cliff waterfall in Kon Chư Răng
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Register with the rangers at Kon Chư Răng, then walk to the cliff fall in Sơn Lang and Đắk Roong, Kbang (A1, A2). This is a forest trek, not a roadside stop.
- How long on site: Nearly two days and nights on the trail (A1), or more than 140 km from Pleiku to the reserve office and then about one hour through the forest (A2). Both stay. Do not average.
- How long including the ride from the base: The more-than-140 km figure is the ride to the office (A2). Drive minutes are Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Arriving without a booking. A1 says register with the rangers first. A self-planned arrival can be turned away.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Register before you go. The trail is not a level path.
