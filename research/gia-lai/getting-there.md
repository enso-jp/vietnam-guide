# getting there

Checked: 21 Oct 2026

Travelers sleep in Pleiku, the highland city inside Gia Lai province. After the June 2025 merger, the province name on the map is still Gia Lai, but the provincial administrative center is Quy Nhơn on the coast. Pleiku remains the airport town and the lake-and-volcano base. Plan the highlands from Pleiku, not from Quy Nhơn, unless the beach is the trip.

- Nearest airport: Pleiku Airport (IATA PXU), called Cảng hàng không Pleiku on the provincial pages. - Getting around on site: In Pleiku, taxis and ride-hail are the practical choice for Biển Hồ, about 7 km north on the provincial scenic list, and for Chu Đăng Ya, about 30 km northeast (S5, S6). Motorbike hire is common in Vietnam; ride yourself only with the proper license. The Chu Đăng Ya approach includes steep, slippery slopes in wet weather (S7).
- Season limit on the journey: Vietnam Tourism’s Gia Lai feature calls November to April the drier months and names November–December for wild sunflowers and ripe rice color, with coffee flowers around February–April (S2). The national tourism article on Chu Đăng Ya names late October to December for wild sunflowers on the volcano (S7).
