# Comprehensive list of local food to try

Checked: 23 Oct 2026

### Two-bowl pho (dry pho)
- Vietnamese name: Phở khô (phở hai tô)
- What it is: One bowl of tossed dry noodles with toppings and a second bowl of hot broth to sip or pour (S12).
- How to eat it: Mix the dry bowl, add herbs and fermented black soybean sauce typical of Gia Lai, and drink the broth separately or combine to taste (S12, S13).
- When to order it: Breakfast through early afternoon; many shops close by mid-afternoon (S12).
- Area, not a shop: Nguyen Van Troi street in Pleiku is the concentration named on the national tourism food page (S12). Dien Hong food street also lists two-bowl pho at night (S8).
- Meal: breakfast
- Neighbor dish to link: `pho` (shared dish page)
- Sources: S8, S12, S13, S26, S27
- Price: Go Vietnam re-read prints a common 2025 range of 35,000–60,000 VND (S12). S26 prints about 35,000 VND. S27 prints 50,000 VND for one regular bowl on Nguyễn Văn Trỗi.

### Grilled chicken with bamboo-tube rice
- Vietnamese name: Gà nướng với cơm lam
- What it is: Charcoal-grilled marinated chicken served with sticky rice cooked in fresh bamboo tubes (S14).
- How to eat it: Break open the bamboo tube for the rice; dip chicken in lime, salt, and pepper (S13).
- When to order it: Lunch through evening; VNA says highland eateries often serve from noon to late evening (S14).
- Area, not a shop: Le Duan street alleys in Pleiku are named at area level in the VNA long-form (S14). Tan Son commune near Chu Đăng Ya is named for grilled chicken after the volcano visit in the national tourism walk-up (S11).
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none
- Sources: S11, S13, S14, S16, S17, S23
- Price: about 200,000 VND a serving (S13); about 250,000 VND on Phạm Ngọc Thạch and Hàn Thuyên (S17); about 300,000 VND (S14, 21 December 2022). All three stay.
- Dip: peanut and sesame salt (S16). Lime, salt, and pepper (S13 caption).

### Sun-dried beef
- Vietnamese name: Bò một nắng

- How to eat it: Grill and dip in yellow ant salt. S16 also names salt, lime and pepper, and chili sauce. S13 names salt-lime-pepper, chili sauce, and Ede weaver-ant salt. S18 says Jrai and Bahnar everyday food.
- When to order it: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED hour
- Area, not a shop: Krông Pa. A Pleiku street was not printed.
- Meal: main
- Neighbor dish to link: none
- Sources: S16, S18, S13, S17
- Price: S17 prints about 500,000 VND/kg to carry home (29 May 2016). A plated price is Check on arrival — UNSOURCED

### Fermented crab noodles

## Food streets and markets

### Dien Hong food street
- Vietnamese name: Phố ẩm thực Điện Hồng
- Kind: food-street
- What the visitor does: Walks the formalized night market with 73 lots, including OCOP souvenir kiosks and food stalls for two-bowl pho, noodles, grilled dishes, and snacks (S8).
- When to go: Evening after the April 2026 opening; exact stall hours Check on arrival — UNSOURCED
- Area: Nguyen Thien Thuat–Tran Phu, Dien Hong ward, Pleiku
- Suggested slug: `dien-hong`
- Photo must show: lit stalls and pedestrians. No licensed photo in this pass.
- Sources: S8

### Nguyen Van Troi pho strip
- Vietnamese name: Phố phở Nguyễn Văn Trỗi
- Kind: food-street
- What the visitor does: Eats two-bowl pho at morning shops along the street named on the national tourism food page (S12). This is a daytime eating strip, not the same as Dien Hong at night.
- When to go: Early morning through midday (S12)
- Area: Central Pleiku
- Suggested slug: do not publish a second page; cover on the food paragraph and shared `pho` dish
- Photo must show: a two-bowl pho set. Use the shared pho dish photo if licensed.
