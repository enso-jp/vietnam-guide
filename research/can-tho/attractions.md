# Research dossier — Can Tho

Slug: `can-tho`
Province: Can Tho. Decision 1519/QĐ-TTg of 2 Dec 2023, as reprinted by the city legal-education portal, plans the pre-merger city: 1,440.40 km², five urban districts (Ninh Kiều, Cái Răng, Bình Thủy, Ô Môn, Thốt Nốt) and four rural districts (Phong Điền, Thới Lai, Cờ Đỏ, Vĩnh Thạnh) (S14). Vietnamese Wikipedia says that after the 2025 rearrangement the city also takes in the former Hậu Giang and Sóc Trăng provinces, about 6,360.83 km², and keeps its administrative centre in phường Ninh Kiều (S15). This dossier is the river-city base travelers already use: Ninh Kiều, Cái Răng, Bình Thủy, and Phong Điền. It does not research Soc Trang town or Nga Bảy.
Kind: city
Corridor: mekong-delta
Base a traveler sleeps in: River rooms in the centre, within walk of Bến Ninh Kiều. Never a hotel brand.

## Why a night here

- The picture they expect: Wholesale boats on the river at Cái Răng, with produce on the poles, or the river town at Ninh Kiều: the Hậu, the quay, and boats. Vietnam Tourism calls Can Tho a river-delta city where the river is everywhere, and names the floating markets, old houses, boat trips, orchards, and temples (S1). The department calls Ninh Kiều the symbol of the city, at the meeting of the Hậu and the Cần Thơ river (S7).
- Where they sleep, as an area: The centre, so the dawn boat is honest. Vietnam Tourism says stay in the centre to reach Ninh Kiều, Chùa Ông, the Bình Thủy house, and Cái Răng (S1). The department quay page says hotels and eating rooms cluster on that waterfront (S7). A river room. Never a hotel brand. Vietnam Tourism’s dining chapter names one resort on the Hậu; that name is under Notes in `food.md` and is not a pick.
- Where the evening is: Bến Ninh Kiều. The department says the quay is lit and crowded after dark, and that river restaurants there run 19:30–21:00 with đờn ca tài tử (S7). The 2016 city portal puts snack stalls on the quay from about 16:00 to 19:00 and tài tử on a yacht from 20:00 to 23:00 (S17). Record both evening windows. The night market listing on Hai Bà Trưng prints 17:00–22:00 (S9).
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): Chợ nổi Cái Răng, the wholesale market the national site and the department both treat as the reason to be on the water at dawn (S1, S6). Bến Ninh Kiều, the quay the boats leave from and the walk after dark (S7, S2). They are pins, not a ranked bar list.
- Hero must include: The floating market or the river town. For the market: several trading boats, the cây bẹo poles with produce hanging on them, and enough river that a card crop still reads as a market, subject through the center (S1, S2). For the river town: the Hậu, the quay, and boats in one color frame, subject through the center, with enough of the far bank that the crop is still a town (S7).
- A crop that would fail: A river bridge under construction. A generic bridge with no boats and no market. The published hero is that construction frame; it fails this brief. Empty sky. A tight slice of one hull that cuts off the poles and the other boats. The cable-stayed Cần Thơ bridge alone, even though the quay looks toward it (S7). A portrait of the bronze statue that cuts the head off the base.

## Attractions (Sight, area of interests, zone, hub, etc.)

### Cai Rang floating market
- Vietnamese name: Chợ nổi Cái Răng
- Kind: sight
- Area: The department listing says phường Cái Răng (S6). Vietnam Tourism’s English page says “downstream of Can Tho River, 46 Hai Bà Trưng, Ninh Kiều” (S1). Those two addresses do not match. Hai Bà Trưng is the quay street in Ninh Kiều (S7). A reprint on the department site says the market is 6 km by road from the centre (S6b). The national dining page also says six kilometres (S3). Do not print 46 Hai Bà Trưng as the mooring.
- What the visitor does: Rides a small boat among wholesale boats of fruit and vegetables. Sellers hang a sample on a long pole, the cây bẹo, so a buyer can see the goods without a shout over the engines (S1, S2). The 2016 portal also describes calling and bargaining (S17). Record both. Breakfast boats sell hủ tiếu, bún riêu, and coffee (S6, S2). The department says the market’s trading culture was put on the national intangible-heritage list in 2016 (S6).
- Time: The department listing prints 60 minutes at the spot (S6). The ride from Ninh Kiều is about 30 minutes on that listing and on the four-day piece (S6, S5).
- Fee: Department listing: đang cập nhật. Check on arrival for a ticket. Boat hire: that same listing does not print a number. The advice-page extract prints 300,000–700,000 VND a boat (S21). Record both. Do not pick.
- Hours, all of them, none chosen: Department listing fields say open 4:00 SA and close 12:00 SA, and the prose says the useful visit is 5:00–7:00 (S6). The 12:00 SA close reads as a full-day form, not a counted market end. Vietnam Tourism’s place page says 4:30–8:00 (S1). The floating-flavour page says be on the water by 5:00 or 5:30, and that vendors pack up by 8:00 (S2). The four-day piece says early morning to midday, rise at 5:00 (S5). The towns page says the market starts around 5:00 and tells the reader to be on a boat by 6:00 (S4). The 2016 portal says start at 5:00 and names 5:00–9:00 (S17). Another portal page says open from 5:00, busiest 6:00–9:00 (S18). The city’s “top 7” page says 4:30–6:30 (S19). A “must-have” portal page says 4:30–8:00 (S20). The advice extract says 5:00–9:00 (S21).
- From the base: Boat from Bến Ninh Kiều, about 30 minutes, or a nearer pier the listing does not name (S6). Road figure: 6 km (S3, S6b).
- When to go: Before 8:00 if the flavour page is the clock (S2). Flood months September–November are the season the place page ties to this boat (S1).
- Mistake to avoid: Arriving with a late tour bus. The wholesale boats are a dawn scene (S2, S5).
- Local interest: Floating market
- Suggested slug: `cai-rang`
- Suggested dot: 5 — the sight the night is for
- Photo must show: Several trading boats, cây bẹo poles with fruit or vegetables, and the river, subject through the center. Not a bridge. unpublished: no licensed photo
- Sources: S1, S2, S3, S4, S5, S6, S6b, S17, S18, S19, S20, S21
