## What it is

- English name: Phong Dien floating market
- Vietnamese name: Chợ nổi Phong Điền
- Kicker: Small farm-boat market on the river at Nhơn Ái
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene: Farmers sell straight from their gardens onto small boats; fewer visitors than Cái Răng (S40). The procuracy names it with Cái Răng as the city's two famous floating markets (S10).
- When: best 5:30–6:30 (S40). Commercial guides: peak 4:30–6:30 (vietnamtourism.com), 4:00–9:00 with 5:30–7:30 busiest (govietnam.travel), 6:00–8:00 busiest (canthoreview.vn). The page prints the Vietnam Airlines window.
- How long on site: UNSOURCED on an official page.
- Mistake: Swapping it for Cái Răng in a caption. Cái Răng is the large wholesale market (S6, S10).
