## What the visitor does

- The scene: A cable-stayed bridge 2.75 km long with a 550 m main span, towers 175.3 m high, and 39 m of clearance for ships (S36). Six lanes: four for cars, two for bicycles and motorbikes (S36). The department calls it the cable-stayed bridge with the longest main span in Southeast Asia when it was finished in 2010, and says you see it from Ninh Kiều (S7). Vietnam Tourism names the bridge as the view from the evening dinner boat at the wharf (S41).
- History: construction began 25 Sep 2004; an approach span collapsed on 26 Sep 2007, official toll 54; opened 24 Apr 2010; it replaced the National Route 1 ferries to Vĩnh Long (S36).
- How long: UNSOURCED. It is a view from the quay or the dawn boat, or a crossing by road.
- Mistake: Making the bridge the picture of the city. The quay and the market are the reason to come (dossier, Ninh Kieu entry).
