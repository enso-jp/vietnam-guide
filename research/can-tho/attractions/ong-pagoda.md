## What it is

- English name: Ong Pagoda
- Vietnamese name: Chùa Ông (Quảng Triệu Hội Quán)
- Kicker: Cantonese assembly hall of 1896 on the quay
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene: A hall built by Hoa people from Quảng Châu and Triệu Khánh to honor Quan Thánh Đế (Quan Công), on 532 m² at 32 Hai Bà Trưng, begun 1894 and finished 1896, most materials brought from China (S37, S23). Incense coils and a deep-red hall (S5). National monument since 1993 (S21).
- Busiest days: full-moon days and Tết; the main rite is Quan Thánh Đế's day on lunar 24/6 (S37).
- How long on site: UNSOURCED on an official page. A commercial page gives 20–40 minutes; not used.
- Mistake: Budgeting a museum hour. The four-day piece treats it as a stop on a stroll (S5).
