- Kind: area

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk the 7,000 m² park on the right bank of the Hậu where it meets the Cần Thơ river (S1, S7). Look across to the cable-stayed Cần Thơ bridge and the islets of cồn Ấu and Xóm Chài (S7). Cross the S-shaped footbridge, about 200 m with two lotus shapes and LED lights (S1; 199 m on S8). After dark, eat at the night market on Hai Bà Trưng, 17:00–22:00, with stalls setting up around 16:00 (S9). River restaurant boats with đờn ca tài tử run 19:30–21:00 (S7); the 2016 portal says 20:00–23:00 (S17).
- How long on site: 120 minutes on the department listing (S7).
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Coming only by day. The quay is lit and crowded after dark (S7).
- Notice: none.

## Getting there

- Mode: on foot. The quay is the base (S1, S7).
