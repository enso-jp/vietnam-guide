## What it is

- English name: My Khanh tourist village
- Vietnamese name: Làng du lịch Mỹ Khánh
- Kicker: Orchard and folk-games park on the Cần Thơ river
- Kind: area

## What the visitor does

- The scene: More than 30 ha on the Cần Thơ river, between the Cái Răng and Phong Điền markets (S38b, S39). Free with the gate: a 115-year-old southern house, a flower garden, pig and dog races at 9:45, 11:00, 13:00, and 15:00, an animal circus at 10:30, 11:30, 13:30, and 15:30, and a craft village making hủ tiếu, coconut candy, and folk cakes (S38). Seasonal fruit picking is priced by the garden (S38). Garden plates: cá rô kho tộ, cá lóc nướng trui, bún nước lèo, lẩu cua đồng, bánh xèo (S29).
- When: late morning after the floating market (S17).
- Mistake: Treating it as the reason to come. The market is the reason; this is the orchard stop.

## Fee

- 149,000 VND adult (over 1.3 m); 99,000 VND child 1 m to under 1.3 m; free under 1 m (S38).
- Extras: pedal boats 50,000 VND per 30 minutes, glass bridge 50,000 VND, pool 50,000 VND, Go Kart 100,000 VND (S38).

## Hours

- 07:30–17:00 daily (S38, S38b). Department listing: 07:00–18:00 (S39). Page prints the operator's hours.

## Getting there

### From Ninh Kiều
- Mode: road, about twenty minutes (S38b, S39); about 10 km (S21). 335 Lộ Vòng Cung, phường An Bình (S38).

### From Trúc Lâm Phương Nam
- A few hundred metres (S21).
