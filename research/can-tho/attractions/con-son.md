## What it is

- English name: Con Son islet
- Vietnamese name: Cồn Sơn
- Kicker: Orchard islet in the Hậu at Bình Thủy

## What the visitor does

- The scene: A 70 ha islet in the Hậu (S41). Visit a fish raft, feed the fish rice, and watch the flying snakehead jump; make southern folk cakes (bánh bò lụi, bánh ít, bánh bột lọc, bánh lá mít, bánh da lợn, bánh đúc, bánh xèo, bánh khọt); eat fruit in the gardens (S41).
- How long: UNSOURCED.
- Mistake: Confusing Cồn Sơn with cồn Ấu, the islet you see from Ninh Kiều (S7).
