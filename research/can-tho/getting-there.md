## From Ho Chi Minh City

### Road
- Distance: Vietnam Tourism prints about 165 km, by motorbike or private car on the Mỹ Thuận–Cần Thơ expressway (S1). Vietnamese Wikipedia prints 155 km between the city centres (S15). Record both. Do not pick one.
- The expressway corridor is a different number. Thanh Niên, 30 Dec 2023, says the Ho Chi Minh City–Can Tho expressway pieces together make 120 km, shorten the old road by 50 km, and take about 2 hours instead of 3.5 hours (S13). Doanh Nghiệp Việt Nam’s inauguration report of 24 Dec 2023 says the same 120 km corridor, “hơn 2 giờ” instead of about 3.5 hours (S13b). The 120 km is the expressway length those articles print, not a third centre-to-centre distance. Record 165 km, 155 km, and 120 km of expressway as three different measurements.
- Speed on the Mỹ Thuận–Cần Thơ section: maximum 90 km/h, minimum 60 km/h, on both of those reports (S13, S13b). Thanh Niên says two-way traffic opened 30 Dec 2023. The inauguration report says temporary use from 07:00 on 25 Dec 2023.
