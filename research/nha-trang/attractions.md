## Why a night here

### The sand, the surf, and the towers on Trần Phú

- The picture they expect: Wake up on the city beach. Vietnam Tourism puts sunbathers on Nha Trang Beach by day, and the 7-reasons page calls Trần Phú a seven-kilometre stretch of sand and water with palms, beach bars, and seafood rooms along it (S1, S2). The bureau’s bay is the water in front of that sand: 19 islands, dry from January to August, rain from September to December (S3). The airport is not the picture. The Pasteur gate is not the picture.
- Where they sleep, as an area: Rooms on the beachfront along Trần Phú, facing the sand (S1, S2). Some nights are on Hòn Tre, where the bureau describes a resort and golf stay and Vietnam Tourism names Vinpearl Resort and Spa Nha Trang Bay for families (S2, S3). A brand is not a pick. The sand is the reason to sleep here. The island is the other bed, reached by the cable crossing.
- Where the evening is: The walking street and night market behind 46 Trần Phú, 137 metres, free (S10). The beach park is walked early and after dark (S14). Vietnam Tourism also names beachfront parties at Sailing Club and Skylight (S1, S2).
- Scene landmarks (few, sourced, no prices): The night market, https://dulichso.khanhhoa.gov.vn/en/article/discover-nha-trang-night-market-walking-street-a-glimpse-of-rural-charm-in-the-city-heart (S10). Sailing Club, named by Vietnam Tourism as a beachfront venue of more than twenty years, with no street number on that page (S2). Skylight, named on the 43rd floor of Havana Hotel, with no street number (S2). They are pins. No prices and no scores. Address of the two venues: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED beyond those sentences.
- Hero must include: The surf, the sand, and the beachfront towers in one frame, subject through the centre. Palms may sit between the sand and the towers. The islands can sit on the horizon. The opened file that does this is Commons `Nha Trang Beach 10.jpg` (S24).

### Po Nagar towers
- Vietnamese name: Tháp Bà Po Nagar, also Tháp Pô Nagar and Yang Po Inư Nagar (S4, S5, S6)
- Kind: sight
- Area: Đồi Cù Lao, about 2 km north of the centre, at the mouth of the Cái, phường Vĩnh Phước on both tourism pages (S4, S5, S6). Address Hai Tháng Tư on the digital page (S5). Address 61 Đường 2/4 on the resource page (S6). Đường 2/4 is Hai Tháng Tư. The street number 61 is only on the resource page. Both stay.
- What the visitor does: Walks the hill to the Cham towers and the statue of the goddess. The digital page says the complex was built from the 8th to the 13th centuries, site area 17,683.6 m², and that only the east door of the main tower is open (S5). The resource page says the main tower was built in 813–817 and repaired in the 11th century, and that four towers remain of six (S6). The visitor also meets a living festival: the national intangible rite of 2012, lunar 20–23 of month 3 on the heritage page and the resource page (S4, S6). In 2026 the police window is wider. See `events.md`.
- Time: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No visit length was printed. It is a hill and towers, not a museum hour the pages describe.
- Fee: The resource page prints 22,000 VND a person a visit (S6). The digital page prints no ticket (S5). The police page says entry is free from 6 May to 11 May 2026 (S12). All three stay. Do not pick one number for the page.
- Hours: 06:00–17:30 on the digital page (S5). 08:00–18:00 on the resource page (S6). Both stay.
- Hill height: over 20 m on the digital page (S5). About 10–12 m on the resource page (S6). Both stay. A 50 m line on a 2017 news page was not opened.
- Tower heights: main tower about 23 m on both pages (S5, S6). South tower about 18 m on both, 13th century (S5, S6). Northwest about 9 m on both (S5, S6). Southeast about 7 m and around the 13th century on the digital page (S5). 7.1 m and the 11th–12th centuries on the resource page (S6). Both southeast pairs stay.
- Rank: national historical-cultural relic on the festival page (S4). Special national relic on the police page and on the 17 January 2025 decision page, Quyết định 152/QĐ-TTg (S12, S25). Both ranks stay.
- From the base: About 2 km north (S5, S6). Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- When to go: Morning for the brick. The festival days for the rites. The solar date moves. Check on arrival except the 2026 police window, which is printed (S12).
- Mistake to avoid: Quoting one hill height, one hour, or one ticket. The published attraction photo is a relief of Shiva. It is not the towers.
- Local interest: The Cham hill
- Suggested slug: po-nagar
- Suggested dot: 4 — the historical stop beside a beach day, not the day itself
