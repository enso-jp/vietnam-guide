## What it is

- English name: Oceanographic Museum
- Vietnamese name: Bảo tàng Hải dương học
- Kicker (one line, what it is): The marine museum at Cầu Đá
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk to 01 Cầu Đá, enter the institute front with both museum signs, then the tanks and specimen halls. Trường Sa park is the same grounds (A4).
- How long on site: 90 to 120 minutes for the museum and the park (A4).
- How long including the ride from the base: The south end of the beach road. Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. The older “about 5 km south” line was not opened and stays out.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Using 89 Trần Phú from the English information block, or arriving on the other page’s clock.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Ward hours 06:00–18:00 (A2). Provincial English hours 07:30–17:00 (A3). Both stay on the page.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: About 40,000 VND adult (A2). A2 says the price can change. A4 lists 40,000 VND adult, 20,000 VND student, 10,000 VND pupil, free under 6, and says the park has no separate ticket.
- Child, height, or senior rule: About 20,000 VND student on A2. Pupil and under-6 figures are on A4 only.
- What the basic ticket includes: Museum and, on A4, the park.
- What costs extra: UNSOURCED.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: A1 leaves the price unstated. Use A2 and A4.

## Hours

- Open: 06:00–18:00 every day, including holidays (A2). 07:30–17:00 (A3). Both stay.
- Last entry, if it differs: UNSOURCED.
- Closed, weekly or seasonal: A2 says every day, including holidays.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: A2, A3. A1 prints neither hours nor a ticket.

## Getting there

### From Trần Phú

- Mode: Follow the beach road south to Cầu Đá.
- Time: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Fare, if any: UNSOURCED.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: A1, A3.

- Address to use: 01 Cầu Đá (A1, and the body of A3). The English information block also lists 89 Trần Phú (A3). That line is a stray address. Do not send people there.
- Founded: fisheries service 14 September 1922; marine-biology museum 1923 (A1).
- Collection, from the provincial English page “according to the institute”: tanks of more than 300 marine species, about 23,000 samples, about 5,000 species, exhibition space about 5,000 m² (A3). The institute museum page itself did not carry those counts when opened (A1, earlier dossier).
- Visits: hundreds of thousands a year (A1).
- Coordinates: not recorded.
