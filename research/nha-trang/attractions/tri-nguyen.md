## What it is

- Kicker (one line, what it is): The rock aquarium on Hòn Miễu
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Boat to Hòn Miễu, land at the stone pier, walk the artificial-rock aquarium (A1, photos).
- How long on site: An island stop. Minutes inside: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- How long including the ride from the base: A bay crossing. Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating 6,000 VND as the aquarium door.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Scenic fee for Đảo Hòn Miễu (Trí Nguyên) is 6,000 VND a person each crossing, plus 3,000 VND at the pier (A2). The boat and any tank ticket are separate.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: Aquarium door: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. It was not in the scenic-fee notice.
- Child, height, or senior rule: On the scenic fee, under 6 exempt, child 6 to under 16 half, Khánh Hòa residents exempt (A2). Accident insurance excluded (A2).
- What the basic ticket includes: The 6,000 VND charge is the crossing.
- What costs extra: Boat fare, and the tank ticket if one is charged at the door.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: A2 for the crossing. Door: UNSOURCED.
