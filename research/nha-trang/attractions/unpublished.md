## Tháp Bà mud baths

- Slug if published later: `thap-ba`
- unpublished: no licensed photo of the pools
- What it is: The oldest and best-known mud baths, on Vietnam Tourism (S39). Communal and private mud, mineral pools, soak at least 20 minutes. No price on that page.
- Address and distance, both kept: 15 Ngọc Sơn, Ngọc Hiệp, about 4 km north, grounds 3 ha, behind Po Nagar, hours 07:00–19:30 daily (S41). Tourism-resource page, same address, about 4 km north, no hours and no ticket in the extract (S42). Vietnam Tourism says “just 6 kilometers” (S39). The 4 km and 6 km conflict stays in the dossier.
- Provincial mud-advice article names Tháp Bà, Trăm Trứng, and I-Resort. A soak of 15–20 minutes on that page is medical advice, not the visit length (S43).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.

## Hòn Lao (Monkey Island)

- Slug if published later: `hon-lao`
- unpublished: no photo where the monkeys or a clear island landmark read
- Ninh Hòa government page: 15 km north, Nha Phu, hundreds of monkeys, circus, swim (S46).
- Vietnam Tourism seven-reasons page: more than a thousand primates (S2). The hundreds and the thousand stay as a conflict.
- Operator longphutourist.com, opened for the unpublished note only: 07:30–16:30, pier tickets (adult 220,000 VND at the pier and 185,000 VND online). Those are operator prices and stay off a public card until a photo exists (S47).
- Photo reject: Monkey Island / Đảo Khỉ panoramio (VCTBR, CC BY 3.0) shows a tidal flat, a thatch-hut shore, and a distant temple. No monkeys.

## Ba Hồ falls

- Slug if published later: `ba-ho`
- unpublished: only one licensed photo
- Ninh Hòa page: about 10 km north of Hòn Lao on QL1A, stream from Hòn Sơn over 660 m, three lakes 200–300 m apart, length over 3 km (S46).
- Operator baho.vn, unpublished note only: about 25 km north. Ticket page 200,000 VND includes water, pool, and skywalk. Hours on the ticket 08:00–16:30 and on the homepage 07:00–16:30. Address Thôn Vạn Thuận, Xã Nam Ninh Hòa versus older Ninh Ích (S48). Conflicts stay here.
- Photo: `File:Suối Ba Hồ 25.jpg`, CC BY-SA 3.0, Tycho/shansov.net. One file. The same file cannot be the second frame.
