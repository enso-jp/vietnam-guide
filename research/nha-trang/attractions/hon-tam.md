## What it is

- English name: Hòn Tằm
- Vietnamese name: Hòn Tằm
- Kicker (one line, what it is): The island beach and the water sports
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Boat to the island, swim the palm beach, then the water sports (flyboard, jet ski) or walk Hoàng Hoa village (A1).
- How long on site: A boat day. A clock on the island: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- How long including the ride from the base: 7 km from Nha Trang (A1). Minutes on the water: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Treating the 8,000 VND scenic fee as the resort or sports ticket.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Scenic fee 8,000 VND a person each crossing, plus 3,000 VND at the pier (A2). Boat, chair, flyboard, and resort are separate.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: Check on arrival for the boat and the resort. Scenic fee only: 8,000 VND for the Hòn Tằm route (A2).
- Child, height, or senior rule: Under 6 exempt, child 6 to under 16 half, Khánh Hòa residents exempt, with the other groups named in the notice (A2). The fee excludes accident insurance (A2).
- What the basic ticket includes: The scenic fee is the crossing, not the beach chair or the sports.
- What costs extra: Boat fare, water sports, resort day. Amounts: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: A2 for the scenic fee. Resort door: UNSOURCED.

## Hours

- Open: Boats leave the island harbor through the day (A1).
- Last entry, if it differs: UNSOURCED.
- Closed, weekly or seasonal: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.

## Getting there

### From the inland pier

- Mode: Boat. Collection points named in the notice: Bến tàu Du lịch Nha Trang and Bến thủy nội địa Phú Quý (A2). Pay at https://muavedulichnhatrang.com.vn (A2).
- Time: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. Distance 7 km (A1).
- Fare, if any: Scenic fee 8,000 VND plus pier 3,000 VND. Boat fare separate and unsourced.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: A1, A2.

- Do not call MerPerle mud photos the city’s Tháp Bà mud baths. Vietnam Tourism’s best-mud page lists Tháp Bà, 100 Egg, and I-Resort, not Hòn Tằm (S39). Hoàng Hoa stays in the body of this card. It is not a second sight.
