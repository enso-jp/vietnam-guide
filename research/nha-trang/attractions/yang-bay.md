## What it is

- English name: Yang Bay
- Vietnamese name: Yang Bay
- Kicker (one line, what it is): The forest waterfall and the lake
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Ride inland to the forest park, walk the falls and the lake (Yang Bay 1, Yang Bay 2, Hố Cho, Yang Khang), and take the mud bath only on the top ticket (A1, A2).
- How long on site: A day. A clock: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. A2 also describes a night in a tent.
- How long including the ride from the base: About 38 km (A1) and about 40 km (A2). Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Going to 77 Yersin in the city, or buying the 120,000 VND gate when the mud is the reason for the trip.
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: 120,000 VND general, 250,000 VND with the mineral bath, 390,000 VND with the mineral bath and the mud bath (A1). A2 leaves the price out.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: 120,000 VND general (A1).
- Child, height, or senior rule: UNSOURCED.
- What the basic ticket includes: The gate (A1). Mineral bath is the 250,000 combo. Mud bath is the 390,000 combo (A1).
- What costs extra: The two combos above.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: A1. A2 lists no price.

## Getting there

### From the centre

- Mode: Road inland to Khánh Phú, Khánh Vĩnh.
- Time: About 38 km (A1). About 40 km (A2). Minutes: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Fare, if any: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: A1, A2.

- Address, both kept: Khánh Phú, Khánh Vĩnh (A1). 77 Yersin, Nha Trang (A2). Use Khánh Vĩnh for the forest park. The Yersin line is a city street.
- The falls are low and sit on a lake (A1). Raglai name: heavenly waterfall (A2). Flower field more than 10,000 m² and a Raglai stage with gongs and the lithophone (A2). Khánh Sơn lithophone also on A1.
- This card is how the mud-bath draw appears. Tháp Bà, the oldest mud baths, stayed unpublished for lack of a licensed pool photo. See `unpublished.md`.
