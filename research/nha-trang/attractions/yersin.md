## What it is

- English name: Yersin Museum
- Vietnamese name: Bảo tàng Alexandre Yersin
- Kicker (one line, what it is): The doctor’s rooms in the Pasteur Institute
- Kind: sight

## What the visitor does

- The scene, in order: Walk Trần Phú to the Pasteur Institute, enter the doors lettered Bảo tàng Yersin / Musée Yersin, then the rooms of letters, instruments, and the resting room with the iron bed (A1, photo).
- How long on site: 30 to 45 minutes (A1). The room is about 100 m² (A1).
- How long including the ride from the base: A walk along Trần Phú. Minutes from a given hotel: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Mistake that wastes the visit: Arriving Saturday afternoon, Sunday, or a public holiday (A1).
- Notice, if one fact would waste the day: Closed Saturday afternoon, Sunday, and public holidays.

## Fee

- Basic price, and who pays it: 26,000 VND a person (A1, 26 April 2026).
- Child, height, or senior rule: Decision 91/2008/QĐ-BTC set 26,000 VND for adults and 5,000 VND for students, pupils, and children (A3). The 2026 ward page states only 26,000 VND (A1). Do not treat 5,000 VND as the current sole price.
- What the basic ticket includes: The museum rooms (A1).
- What costs extra: UNSOURCED.
- Source, or UNSOURCED: A1, A3.

## Hours

- Open: 07:30–11:30 and 14:00–17:00, Monday through Saturday morning (A1).
- Last entry, if it differs: UNSOURCED.
- Closed, weekly or seasonal: Saturday afternoon, Sunday, and public holidays (A1).
- Source, or UNSOURCED: A1. The relic page’s 09:00–22:00 line reads as a CMS default for the complex and is not the museum door (A2).
