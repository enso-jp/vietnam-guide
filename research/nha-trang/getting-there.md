## Province

Nghị quyết 202/2025/QH15, clause 13, joins the whole area and population of Ninh Thuận and Khánh Hòa into one province that keeps the name Khánh Hòa. The reprint prints 8,555.86 km² and 2,243,554 people, borders with Đắk Lắk, Lâm Đồng, and the East Sea, passage on 12 June 2025, and new provincial governments from 1 July 2025 (S17). Nha Trang is a city inside that province. It is not the province.

Older ward names are still what the tourism pages print: Vĩnh Phước, Lộc Thọ, Vạn Thạnh, Phước Tân, Phương Sơn (S5, S6, S8, S10, S13, S19). The Long Sơn page also says newer wording puts the pagoda in phường Tây Nha Trang (S8). Both layers stay. A gazette PDF for the 2025 commune rearrangement was not opened, so this dossier does not copy a four-ward list out of a commentary.
