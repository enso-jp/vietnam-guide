## Events and to-do

### Po Nagar festival
- Vietnamese name: Lễ hội Tháp Bà Pônagar, also Lễ hội Thiên Y A Na Thánh Mẫu and Lễ vía Bà (S4)
- Kind: festival
- When: The heritage page and the resource page say every year from the 20th to the 23rd of lunar month 3 (S4, S6). The police page for 2026 says 6 May to 11 May 2026, lunar 19–25 of month 3 (S12). The lunar window and the 2026 solar window are not the same span. Both stay. A later year’s solar dates: Check on arrival. The date moves.
- Where: Tháp Bà Pônagar, đồi Cù Lao, phường Vĩnh Phước (S4).
- What happens: Robe change, lanterns on the river, a peace rite, a noon offering, a traditional rite, hát bội, a thank-offering, bóng dance, and a water-and-fruit contest (S4). The police page says the 2026 festival has twelve groups of activities, including the water procession, the robe change, a palanquin, the lanterns, and the peace rite (S12).
- Clock conflicts, all kept: the heritage page puts the traditional rite at 4:00–6:00 on the 23rd (S4). The resource page puts that rite at 6:00–8:00 on the 23rd (S6). Lanterns 19:00–21:00 on the 20th, and the robe change at noon on the 20th, agree on both of those pages (S4, S6).
- Fee: Free from 6 May to 11 May 2026, on the police page (S12). Outside that window the resource page prints 22,000 VND and the digital page prints no ticket (S5, S6). See the towers entry. Do not pick one.
- Not the same as attraction slug: po-nagar
- Photo must show: The gathering, the robe change, or the lanterns on the river. A tower with no rite is the attraction photo, not the festival.
- unpublished: no licensed photo of the festival. The tower frame is the monument.
- Sources: S4, S5, S6, S12

### Sea festival, and the 2025 replacement
- Vietnamese name: Festival Biển, and for 2025 Lễ hội Văn hóa-Du lịch biển, theme “Nha Trang say Hi!” (S11)
- Kind: festival
- When: VietnamPlus, 9 May 2025, says the province paused the 11th sea festival and replaced it with the cultural-tourism sea festival, printed as 7 June–9 July 2025 (S11). A second page that prints 7–9 June was not opened, so that shorter range is not added. The deputy chairman said the aim was another sea festival in 2026, when conditions allowed (S11). That is not a fixed 2026 date. Check on arrival.
- Where: Nha Trang. The story names a yến sào rite on Hòn Nội, a trầm hương rite at Po Nagar, and an international food event at Công viên Trần Phú (S11).
- What happens: The story says 36 cultural, sport, and arts events, a carnival parade, an international cultural exchange, and a release of aquatic stock (S11). Ten previous sea festivals over 22 years, on the same story (S11).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED. No ticket in the story.
- Not the same as attraction slug: beach, and not po-nagar. The trầm rite uses the towers as a stage. It is not the towers card.
- Photo must show: The parade or the beach-park event. The VietnamPlus lead image is described by that page as Po Nagar. A tower is not the carnival.
- unpublished: no licensed photo of the 2025 festival was opened
- Sources: S11

### Island boat day
- Vietnamese name: Not printed as a festival name (S1, S3)
- Kind: activity
- When: A day in the dry months, January–August (S1, S3). No daily departure clock was opened.
- Where: The bay. Hòn Mun, Hòn Tre, and the smaller islands named in `attractions.md` (S2, S3, S14).
- What happens: A boat day for swimming, snorkelling, and the cable island. The bureau also names glass-bottom boats at Hòn Mun (S3).
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Not the same as attraction slug: hon-mun and hon-tre. This is the circuit, not a second card for each island.
- Sources: S1, S2, S3, S14

### Mud bath
- Vietnamese name: Not printed (S1, S2)
- Kind: activity
- When: A day. No season limit beyond the city’s own calendar was printed.
- Where: Hills just north of the city (S1). Vietnam Tourism says three places and names I-Resort as the most popular, with a water park (S2). The name is a pin in `food.md` under Notes. do not publish as a pick.
- What happens: Sit in warm mud, then mineral water (S1, S2). The page also names a coffee massage and a chocolate wrap at the indulgent end (S2). No price.
- Fee: Check on arrival — UNSOURCED.
- Not the same as attraction slug: none. Yang Bay’s mud bath is a different place, in `side-trips.md` (S22).
- unpublished: no licensed photo of a mud pool
- Sources: S1, S2
