# Destination page standard

Owner feedback from the Da Nang pages, 21 Oct 2026. Da Nang is the example. Every destination follows the same rules with its own interests. Do not copy Da Nang’s beach groups onto another city.

How the page looks is `.cursor/skills/ui-ux/SKILL.md`. What a city setup contains is `.cursor/skills/city-setup/SKILL.md`. This skill is the standard both follow. The shared template reads each place’s own records. Do not hardcode one city’s venues into a template.

## Attractions

- A city or town page lists at least 15 real attractions. High-demand places carry more: Hanoi, Ho Chi Minh City, Da Nang, Hoi An, Hue, Ha Long, Sapa, Ninh Binh, Phu Quoc, Nha Trang, Da Lat.
- Scale the count with how much travelers actually do there. A single monument or park lists every sourced sight and does not pad the list with invented stops.
- The page shows 6 attraction cards. The rest sit behind a control labeled “Show all”.
- That control is a button under the cards, the full width of the list. It is not a card and it does not take a slot in the grid.
- On a wide screen the list is three cards a row, so those six are two rows and then the button. Narrower widths use fewer columns. Cards fill each row. A short line of text does not open a gap. Do not use a masonry layout.
- “Show all” reveals the rest in that same packed grid. The control stays under the full list and can collapse it. Area names stay on the attraction records. They do not start a new card grid.
- Thin stubs are not done. A finished attraction has a kicker, a sourced notice, body copy, a second photo, getting there, and hours and fees. An unsourced fee or hour stays “Check on arrival”.

## Interests

Interests are real groupings for that city, not a mixed set of tags that share one photo.

Dining and F&B is the first interest card when this place has a food guide. It opens that guide. Do not invent the card for a monument or a cave. Shopping, Resort Living, the photo gate, and the six tourism regions are `.cursor/skills/city-setup/SKILL.md`. The interest row is one slider in `.cursor/skills/ui-ux/SKILL.md`.

Da Nang’s groups, as the owner named them:

- Dining and F&B. Opens the food guide.
- Beach lifestyle. Opens to Kalà Kalà, East West, coffee, and the other beachfront places.
- Beach sports. Opens to the sports available there, with detail.
- Nightlife. Opens to the clubs and pubs.
- Culture. Opens to the museums and local sites.
- River walk. Opens to the river: coffee, coconut, the walk, the spinning Han River bridge, Han Market.

Other cities write their own groups from how people actually use that place.

Each interest card has its own photograph. The same picture is not repeated across Beach, An Thuong, and Beach night, or across any other set of cards.

## Food

The city food guide is photo-led. Dish photo cards and short captions. Not an essay, and not a list of hours. A section without a real photo stays without one. Do not duplicate another section’s photo to fill the gap.

Each dish page covers the history of the dish, why it matters culturally, texture, taste, and how to best enjoy it, plus a licensed photo and a second when one exists. Leave out any of those points you cannot source. Do not invent them.

## Events

Event pages need the same depth as attractions: what happens, when, where, and a sourced photo. An unsourced date stays unstated.

## Photos

Credit every photo. One subject per card. The owner’s Da Nang screenshots showed one beach-and-tower photo on Beach, An Thuong, and Beach night, and a food guide that is almost all text. Both fail this standard.
